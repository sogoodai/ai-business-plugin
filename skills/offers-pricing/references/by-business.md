# Pricing defaults by business type

Starting structures, not prescriptions. Where a sibling skill owns the segment, its pricing reference has more depth and wins on segment detail; this file keeps the cross-business view consistent. Sources: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Defaults table

| Business type | Price unit | Floor rule | Structure that works | Deposit or payment | Discount stance | Sibling skill |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home and field services (cleaning, pressure washing, lawn, pest, HVAC, handyman) | Flat per job; per visit for recurring | Minimum charge $130 to $250 per visit; $100 to $200 per field hour | Good-better-best on every quote, optional lines, recurring plans with falling per-visit price | 30 to 50% on material-heavy work; trip or diagnostic fee | Bundle discount as a line; three preset margins; walk away at floor | home-services |
| Appointment services (hair, lash, nails, massage, esthetics) | Per service, per provider level | Required hourly rate; long work priced by the hour | Express tier, premium tier, longer sessions at lower hourly rate, paid add-ons | Card on file; 50% for new and long services | Add-on instead of % off; friend rates dated | beauty-business |
| Fitness, studios, classes | Membership or class pack | One spot of four covers a small-group session | Intro offer converting to membership; pay-as-you-go if peak is full | Card on file, dunning before launch | Intro is a defined trial, not a discount | fitness |
| Coaching, tutoring, lessons | Package or term, not hourly | Shorter sessions keep full price | Outcome package, group next to 1:1, prepaid blocks | Prepaid blocks; plan priced 10 to 20% above pay-in-full | Hold firm; no bulk discount by default | education-coaching |
| Courses and digital products | One-time, tiered | $8 profit floor on marketplaces after fees | Self-paced / plus community / plus 1:1 | Installments for higher tiers | Launch price with a real date | digital-products, education-coaching |
| Agencies, freelancers, consultants | Retainer or project | Required hourly rate x estimated hours x risk | Productized packages, paid audit, defined allowance, retainer for changes | 50% upfront on projects; month 1 as paid pilot | Raise annually by clause; price difficult clients up | agency-freelance, professional-services |
| Restaurants, cafes, food trucks | Per item, per event minimum | Plate cost with yield; 3x ingredients + packaging for takeaway (operator) | Entry-price anchor, folded extras, catering minimums | Event minimum or guarantee; deposits for catering | Bounded off-peak occasions; recognition over discounts | restaurant |
| DTC ecommerce brands | Per product, bundles | 3x landed cost and $25+ when buying traffic | Bundles, quantity breaks, threshold free shipping, post-purchase offers | BNPL above about $250 | One or two real events a year; 10 to 15% beats 40% | ecommerce-brand |
| Handmade, Etsy, print on demand | Per item plus shipping profile | $8 to $10 profit after all fees | Premium-materials tier, custom upgrades, multi-item shipping | Full payment before custom work | Avoid permanent sales | art-handmade, ecommerce-brand |
| Amazon and marketplaces | Per listing | 45%+ gross after fees and storage (operator gate) | Pack sizes, Prime premium | n/a | Small flat coupons before deeper cuts; watch Buy Box | ecommerce-brand |
| SaaS and apps | Per month by value metric | Value at least 10x price to the buyer (practitioner heuristic) | 3 tiers on a value metric, annual at about 2 months free | Card-required trial only if signup quality allows | Never discount to acquire; discount upgrades or annual | saas-apps |
| Creators | Per product, per sponsorship | Minimum sponsorship rate; UGC priced apart from posting | Low-ticket digital products, then higher tiers | Upfront for brand deals | Founding price for memberships | creator |
| Events and weddings | Package by hours | Minimum hours (some move to 8) | Packages, paid extras gallery, travel fee plus rate | 50% to book; balance before the date | Weekday and off-season packages, not cuts | events-weddings |
| Local retail | Per item, gift cards | Keystone (2x) wholesale cost | Bundles, add-on at counter, gift cards | n/a | Event-based, not always-on | local-retail |
| B2B, wholesale, manufacturing | Price list with volume tiers | Wholesale at about half retail; 30% off with $500 minimum (handmade) | Tiers by order size, commitment and annual volume | Prepaid first order, then terms; deposit funds materials | Volume pricing only on volume ordered; enforce MAP | b2b-manufacturing |
| Rentals (equipment, trailers, event gear) | Daily, with weekly and monthly rates | Cover depreciation plus delivery | Install vs DIY pickup options, event packages | Deposit plus damage hold | Weekly and monthly multipliers | local-retail, events-weddings |

## Notes by type

### Home and field services
- Quote fixed job prices from an internal hourly model. A commercial cleaner prices 98% of accounts flat monthly on a one-year auto-renewing contract (1,990 upvotes).
- Price by visible cost drivers: stories and access for house washing, dog count for pet waste, bedrooms for cleaning (one owner publishes flat rates by bedroom count, lost a couple of jobs early, then discount requests stopped).
- Keep trip and diagnostic fees; do not credit them by habit (HVAC contractor). Appliance repair: $110 covers the first 30 minutes on site including diagnosis (33 upvotes).
- Phase large jobs: post-construction cleaning priced per phase at $0.10 to $0.15, $0.15 to $0.25 and $0.05 to $0.10 per sq ft for rough, detail and touch-up (3 upvotes, rough benchmark). Glass and high dusting as separate lines after a walk-through.
- Same-day or next-day availability is a premium, if capacity is protected for it (339 upvotes).
- Recurring plans: define inspection plus listed tasks and quote discovered repairs separately.
- Loss leader to preferred vendor: a carpet cleaner sold cheap carpet work to apartment managers, then air ducts, odor fogging, 24/7 water extraction and dryer vents at high margin (6 upvotes).

### Appointment services
- Provider-level pricing moves with each provider's utilization and waitlist, not across the board.
- Tip-inclusive pricing ("we don't accept tips") worked for one esthetician: same total spend, regulars felt they saved, new guests booked because of it (35 upvotes).
- Prep work announced and priced separately (detangling, clarifying) with time reserved (338 and 313 upvotes).
- Paid consultation credited toward the service.
- Surcharges stored in the client profile so they apply next time.

### Coaching, tutoring, lessons
- Sell outcomes and terms: $2,500 a quarter with weekly meetings, 7 contracts, $17,500 (187 upvotes). Group at $2,000 next to 1:1 at $8,000 (12 upvotes).
- Lessons are reserved time; prepay 4+ and price single lessons higher.
- Private music lessons priced at or just under the local school's family rate, roughly double what the school pays teachers (20 upvotes). In-home lessons carry a premium: $50 per 30 min, $80 per 60 min (24 upvotes).
- Marketplace net: Preply takes 33% falling to 18% after 400 hours; a $20 lesson nets about $14 (30 upvotes). Move repeat students to direct billing where terms allow.
- Paid pilot for the first 3 to 5 clients at $300 to $500 for 30 days, framed as a feedback group (proposal).

### Agencies and freelancers
- Fee models practitioners use: % of ad spend with lower rates above thresholds; 10 to 15% revenue share plus a monthly minimum; retainer $1,500 to $2,000 plus a per-lead fee; no fee until profitable for one affiliate client (43 upvotes).
- Hourly floor includes overhead: to net $75 an hour bill about $100, so a $400 a month client buys at most 4 hours of everything (4 upvotes).
- Show three budget options next to the prospect's budget ($500 / $1,000 / $1,500 with work and expected outcome).
- Complexity multiplier: X when tools integrate cleanly, 3X when they do not (9 upvotes).
- Clients who left and came back paid double the old monthly fee (105 upvotes).
- Paid audit instead of a free trial; productized entry offer with a fixed outcome and deadline.
- Define the post-delivery allowance (for example 10 hours of tweaks) and offer a retainer (one suggested $250 a month) for continuing changes.

### Restaurants and food trucks
- App menus: markup = in-store price / (1 - commission): 15% needs +17.6%, 30% needs +42.9% (restaurant skill).
- Event floor for trucks: $400 to move plus $100 per open hour plus $20 an hour per extra staffer (38 upvotes); "$500 to show, guaranteed" with sales counted against it (operator); private events $1,200 for 2 hours plus $400 per extra hour (11 upvotes).
- Price catering from the profit target for that date: $750 for a June Saturday evening vs $500 for a November weekday lunch, plus food, labor and mileage (206 upvotes).
- One conspicuously cheap anchor item keeps budget guests coming.
- Gift cards for dine-in only and not valid at peak keep them from displacing paying covers (7 upvotes).

### DTC and handmade products
- Raise the price of your most labor-intensive bestseller first.
- Include shipping in the price for single-item buys; set a threshold for baskets. Flat $3 shipping across 5,500 small items worked for a craft supply store; heavy international orders had to move to actual rates.
- Collect duties at checkout for cross-border buyers.
- Wholesale: 30% off retail with a $500 minimum works for one maker supplying galleries (23 upvotes); a $100 minimum with free next-day delivery won small accounts from big distributors (13 upvotes).
- Protect MAP: about 10 unauthorized sellers dropped one product from $79.99 to about $65 after a loose wholesale push (35 upvotes).
- Expensive custom work: full payment and a stated production window.

### SaaS and apps
- Value metric test: does the customer get more value as they use more of it?
- Free tier limit at a natural usage threshold most targets exceed: a wedding app's free tier covers 100 guests when most weddings have 120 to 200+ (558 upvotes, "best conversion driver").
- Event-length products sell as passes, not monthly plans.
- A $19 a month Pro plan converted 0.24% of 40,000 monthly visitors and was dropped (84 upvotes). Check paid conversion before building a paywall.
- Hard paywalls convert about 5x more users to paid within 35 days than freemium with similar one-year retention (RevenueCat 2026, cited by coreyhaines31).

### Creators
- UGC priced separately from posting: $500 to $12,000, averaging about $700; sponsored posts $1,000 minimum at 450,000 combined followers (177 upvotes).
- A $20 guide pushed in about 20 Reels a month sold 300+ in month one (278 upvotes, authenticity questioned).
- Paid live class next to free content: 25-seat candle-making classes every 2 to 3 months filled almost every time while the same material stayed free online (1,888 upvotes).

### Events and weddings
- Weekday and off-season packages protect peak dates.
- Travel: rate plus travel costs plus a fee; keep the normal rate and negotiate accommodation separately for destination work.
- Count travel or hike time as paid coverage (209 upvotes).
- Archive fees for old sessions: $50 to $100 delivered by link (111 upvotes, proposal).
- Gallery hosting: 2 years included, $50 a year after (132 upvotes).
