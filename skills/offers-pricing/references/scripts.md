# Pricing scripts, verbatim

Wording practitioners used or that the research supports. Fill brackets with real facts; never invent a benefit, cost increase or deadline. Quotes marked with upvotes come from Reddit threads listed in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Price increases

**Retainer or recurring client, 60 to 90 days ahead** (7 of 22 clients left, revenue +12%; 196 upvotes):
> Hey [name], I wanted to give you a heads up that starting January my rates will be [new rate]. This reflects the growth in my skills, the results I've been delivering for your business, and an adjustment to market rates. I completely understand if this doesn't fit your budget and I'm happy to recommend someone else if needed. If you'd like to continue working together at the new rate, no action needed on your end.

**Subscription or membership, 30 to 60 days ahead** (research report, 2026):
> On [date], your price will change from [old price] to [new price]. Since your current price was set, we've added [specific benefits], improved [measurable service level], and absorbed higher [relevant cost, if true]. Your service and billing terms otherwise stay the same. If you'd like to review the best plan for your current usage before the change, reply to this email.

**Text to regular clients:**
> Hi [name], quick heads up: from [date] my [service] will be [new price]. Anything you book before then stays at [old price]. Thank you for being one of my regulars.

**Prepay to keep the old rate:**
> From [date] my rate goes to [new]. If you'd like to keep [old], you can prepay a block of [5 or 10] sessions before [date].

**Cost-driven product increase** (shown as its own line, proposal, 6 upvotes):
> Our [material or import] costs rose [x]% this year. We added [$ amount] to cover it, not a cent more. If those costs come down, this goes away.

**In the chair or on the phone, when a client mentions it:**
> Yes, prices went up this year, like most things. Same care, same time with you. Shall I hold your usual slot?

**Annual contract clause:**
> Fees are reviewed each January and may increase by up to [10]%. We will confirm any change in writing 60 days before it applies.

## Holding price

**"My friend got it cheaper"** (prospect returned 3 days later and paid $150 CAD; 20 upvotes):
> I can assure you the lowest I've ever done this was $120, and that was because they brought multiple things to fix.

**"Can you do it cheaper?" (service):**
> I can't do the same job for less, but I can do less of the job. For [lower budget] I can [smaller scope]. Which would you prefer?

**Budget below your price** (photographer, 13 upvotes):
> My full-day coverage is [price]. If that's above your budget, a lot of couples choose [5] hours instead of [10] and we plan the timeline around the photos that matter most to you.

**Discount request because the client is small** (cleaner, 10 upvotes):
> The price is based on the work, not the size of my office.

**Last-ditch counter on an already discounted package** (r/sweatystartup, when you can afford to lose the job): decline politely and stop. "That's already our package price. I understand if it doesn't work this time."

**Corrective work after a cheaper competitor** (18 upvotes, replies agree):
> Fixing this is a different job from the original clean. It's [full price], same as my first quote.

## Quoting with options

**Good-better-best on a service quote:**
> Here are three ways to handle it.
> 1. [Repair]: fixes today's problem. [$]
> 2. [Repair + prevention]: fixes it and [stops it coming back for X]. [$] Most customers choose this.
> 3. [Full replacement + protection]: [outcome], [warranty]. [$]
> Optional add-ons: [item, $], [item, $].

**Price the difference:**
> Same-day service is +$40. Annual billing saves you $240 a year.

**Water supply option** (pressure washing, 35 upvotes):
> We are more than happy to provide the water, so we don't have to use yours. There will be an additional $200 charge for us to bring water, due to the increase in labor of having to fill tanks, and make trips back to the shop. Or, we can use your water for $250.

**Small-change threshold** (web services, 3 upvotes):
> Text edits under 15 minutes are included. Anything bigger gets a quote first.

**Intro price with an end** (3 upvotes):
> Your first project is 20% off as an intro rate. Normal rates apply after.

**Launch special that does not reset the price** (computer repair, first customer rebooked at normal price):
> This is a startup special, not my regular pricing: [package] for [price] until [date].

## Discounts done right

**Visible discount line:**
> [Service A] $[full]
> [Service B] $[full]
> Bundle discount (booked together) -$[x]
> Total $[y]

**Hardship discount tied to eligibility** (306 upvotes):
> Low income / government assistance: [x]% discount.

**Portfolio discount tied to usage rights** (client chose full price instead; 308 upvotes for the approach):
> The use of your photos for marketing offsets the $900 I discounted from my normal fees. If you wish to deny the use of the photos for marketing purposes, I will have to charge my normal rate.

**Friend or family rate:**
> For you it's [rate] for this one, my gift. From the next visit it's my normal [price], same as everyone.

**Ending a friend rate that ran too long:**
> I've loved having you in the chair. I've kept you at [old] for a long time, and from [date] I need to move you to my regular [price]. If that doesn't work, I completely understand.

**Friend asks you to work their wedding "as a guest"** (19 upvotes, a reply said increase the amounts):
> Before retiring, I was charging $1,599 for 4 hours of coverage, but since you're friends I would only be charging $500 for 4 hours, as that will reimburse me for travel, feeding myself, the hours on the day and the work on your gallery afterwards.

## Offers and upsells

**Cart upsell:**
> Add 1 more product for 10% off.

**Post-purchase companion offer** (6% take rate; 2,730 upvotes):
> So your travel partner doesn't feel left out: add a second one at [deep discount], one click.

**Bundle as bonus** (387 upvotes; test on profit):
> Get [A] for $45 and we'll include [B] and free shipping if you order today.

**Take-home product included in the service** (no objections reported):
> This service today costs $[X] and includes a shampoo and conditioner as a bonus for you to maintain your color at home.

**Upgrade check during a service:**
> Are we upgrading to deep tissue today? No? Then we'll keep the pressure at a nice 6 to 6.5, and if at any time you'd like more, we can upgrade you.

**Contrast upsell on site** (pressure washing, worked):
> Here's the strip I did by accident. Want the rest of the pool deck to match? It's [$].

**Paid audit instead of a free trial** (agency):
> I don't do free trials, but I do a 7-day audit at [price]. I'll show you exactly where the budget is wasted, and you decide whether to hire me after.

## Guarantees

**Specific guarantee** (research report, 2026):
> Try [offer] for [period]. If [specific promised condition] is not met, contact us at [channel] and we will [refund / redo / credit] the [defined amount]. No hidden eligibility rules.

**Service redo guarantee:**
> If anything we cleaned isn't right, tell us within 48 hours and we'll come back and redo it free.

**Conditional outcome guarantee:**
> Do [the steps / attend all sessions / use as directed] for [period]. If you don't get [result], we [refund in full / keep working free until you do].

## Payment terms

**Deposit on booking:**
> To hold your date I take a [50]% non-refundable deposit; the balance is due [on the day / 7 days before].

**Lessons as reserved time** (15 and 44 upvotes):
> You're not paying for lessons. You're paying to hold a regular time slot in my week.

**Installments:**
> It's [$3,000] in full, or 3 payments of [$1,100]. The first payment holds your start date.

**Wholesale counter-offer** (candle maker, 925 upvotes):
> I'd love to work together. To start, I can do [smaller quantity] prepaid. If it sells through, we expand and talk about terms.

## Save flow and cancellation

> What's the main reason you're leaving?
> (Temporary) Would you like to pause until [date] instead? Nothing will be charged until then.
> (Wrong fit) [Lower tier / different frequency] might suit you better: [details].
> (Price) [Targeted, temporary concession, only if margin supports it.]
> Otherwise: Done, your plan is cancelled as of [date]. If you ever want it back, [link].
