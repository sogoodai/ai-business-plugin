## Gaps: no robust benchmark exists for

- Price elasticity by small-business category. Measure it with the owner's own cohorts.
- Optimal deposit size, guarantee length or number of tiers.
- Win rate by quote price in local services, beyond vendor anecdotes.
- Membership churn for local service memberships outside beauty and fitness vendor data.

Say so instead of inventing one.
