# Pricing and offer mistakes that cost money

Two tables: what the data and arithmetic show, and what operators report losing. Sources: [sources.md](sources.md).

## From the data

| Mistake | What it costs | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Judging a price cut on conversion rate | -10% price and +10% conversion = 0.99x revenue per visitor and lower profit (Stripe pricing review framework, 2025) | Judge on contribution per visitor or lead |
| Matching a 20%-off competitor at 40% margin | Halves gross profit per unit; needs 2x units to stand still | Hold price, add value, or segment with a lighter offer |
| Frequent discounting | One 20% coupon raised expected future discount frequency and depth; modeled long-run profit loss above 10% (randomized retailer trial, 2023) | Value-adds first; cash discounts with a reason, an end date and a target segment |
| No post-purchase or in-quote add-ons | Median +18% session value from post-purchase offers, top quartile +33% (Funnelish 2026); optional quote items accepted 25 to 50% (Jobber 2026) | One adjacent add-on before checkout or in the quote, one after |
| Only 16% of home-service quotes use good-better-best (Jobber, Dec 2025) | Yes/no quotes forgo the upgrade buyers would have taken | Three real options on every eligible quote |
| Chasing subscription churn at month six | About 61% of voluntary churn happens at renewals 2 and 3 (Recharge 2026) | Onboarding, skip and save offers before reorder 2 |
| Guarantee as a badge | Trust badges alone -6.61% add-to-cart (not significant); guarantee copy alone +28.34% (Ingversions 2025) | Specific promise in words, near the buy button; test by device |
| Loud risk copy ("TAKE NO RISK!") | Lowered engagement and later purchases in some of three field experiments, 452,632+ consumers (2024) | Calm, specific mechanism |
| Assuming BNPL and post-purchase upsells stack on Shopify | Native post-purchase offers do not show for Klarna, Affirm, Afterpay or some wallet payments (Shopify docs 2026) | Net the BNPL lift against lost upsell exposure |
| Copying a 14-day trial | 62% of trial products use 14 days; a randomized 680,588-user test found trial length changes adoption and timing, not immediate conversion (2025) | Test length against your product's time-to-value |
| Annual discount set by habit | At 3.2% monthly churn the break-even annual discount is 16%; 25 to 30% gives money away | Compute from your churn; default 2 months free |
| Charm pricing as a strategy | Near-zero between-subject effect in a 2023 meta-analysis; 2022 preregistered replication failed | Test only when the left digit changes; round prices for premium |
| Using 2024 click-to-cancel guidance | US rule vacated July 8, 2025; UK added new subscription duties in 2026 | Check current law per market |
| Fake former prices and resetting countdowns | EU requires the lowest 30-day price for any reduction claim; US FTC treats fictitious former prices as deceptive | Only real reference prices and real deadlines |
| Hidden mandatory fees | Banned for all prices in the UK (DMCC, April 2025) and California (SB 478, 2024); US FTC rule for tickets and lodging (May 2025) | Show the all-in price first |

## Reported by operators

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Discounting because you like the customer | Detailer charged $1,000 on a $2,000 job: about 40 hours plus 4 hours' daily driving (2,813-upvote thread) | Normal price; a gift is a gift, stated as such |
| Underpricing an unfamiliar job | $600 quote for mold remediation others price $4,000 to $7,000; PPE alone $100+ (127 upvotes) | Scope in person, test a unit, price the risk or decline |
| Missing cost lines on big quotes | Commercial HVAC job planned at a healthy margin cleared 8% (callback, refrigerant, drive time, rental) | Estimate template with every recurring line |
| Discount without a stated end | $250 sympathy rate became the expected price; a $600 follow-on quote lost that client and the referrer (612 upvotes) | Write "one-time" and the full price on the invoice |
| Giving away extra requests | 15 small post-project requests = 12 unpaid hours ($600), then a 1-star "hidden fees" review when charging started (876 upvotes) | Price from request number 2; define an included allowance upfront |
| Friend discount with no end | 50% off for 18 months on 2 to 3 hour visits, no tip; it became the standing rate (107 upvotes) | End date in writing, or quiet slots only |
| Stale discount links | Former client booked an old private rate months later (141 upvotes) | Expire every link and code |
| Flat coupon with no minimum | $5 abandoned-cart coupon turned a $6 item into a $1 order (r/Etsy) | Minimum order on flat coupons; audit when adding cheap items |
| Coupon leaked to another store | Germany-only 30% code used on the Austrian store: 400 orders, about EUR 18,000 lost (36 upvotes, disputed) | Restrict codes by market, product and use count |
| Coupon that fails at checkout | Customer tried a printed insert coupon and it did not work (150 upvotes) | Test every code end to end before printing |
| Free giveaway to "get the name out" | Oil change giveaway: people brought several cars and demanded more; 50%-off first clean got 2 leads from 100 flyers, both ghosted | Paid intro with a clear normal price after |
| Trial gift before attendance | Free guitar for booking a trial: 2 bookings, 0 attended, both kept the guitar (23 upvotes) | Gift on completion of the first paid lesson |
| Underquoting for reviews | Cleaner lost money on early jobs bought with low quotes (36 upvotes); discounts for reviews also break Google policy | Premium list price, temporary launch discount shown as a line, ask every client for a review without incentive |
| Discount for promised future volume | "I'll be back with a bigger order": returning customers countable on one hand in a year (r/smallbusiness) | Volume pricing applies to volume actually ordered |
| Discount justified by expected referrals | 25% off client referred more problem clients expecting 25% off (300 and 58 upvotes) | Referral reward paid after the referred job |
| Paying for a 3PL or POD premium blindly | POD hoodie at about $40 landed against $8 to $10 blanks left 10 to 15% profit (42 upvotes) | Landed cost per channel before choosing fulfillment |
| Marketplace fee blindness | Etsy $18.99 bestseller lost $0.80 per sale once all fees counted (35 upvotes); Redbubble kept 73 to 84% of earnings in several reports | Per-order fee audit; reprice or delist |
| Processor headline rate | Gym pitched about 5%, paid close to 10% | Effective rate from statements |
| Free shipping on heavy international orders | Several heavy orders at a flat international fee lost "substantial" money (craft supply seller) | Actual international rates or weight bands |
| Always-on sale | Frequent buyer refuses stores that always have a sale (16 upvotes); an always-on 10% was dropped with no visible sales loss (82 upvotes) | One or two real sale events a year |
| Cutting markup to buy volume | Markups 30% to 20% to 10%: 10x sales, 500 customers, still losing money (29 upvotes) | Raise price, accept lower volume |
| Long deep launch discounts | 6 to 7 months of launch discounts "burning money"; 33% net after raising (13 upvotes, disputed) | Launch price with a date, then list price |
| Giving away inventory to buy Amazon rank | 250+ units (half a shipment) for a brief page-one spot, about $4,000 loss | Small coupon and ads with a margin cap |
| Accepting net 60 and sale-or-return from a big buyer | Would have tied up inventory cash; the buyer refused a smaller prepaid trial (925 upvotes) | Prepaid trial order, then terms |
| Lowering price repeatedly for the same scope | Buyer distrusted the first quote after 2 to 3 cuts (11 upvotes) | Every reduction removes scope |
| Surprise surcharges | $2 dressing ended a twice-monthly regular; a missed long-hair surcharge caused a dispute | Fold into the price, or state before the service |
| High-pressure close with falling price | $7,400 coaching quote, cuts, one-hour deadline, threats: prospect left and warned others (186 upvotes) | One price, one honest deadline, walk away calmly |
| Annual memberships without payment recovery | About 23% of discounted annual gym members stopped paying on failed cards | Card updater, retries, dunning before launch |
| Late fee after you moved the appointment | $15 fee for 5 minutes lost a new client (70 upvotes) | Full price, shortened service, no fee |
