# 90-day plan

For an existing wedding or event business with at least one season of history. A brand-new vendor first builds 2 to 3 real or clearly disclosed showcase events and a price page (r/weddingvideography 18: 2 to 3 showcase weddings, 27 upvotes), then joins at week 1. Order follows the research pass (2026): instrument, repair conversion, build owned demand, build the referral engine, test one paid channel, then monetize empty inventory. Never buy traffic before weeks 1 to 4 are done.

Each phase ends with a short owner report: what changed, the numbers, what they need to decide.

## Weeks 1 to 2: instrument

**Do:**
- Export 12 to 24 months of inquiries from the CRM or inbox. Backfill at least the last 20 to 50 completed inquiries by hand if needed (research pass; an operating number).
- Add mandatory fields: first source, last trigger, named referrer, event date, venue, guest count or scope, budget band, qualified (yes/no), first-reply timestamp, two-way (yes/no), consult or tour, proposal value, signed, collected, direct job cost, lost reason.
- Add both questions to the form and call script: "How did you first hear about us?" and "What made you reach out today?"
- Merge every inquiry channel into one inbox: website, email, text, Instagram DMs, marketplace messages, phone (inquiries split across six channels, HoneyBook 2026).
- Compute the scorecard in [economics.md](economics.md): stage rates by vendor funnel, median first reply (weekday hours vs evenings and weekends), cohort booking rate by source, contribution per booking, required average booking, CMER by source, peak dates open for 12 and 24 months.

**Exit check:** every new inquiry carries source, date, venue, budget band and timestamps; the scorecard exists with denominators and cohort dates.

**Owner questions:** income target and capacity per year; which dates are "prime"; which offers they refuse; contract end dates for every paid listing.

## Weeks 3 to 4: repair the reply and the price page

**Do:**
- Instant useful auto-reply (availability, starting price, typical spend, booking link) plus a plain SMS pointing to the email ([scripts.md](scripts.md) 1 and 2).
- Human reply inside an hour in waking hours, 7 days a week in season. Phone-ready templates.
- Test deliverability: send the reply to a fresh Gmail account and check the tab it lands in.
- Price page: starting price, typical spend, what changes the price, minimums, inclusions, findable in under a minute.
- Follow-up cadence of 4+ touches over 10 to 14 days; self-scheduling link for calls or tours ([inquiry-to-booking.md](inquiry-to-booking.md)).
- Booking rule and footer on every pre-booking email; contract link expiry.
- Contract gaps from [contracts-payments.md](contracts-payments.md) sent to the owner's lawyer as a brief.

**Exit check:** median first reply under 1 hour including evenings for the last 2 weeks; price visible on the site; every open inquiry has a scheduled next touch.

**Owner questions:** approve the starting price and typical range for the site; approve the deposit and balance schedule; who covers evenings and weekends.

## Weeks 5 to 6: owned demand

**Do:**
- Google Business Profile check: category, service area (address hidden if no storefront), services, photos, review link (`local-seo`).
- Homepage = primary city and service; service pages not geo-targeted; one page per real secondary market; one evergreen page per venue with search demand where the owner has worked; one /reviews page; minimum-field contact form ([lead-sources.md](lead-sources.md)).
- Niche pages where the owner has real work: courthouse, elopement, micro wedding, LGBTQ+, cultural and religious weddings, destination, venue types.
- Decision content that answers expensive questions in text: "what does [category] cost in [city]", "what's included in a venue rental", rainy-day galleries.
- Corporate page with specs as text if the business can serve corporate ([seasonality-and-corporate.md](seasonality-and-corporate.md)).
- Instagram: carousel of finished work after every reel, venue tags, send-worthy hooks; KPI = sends per reach and watch time (`organic-social`).

**Exit check:** pages live and indexed; GBP complete; three send-worthy posts published with sends per reach recorded.

**Owner questions:** which venues and niches they want more of; which real weddings can be published (consent).

## Weeks 7 to 8: referral engine and reviews

**Do:**
- Build the partner table: top 20 to 30 venues, planners and vendors by overlap ([referrals-and-reviews.md](referrals-and-reviews.md)).
- Send each one something useful first (vendor gallery, a referral, open-day help), then the preferred-list pitch ([scripts.md](scripts.md) 13).
- Google review on each venue worked in the last season with photos and handle.
- Review system: same request to every client 1 to 3 weeks after delivery; one reminder; reply to all reviews.
- Wedding-day visibility plays chosen with the owner (day-before post, teaser within days, same-day slideshow or print).

**Exit check:** 10+ partners contacted with a give; referrals sent and received logged by name; review requests automated; reviews in the last 2 years at 5+ on the main platform or a plan to get there (WeddingPro 2026).

**Owner questions:** which partners they'd refer to with confidence; any commission or bundle deals they'd consider.

## Weeks 9 to 10: one paid channel, properly

**Do (only if weeks 1 to 4 pass):**
- Photo, video, DJ: Google Search on geo + service, venue and niche terms. Conversion = submitted inquiry only. Negatives for jobs, courses, cheap, free, school, travel.
- Visual venues, florals, rentals, decor: Meta to a price guide, cost calculator or brochure, instant follow-up, self-scheduled tour (`paid-ads`).
- Set kill and scale rules before launch: `max CPL = contribution x booking rate / target CMER` ([economics.md](economics.md)).
- Marketplace decisions: keep, cut or renegotiate on cohort CMER after the booking lag; read auto-renew and cancellation terms.

**Exit check:** conversion tracking verified with a test inquiry; budget, max CPL and review date approved.

**Owner questions:** monthly test budget; target CMER (start at 3); which dates the ads should fill.

## Weeks 11 to 12: monetize empty inventory

**Do:**
- List open weekdays and off-months for the next 12 months.
- Launch one defined product for them: micro wedding, courthouse, proposal, weekday corporate, holiday event, office florals, uplighting rental ([seasonality-and-corporate.md](seasonality-and-corporate.md)). Own page, own form, never on peak dates.
- Price review: open peak dates vs last year; if prime dates fill earlier than target, test 5 to 10% on new inquiries ([pricing-packages.md](pricing-packages.md)).

**Exit check:** off-season product live with its own page; price decision made with the break-even math.

## Day-90 report

Report only metrics with a denominator and cohort date (research pass, 2026):

- qualified inquiries, by source
- median first reply, weekday vs evening and weekend
- two-way rate, consult or tour rate, proposal rate, booking rate (per vendor funnel)
- average booked value, contribution per booking
- CAC and CMER by source, booking ROAS and collected-cash ROAS for paid channels
- referrals sent and received by partner
- reviews in the last 2 years, new reviews this quarter
- peak dates open for the next 12 and 24 months
- top 3 lost reasons
- the next three decisions for the owner

Cut channels whose cohorts are mature and below target; keep immature ones on watch with a date.
