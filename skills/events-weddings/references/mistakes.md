# Expensive mistakes

Two tables: what the data shows (measured, or arithmetic on measured numbers) and what practitioners report losing. Sources in [sources.md](sources.md).

## From the data

| Mistake | What it costs | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Choosing channels by cost per lead | Tahoe venue: WeddingWire $21, The Knot $29, Google $136 per lead, with no booking data; with Mikla tour rates the gap per tour shrinks from 6x to under 3x and depends on booking value (IVGID 2023 to 2024; Mikla 2026) | Cohort CMER per source after the booking lag ([economics.md](economics.md)) |
| Treating marketplace leads like direct ones | Knot leads tour at 10% vs 25.4% for direct email; 66% never reply after the first answer (Mikla 2026) | Faster reply, price and link in the first message, move to text or email; budget more labor per marketplace lead |
| Replying next morning to evening inquiries | 30% vs 23% booking for under 1 hour vs over 24 hours; 42% of inquiries arrive after hours (Reunion 2026, correlation). 25 qualified a month x 7 points x $2,500 = $4,375 a month exposed | Instant useful auto-reply plus SMS, human within the hour 7 days a week in season |
| Replying once and waiting | About a third of venue tours happen only after a follow-up (Mikla 2026) | 4+ touches over 10 to 14 days, then close the loop |
| Hiding prices | 23% of Irish couples won't inquire with a venue that hides prices, up from 19% (WeddingDates 2026); 77% rank price first when choosing whom to contact (WeddingPro 2026) | Starting price, typical spend, drivers, minimums on the site |
| Counting weak ad conversions | Bidding trained on clicks and page views; an account counting only submitted forms went from 0.99% to 2.43% click-to-inquiry (Live PPC Ads 2026, agency case) | Only submitted inquiries are conversions; negatives for jobs, courses, cheap, free |
| Discounting the whole year to fill winter | 76% of weddings are May to October, December 1% (The Knot 2026): a blanket discount sells scarce June Saturdays cheaper | Separate weekday and off-season products; peak dates at full price |
| Small package on a peak date | Example: $4,800 date sold for $2,200 = $2,200 of displacement cost ([economics.md](economics.md)) | Accept only packages at 80%+ of the date's normal contribution on the top 20 dates |
| Social engagement as the sales KPI | Social averaged 14.7% of bookings vs 40.2% for referrals (81-vendor survey, 2026) | Track sends per reach and inquiries named "Instagram"; fund referrals first |
| Recycled or watermarked Instagram content | Recommendation reach favors original content, watch time and sends (Buffer summary of Meta, 2025 to 2026) | Original reels plus a carousel of finished work |
| Review gating or incentives | Google can block new reviews, remove reviews or show a warning; FTC rule (2024) bans sentiment-conditioned rewards | Same neutral request to every client |
| "Non-refundable" as a magic word | UK and Australian guidance: retained sums must match the date, work done and likely loss (Sprintlaw 2026) | Lawyer-reviewed staged cancellation tied to real loss |
| Assuming marketplace pricing is a national rate | No public rate card; varies by market and category (The Knot Worldwide 2025 to 2026) | Get the quote for your market and category; month-to-month |
| Blending vendor types into one close rate | A venue tour rate and a florist consult rate measure different things (research pass 2026) | Stage rates per vendor funnel |
| Buying a "20% close rate" benchmark | No public category quartiles exist (research pass 2026) | Learn the owner's rate from 12 to 24 months of cohorts |

## From practitioners

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Waiting on an undecided first couple while two others wanted the date | All three lost (r/WeddingPhotography 88) | First signed contract plus fee gets the date |
| Booking 6 days out with only a verbal venue name | Wrong venue, an hour searching, full refund (r/WeddingPhotography 241) | Full addresses and both partners' phones at signing |
| Accepting incomplete venue details | 11-hour, three-location itinerary 72 hours before the wedding (r/WeddingPhotography 1150) | Locations, hours and start time before taking the retainer |
| Unanswered itinerary, no reconfirmation | Missed wedding, full refund, bad review threat (r/WeddingPhotography 1641) | Final-month meeting, reconfirm 2 days out, call the venue if silent |
| Associate without clock times | Paid 8 hours on a 6-hour booking (r/WeddingPhotography 333) | Written start, end and extension rule |
| Not checking parking at every location | $75 for under an hour at a hotel first look (r/WeddingPhotography 333) | Check every location, reconfirm 30 days out, build into price |
| Payment exception for a friend | One-third of the balance arrived, rest unpaid after the wedding (r/WeddingPhotography 98) | Balance 30 days out, no exceptions |
| Exception for a planner's niece | A week of ghosting before payment (r/WeddingPhotography 110) | Same terms for everyone |
| Delivering before payment for friends of friends | Unpaid balance plus quality complaints (r/photography 1440) | Nothing ships before the balance |
| $250 retainer | 4 cancellations in a year and haggling (r/WeddingPhotography 128) | 30 to 50% booking payment |
| Taking full payment a year early and spending it | Couple cancelled, $3,000 refund minus deposit (r/weddingvideography 33) | Hold unearned cash apart |
| Booking more than a year out at a portfolio rate | Dispute handled at $900 after prices had risen (r/weddingvideography 18) | Cap far-out dates at old prices or add a price-review clause |
| 4-hour package on a wedding | Ceremony 45 minutes late, portraits cut to 10 minutes (r/WeddingPhotography 208) | 8-hour minimum on full weddings; short packages on weekdays |
| Smallest package available on a peak Saturday | Couple downgraded by about 1k after booking (r/WeddingPhotography 46) | Remove it from peak dates |
| $1,000 off a 200+ guest August wedding demanded | Held the price, lost the booking, relieved (r/WeddingPhotography 137) | Hold; offer scope changes |
| $600 full day for a friend of a friend who started at $400 | Complaints and legal threats despite 300 photos vs 250 promised (r/WeddingPhotography 284) | Normal price and contract, or decline |
| Heavily discounted fill-in weddings | More complaints than full-price weddings (r/WeddingPhotography 113) | Tight written scope, or skip |
| Underpricing video at $800 | 78 weddings, 100-hour weeks (r/weddingvideography 16) | Required-booking math; double and measure |
| Pricing too low for the market | Couples loved the work and declined because the price made them doubt it (r/WeddingPhotography 50) | Price at or above the local middle with proof |
| Short video booking priced by hours on site | 28+ editing hours for a 4-hour booking (r/weddingvideography 19) | Price total production hours |
| Undefined film lengths | Length dispute (r/weddingvideography 18) | Min and max per film in the package |
| Setup and ceremony film not defined | Disputes over arrival time and a full ceremony clip (r/weddingvideography 19) | Coverage includes setup; list every film |
| Body reshaping not excluded | Refund (r/WeddingPhotography 80) | Say it at the consult, price it |
| 800px web files delivered without explanation | Couple printed at home, complained of grain 3 months later (r/photography 1440) | Explain web vs print; print through a lab gallery |
| Livestream on an untested platform | Relatives couldn't watch; public complaint (r/weddingvideography 23) | Rehearse the exact platform and guest access |
| One camera, one setting for the family photo | All 10 frames out of focus (r/WeddingPhotography 410) | 2 to 3 frames per body |
| Only the couple's side covered in the ceremony | Complaint about the groom's family (r/weddingvideography 139) | Both families in the shot list |
| Using an unsolicited dinner-break reveal | Meal break gone, couple declined to watch (r/WeddingPhotography 286, 64 upvotes) | Mini album in the card box or a next-day email |
| Sitting on a known double booking | Lost a referral partner for good (r/WeddingPhotography 1641 thread) | Disclose immediately |
| Disclosing a possible associate late in the consult | Couple ready to sign booked elsewhere (r/WeddingPhotography 49) | Disclose and show the associate's portfolio first |
| Hiring an unvetted second shooter | Second shooter had an affair with the groom; refund demand (r/WeddingPhotography 1287) | Vet second shooters like employees |
| Raw-only discount package | Client couldn't open the files, tried to withhold payment (r/WeddingPhotography 141, 121 upvotes) | Test a sample file first, or don't sell raw-only |
| Expenses-only destination barter | Labor with no money and no local booking value (r/WeddingPhotography 223, 221 upvotes) | Travel plus a fee, capped hours |
| Generic street address on the Google profile | Profile suspended after 5 years of reviews and top-3 ranking (r/GoogleMyBusiness 18) | Service-area business with hidden address (`local-seo`) |
| Instagram Collaborations on portfolio posts | Posts deleted when collaborators deleted their accounts (r/WeddingPhotography 92) | Tag instead of collaborating on posts you need to keep |
| Bringing a live bid to a paid coach who competes | Coach underbid and took the job after a $3,600 program (r/WeddingPhotography 232) | Never share identifiable live bids with competitors |
| Marketplace contract signed on a sales call | Cancellation refused 5 days later, debt collection (r/weddingvideography 19) | Terms in writing, month-to-month |
| Free headshots emailed to 30+ strangers | 3 takers, no bookings (r/WeddingPhotography 119) | Serve partners you've worked with |
| General January bridal show | 0 bookings vs 5 at a niche show (r/WeddingPhotography 119) | Pick shows by audience |
| Accepting a gig from a venue with a payment history | Three earlier DJs never paid (r/DJs 139) | Ask the current act before accepting |
| Buying one-off decor for a client | Inventory that never rents again (r/sweatystartup 18) | Recover full cost on the first rental |
| Producing a bigger repeat event without a deal | No bonus on an $80K net event (r/EventProduction 25) | Negotiate a profit share first |
| Florist pricing at 2.5 to 3x in a high-cost area | Clients ghosted at the total anyway (r/florists 886) | Find the right customer and design to their budget, don't cut the multiplier |
| Fixed-price florist contracts during wholesale increases | Expected losses at a 10% margin (r/smallbusiness, 175 upvotes) | Substitution and price-change clauses |
