# Expensive mistakes

Two tables: what the data shows (measured, or arithmetic on measured numbers) and what practitioners report losing.

## From the data

| Mistake | What it costs | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Buying leads nobody answers | 57% of inquiries got no reply; paid leads became members at 3 to 6% (Keepme 2026) | One inbox, named owner per channel, instant reply with two times, after-hours self-booking |
| Optimizing Meta to CPL | $27.11 median lead = $452 to $904 media per member at 3 to 6% (WordStream 2026 x Keepme 2026, arithmetic) | Optimize to booked, attended and member events; judge on cost per retained member |
| Optimizing Search to CPL | $67.36 lead = $1,123 to $2,245 per member at 3 to 6% (arithmetic) | Same; Search may convert better, measure it |
| No reminder before a booked visit | 55% of operators send none; only 4 to 8 of 100 paid leads reach a visit (Keepme 2026) | Confirmation, day-before reminder with reschedule link, same-day reschedule on a miss |
| Ignoring one point of retention | 92.7% to 93.7% at 100 members x $179 is about $26,000 a year (Two-Brain 2026, scenario) | Model retention value before approving ad budget |
| Default discounting | No-discount gyms earn about $2,900 more a month at the median (Two-Brain 2026, confounded) | Dated one-week offers for dry spells; paid intro instead of permanent cuts |
| Free or endless trials | Intro buyers retain 54% at 90 days vs 35% without (Mindbody 2025, association) | Paid, short, multi-visit intro; consult-first for gyms |
| Counting all ClassPass revenue as incremental | Studios fill under 36% direct, but peak classes would sell anyway (ClassPass data) | Off-peak inventory only; net out displaced direct revenue per class-hour |
| Judging a microgym against big-box retention | 66.4% annual (about 3.4% monthly) vs 7.3% monthly median for microgyms | Compare with the closest sample, then with your own cohorts |
| Underpricing to a "market rate" | US PT middle half spans $50 to $110 (iPT 2026, stale pages) | Price from coach-hour contribution, capacity and package contents |
| Online coaching run as an acquisition machine | 45% of clients left by month 12, median tenure 3.2 months (Coachway 2026) | Weekly human check-in, tenure by source, then grow |
| Paying for "GEO" tricks | Google says no special files, schema or markup are needed for AI features (2025 to 2026 docs) | Accurate GBP, useful local pages, normal structured data |
| Review incentives or gating | Removed reviews and profile restrictions under Google policy | Same honest ask to everyone |
