# 90-day plans

For an existing or stalled business. Order matters: measure, fix lead conversion, fix activation and retention, then scale acquisition. The order follows the evidence (unanswered leads, first-month activation, retention value), not a validated formula. Each phase ends with a short owner report: what changed, the scorecard, what they need to decide.

## Shared spine (every sub-model)

**Days 1 to 14: baseline.**

- Import 6 to 12 months of leads, bookings, attendance, sales, cancellations, freezes and failed payments.
- Set the stage taxonomy and lost-reason list ([lead-to-first-visit.md](lead-to-first-visit.md#stage-taxonomy-set-it-up-on-day-1)).
- Compute ([economics.md](economics.md)): response time by channel, set, show and close rates by source, monthly churn split four ways, 30/60/90-day cohort retention, mature churn, revenue per active member, cohort LTV, member CAC by source, effective processor rate.
- Compare with the closest rows in [benchmarks.md](benchmarks.md), then write the three biggest gaps in money.
- Google profile audit: categories, services, hours, booking link, photos, review request flow; `HealthClub` or closest schema on the site (depth: `local-seo`).
- Ask the owner: approved offers, price authority, discount rules, consent held, who answers leads after hours.

**Days 15 to 30: lead conversion.**

- One inbox, owner per channel, instant reply with two times, after-hours self-booking, Meta Leads webhook.
- Confirmation and day-before reminders; same-day reschedule on a miss.
- Pre-visit intake form; intro session rebuilt as diagnose, coach, prescribe ([scripts.md](scripts.md#no-sell-intro-session)).
- One intro offer per service line.
- Exit check: median reply in staffed hours under 5 minutes; every lead has a stage and lost reason.

**Days 31 to 45: activation and retention.**

- First-month attendance dashboard (visits at days 7, 14, 21, 30) and the trigger table in [retention.md](retention.md#attendance-triggers-studios-gyms-boxes).
- Dunning and card-expiry flow live (`email-sms-loyalty`).
- Freeze and cancel flow checked against the member's auto-renewal law; one optional save offer ([retention.md](retention.md#freeze-and-cancel)).
- Milestone recognition and a referral ask after real milestones.

**Days 46 to 60: acquisition.**

- Only now scale paid media; offline conversions to Google and Meta; kill on cost per attended visit and per retained member.
- Local pages per modality or service and area; review ask to every client.
- One partnership channel (apartments, hotels, professional referrers, a local paper).

**Days 61 to 75: product and capacity.**

- Contribution per coach-hour and class-hour; semi-private equation; pack vs membership check; aggregator incrementality per class-hour.

**Days 76 to 90: optimize.**

- Rank constraints by dollars (value of moving each stage one realistic step). Run one controlled change at a time with a predeclared metric.
- Weekly scorecard from now on: leads by source, response time, set, show, close, member CAC, intro conversion, first-month visits, 30/60/90 retention, mature churn, revenue per member, LTV by cohort and source, reviews, local-search leads, class fill, contribution by service line.

## Track: personal trainer in a gym

- **Days 1 to 30:** daily floor targets (10 conversations, one taught movement), gym hours you want to sell, inductions requested, staff told your specialty, lists texted where the gym allows. Rebuild the intro session around your own coaching.
- **Days 31 to 60:** 90-day client arc (wins, benchmark, technique); first-workout and day-after texts; mid-package feedback message at week 4; referral ask after the first benchmark is beaten.
- **Days 61 to 90:** move clients to monthly billing by frequency if the gym allows; raise the new-client rate if near capacity; transfer low-rate clients if a commission scheme exists.
- **Exit check:** consults a week, close rate, roster, renewals.

## Track: independent or in-home PT, PT studio

- **Days 1 to 30:** Google profile and reviews from every current client; a page per service and area with "from" pricing; two-phone lead alerts; terms walkthrough before e-signature.
- **Days 31 to 60:** monthly autopay by weekly frequency with same rates; vacation and no-show policy in the agreement; professional referral network (PT, chiro, naturopath, massage); 15 former-client texts a month.
- **Days 61 to 90:** semi-private tier if the equation works; rent cap negotiated; price raise for new clients if near full; owner hours priced in the margin.
- **Exit check:** roster at target rate, top-3 revenue concentration, tenure, coach-hour contribution.

## Track: boutique studio

- **Days 1 to 30:** class fill by class-hour heatmap; paid multi-visit intro at about half the unlimited with 14 to 30 day expiry; intro sequence (day 0, after first visit, midway, 3 days before expiry); gift intro product; street sign if there is foot traffic.
- **Days 31 to 60:** 5-visit first-month target with day 7/14/21 checks; teach every booked class; filming and consent rules posted and enforced; absence triggers at about 5, 10, 14 days.
- **Days 61 to 90:** aggregator audit per class-hour (cut peak exposure, keep off-peak); move weak slots at the schedule change after a member survey; test one new format as a limited beginner series alongside existing classes; rollover on fixed-count memberships.
- **Exit check:** intro-to-member rate, first-month 5+ visit share, class fill off-peak, direct booking share, churn.

## Track: small gym or box

- **Days 1 to 30:** speed-to-lead with set, show, close tracking against 33.3% and 62.5% medians; replace any "not a good fit" price reply with a range plus a visit; consult-first intro; terms walkthrough.
- **Days 31 to 60:** dunning (failed-card churn was 23% in one gym); first-90-day coach touchpoints; community rituals that are countable (no person left behind, monthly event, member introductions).
- **Days 61 to 90:** coaching tiers (group, semi-private, PT) priced by coach-hour; apartment portal and hotel day-pass partnerships; offline conversions before raising ad budget; graduation path from coaching to access.
- **Exit check:** set, show, close; monthly churn vs 7.3%; failed-payment churn; member CAC.

## Track: online coach

- **Days 1 to 30:** if under 5 clients, direct texts and DMs to warm contacts and in-person clients who train alone; intake form; two tiers by accountability; written messaging hours.
- **Days 31 to 60:** weekly check-ins at 10 to 15 minutes each, tied to last week's update; Monday and Thursday touchpoints; 90-day expectation; case studies with permission from the first clients.
- **Days 61 to 90:** content funnel with keyword DMs and source tracking; 3 to 5 niche communities by life stage; qualify before the calendar link; workload and effective hourly check before price changes.
- **Exit check:** active clients, consult-to-client, month-3 retention vs 82%, effective hourly rate.
