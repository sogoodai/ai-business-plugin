# By sub-model and modality

Each block: how it grows, the number to watch, price anchors, tricks, traps. Numbers carry their source; anything without one is a mechanism to test. Where the inputs had no data (martial arts, dance, cycling), this file says so instead of guessing.

## Personal trainer inside a commercial gym

- **Engine:** floor relationships, inductions, staff referrals, the gym's birthday and new-signup lists, gym-tagged Stories ([getting-clients.md](getting-clients.md#inside-a-commercial-gym-pt)).
- **Watch:** conversations a day (target 10), consults a week, close rate, sessions sold per month vs quota.
- **Tricks:** sell your own coaching in the intro session, not the gym template (quota beaten 6 to 7 months running, r/personaltraining 122); run the session as real coaching with "we" language (88); sell transfers of lower-rate clients for commission when full (232); 30-minute twice-weekly format for 20 to 24 clients (87).
- **Traps:** pitching before familiarity (operators needed about 6 weeks of favors first, 221); correcting another trainer's client without asking that trainer; leaving without a client relocation plan (51).

## Independent and in-home personal trainer

- **Engine:** Google profile and reviews, client and professional referrals, apartment and condo partnerships, Thumbtack as a starter channel ([classpass-and-aggregators.md](classpass-and-aggregators.md#other-aggregators-and-lead-marketplaces)).
- **Watch:** roster at target rate (operators suggest 8 to 10 committed direct clients to start, 309), revenue from top 3 clients, client tenure, hours including travel.
- **Price anchors:** US $75 median single session, $460 monthly 1:1 plan; UK £45 (iPT 2026). Operators at $89 (148), $130 1:1 and $180 semi-private (81), $140 to $165 in affluent areas (132, 89).
- **Tricks:** monthly autopay by weekly frequency (309); 6-week vacation notice (309); explain terms out loud before signing (97); start while employed with under $300 a month overhead (309).
- **Traps:** expiring packs; ad hoc sessions; no minimum frequency (97); overloading past about 20 clients until quality slips (56).

## PT studio owner (1:1, semi-private, small group)

- **Engine:** own Google demand and website (75% of acquisition for a $191,000 studio, 364), referrals, semi-private to raise revenue per coach-hour, staff trainers.
- **Watch:** contribution per coach-hour by format, owner hours delivered, trainer utilization, client tenure by trainer.
- **Tricks:** semi-private with individual programs in Trainerize (83); 4-spot sessions that break even at 1 (r/smallbusiness 535); referral network routing overflow to staff trainers for a split (89); a local newspaper column (59).
- **Traps:** margins that depend on the owner training half the sessions (83); paid trainer hours added before demand; one format for everyone (the $191,000 owner is moving away from groups toward 1:1, others built 75% of revenue on semi-private; run the numbers).

## Boutique studios (shared rules)

- **Engine:** street visibility and Google for the neighborhood, paid multi-visit intro, first-month activation, reliable schedule, gift intros, aggregator yield on off-peak classes.
- **Watch:** intro-to-member rate, first-month visits (5+ retains at 90%+, ABC 2026), class fill by class-hour, direct share of bookings, monthly churn split voluntary vs failed card.
- **Spend context:** studio members average $69 a month vs $27 at smaller gyms (ABC H1 2026).
- **Traps:** last-minute class cancellations (r/pilates 442), ClassPass on peak classes, filming and influencer content without consent (320, 102, 42), replacing a loved format outright (r/YogaTeachers 62).

### Yoga

- **Timing:** 4 pm slots empty, 5:30 and 7 pm full, 4:30 about one-third of morning attendance; attendance softens in spring and summer (r/YogaTeachers, 60).
- **Format risk:** many yoga studios are losing students to hot pilates; one suburban studio grew from 3 to 12 classes a day after adding it, weekend pilates often at its 45-person cap (62). Add a limited beginner mat pilates series first; one yin studio's sold out (62). Keep the classic hot class while you test.
- **Privates:** prepay in full, 24-hour reschedule rule in writing (40); a discounted 4-session prepaid package covers late cancels from money already collected (40, comment at 55).
- **Workshops:** paid minimum before buying equipment ($500 of sound bowls, zero attendees, 40).
- **Teaching staff growth:** new teachers sub often while their own classes grow from 0 to 4 (60); cover a gym's no-show teacher to get hired (73); paid juice-bar Saturday classes instead of free park classes in high-cost cities (57).
- **Consent:** flip cards by the mat, private opt-outs (103).
- **Content:** a gentle-yoga teacher saw no attendance change after quitting local Facebook posting (162). Not every channel fits a quiet practice.

### Pilates and reformer

- **Demand:** pilates draws 2 to 3x yoga attendance at one studio (r/YogaTeachers, 14-upvote reply); reformer seats are scarce, so seat yield and peak protection matter more than volume.
- **Unlimited memberships need reliability:** $350 unlimited cancelled after the studio cancelled within the hour three times (442). Teach the one-person class or cancel the night before with membership days added (39).
- **Rehab-adjacent clients:** detailed injury intake, coordination with the physical therapist, 10 private sessions before classes (562).
- **Gift intro packages** bring long-term regulars (562). Street signs with a free intro class still find neighbors (183).
- **Filming bans,** enforced, especially with influencer members (320, 102).

### Indoor cycling

- No cycling-specific data in the inputs. Apply the studio rules: bike-level fill by class-hour, instructor-level retention (members follow instructors), aggregator spots only on underfilled rides, first-month 5-visit target. Measure instructor fill and retention before changing playlists or formats.

### HIIT and bootcamp

- **Price anchor:** small group or bootcamp membership $189.50 US, £95 UK a month (iPT 2026).
- **Specialist group classes pay:** a gym trainer teaching only HIIT group classes brings in about $250 a class (secondhand, r/personaltraining 285).
- **Challenges** are common here; run them only with a written business case ([intro-offers-and-pricing.md](intro-offers-and-pricing.md#six-week-challenges)).
- **Trap vs semi-private:** a same-workout class competes on price; individualized semi-private competes on coaching (48).

### Barre

- A barre owner in a tourist area cancels single-enrollee classes the night before and extends unlimited members by 1 to 2 days (r/pilates, 39). Seasonal towns: plan schedule by season and protect locals' memberships when tourist demand peaks.

### Martial arts

- No martial-arts data in the inputs or the research pass. Mechanisms to apply and measure: kids programs are bought by parents, so use parent-buyer funnels and progress reports (`education-coaching`); curriculum ranks or belts work as natural milestones for the attendance-trigger system ([retention.md](retention.md#attendance-triggers-studios-gyms-boxes)); a boxing background differentiates PT for affluent recreational clients (r/personaltraining, 285). Track adult and kids programs as separate cohorts.

### Dance

- No dance-studio data in the inputs. Kids and teen programs follow parent-buyer and term-based patterns (`education-coaching`); adult drop-in classes follow studio rules (intro offer, class fill by hour, first-month visits). Recitals and showcases are natural deadlines and milestones; measure re-enrollment per term as the retention number.

## Small gym, CrossFit or functional box

- **Engine:** consult-first sales, fast lead response, coaching tiers (group, semi-private, PT), community events, apartment and hotel partnerships.
- **Watch:** set rate (median 33.3%), close after attended visit (median 62.5%), monthly churn (median 7.3%), first-90-day retention, failed-payment churn (Two-Brain 2025 to 2026).
- **Tricks:** free PT session plus two weeks of small group, sold from a phone CRM with no front desk, LTV about $1,800 (r/smallbusiness 535); graduation from coaching into access membership (535); "no person left behind" finishing culture (r/crossfit 567); outdoor WODs in new neighborhoods with family rates (1,846 thread); apartment portal offers ranked by distance and unit count (r/smallbusiness 1,353).
- **Traps:** "If your first question is about the fee, then we're not a good fit" (four boxes, r/crossfit 162); discounted annual plans without dunning (23% lost on failed cards, 1,353); processor fees pitched at 5% that land near 10% (1,353); assuming a previous owner's members return (400 of 750, 1,353); routine discounting (no-discount gyms +$2,900 a month median, Two-Brain 2026, confounded).
- **Two-Brain's 2026 headline:** gym owners earn less per hour than their coaches. Price owner hours before celebrating margin.

## Online fitness coach

- **Engine:** warm network for the first 3 to 5 clients, then case studies, niche communities by life stage, short video with keyword DMs, YouTube to Instagram DM, referrals.
- **Watch:** consults a week, consult-to-client, active clients (median coach has 5, 81% have under 20, Coachway 2026), month-3 and month-6 retention vs 82% and 65%, effective hourly rate.
- **Price anchors:** $199.50 US, £99.50 UK, €181 Nordic median a month; operators say $150 to $300 to start ([intro-offers-and-pricing.md](intro-offers-and-pricing.md#online-coaching-pricing-and-tiers)).
- **Tricks:** two tiers by accountability; Monday and Thursday audio check-ins (182); 10 to 15 minute weekly check-ins tied to last week's life update (184); intake form before programming (50); 2 to 4 short workouts a week to start (182); bounded messaging hours (182, 98); convert in-person clients who already train alone (51); 90-day expectation without lock-in (184).
- **Traps:** $50 a month at 15 clients and 20 hours a week (95); a $19 info program that sold one copy (91); 24/7 access promises (98); growing followers without growing tenure. Sales calls, applications and challenges in depth: `education-coaching`.

## Hybrid (in-person plus online)

- 48% of trainers in Trainerize's 2026 survey call hybrid their primary model (vendor survey). An in-person trainer added $525 a month virtual coaching with about 3 hours of onboarding in week one and little after, and $80 consults (89). Use online as the step-down when a client moves, travels or cannot afford 1:1, and as the graduation path after a 1:1 block.
