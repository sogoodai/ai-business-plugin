# Scripts

Adapt names, times and prices. Keep it short and warm. "Operator" lines are verbatim from practitioners (thread upvotes); "research" lines are suggested copy from the research pass; "default" lines are ours, written to the rules in this skill. Automated messages never claim a person typed them, and never contain an offer the owner has not approved. Marketing messages need consent; reminders and service messages are a separate system (`email-sms-loyalty`).

## Lead replies and intro offer follow-up

- **Immediate SMS (research):** "Hey [First], it's [Coach] from [Business]. I saw you're looking for help with [goal]. I can get you in [option A] or [option B]. Which works better?"
- **Question-first DM (research, only if you will store the answer):** "Thanks for reaching out. What are you hoping to change over the next few months, and what have you already tried?"
- **After-hours auto-reply (default):** "Thanks for your message. We're closed right now, so this is an automatic reply. You can grab an intro spot any time here: [link]. [Coach] will text you in the morning."
- **Price question, gym or box (default):** "Fair question. Coaching memberships run [$X to $Y] a month depending on how often you train. Most people start with [intro]. Want me to hold [option A] or [option B] so you can see if it fits?"
- **Price question, PT walk-up (operator, 156):** give a goal-based range, "75 to 160 depending on what you want to achieve", then ask about goals.
- **Budget and timing qualifier (operator, 156):** "When would you like these sessions and what is your budget?"
- **Intro offer bought, day 0 (default):** "You're in. Your [14-day] pass starts with your first visit. Most people who love it come [3] times a week, so try a few different times and coaches. Here's the schedule: [link]. Anything I should know before your first class?"
- **Intro, after first visit (default):** "Great to have you in [class] today. How does the body feel? [Coach] has [next class] on [day] if you want to keep the momentum."
- **Intro, midway (default):** "You've done [2] visits so far. Want me to book your next two now so the week is sorted?"
- **Intro, 3 days before expiry (research, adapted):** "Your pass ends [date]. If it's been a fit, let's pick the best ongoing option for how often you come. I can do a 10-minute chat after your [day] class, or here are the options: [link]."
- **Intro expired, not converted (default):** "Your intro wrapped up. No pressure at all. If timing was the issue, [one ongoing option] starts whenever you're ready. Anything we could have done better?"
- **Low-pressure close after a consult (operator, 73):** "So, would you like to try out a few sessions?"

## Booking and reminders

- **Straight after an inquiry call (operator practice, 200):** "Great talking with you. Here are three times this week: [A], [B], [C]. Pick one and fill in this short form before you come: [waiver and intake link]."
- **Day-before reminder (default):** "See you tomorrow at [time] at [address]. Parking: [tip]. Wear: [what]. Need to move it? [link]."
- **Missed first visit (default):** "No worries, life happens. Want [new time A] or [new time B] instead?"
- **Private session prepay (operator practice, r/YogaTeachers 40):** "Private sessions are paid in full when booked. You can reschedule free with 24 hours' notice; inside 24 hours or a no-show, the session is used and a new booking needs a new payment."
- **Workshop minimum (default from a 40-upvote thread):** "This workshop runs if [6] people have paid by [date]. If it doesn't fill, you get a full refund."

## No-sell intro session

Diagnose (research and operators 92, 156):

- "What changed recently that made this worth solving now?"
- "What have you tried already?"
- "What normally gets in the way?"
- "How many days a week can you realistically commit?"
- The why ladder for bigger packages: "Why?" "Why now?" "Why does that matter?" "Walk me through that." "What happens if this doesn't change?"
- Reflect (operator, 92): "So this isn't about weight, it's about feeling like yourself again. That's what we help with, and this is how: [plan]."

Prescribe (operator, 156; research):

- "We have a variety of options for personal training, would you like to hear about them?"
- "Based on what we've discussed for your goal of [X], it would be best for you to work with us for [6 months] at [X times a week], does that seem fair?"
- "Based on that, I'd recommend [specific option] because [reason]. Would you like to start on [date]?"
- Talk about the future as "we": "In the first month we'll get your squat pain-free, by month three we'll..." (operator practice, 88).

Terms before signing (operator, 97):

- "Just so we're clear, here's how the billing works: [date], [amount], [cancellation notice], [no-show policy], [when I answer messages]."
- "I want to make sure there are no surprises for you down the road."

## Close and objections

- **Spouse (operator reply, 156 thread):** "Thank you for sharing that. Didn't you say your wife was supportive earlier?" Then: "Would it help if she came to the next session with you?"
- **Money (same):** "What numbers would you need to move around?"
- **Sticker shock:** stop talking. Let the silence run.
- **Discount request (operator, 97 thread):** "I don't offer discounts, but I also don't charge you upfront."
- **Genuine budget gap (default):** "I don't want you stretching for this. [Semi-private / group / online] gets you [what] for [$]. Want to start there?"
- **90-day expectation (operator, 184):** "I want you to give this a real shot before you decide whether it's working." No lock-in.

## Onboarding and check-ins

- **After the first workout (operator, 80):** "Hey Sam great job today on your first workout. Hope your body is feeling ok, looking forward to seeing you in our next session."
- **Day after any workout (operator, 127):** "How are you feeling after yesterday's workout?"
- **Habit opener (operator, 240):** "How does the body feel today?" Follow-ups: "What did you eat? Did you sleep well? How was the bike ride we planned?"
- **Easy-start expectation (default from operator, 81):** "The first week or two will feel easy on purpose. We're learning how you move, then we build."
- **One-food change (operator, 779, cleaned up):** "What one food item do you think is hurting you the most? How can you modify it?"
- **Mid-package feedback (operator, 80):** "First of all congrats on making it this far, many quit after the first week and you're already at [x] days! Just for service purposes, what are some things you're loving right now and what would you like to see more of? I want to make sure the hour you're in is the best possible one for you."
- **Before a new phase (operator practice, 46):** "New block starts next week. Anything you want changed or added? Any exercise you'd be happy never to see again?"
- **Online check-in reply (operator practice, 184), not "looks good":** "Your squat's been stuck at [x] for two weeks, and you mentioned work's been heavy. How's sleep been? I've dropped the volume this week and kept intensity. Nice job getting all four sessions in."
- **Messaging hours (operator, 182):** "I reply Monday to Thursday 9 to 6 and Friday mornings. Messages after Friday 2 pm get answered Monday."

## Absence and attendance

- **First absence, about 5 days (research):** "Hey [Name], haven't seen you this week. Want me to hold you a spot in [usual class/time]?"
- **Second stage, about 10 days (research):** "You'd told us [goal] mattered because [reason]. Your routine's gone quiet for a bit. Anything changed that we should adjust around?"
- **Coach call, about 14 days (default opener):** "Hey [Name], it's [Coach]. No agenda, just checking in. What's been going on?"
- **Milestone (default):** "That's visit number [50]. You've come [x] times a week since [month]. Proud of you." A referral ask can follow a genuine high point, never in the same message as a bill.
- **Studio cancelled a class (default, barre owner practice):** "We had to cancel [class] tomorrow, sorry. We've added [2] days to your membership."

## Freeze and cancel save

Check the member's auto-renewal and health club law first ([retention.md](retention.md#freeze-and-cancel)). One save attempt, clearly optional, then process the cancellation.

- **Open (default):** "Of course, I can sort that for you. Can I ask what's changed? It helps us get better."
- **Moving (default):** "Congrats on the move. I'll cancel effective [date]. Want me to send you a couple of gyms we trust near [new city], or would online coaching help in between?"
- **Injury or medical (default):** "Let's not lose what you've built. We can freeze your membership until [date] at no charge and I'll send you a short plan your physio can okay. Or I cancel it now, your call."
- **Schedule (default):** "Would a different time or [2x a week] fit better? If not, I'll cancel it now."
- **Money (default):** "Totally understand. [Lower tier] is [$] and keeps your spot in [class]. If that doesn't work either, I'll cancel it now."
- **Not seeing results (default):** "That's on us to fix. Can I book you 20 minutes with [coach] to reset the plan before your last billing date? If you still want out after, I'll cancel."
- **Still leaving (default):** "Done, you're cancelled effective [date] and you won't be charged again. Here's your training record so you keep your numbers. You're welcome back any time."
- **Freeze restart reminder (default):** "Your freeze ends [date]. Want me to book your first session back? [A] or [B]."

## Price increase

- **Short notice to current clients (operator, 191):** "I'm raising my prices by 5 dollars next month to cover my expenses." No big announcement.
- **Goal-framed version (default, built from the 156-upvote lost-client story):** "Hi [Name], from [date] your monthly rate moves from [$X] to [$Y]. Over the last year we've [their progress]. Next we're working on [their goal], and I'm excited for it. Any questions, just ask."
- **Never:** reasons about your own household costs or assumptions about what the client can afford.
- **New-client rate (default):** "Our current rate for new clients is [$Y]. Existing clients stay on their rate until [date]."
- **Moving to autopay with the same rate (operator practice, 309):** "From [date] payments run automatically on the 1st, same rate as now, so you never have to think about invoices again."

## Referral ask

- **Direct (default, from a 12-year coach's practice, 129):** "I'm taking on two new clients next month. Who's one person in your life who'd get as much out of this as you have?"
- **Spouse (operator practice, 59):** "How's [partner] doing? You mentioned they wanted to get moving. Want to bring them Thursday? Their first two weeks are on me."
- **Gift intro (default, from the 562-upvote gift story):** "Want to give someone your favourite class? You can gift a [3-class intro] here: [link]."
- **Professional referral thank-you (default):** "Thank you for sending [client]. With their OK, I'll send you a short progress note at week 6."

## Reviews

- **Uniform ask (research):** "Thanks for training with us today. If you're willing, here's our Google review link. Please share your honest experience: [link]."
- Never attach a reward, raffle or free class. Never send only happy clients.

## Win-back

- **Former client, personal (operator, 80):** "Hey Sam just wanted to say Merry Christmas and I hope you and your family are having a wonderful day. Hope your daughter Maddie is still crushing her soccer."
- **Seeing them in person (operator, 59):** "How have you been?"
- **Quiet member, no offer (default):** "Hi [Name], it's been a while since your last [class]. Everything OK? [Coach] has [A] or [B] this week if you'd like to come back in."

## Policies and boundaries

- **No-show pattern, long-term client (operator practice, 91):** "Your travel schedule makes it hard to keep this slot, and I need to open it for someone else. I'd love to keep training you whenever my schedule has space. Just text me."
- **Vacation (operator practice, 309):** "For trips of a week or two, let me know six weeks ahead and we'll front-load or make up the sessions. Billing stays the same."
- **Filming (default):** "No filming or photos in class, including of the studio. Our members train here to switch off. Thanks for respecting it."
- **Touch consent card labels (operator practice, 103):** "Yes, please" / "No, thanks".
- **Instructor reassurance (member quote, 183):** "Don't hurt yourself, it's just Pilates."
- **Workout intensity reminder for coaches (operator, 779):** "You will never lose a client for too easy of a workout but you will lose them for too hard of a workout."
