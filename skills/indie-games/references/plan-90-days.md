# A 90-day operating plan

This schedule is a proposed workflow, not a sourced performance promise. Move event dates to match current official eligibility and the team's build readiness.

| Phase | Work | Exit decision |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1 to 15 | Inspect comparables, define hook, audit page and instrumentation, establish baseline | Can a player explain the loop from the clip? |
| Days 16 to 30 | Test capsule and trailer openings; watch first-time demo sessions; patch friction | Is the page or the build losing qualified interest? |
| Days 31 to 45 | Build genre-matched creator list; prepare individual pitches and press kit | Is coverage producing qualified visits and wishlists? |
| Days 46 to 60 | Run the eligible festival or demo beat; compare baseline-adjusted lift | Repeat the converting audience and creative |
| Days 61 to 75 | Test paid acquisition only under the economic ceiling; prepare launch assets | Can cash and build quality support launch? |
| Days 76 to 90 | Coordinate release if ready, or schedule the next evidence-backed beat | Inspect refunds, reviews and cohort proceeds before scaling |

For an already released game, replace wishlist forecasting with net sales, refund reasons and update response. For a stalled unreleased game, fix the bottleneck before scheduling another event. Report owner time and cash spent alongside results. Use [economics](economics.md) and [pricing-and-launch](pricing-and-launch.md).
