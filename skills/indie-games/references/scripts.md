# Message templates

Templates below are original working copy, not practitioner quotations. Replace every bracket and verify the recipient's recent coverage. Prepare messages for owner approval before sending.

## Creator pitch
Subject: [Game]: [specific mechanic] for your [comparable game] audience

Hi [name], your [specific video] focused on [player behavior]. In [game], players [core loop], but [distinctive twist]. Here is uninterrupted gameplay: [link]. Playable build: [link]. Your individual key: [key]. It runs on [platform], supports [relevant features], and takes [tested duration] to reach the hook. Coverage is welcome from [date/time zone]. No obligation. Would this fit your channel?

Follow-up: We fixed [friction] and added [visible change]. Here is the new clip: [link]. Your build/key is still available above.

## Trailer channel or press
Subject: [Game] [announcement]: [readable premise]

[Game] lets players [verb] by [twist]. [Announcement] happens on [date]. Trailer: [link]. Downloadable footage and stills: [link]. Store and playable build: [links]. [One relevant reason this audience will care]. Please confirm any embargo before accepting it.

## Snowball pitch
[Relevant creator] recently played [game] and highlighted [mechanic]. Your coverage of [comparable] suggests the same mechanic may fit. Here is the exact gameplay moment: [link], plus a playable build: [link].

## Store description and community hook
Play as [role]. [Core action] to [goal], while [constraint changes the decision].

Post title: [Unexpected player action] in a [recognizable genre]. Show that action immediately. Name the game and platform where community rules permit. Ask about a specific decision, not generic support.

## Review reply
Thanks for explaining [specific friction]. [Verified fix or current behavior]. If you want to share reproduction details, [support route]. Never ask the reviewer to change their rating.

## Publisher questions
Who performs each marketing deliverable? What spend is committed? Which costs recoup, in what order, and from which territories? Who controls price, keys, IP and sequels? When do rights revert? What reports and audit rights do we receive?
