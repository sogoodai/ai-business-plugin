# Mistakes and replacement decisions

| Mistake | Cost or evidence | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Lead with autobiography | Dawnfolk trailer feedback criticized the long personal opening; no revised conversion measured | Show the loop before the personal story |
| Show a camera players cannot use | Operator reported most comments requesting that camera | Record the shipped perspective |
| Enter a festival with a rushed demo | Horror-fest operator reported weak traffic | Fix onboarding before a scarce event |
| Treat upvotes as purchases | Many supplied showcases report no sales attribution | Track visits, wishlists and purchases separately |
| Scale on cheap installs | CPI alone excludes retention and proceeds | Compare cohort contribution LTV and payback |
| Buy to a supposed visibility threshold | Valve does not promise a minimum wishlist threshold | Optimize qualified demand and cash risk |

Practitioner cases and dates: [sources](sources.md), collected 2026. These are anecdotes, not causal estimates.
