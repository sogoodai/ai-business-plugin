# Mobile, itch.io and console

## Mobile
Separate premium purchases from ad-funded and in-app-purchase games. Read the mobile rows in [benchmarks](benchmarks.md) before comparing CPI: country, OS, genre and acquisition source must match. Vendor aggregates are not a profitability target.

Soft-launch a stable build. Join acquisition cohorts to retention, payer conversion, refunds, ad revenue and store proceeds. Compute contribution LTV after store fees and variable operating costs. Cap acquisition below conservative contribution LTV and within the owner's cash payback window. Cheap installs with weak retention are a failed test, not a scaling opportunity.

Test store screenshots against the actual first session. A fake-ad hook can attract installs while increasing disappointment. Report creative cohort retention alongside install cost. Route account mechanics to `paid-ads`.

## itch.io
Use browser play to remove download friction when the game supports it. Put the Steam wishlist link inside the playable build and on the completion screen. Separate web sessions, downloads, payments and Steam clicks; they are different denominators.

An operator reported most initial traffic coming from Reddit before appearing in Popular listings; this does not establish a ranking formula. See the r/godot browser-game thread in [sources](sources.md), collected 2026. Treat itch.io as a testing and discovery channel, not a predictable Steam sales multiplier.

## Console
Confirm platform approval, build readiness, certification and store asset requirements before promising a date. Ask the publisher who owns store relationships and promotion submissions. Do not apply Steam wishlist conversion to console. No defensible console conversion benchmark was supplied.
