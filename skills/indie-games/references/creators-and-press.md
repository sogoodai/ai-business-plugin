# Creators, trailer channels and press

Creators are the main paid-or-free distribution network for indie games. Treat them as a list you qualify, a send you track and a result you measure in wishlists. If the client is a gaming creator selling their own channel, use the `creator` skill instead.

## Build the list

- Find creators who covered adjacent games in the last 90 days: search YouTube and TikTok for the 5 to 10 comparable games, Twitch directory by category, and the coverage lists on those games' Steam pages ("Steam curators" and news posts).
- Log per creator: recent median views (not subscribers), genre focus, PC/Steam audience vs mobile viewers, country and language, sponsor saturation (promoted a competitor in the last month), AI policy, contact.
- Mix by size: about two small or medium creators per large one (under 10k, 10k to 100k, over 100k followers) (r/gamemarketing 44).
- Mobile-heavy audiences lose wishlists at the Steam login and Steam Guard step; prefer creators whose viewers watch on desktop (r/gamemarketing 27).
- Screen for no-AI coverage policies before personalizing a pitch for an AI-assisted game; at least 10 contacts declined for that reason in one campaign (r/gamemarketing 51).
- Warm leads first: creators who already liked or commented on your posts (HTMAG Momento case, 2024), journalists who covered an earlier version (POLYSTRIKE got about 60% of reveal traffic from press, mostly warm contacts, r/gamemarketing 76).
- Sub-1,000-subscriber YouTubers who post daily: individually tiny, but some post about the game for weeks and fill YouTube search for the title (r/gamedev 1,306, unmeasured claim).

## Outreach mechanics

- Put the key in the first email. Waiting for a reply loses people (r/gamemarketing 51).
- Send two weeks before the beat (demo, Fest, launch) so creators have time to record (r/gamemarketing 44). A month before launch for Let's Play videos (r/Unity3D comment).
- One follow-up after 5 to 7 days for press, about a week for creators. Persistence on trailer channels works: GameTrailers accepted one trailer on the fourth attempt (r/gamedev 936), and Indie Games Hub answered a forwarded email chain with the subject "SPECIAL REQUEST PLEASE READ!!" because the first had been buried (r/gamemarketing 29).
- Send from one professional company address; a three-person team saw the most replies on the company address (r/gamemarketing 44, unproven).
- Reuse the first creator video in later pitches so creators see real gameplay (r/gamemarketing 51).
- Track keys activated separately from replies: 35 activations vs 15 replies on 500 contacts showed silent interest (r/gamemarketing 44).
- Fewer, better pitches beat volume. After 2,232 emails for a snake roguelite, the biggest video had about 2,000 views and the developer planned fewer, genre-qualified pitches (r/gamemarketing 51).
- Expect a power law: in a 1,000+ creator campaign, about 5% of coverage drove 80% of impact (r/gamemarketing 37). One 180k-subscriber roguelike creator's 30-minute demo video (about 40k views) produced the largest spikes while small creators moved little (r/gamemarketing 44).
- Ask live. A Twitch streamer with about 100 viewers queued the playtest when asked in chat and later uploaded an hour-long video: +47 wishlists vs about 5 per 3,000-view short (r/gamemarketing 16).
- Join the stream: stay in chat for the whole session, collect feedback and clip footage, even if it sells nothing (r/gamemarketing 18).
- Watch creator recordings for usability failures and invite creators back after fixes (r/gamemarketing 51).
- Free playtests and demos get found: big YouTubers covered an open playtest one to two weeks after launch without any outreach (r/gamedev 2,332); a "fairly big" YouTuber found a demo unsolicited, and one post-launch video brought "a few hundred" of 514 sales (r/gamedev 869).

## Trailer channels (the highest-yield press for unknowns)

- **IGN and GameTrailers:** email trailers@ign.com and videowire@ign.com offering a premiere of an unreleased trailer, with an unlisted YouTube link, the 1080p/60fps MP4 on Google Drive, press kit and Steam link. Follow up after about 13 days. GameTrailers published without replying; about 230,000 views and about 2,300 wishlists (r/gamemarketing 38). Another short email mentioning a mid-January free demo and prior coverage was posted on GameTrailers the next day, then on IGN's main channel (r/gamedev 1,626). Script in [scripts.md](scripts.md).
- **Indie Games Hub, Indie Game Joe, Best Indie Games, Game Trailers:** endorsements from these converted better than the developer's own posts (r/gamemarketing 86). Indie Game Joe's X post (about 650,000 views) reached journalists and bigger creators who then covered the game (r/gamemarketing 145).
- **Snowball:** right after one outlet features you, tell the next one. Indie Games Hub featured Koromi immediately after hearing about the GameTrailers feature (27,000 more views) (r/gamemarketing 38, a Chris Zukowski tactic).
- **Pitch before the announcement:** send the trailer and press kit to trailer channels and outlets before the page goes live, so coverage lands on reveal day (1,000+ wishlists in two weeks for a second game with no audience, r/gamemarketing 197).
- Free channels for developer-made videos exist (e.g. @IndieGameInn, new, unmeasured).

## Press

- Cold press rarely moves an unknown game. Small outlets, IndieDB devlogs and even an award article produced no meaningful conversion for one developer (r/gamemarketing 37); 40 targeted journalist emails produced nothing for a Kickstarter, while one organic GamesRadar article brought about 50 backers (r/gamemarketing 38).
- Press works for news: a funding round, a novel mechanic, unusual data, a milestone, or a story. ZOMBUTCHER's candid article about a failed publisher relationship on a local gaming site went viral and gave the page its first boost (r/gamemarketing 96). Commenters cited Vlambeer turning a stolen backpack and a clone into coverage (r/gamedev 3,035).
- Subject line: genre + hook + timing. Hook in the first sentence, no attachments, links only, one follow-up after 5 to 7 days ([presskit.gg](https://presskit.gg/field-guides/how-to-write-press-email-indie-game)).
- PR agencies: Eastshade ranked Player Two PR second in impact (r/gamedev 2,013); WarriOrb's agency produced no significant launch coverage and the game missed New & Trending with 16,000 wishlists (r/gamedev 3,385). Ask for named outlets they placed similar games in during the last 12 months before paying.

## Regional

- **Japan:** major Japanese outlets (Denfaminicogamer) covered BOMBANANA and Japanese wishlists "exploded" (r/gamemarketing 145); a Japanese article after a GameTrailers feature drove strong growth for Marble's Marbles (r/gamedev 936); IGN's second channel gave POLYSTRIKE's team an unexpected Japanese bump (r/gamemarketing 96). Include Japanese outlets for co-op and easily understood premises.
- **China:** a partner who set up BiliBili and RedNote accounts made China 30% of wishlisters and 70% of about 5,000 demo players (r/gamemarketing 23). A Douyin streamer started a viral wave a year after Early Access (r/gamedev 1,317). Expect higher refunds (22% for a 60%-China title).
- **Brazil and Portugal:** Brazilian interest grew from day two of BOMBANANA's Fest at about 10,000 wishlists a day; a Portuguese localization TikTok found streamer Wuant (2,000 Twitch viewers, about 30,000 YouTube views) (r/gamemarketing 30).
- **Russia-adjacent:** VK ads at $0.65 per wishlist burned out fast (r/gamedev 924).

## Paid creators and platforms

- Start with free keys. Pay only after free coverage proves creator audiences convert for this game.
- Compare paid campaigns on cost per tracked wishlist, never views: a campaign with 600k views and 1,700 wishlists beat one with 865k views and 1,128 (r/gamemarketing 27).
- TikTok creator agencies can fail by genre: $5,000 bought under 500 wishlists for a casino-themed roguelike deckbuilder (r/gamemarketing 45). Test with a small slice first.
- Run a free small-streamer pilot to test the process, not to judge the channel (8 to 10 viewers, 0 wishlists, r/gamemarketing 18).
- Synchronize: many small and mid creators posting the same days produce a measurable spike; Trapster's 50-creator, two-day push at $0.24 per wishlist and 21 creators at $0.15 are vendor best cases (2026). Lurkit's Dune: Awakening campaign staged creators across demo, closed beta, open beta and launch with 6,000+ keys (AAA, vendor).
- Tools and services mentioned by operators: Woovit ($60 for a month was "good value for the effort", r/gamedev 1,941), Keymailer, Lurkit, Word On The Block (stream overlay ads, CPM, no minimum, unmeasured), Discover Games (sponsored slots plus pay per verified wishlist and demo play, vendor post), Mad Octopus creator email database ($10 per 1,000 emails, unverified). Discover Games also ran a launch grant (one funded creator campaign, four $500 credits) for self-published games with a live page.
- Disclosure: a free key can be a material connection. Paid or gifted coverage must be disclosed by the creator; see `creator` for disclosure rules.
- Zorka's 2025 to 2026 data: gameplay-led creatives outperformed voice-over ones and audiences with no recent ads outperformed saturated ones (vendor model). Use as a screening prior.

## Measurement

- One UTM per creator or outlet (utm_source=youtube, utm_campaign=demo_push, utm_content=creatorname).
- Most creator traffic will not carry the UTM (viewers search Steam). Read daily wishlists against the baseline in the 72 hours after each video.
- Keep one sheet: creator, size, median views, key sent, activated, replied, published, views, link visits, wishlist lift, cost.
