# Expensive mistakes

Two tables: what the data and platform rules show (measured, or our arithmetic on published numbers), and what practitioners report losing.

## From the data and platform rules

| Mistake | What it costs | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing $3 to $5 downloads without fee math | Etsy takes about 19% of a $5 sale, 34% on an Offsite Ads order; Gumroad direct about 29%; a $1 Gumroad sale keeps ~7 cents (our math on Nov 2026 fees) | Price $9+ or bundle; keep cheap SKUs only as discovery items |
| Sending your own audience to a 30% marketplace route | $15 of a $50 sale on Gumroad Discover or Lemon Squeezy Marketplace vs about $5 plus fixed fee direct (report, 2026) | Link socials, email and YouTube to direct checkout |
| Broadcast-only email | Campaign placed-order rate 0.16% vs 2.11% for flows; $0.155 vs $3.41 per email (Klaviyo 2026, Omnisend 2025) | Delivery, bridge, clicked-not-bought and post-purchase flows before any ads |
| Optimizing likes and impressions | Pinterest separates pin clicks from outbound clicks; engagement does not equal checkout (Pinterest Analytics) | Report outbound clicks, signups and sales by source |
| Mass-producing AI commodity inventory | Etsy ban on bot listings and AI functional guides (Oct 2026); excluded from Creative Market's ChatGPT search (Nov 2026) | Original, human-checked products; disclose AI art |
| Believing frequency causes reach | Pinterest sets no quota; TikTok brand views fell 23% YoY while posting rose (Socialinsider 2026) | Fixed test windows, then keep the cadence that holds quality |
| Converting every idea to Reels | Carousels 0.55% vs Reels 0.52% engagement, carousels lead on saves (Socialinsider, 2025 data) | Carousels for frameworks and previews, Reels for demos |
| Letting disputes run | Gumroad pauses payouts above 1% chargebacks and imposes refund policy above 1% of customers disputing | Refund defects the same day; 1% is a hard line |
| "No refunds on digital" as a shield | Etsy, Gumroad and EU and UK law can force refunds anyway | Refund-on-defect policy in plain words |
| Assuming Canva Pro means resale rights | Canva license forbids distributing Pro content standalone | Audit elements; template-in-Canva only; provenance sheet |
| Trademarked words because tools show demand | Repeat infringement can end the shop, not one listing (Etsy IP policy) | Generic descriptive phrases only |
| Forecasting from old platform medians | Gumroad's 2020 "$70 median" predates every current fee and market | Benchmark your own cohort: visits, conversion, AOV, refunds, repeat |
| Shopify for EU-heavy digital sales without a VAT plan | You remain responsible for registration and filing | Merchant of Record (Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy) or Payhip remittance |
