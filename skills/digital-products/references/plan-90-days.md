# 90-day plan: from zero to the first $1,000 a month

Goal: by day 90, know which **product x query x channel x offer** combinations make money, and have a path to $1,000 a month in net revenue. Many sellers reach it later than day 90; the plan's exit is evidence, not a promise. Operators report it can take months: $28 in month one to $3,700 by month eight for an ebook catalog; $40 to $600 a month over several months on Canva Creators; programmatic SEO ranked only around month seven (r/passive_income 304 pts, r/sidehustle 686 pts, r/juststart 53 pts).

The target math ([economics.md](economics.md)): at a $15 average order on Etsy you need about 76 orders a month (2.5 a day); at $29 direct, about 37. At an operator-reported $0.45 to $0.63 revenue per visitor, that is 1,600 to 2,200 qualified visitors a month. Every week, report orders, visitors, RPV and the gap to these numbers.

A stalled business starts at days 1 to 15 with its own data (90 days of orders by SKU and source) and skips steps already done.

## Days 1 to 15: choose the market, set up the money

**Do**
- Pull 30 to 50 candidate problems from Etsy Marketplace Insights, Pinterest Trends and search, TikTok Creator Search Insights (content gap), Reddit and Facebook requests, complaint searches ("frustrated", "wish", "hate") and the owner's own expertise ([product-selection.md](product-selection.md)).
- Score each 1 to 5 on demand, competition, urgency, visual demonstrability, adjacent SKUs and legal or IP risk. Shortlist 3 niches with a buyer you can name in one sentence.
- Pick the platform pair: one marketplace (Etsy for printables, planners, wedding, kids; Creative Market for assets; Gumroad for productivity) plus one direct checkout with Merchant of Record if EU or UK buyers are likely ([platforms.md](platforms.md)).
- Build the fee model per price point, including Etsy Offsite at 15%.
- Create a license provenance sheet; audit every font, photo, mockup and Canva element.
- Set up the email tool, a free bonus page, and a monthly list export.

**Exit check:** 3 niches with demand evidence and exact buyer phrases; fee model done; no trademark or license risk in any planned product.

## Days 16 to 30: ship 3 to 5 products and the conversion assets

**Do**
- Build one hero product per niche in the smallest useful format, plus one bundle in the most promising niche and one checkout bump.
- Listing per [scripts.md](scripts.md): base phrase first, 13 tags, image stack (result, what's included, real page, edit view, sizes, how delivery works, bundle, license), 5 to 10-second screen-recording video, "DIGITAL DOWNLOAD" in image and first line, required acknowledgment field on Etsy.
- Delivery PDF with 3-step guide, account requirement, 2-minute Loom, support line, free bonus with opt-in.
- Test the duplicate link from another account (Notion), open every file on phone and desktop, run spellcheck, and make a real test purchase.
- Lead magnet that is a partial solution; flows: delivery, quick win, next problem, bridge offer, clicked-not-bought, post-purchase.
- Pre-launch tell the warm network and any community you belong to; answer 3 explicit requests where they were asked.

**Exit check:** 3 to 5 live listings or product pages, instrumented (Etsy Stats or analytics with UTM per channel), flows live, a test purchase passed. First sale is a bonus, not the target.

## Days 31 to 45: build the search library

**Do**
- Pinterest: claim the site, Rich Pins on, one board per query cluster; 15 to 25 distinct pins across the 3 niches, one query each, static 1000 x 1500, linked to the matching page; mostly how-to angles ([pinterest-and-social.md](pinterest-and-social.md)).
- 8 to 12 short videos or carousels from the same buyer questions: screen demos, before and after, three mistakes, manual vs template, FAQ. Search phrase in the hook, on screen and in the caption. Daily posting is fine during this test window.
- One searchable long piece a week: a YouTube tutorial of the exact job, a blog answer to a forum question, or a free-chapter post in a community.
- Submit free products to Notion Marketplace or template directories where relevant.

**Exit check (day 45):** top 20% of pins by outbound clicks identified; TikTok and Instagram ranked by link clicks and profile actions, not views; 50+ email subscribers; listing CTR and conversion visible per listing. Any listing with real impressions and under 1 click per 100: change image and title. Clicks and no sales after a few hundred visits: change preview, scope or price.

## Days 46 to 60: double down on winning messages

**Do**
- For each winning query or message, make 3 new executions (new image or demo, same query). Drop channels with views but no clicks after 30 days.
- Expand the winning niche: 3 to 5 variations (neighboring buyer, next step in the job, seasonal version), then the bundle with an upgrade-without-double-paying upsell.
- Kill or rewrite products that had exposure and no sales.
- Email weekly: one useful tip, one product mention, subscriber-first access to new releases.
- Paid only if a listing already converts organically: Etsy Ads at about $1 a day on that listing, or a promoted pin with proven outbound clicks. Judge on orders and contribution ([economics.md](economics.md#9-paid-traffic-break-even)).

**Exit check:** one niche carries most sales; RPV measured by channel; at least one product with repeat organic sales; list growing every week.

## Days 61 to 75: raise order value and retention

**Do**
- Test exactly one change at a time: a commercial license tier, then a bump, then a bundle price. Track attach rate and net revenue per checkout; keep only changes that raise it without denting core conversion.
- Post-purchase: onboarding email immediately, tip at day 2, review request after a success moment, cross-sell at day 14.
- Recruit 5 to 10 affiliates or matched creators for direct-checkout products: gift first, terms after ([scripts.md](scripts.md#affiliate-and-creator-outreach)).
- Measure support messages per order; fix delivery if above 1 in 10. Refund defects the same day.

**Exit check:** average order value up vs day 45; refund and dispute rate under 1%; support load under 1 message per 10 orders.

## Days 76 to 90: prune, protect, plan the next 90

**Do**
- Prune the bottom half of SKUs that had exposure and no strategic role; keep entry products that feed the list.
- Build the next product from evidence: search terms in Etsy Stats, Search Console queries, email clicks, support questions and buyer requests. Use buyer wording in titles.
- Seasonal calendar for the next 6 months; build 6 to 8 weeks ahead of each buying window.
- Protection: provenance sheet complete, source files and timelapses archived, instant download off on bestsellers if theft appears, list exported, a second sales channel live.
- One dashboard: visitors by source, listing impressions and CTR, conversion, AOV, net contribution, refund and dispute rates, flow click and order rates, opt-in rate, Pinterest outbound clicks, revenue by first-touch channel.

**Exit check (day 90):** a written answer to "which 3 buyer problems convert, which 2 channels bring them, which price ladder maximizes net revenue, which flows create second purchases, which channels failed." If net revenue is under $1,000 a month, compute the gap in orders and visitors and choose one lever: more traffic to proven listings, higher AOV, or a better-converting niche. Do not start a second business before this one reaches $1,000 a month (r/passive_income, 1,148 pts).

## Owner questions at each checkpoint

- Day 15: Which of these three buyers would you enjoy serving for two years?
- Day 30: Can you show your face, voice or only your screen?
- Day 45: Which channel do you not hate doing every week?
- Day 60: May we raise the price on the winner and test a commercial license?
- Day 90: Reinvest in the winning niche, or add a second niche?
