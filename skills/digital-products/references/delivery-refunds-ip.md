# Delivery, refunds, reviews, piracy and IP

On a digital product, support time and disputes are the main costs and platform standing is the main risk. Design delivery so buyers never need to write, refund defects fast, and keep proof of originality and licenses.

## Delivery that prevents messages

The delivered file is a product page in itself. For every product:

1. **A 3-step setup guide with screenshots** beside the access link, never a bare "Click here" (r/canva, 43 pts).
2. **The account requirement before the link:** "You only need a free Canva account" (or Notion Free, Google account, Excel 365, GoodNotes) (r/canva).
3. **A 2-minute Loom per product** showing the main edits (change colors, swap photos, duplicate a page), plus a playlist of common how-tos. A seller who does this reports rarely getting questions and many reviews praising the tutorials (r/canva comment).
4. **Where to find the file on Etsy:** buyers cannot download in the Etsy app. Send a short video showing exactly where the file is (Purchases on the website); text alone did not stop the questions (r/canva comment). For "can't unzip" messages, attach the PDF directly in an Etsy message (r/EtsySellers comment, 22 pts).
5. **Notion templates:** test the duplicate link from a different account. Relations and links can still point to the creator's pages, which buyers cannot edit (r/Notion comment).
6. **A completeness check before release:** page count, every link opens, no truncated text, no placeholder words. An ebook shipped with a chapter ending at "The key to..." (a $19 refund) and broken product IDs in emailed links cost $47 in credits (r/juststart, 43 pts). A children's crossword answer key contained a profanity after multiple proofreads; the seller pulled the listing, refunded every buyer and contacted each one (r/Etsy, 1,104 pts). Run a spellcheck and a profanity scan on generated or templated text.
7. **Test-buy monthly** with a real card. A Stripe price ID change left every checkout failing silently for 14 days (r/juststart, 43 pts).
8. **For local-storage HTML tools:** include JSON export and import so buyers can move devices, and a dark mode (r/passive_income, 614 pts).

Support load rule: if more than 1 in 10 orders triggers a message, fix delivery before adding products. One seller closed a $50 to $100 a month Etsy shop because a few messages were not worth it (r/passive_income, 997 pts).

## Refunds and disputes

**The law and the platforms override "no refunds".**
- **Etsy:** a buyer who downloaded a file opened a not-as-described case after the seller refused a refund and restated the policy in a reply to the 1-star review; Etsy ruled for a full refund (r/EtsySellers, 260 pts). The seller's own lesson: refund at once.
- **Gumroad:** may refund within 90 days to prevent chargebacks; above 1% of customers disputing, it can impose a refund policy; above 1% chargebacks by volume, it pauses payouts (Gumroad Help, 2026). Treat 1% as a hard line.
- **EU:** consumers have a 14-day withdrawal right for distance contracts; for digital content not on a tangible medium it ends once supply starts with the buyer's express consent and acknowledgment that they lose the right. Etsy tells EU buyers they give up cancellation when they download. **UK:** the Consumer Rights Act gives remedies for faulty digital content, including refunds paid within 14 days of agreeing entitlement.

**Policy wording** (synthesized starting point, not legal advice; adapt to local law):

> Because this is an instant-access digital product, change-of-mind refunds are not normally offered where local law permits. If the files are defective, won't open, are materially different from the listing, or you were charged in error, contact [email] with your order number and we will fix it or refund you.

**Refund economics:** a proactive $15 refund beats a dispute that threatens payouts (report, 2026). Operators report half of buyers refunded for a broken product came back and bought again within 30 days (r/juststart, 43 pts), and a buyer refunded for a file error, then given the corrected file free, repurchased and left the shop's first 5-star review (r/Etsy comment).

**Evidence when Etsy rules against you on a custom digital order:** request a second mediator, ask which policy and which listed feature the ruling relies on, resubmit dated delivery screenshots. One seller recovered every dollar after a month (r/Etsy, 561 pts). Mark custom orders complete by the due date to avoid the case in the first place.

## Reviews

- **Write replies for future buyers, not the reviewer.** A 302-pt comment endorsed replies aimed at future buyers; an 80-pt comment said a negative review on a digital file would not stop a purchase but a defensive reply would (r/EtsySellers, 951 pts). Template: "This is what the listing states; I've sent you a message to resolve this" (r/Etsy, 941 pts).
- **Fix the listing, not the product, when the review is about expectations:** add a preview of the actual page and say so in the reply.
- **Technical complaints:** ask for a screenshot or video of the error. Leave plainly mismatched complaints ("Canva template doesn't work in Word") unanswered (r/Etsy, 10-pt comment).
- **Report before replying** on Etsy when the review may be removable; a public reply has blocked removal for some sellers (r/Etsy, 1,286 pts; Etsy rule unconfirmed). Duplicate reviews from one order can be reported (3 of 4 removed in one case).
- **Never offer anything for a review or a review change.** Ask everyone, after a success moment, in neutral words ([scripts.md](scripts.md#review-request)).
- **Snark only fits a snark brand with fans.** A journal brand shares absurd reviews ("the journals were blank") with its audience, who buy more after; this works off Etsy, with fans (r/Etsy, 282 pts).

## Piracy and theft

- **Prevention has diminishing returns.** Watermark previews and state the license, but do not cripple delivery for honest buyers (report, 2026).
- **Watermarks trace, they do not stop.** A buyer found a stolen design on a diamond-painting kit, searched the signature watermark and bought from the original artist (r/EtsySellers, 670 pts). Another found an Amazon planner using the original Etsy photos with watermarks and shop name intact; reports failed (r/EtsySellers, 659 pts).
- **Bulk theft:** one buyer bought 1,200+ designs in four overnight orders despite personal-use terms; copies appeared in about 25 Temu shops and retaliatory reviews cut sales about 20%. Cancelling did not stop the downloads. A seller who turned off instant download on bestsellers reports no incident since (r/Etsy, 668 pts).
- **Takedowns:** keep dated source files, layers, timelapses and first-publish screenshots. Etsy removes material after compliant IP reports, accepts DMCA counter-notices, and may restore material 10 business days after a counter-notice unless legal action follows (Etsy IP policy, 2023, possibly outdated but still referenced).
- **Turn copies into proof:** a copied designer was advised to compile copycat listings, their visible sales and major-retailer appearances into a portfolio to pitch design work (r/EtsySellers, 11-pt comment).
- **"How do you make this?" messages:** "It's cool you're interested in [craft]! I've been perfecting my skills for [x] years. I'm not much of a teacher though, here's a link to help you get started." (r/EtsySellers, 938-pt comment). Or sell a beginner guide that leaves out your advanced methods.

## Your own IP risk (the bigger one)

- **Trademarks and names:** never put brands, characters, celebrities or artists in titles, tags or file names, even when keyword tools show demand. Repeat infringement notices can end the shop, not just the listing (Etsy IP policy). Reject Etsy's AI title suggestions that insert names ("Basquiat-Style": 3 violations, a legal letter, all listings removed, r/EtsySellers 345 pts).
- **Canva:** the Content License Agreement does not allow distributing Canva Pro content as a standalone item. Selling a template used inside Canva is allowed; selling Pro elements exported as files is not. A design with licensed content transfers to one client under specific conditions. Audit every element.
- **Fonts and mockups:** check the font license allows embedding in sold templates and commercial use; many free fonts are personal-use only. Mockup licenses often forbid use in products for resale.
- **Provenance file:** for every SKU keep a sheet: element, source URL, license type, date, receipt. It answers platform reports and buyer questions in minutes.
- **PLR and AI:** PLR content must be substantially changed and its license checked for resale rights; AI output must be original, accurate and disclosed where the platform requires.

## Etsy AI rules in practice (22 Oct 2026)

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|
| Seller-directed AI art with disclosure | Bot-generated listings |
| AI as a tool inside your own design process, disclosed per policy | AI-generated functional guides: patterns, recipes, instructions |
| Original templates and printables you designed | Prompt bundles (banned since July 2024) |
| Mockups that show the real product | Misleading AI imagery of what buyers get |

Agent rule: if a product's value is instructions (pattern, recipe, how-to guide), a human must write and test it. Keep proof.

## Account protection

- Keep personal and shop purchases separate; never use a card linked to a suspended account (a 12-year shop was suspended over it, r/Etsy 582 pts). If suspended, the second appeal must name the specific innocent mistake; the generic first appeal failed.
- Diversify before you need to: one direct store and an email list from month one.
