# Scripts and templates

Fill the brackets with the owner's facts. Never insert a number, review count, testimonial or earnings claim the owner cannot document. Plain words, one call to action per message. Where a line comes from a practitioner or source it is marked; unmarked templates are written for this skill from the mechanics in the other references.

## Etsy listing templates

**Title formulas** (base phrase first, then the buyer, format and use; one intent per listing):

- Planner or tracker: `[Condition or situation] [Tracker type] Printable, [Buyer] [Planner type], [Format] A4 and US Letter`
  Example: `Hashimoto's Symptom Tracker Printable, Thyroid Health Journal for Women, PDF A4 and US Letter`
- Canva template: `[Template type] Canva Template for [Profession], Editable [Platform] [Asset], [Count] Designs`
  Example: `Realtor Instagram Templates for Canva, Editable Real Estate Social Media Posts, 30 Designs`
- Spreadsheet: `[Job] Spreadsheet for [Buyer], Google Sheets and Excel [Tool type], [Key outcome]`
  Example: `Wedding Budget Spreadsheet for Couples, Google Sheets Wedding Planner, Payment Tracker`
- Notion: `[Job] Notion Template for [Buyer], [System name], [Key outcome]`
- Pattern: `[Item] Crochet Pattern PDF, [Skill level] [Style] [Item], [Size range]`
- Wedding printable: `[Item] Template, Editable [Item] for Weddings, [Style] [Format]`

**Description, first lines** (the part buyers see before "more"):

```
DIGITAL DOWNLOAD. Nothing will be shipped.

[One sentence: who it is for and what they can do after 10 minutes.]
Example: For newly diagnosed Hashimoto's patients who want to see which foods, sleep and stress patterns line up with flare days.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- [Count] pages: [list the page types]
- Sizes: A4 and US Letter
- Files: [PDF / Canva link / Google Sheets link / .xlsx]

WORKS WITH
- [Free Canva account / Google Sheets / Excel 365 / GoodNotes, Notability]
- Not compatible with [Word / Apple Pages / Canva without login], please check before buying.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Buy and download from Purchases on the Etsy website (not the app).
2. Open the PDF and click the link to [Canva / Google Sheets / Notion].
3. Make your copy and start with page [X].

THIS IS NOT FOR YOU IF
- [Disqualifier: you need a physical journal / you use Apple Numbers / you want medical advice]

LICENSE
Personal use. You can't resell, share or redistribute these files, even edited. Commercial license: [link to listing].

QUESTIONS
Message me before buying if you're unsure it fits. I reply within [24 hours].
```

**13 tags pattern:** 3 exact buyer phrases, 3 format phrases ("printable pdf tracker", "canva template"), 3 situation phrases, 2 recipient or occasion phrases, 2 synonyms. All multi-word, no brand or character names, no repeats of near-identical phrases.

**Image text, slot by slot:** 1 "[Outcome] in [time]" over the filled-in product; 2 "What's included: [count] pages"; 3 close-up of one real page; 4 "Edit in free Canva" or "Works in Google Sheets"; 5 "A4 + US Letter"; 6 "How you get it: 1, 2, 3"; 7 "Get the full bundle"; 8 license icons.

**Required personalization field:** `Type YES to confirm you understand this is a DIGITAL DOWNLOAD and nothing will be shipped.` (r/EtsySellers, 225-pt comment; imperfect.)

```
FAQ
- Can I use it on my phone? [answer]
- Does it work in [other tool]? [answer]
```

Outcome anchor example (coreyhaines31 offers example, MIT): "20 Notion templates that turn your scattered docs into a working marketing OS in one Saturday", with a 10-minute "do this first" Loom as the speed bonus.

Copy rule from practice: lead with the concrete time saved, not the technology. "One click, everyone seated: your seating chart in 5 minutes instead of 5 hours" beat leading with "AI-powered" (r/Entrepreneur, 557 pts).

## Faceless video hooks

Built on the report's structure (state the buyer's loss, show the before, show one fix, name the search phrase):

1. "If you're a [buyer] still [painful manual task], watch this." (Screen: the messy before.)
2. "I built this because [buyer] keeps losing [money/time] to [failure]." (Screen: the error.) "Here's the one tab that fixes it."
3. "Three mistakes in your [document type] that cost you [outcome]." (Slides, one per mistake, last slide the fix.)
4. "Building a [template] by hand vs with this." (Split screen, timer.)
5. "Search '[exact product phrase]' if you want the full version." (Closing line; matches TikTok search.)
6. "Your [document] in 5 minutes instead of 5 hours." (Fast-forward fill-in.)

Avoid "POV: you found the template that changes your life" unless the next second proves it. Make the colors vibrant and end at the payoff (r/Entrepreneur, 1,068 pts).

```

I've sent you full access here: [gift link]. No strings, I'd just like to know if it's useful for the way you [do the task].

[Name]
[One-line who you are]
```

**Follow-up after they used it** (5 to 10 days later):
```
Subject: Re: [Product] for your readers' [problem]

Hi [Name], did [product] fit how you work?

If you think your readers would use it, I run a small partner program: [X]% of every sale, paid monthly after the [Y]-day refund window, with a [Z]% code for your audience. I can send screenshots, a short demo and copy you can edit.

If it's not a fit, no problem at all. Thanks for looking.
```

```
- Commission: [X]% on every sale through your link or code, [one-time / for 12 months].
- Cookie: [30] days. Code [CODE] gives your readers [Z]% off.
- Payout: monthly via [method], after the [Y]-day refund window.
- Assets: [link to folder: screenshots, 20-second demo, pin images, swipe copy].
- Rules: disclose the partnership (#ad or "affiliate link"); no paid search on my brand name; no coupon sites.
```

## Review request

Send after a success moment (day 7 to 14, or after they reply with a win), to every buyer the same way, with no reward. Wording from the research (2026), adapted:

```
Subject: How is [product] working for you?

How did [product] work for you? If it helped, an honest review explaining what you used it for helps other buyers decide: [review link].
If anything is unclear or not working, reply here first and I'll help.
```

## Replies

**Refund for a defect:**
```
I'm sorry, that's my mistake. I've refunded you in full. The corrected file is here, yours to keep at no charge: [link]. Thank you for telling me.
```
(A buyer treated this way repurchased and left the shop's first 5-star review, r/Etsy comment.)

**Accidental or change-of-mind purchase, where you choose to refund:**
```
No problem, I've refunded your order. If you want a [product type] that fits [their need] better, [other listing] might be closer.
```

**Change-of-mind where local law allows you to decline** (decline only when there is no defect and no confusion created by the listing; otherwise refund):
```
Thanks for reaching out. Because the files were delivered instantly, I don't normally refund a change of mind. If something isn't working or isn't what the listing described, send me a screenshot and I'll fix it or refund you.
```

**Technical problem in a review or message:** `Please send me a screenshot or video of the error so I can help you get it working.` (r/Etsy comment)

**Public reply to an unfair review:**
```
Thank you for the feedback. The listing states [fact: "this is a Canva template; it needs a free Canva account"]. I've sent you a message to sort this out.
```

**"How do you make this?":** `It's cool you're interested in [craft]! I've been perfecting my skills for [x] years. I'm not much of a teacher though, here's a link to help you get started: [beginner resource].` (r/EtsySellers, 938-pt comment)
