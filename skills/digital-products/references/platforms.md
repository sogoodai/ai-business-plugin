# Platforms

Marketplaces supply buyers you do not have; direct checkouts keep the margin on buyers you bring. Decide per traffic source, not per business. Fees and fee math: [benchmarks.md](benchmarks.md) and [economics.md](economics.md). General Etsy search, ads, reviews and case handling live in `ecommerce-brand` (references/etsy.md); this file keeps the digital-download angles.

## Etsy for digital downloads

**How ranking treats you (Etsy Seller Handbook, Aug to Oct 2025):** query relevance, listing quality (clicks, favorites and purchases after search), customer-service quality (reviews, message response, cases, policy compliance), recency and personalization. A new listing gets a small temporary boost so Etsy can collect data; it is not a reason to relist.

Optimization order, which follows Etsy's disclosed mechanics:

1. Pick a query with demand and beatable competition (Etsy Marketplace Insights, since Nov 2025, shows real Etsy search frequency and competition; prefer it to third-party tools).
2. Make the listing semantically match it: base phrase first in the title, 13 multi-word tags, the most specific category and all attributes. Put spaces around dashes and slashes that separate phrases ("Budget Planner - Printable", not "Planner -Printable"); operators report "-Gift" gets read as the keyword (r/EtsySellers, 276 pts, unverified).
3. Win the click: image one shows the actual product doing its job at thumbnail size (filled-in page, dashboard with numbers, the font set as a word), and the price reads fair next to competitors.
4. Win the purchase: preview of real pages, what is included (file types, sizes, page count), compatibility (Canva Free, Google Sheets, Excel 365, GoodNotes, Notability, A4 and US Letter), the license, and "not for" lines.
5. Only then expand into adjacent keywords with new listings.

**Digital-specific listing rules**

- **Image stack for templates and printables:** (1) hero result at thumbnail scale, (2) what's included as a grid with counts, (3) a close-up of an actual page, (4) the editing view ("edit in free Canva"), (5) sizes and formats, (6) how delivery works (3 steps), (7) bundle or upgrade cross-sell, (8) license summary. Operators report a single preview of an actual pattern page turned a 1-star review into buyers wanting to explore the shop (r/EtsySellers, 951 pts).
- **Video slot:** a 5 to 10-second screen recording of the template being edited or a timelapse of drawing (answers "is this AI?" accusations, r/Etsy, 542 pts). For finished-sample products (patterns), a slow spin on a painted lazy Susan against a curved kraft-paper backdrop (r/EtsySellers, 349 pts).
- **Make "digital" impossible to miss:** "DIGITAL DOWNLOAD, nothing will be shipped" in image 1 or 2, first line of the description, and a required personalization field "I understand this is a digital download" (r/EtsySellers, 225-pt comment). It is imperfect: buyers type YES and still ask for the file a minute later.
- **Physical-shop owners adding patterns:** a $6 pattern beside the finished booties sold a handful a year and almost every buyer thought they bought booties (r/EtsySellers, 239 pts). Put patterns in a separate shop or section with a different first image style (flat lay of the PDF plus the item).
- **About section filled.** A buyer spending $200 to $1,000 a month on Etsy says she skips shops with an empty About section (r/Etsy, 941 pts).
- **Instant download vs manual:** instant for most; turn it off on bestsellers if bulk catalog theft starts (one buyer pulled 1,200+ designs overnight in four orders, r/Etsy 668 pts).
- **Custom digital orders:** mark the order "complete" on or before the promised date, not only deliver the file. A $500 custom order delivered on time was refunded because it was marked late; recovery took a month of escalation (r/Etsy, 561 pts).
- **Personalization limits:** offer only changes that take 5 to 10 minutes (name on a cover, text in a card); unlimited requests eat the margin (r/Etsy, 315 pts).
- **Remove dead weight carefully:** operators delete listings with views and no conversions; Etsy says recency is a small boost, so delete for conversion reasons only, never relist for a bump.

**Etsy policy specifics for digital sellers**

- **AI (22 Oct 2026):** bot-generated listings banned; AI-generated functional guides (patterns, recipes, instructions) banned; prompt bundles banned since July 2024; seller-prompted AI art allowed with disclosure; buyers can report misleading AI imagery and Etsy is strengthening detection ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1522733284810)).
- **Fee avoidance:** never cancel ad-attributed orders or charge fake shipping to push buyers to your site. A $20,000 shipping fee on an SVG made a ready buyer refuse the seller's website and consider reporting it (r/Etsy, 602 pts).
- **Account concentration:** a 12-year six-figure shop was suspended over a payment card linked to a suspended account; the own site made 1/12 of Etsy revenue. A second appeal naming the exact innocent mistake restored it after a generic appeal failed (r/Etsy, 582 pts).
- **Search outages:** sellers have reported listings missing from marketplace search while still reachable by URL and shop search. Diagnose with an exact-title search, the direct URL and in-shop search. Some report a Vacation Mode on/off toggle restoring visibility for about 24 hours; others warn it can delay reindexing. Treat as a last resort, not a fix (r/Etsy, 416 pts).

## Gumroad

- **Fees:** 10% + $0.50 plus processing direct; 30% on Discover; 5% + $0.50 on direct sales above $20,000 in a calendar month. Merchant of Record since 1 Jan 2025: Gumroad collects and remits sales tax and VAT where applicable.
- **Discover eligibility:** $100 from genuine sales, a risk review averaging about 3 weeks, at least one sale on the product, a category set, ratings on. Do not optimize for Discover before direct sales prove conversion.
- **Discover copy:** titles and descriptions in the buyer's search words (operators report Discover brought discovery once titles matched buyer phrases, r/Entrepreneur 251 pts). One operator: 2,647 Discover views, 132 sales, 5% (r/passive_income, 451 pts).
- **Discover is a paid channel.** At 30%, treat it as CAC. Put a free bonus with email opt-in inside the product so Discover buyers join your list (see [email-launches-affiliates.md](email-launches-affiliates.md)).
- **Upsells:** three types at checkout: add a product, replace the cart with another (upgrade to the bundle without paying twice), or upgrade to another version (Gumroad Help). Bundles are native.
- **PWYW:** minimum and suggested price are separate fields; $0 minimum allowed, any positive payment at least $0.99.
- **Risk:** Gumroad may refund within 90 days to prevent chargebacks, imposes a refund policy above 1% of customers disputing, pauses payouts above 1% chargebacks.
- **Operator warning:** splitting a catalog across several niche Gumroad accounts to compare demand worked but the email and account overhead stopped scaling (r/Entrepreneur, 251 pts). Use one account with tagged products.

## Lemon Squeezy

- 5% + $0.50, Merchant of Record. Marketplace sales 30%. Abandoned-cart recovery adds 5% on recovered orders; affiliate orders add 3%. Good for software-adjacent products (Notion with scripts, code, plugins, license keys) and for EU-heavy audiences.

## Payhip

- Free 5%, Plus $29 + 2%, Pro $99 + 0%, plus Stripe or PayPal; no features gated by plan. Remits EU and UK VAT and US and Canadian sales tax on digital sales.
- Marketplace (launched 2024): no extra fee, requires $10 in genuine sales and a store review (target about 10 days).
- Cross-sells across product types expanded in 2024 to 2025; coupons, affiliates, memberships and courses in one place.
- Break-evens: Free to Plus at $967 a month in sales; Plus to Pro at $3,500.

## Stan

- $29 Creator or $99 Creator Pro, 0% platform transaction fee, processor fees apply. Pro adds order bumps, upsells and funnels. Stan describes itself as a store for an audience you bring: it creates no demand.
- Stan's own 2025 data: average sale $67; downloads $51, courses $96, meetings $191 (means, vendor-reported).
- Best for Instagram and TikTok sellers who send traffic from a link in bio and want bumps without building a site.

## Shopify

- Basic $29 a month annual or $39 monthly; online cards from 2.9% + $0.30; no platform fee on digital; Shopify Digital Products app up to 5GB files.
- Not a Merchant of Record: you register and remit tax where required (Shopify tax services help, the obligation stays yours). Choose it when you also sell physical items, want full design control, or already run a store.
- Benchmark context only: 1.3% median conversion, 2.8% top 20% (Littledata, mostly physical, Nov 2026).

## Creative Market

- 50% default commission; enforced minimum prices since 16 Apr 2026 (templates $9 / $14 / $36, graphics $6 / $9 / $24, photos $3 / $4 / $5 for personal, commercial, extended). Commercial license covers up to 5,000 end products, then extended or another license.
- **AI-mediated discovery (2026):** Creative Market launched conversational font search (March 2026) and a ChatGPT integration (6 Nov 2026) that surfaces fonts, templates and illustrations with previews and prices. AI-generated products are excluded. Write titles and descriptions that describe style, use and format in plain words ("rounded geometric sans for kids' brands", "editable Canva Instagram templates for realtors"), keep categories exact.
- Use as a discovery channel; keep a direct store for repeat buyers and larger bundles.

## Templates marketplaces and directories

- **Notion Marketplace:** list a narrow free template as an entry product. One free "Movie Tracker that actually works" was featured first in Notion's official template email about six months later, above paid templates (r/Notion, 213 pts; selection reason unknown).
- **Template directories:** free directories with newsletters accept free and paid templates (NotionEverything accepted submissions via form; another launched with 50 free templates and 5 new ones a week, r/Notion 669 pts). Submit every product; it costs nothing.
- **Canva Creators program:** pays on Pro usage of your templates. Operators report $40 in month one rising to $600 to $700 a month after building a catalog of ad templates researched in Meta's Ad Library, and a separate contributor at ~$600 a month with $30,000 lifetime; both say admissions and approvals got stricter (r/sidehustle 686 pts, r/passive_income 746 pts). Treat as a side channel; you do not own the buyer.

## Ebooks and audio

- **Amazon KDP:** search-driven. Titles name the problem and reader ("First Time Mom Guide to C-Section Recovery") (r/Entrepreneur, 5,128 pts, second-hand). Never fill keyword fields with unrelated trending terms: two books at about 20 sales a month fell to 4 and 6, then 1 and 0, and removing the keywords did not restore them; ads did (r/sidehustle, 159 pts). Continuous publishing under the same pen name kept visibility; stopping lost momentum (543 pts).
- **Draft2Digital:** free upload, takes about 10% of earnings, wide distribution (operators report, verify current terms).
- **Free ebook as a funnel:** publish free on Smashwords to get a Barnes & Noble listing, then ask KDP to price-match to free; follow the Smashwords style guide and remove Amazon links or it is rejected (r/juststart, 91 pts; historical workflow, verify).
- **Audiobook from long articles:** one live audiobook earned a few thousand dollars in a year untouched (r/juststart, 91 pts).
- **Teachers Pay Teachers, Udemy, Skillshare:** see `education-coaching` (marketplaces).

## Stock assets and fonts

- **Niche libraries beat general stock:** a photographer reports about £2,000 a month from a book-cover image library that finds buyers and pays monthly royalties (r/sidehustle, 310-pt comment).
- **Freepik vs Canva:** a contributor says Freepik pays a better rate but takes longer to earn (r/passive_income).
- **Fonts and graphics:** Creative Market plus your own store with personal, commercial and extended tiers; a free personal-use weight on directories as the entry product (common practice, no data in our inputs).

## Tax and Merchant of Record

| Platform | Who handles EU/UK VAT and US sales tax on digital |
|---|---|
| Gumroad | Gumroad, as Merchant of Record (since 1 Jan 2025) |
| Lemon Squeezy | Lemon Squeezy, as Merchant of Record |
| Payhip | Payhip remits EU and UK VAT and US and Canadian sales tax |
| Etsy | Etsy collects and remits marketplace taxes where required |
| Shopify | Tools only; the merchant registers and files |
| Stan | Not confirmed in our sources; check before selling into the EU |

Rule: if more than a handful of buyers are in the EU or UK and the owner will not register for VAT OSS, sell direct through a Merchant of Record even at a higher fee.

## Platform selection checklist

1. Where does the buyer search? (Etsy for gifts, printables, planners, SVGs; Creative Market for designers; Gumroad and Notion for productivity; Amazon for books.)
2. Do you bring traffic? If yes, direct checkout. If no, a marketplace first.
3. Average order under $9? Avoid platforms with a $0.50 fixed fee; bundle.
4. EU and UK buyers? Merchant of Record.
5. Need bumps, upsells, affiliates, memberships? Gumroad, Payhip, Stan Pro, Lemon Squeezy all do some; pick by fee at your volume.
6. One marketplace plus one direct store is the default. A second marketplace only after the first has a proven winner.
