# Benchmarks

Three kinds of numbers live here. Keep them apart when you report:

- **Measured:** sector datasets (FEP), large benchmark studies (M+R, GivingTuesday Data Commons), platform documentation, randomized or controlled experiments.
- **Vendor cohort:** software companies' customer data (Blackbaud, Instrumentl, Raisely, OneCause, GiveSmart, Ministry Brands, Pushpay, RaiseNow). Real data, but their customers, not the sector.
- **Operators report:** Reddit practitioner numbers with upvotes. One organization's experience.

Rules before comparing: M+R reports medians; its "small" cohort is under $1M in **online** revenue, which is bigger than most clients here. FEP is all-channel US retention; M+R is online-only. Never compare a mean gift to a median, an online retention rate to FEP, or a vendor cohort to a census without saying so. "Year" is the year the data describes; publication follows when different. Top quartile is "not published" when the source does not publish it. Do not invent one.
