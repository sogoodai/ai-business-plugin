# Expensive mistakes

Two tables: what the data shows (measured, or arithmetic on measured numbers) and what practitioners report losing.

## From the data

| Mistake | What it costs | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Buying acquisition while ignoring first-year retention | At 24% new-donor retention, 1,000 new donors become 240 next year (M+R 2026) | First-gift journey and monthly upgrade before scaling acquisition; report first-to-second gift rate by source |
| Optimizing Meta for cheap leads | 0.03% donor conversion vs 0.47% for donation optimization; cost per donor 347% higher (NextAfter 2026) | Optimize to the donation event; report cost per first donor and 90-day revenue |
| Sending traffic to a weak mobile form | Small-org mobile completion 4% vs 8% overall; mobile is 52% of visits but 28% of revenue (M+R 2026) | Fix the form, add wallets, then buy traffic |
| Judging a page test on conversion alone | A specific-impact headline doubled conversion but average gift fell from $115.69 to $40.72 (NextAfter 2026) | Judge on revenue per visitor |
| Homepage carousel because every program wants a slide | Static hero doubled featured clicks and lifted primary CTA clicks 238% (NextAfter 2026) | One static hero, one primary action |
| Not asking for recurring gifts | Median org has about 4% recurring; half added none in a year (GivingTuesday 2025), while 71% of sustainers last 12 months (M+R 2026) | Monthly-first test, post-gift upsell, sustainer journey |
| Killing prospect mail on first-gift ROI | Prospect mail returns $0.41 per $1 at gift one, active mail $4.51 (M+R 2026); cutting acquisition kills future renewals | Decide on 12 to 24 month cohort value |
| Treating Ad Grants as a donation machine | $793 of grant per donation, $0.17 back per grant dollar (M+R 2026) | Optimize the grant to service, volunteer and sign-up conversions; donations come through the journey |
| Letting Ad Grants CTR slide | Two months under 5% can deactivate the account (Google 2026) | Weekly keyword pause rule, brand campaign, negatives |
| Fake Ad Grants conversions (page views) | Google treats near-100% conversion rates as tracking errors; Smart Bidding optimizes toward junk | Real mission conversions only |
| Reporting event gross | Median cost to raise $1 is $0.18 (Marts & Lundy 2025); galas often run $0.40 to $0.60 once staff time is counted | Net per staff hour, new donors and prospects per event |
| Chasing grant volume | About 14% cold win rate (Instrumentl 2026); under 10% at volume means bad fit, not bad writing | Fit gates, pre-calls, renewals first |
| Mass thank-you calls on faith | No effect on retention in a 500,000-donor randomized trial (AEJ 2023) | Calls for cultivation tiers; test mass calls with a holdout |
| More email to raise more | 2024: 62 messages per subscriber, revenue -11%; 2025: 50 messages, +16% (M+R, observational) | Cadence by segment; judge on revenue per 1,000 and unsubscribes |
| Believing the 48-hour church follow-up rule as fact | No credible modern study; only 21% of newcomers recall contact (Lifeway via Passport 2026) | Same-day text, then measure second- and third-visit rates |
| Leaving Gift Aid unclaimed (UK) | 25p per £1 of eligible gifts lost (HMRC) | Declaration on every form and reply card; claim two years back |
| Missing the $250 acknowledgment (US) | Donor cannot claim the deduction; trust damage (IRS Pub 1771) | Automated receipts with the required lines; January statements |

## From practitioners (Reddit, anecdotal)

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead | Upvotes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ignoring a grant reapplication email | $200,000 of grant funding that year | Shared renewal calendar with two owners | 82 |
| Not tracking restricted grant spending | Only $20,000 of documented expenses for a $50,000 grant at report time | Track restricted funds by grant in the books | 73 |
| Raising the minimum gift to stop card testing | Still 25 to 30 fake charges a day, thousands in fees, donation page taken down | Honeypot, time-to-submit, reCAPTCHA v3, velocity filter | 93 |
| Strict billing-address matching against fraud | Did not stop fraud, caused the most problems for real donors | Bot defenses before the processor | 93 |
| Board changing the gala date after deposits and save-the-dates | Three rounds of vendor rebooking 2.5 months out, staff time | Lock dates with board and top donors before deposits | 117 |
| Running a 20-year gala on habit | $320K gross, $175K net, over a quarter of sponsors' seats empty | Smaller format, sponsors to programs | 180 |
| Gala goal set by hope | $355k raised against $500k, staff burnout | Gift table and pre-committed lead gifts before setting the goal | 188 |
| Scolding email to donors | 4x opens, trust crisis, years of net losses | Never shame; story and thanks | 258 |
| Replacing segmented emails with blasts to purchased contacts | 30% membership revenue decline (several changes at once) | Separate member and prospect journeys, no purchased lists | 109 |
| Free pickups of in-kind donations | About $400 to collect unusable books | No-pickup policy, acceptance policy | 82 |
| Annual merchandise donation quotas with leadership overriding valuations | Extra dumpster, weak shop sales | Staff valuation stands | 82 |
| Big unrestricted windfall spent on fast growth | Org closed two years after a $20M unrestricted grant | Spend a fixed share a year; no permanent staff on one-time money | 149 |
| Cultivating a long-time donor without checking who drove the giving | Meeting ended with "So what do you want from me?"; the late wife had been the giver | Check household and tribute history first | 157 |
| No stewardship of a large-capacity donor | A billionaire's two $1,000 gifts with no record or follow-up | Log source and reason for every gift above the median | 174 |
| Comparing ticketing platforms on quoted fees | Quotes excluded card processing; hardware incompatibility found late | Compare net per ticket at your price; check box-office devices | 78 |
| Counting on winning chargebacks | Lost every dispute despite signed terms and scanned tickets | Price the loss in; clear refund policy | 367 |
| Prechecked recurring and "double" boxes | $122 million in refunds (political fundraising case, thread title) | Visible defaults only | 138 |
| Commission-based grant writing | Writers refuse; violates the AFP Code | Pay per hour or per proposal | 72 |
| Personal data in Form 990 | A DV grantee's physical address published in a funder's Schedule I | Review 990 schedules for PII before filing | 89 |
| Ignoring GoFundMe or platform pages created without consent | Donations through a page you do not control, no donor data | Claim or remove; push your own page | 79 |
