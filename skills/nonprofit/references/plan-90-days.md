# 90-day plan

For a new or stalled small nonprofit, charity or church. Order matters: measure, then convert and retain, then acquire, then build the personal pipeline, then scale what worked. A cause startup with no history does the "Cause startup" priorities in [by-model.md](by-model.md) first and joins at day 31.

Each phase ends with a one-page owner or board report: what changed, the scorecard, what you need them to decide.

## Days 1 to 14: measure and fix the page

**Scorecard** from 24 months of gift-level data ([economics.md](economics.md)):

- donors, gifts and revenue by year and by source
- overall, new-donor and repeat-donor retention; first-to-second gift rate at 90 and 365 days
- active monthly donors, recurring share, failed-payment rate
- revenue per 1,000 fundraising emails, list size, unsubscribes
- donation page completion on mobile and desktop, revenue per visitor
- events: gross, direct cost, staff hours, net per staff hour, new donors
- grants: active, pending, renewals in 12 months, win rate cold vs renewal
- top-25 donors by RFM plus engagement
- church: first-time guests a month, second- and third-visit rates, recurring share of giving units

Compare with [benchmarks.md](benchmarks.md) (M+R reference points: 24% new-donor retention, 66% prior, 71% sustainers at 12 months, 0.05% email response, 8% mobile completion). Write the three biggest gaps in dollars.

**Fix the donation page** ([acquisition.md](acquisition.md) section 1): wallets, few fields, monthly visible, outcome headline, proof line, fee-cover option unchecked, fraud defenses. Test on two phones.

**Instrument:** GA4 and processor events for donation, monthly sign-up, volunteer sign-up, email sign-up; UTM on every email and post. If eligible, apply for or repair Google Ad Grants and make conversion tracking real.

**Ask the owner:** who signs appeals; what impact units finance and program staff will stand behind; whether the board will fund a match; which events are sacred.

**Exit check:** scorecard live, page fixed, tracking firing, receipts carry the legal lines.

## Days 15 to 30: retention engine

- First-gift journey: receipt, week-one impact, week-two question, a non-money action, monthly ask at weeks 4 to 8 ([retention-and-recurring.md](retention-and-recurring.md), copy in [scripts.md](scripts.md)).
- Sustainer journey and failed-payment recovery (card updater, retries, update link, calls over $25 a month).
- Lapsed sequence for LYBUNT and SYBUNT.
- Volunteer tagging and the after-shift thank-you.
- Church: QR guest capture and the same-day text sequence ([churches.md](churches.md)).
- One recurring proposition and an A/B test: monthly-preselected versus one-time-preselected with monthly callout.
- Story bank with consent fields; collect 5 stories ([volunteers-and-impact.md](volunteers-and-impact.md)).

**Exit check:** every new gift, volunteer and guest enters a journey automatically; monthly test running.

## Days 31 to 45: acquisition tests (two or three channels only)

Priority: existing-list reactivation, Google Ad Grants and search pages, volunteer and P2P acquisition, local business partnership, then paid Meta and paid search.

- Ad Grants: intent clusters with their own landing pages, real conversions, 5% CTR guard, Maximize Clicks only until 15 to 30 conversions a month.
- Paid Meta (if budget): donation-optimized; report cost per first donor and 90-day revenue, never CPL alone.
- One local business partnership with a product or night tied to the mission.
- Recruit 10 to 20 ambassadors for the next campaign.

**Exit check:** cost per first donor and first-to-second gift rate visible by source.

## Days 46 to 60: major gifts, grants, board

- Top-25 list (top 50 if someone owns it), each with a stage and one human next step; weekly 30-minute pipeline review.
- 12-month grant calendar: renewals and reports first, then qualified new funders scored on fit; pre-call before applying; master boilerplate updated.
- If cold win rate is under 10% at volume, audit prospect fit before adding applications.
- Board: 100% giving confirmed, quarterly menu sent, actions logged in the CRM.
- Restricted funds tracked by grant in the books.

**Exit check:** named next step for every top prospect; grant calendar shared with two owners; board menu choices in.

## Days 61 to 75: campaign build

Depending on the calendar: year-end and GivingTuesday ([appeals-and-campaigns.md](appeals-and-campaigns.md)), spring appeal, or a fiscal-year-end push.

- Match or challenge gift secured from the major list or a board member.
- Segments, suppression rules, landing page with progress bar, mail piece, email and text calendar.
- Major donors asked personally before the mass campaign.
- Event decision: keep, shrink or replace, using net per staff hour and new prospects.

**Exit check:** campaign assets approved; lead gifts committed.

## Days 76 to 90: scale winners, cut vanity metrics

- Review every test on incremental net revenue and durable supporter outcomes.
- Scale paid only where cost per first donor and early cohort value justify it.
- Keep acquisition mail only where projected 12 to 24 month value supports it.
- Expand monthly-first if recurring volume rose without an unacceptable drop in one-time revenue.
- Write the impact update for donors and the one-page dashboard for the board:

```
new donors | cost per first donor | first-to-second gift % | 12-month retention | recurring donors | recurring revenue | revenue per 1,000 emails | donation page conversion | qualified grant pipeline $ | major-gift pipeline $ | volunteers who gave | net fundraising cost per $1
```

**Goal at day 90:** one repeatable acquisition source, a working second-gift and recurring engine, a named major and grant pipeline, and measurement the board trusts.
