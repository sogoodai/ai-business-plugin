# By sub-model

Pick the row in SKILL.md first, then read the matching section. Everything else in the skill applies with the weighting below.

## Small charity (registered, under about $1M, 0 to 5 staff)

**Shape.** A few hundred to a few thousand donors, one person doing development part-time, an annual event, year-end appeal, some foundation grants.

**Priorities, in order**

1. Donation page and fraud defense ([acquisition.md](acquisition.md)).
2. First-gift journey and a monthly program ([retention-and-recurring.md](retention-and-recurring.md)). This is where a small charity can beat the median fastest: the median org has about 4% of donors on recurring (GivingTuesday 2025).
3. Year-end as a six-week campaign with a match ([appeals-and-campaigns.md](appeals-and-campaigns.md)).
4. Top-25 major-gift list with the ED owning it. Small orgs got 51.7% of revenue from major gifts in 2025 (Blackbaud Institute), so even a part-time major-gift rhythm matters.
5. Grants: renewals first, then 6 to 12 well-qualified new funders a year.
6. Event: keep one if net per staff hour holds; otherwise swap for house parties or a site-visit series.

**Tricks**

- Ask every first-time donor why they gave. Small lists are small enough to read every answer.
- The ED signs and makes 5 calls a week to recent donors above the median. It is cultivation, not mass gratitude ([retention-and-recurring.md](retention-and-recurring.md) on the call evidence).
- UK small charities: Gift Aid declarations on every eligible gift and the Gift Aid Small Donations Scheme for cash and contactless where eligible (HMRC).

**Watch-outs.** One person cannot run P2P, a gala, 40 grants and a monthly program at once. Cut to two engines.

## Community nonprofit (local service org: food, youth, arts, housing, animals)

**Shape.** Grants and contracts are a large share; volunteers are many; local businesses and congregations are partners; service users search on Google.

**Priorities**

1. Grant pipeline by fit, renewals calendared, restricted funds tracked by grant ([grants-major-gifts-board.md](grants-major-gifts-board.md)).
2. Diversify toward unrestricted individual giving. Federal and government funding can stop mid-month; organizations in 2025 had days to close books and invoice after stop-work orders (152 upvotes), and one org with 13 grant-funded staff had about 30 days of reserves (263 upvotes). Aim for at least 3 months of operating reserves and no single funder over a third of revenue.
3. Volunteer-to-donor path ([volunteers-and-impact.md](volunteers-and-impact.md)). Your volunteer file may be twice your donor file (1.96 volunteers per donor in hunger and poverty orgs, M+R 2025).
4. Google Ad Grants for service seekers and volunteers ([acquisition.md](acquisition.md)).
5. Local business partnerships with something the business wants ([acquisition.md](acquisition.md) section 8).
6. Google Business Profile for every site people visit: `local-seo`.

**Tricks**

- A funding cut is a legitimate appeal reason once confirmed: what was cut, what it paid for, the gap, the deadline (86 upvotes).
- Scrubbing website language did not save one org's federal funding (631-upvote comment), while another reported passing a funder's legal audit after adjusting external wording (6 upvotes). Decide this as a governance question with counsel and the board; do not treat it as a marketing tweak.
- Arts and performing arts orgs: ticket buyers are a donor prospect list. Add a donation option at checkout and a post-show thank-you with a "become a member" ask. Compare ticketing platforms on net per ticket at your real price.

## Church

**Shape.** Members give weekly or monthly; growth is guests becoming regulars; giving is teaching and habit, not appeals.

**Priorities**

1. Guest funnel: capture, same-day text, second and third visit, group or serving ([churches.md](churches.md)).
2. Recurring online giving as the default; wallets, QR and text-to-give.
3. Groups and serving teams as the retention engine.
4. Google Business Profile and a "plan your visit" page.
5. Building or mission campaigns with a gift range chart and a quiet phase.

**Tricks**

- Count second-visit rate monthly; it is the number that predicts growth.
- Use the "give every week / every month" framing; most members already think in weekly terms.
- Year-end giving statements by 31 January, with a December project offering.

**Watch-outs.** Never put guests into giving appeals. Do not copy charity-style urgency into worship communication.

## Cause startup (under 2 years, founder-led, no list)

**Shape.** One founder, a handful of volunteers, maybe a fiscal sponsor, no donor history.

**Priorities**

1. **Check the field first.** r/nonprofit moderators and members repeatedly advise partnering with an existing organization instead of starting a new one (85 and 261 upvotes); funders ask the same. Write one paragraph on why this does not already exist.
2. **Legal home:** own status (501(c)(3) in the US, registered charity elsewhere) or a fiscal sponsor. Sponsors charge a fee, often a percentage of income; many grants and Google Ad Grants need the org's own validated status.
3. **First 100 donors from the warm network:** a personal list of 150 to 300 people, a short personal email or text from the founder with a specific first project and a small goal, then a thank-you and update to every donor. A founder who "begged everyone and anyone for small scraps for a year" then landed a $30k grant (207 upvotes): small gifts build the track record funders look for.
4. **First board:** 3 to 5 people who give, open doors and do work; avoid a board of friends who only advise.
5. **One small family foundation or community foundation grant** in year one; first grants are often multi-year once you have outcomes (175 upvotes).
6. **One cheap, mission-fit event** (a trivia night drew 60 people for a first fundraiser, 69 upvotes) instead of a gala.
7. **Email list from day one** with consent, and a one-page website with a donation page.

**Tricks**

- Ask early donors to become monthly at $10 to $25; 30 monthly donors at $20 is $7,200 a year of predictable money.
- Publish simple outcomes from the first quarter; trust is the startup's only asset.
- Do not volunteer to write grants for free hoping to turn it into paid work without a written scope (86 upvotes); the same applies if you recruit volunteer grant writers: scope it.

## Membership organizations and associations

Members pay dues for benefits; donors give for impact. Run them as separate journeys. One association that collapsed member and prospect cadences into blasts lost 30% of membership revenue (109 upvotes). Renewal reminders 60, 30 and 7 days before expiry, auto-renew as default, and a donation add-on at renewal.
