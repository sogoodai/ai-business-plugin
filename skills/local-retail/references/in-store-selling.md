# In-store selling: conversion, ATV and UPT

The cheapest revenue in a shop is from people already inside. Flame Analytics 2026 puts fashion conversion at 15 to 30%; Shopify (citing Dor, 2026) gives 15 to 25% for apparel and 5 to 12% for luxury. No credible ATV or UPT benchmark exists for independent specialty shops, so compare each shop to itself by weekday, daypart, staff member and category.

## Find the leak

| Pattern in the data | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Conversion drops at peak hours | Understaffed; queues; no greeting | Staff to traffic, not to past transactions; add a second till or mobile POS at peaks |
| Conversion low all day, entries high | Browsers find nothing at their price, or the store looks picked over | Price-point tables near the door; refill hero stock; clear "under $X" zones |
| Fitting room busy, few sales | Wrong sizes, no styling help, bad mirrors or light | Size runs in stock; staff bring the second size and one companion piece |
| One staff member converts far better | Selling skill | Pair shifts; copy their greeting and add-on lines |
| ATV flat, UPT low | No companions near heroes | Build attach lines and displays |
| UPT fine, AUR falling | Mix shifting to cheap items | Check if deliberate (a downturn) or an accident of displays |

RetailNext's Balsam Brands case cut labor cost 31% with traffic-based scheduling (vendor case). The point for a small shop: schedule against entries by hour, because a store can look quiet in transactions while it is busy with unserved browsers.

## The first five meters

- **Decompression zone:** keep the first 1 to 2 meters inside the door clear of key product; people do not register it while adjusting (Paco Underhill, Why We Buy, 1999 observation research; old, still standard practice).
- **First table:** newest, highest-margin arrivals and one clear price point. Change it weekly so regulars see something new.
- **Price-point tables** ("under $25", "under $50") in gift shops and holiday season. They remove the search cost of "what can I afford".
- **Sightlines:** customers should see the till and a person from the door. A visible person raises entry and comfort.

## Raising UPT: companions, not "anything else?"

The mechanism is lowering the shopper's search cost, not pushing (research synthesis 2026). For each hero product, name one companion and put it within arm's reach.

| Shop | Hero | Companion |
|---|---|---|
| Bookshop | Gift hardback | The edition people pair with it, bookmark, card, wrap |
| Florist | Wrapped bouquet | Vase-ready version, card, chocolate or candle |
| Boutique | Trousers | Two tops that fix the proportion, belt |
| Gift shop | Candle | Matches, card, gift wrap |
| Vintage | Record player | Records, cleaning kit |
| Antiques | Furniture piece | Delivery, a lamp or mirror from the same era |

Staff lines: [scripts.md](scripts.md#staff-greeting-and-add-on-lines). Measure attach rate per hero and per staff member weekly.

## Raising ATV without discounting

- **Gift with purchase above a threshold** set about 20 to 30% above current ATV (if ATV is $45, set $55 to $60; a rule of thumb, test it against the shop's own ATV distribution). The gift costs little at cost: supplier samples bundled as a "$19.99 value, while supplies last" during slow periods (r/smallbusiness, 791 points, 118-upvote comment, proposed).
- **Free mystery item over $100** (vintage capsule eggs at $0.50 cost, r/Flipping, 1,125 points).
- **Bundles for one-off stock:** a complete, condition-matched glassware set sells as one lot more easily than single pieces (r/vintage, 1,036 points, speculative comment). Clear low-value media with steep quantity tiers (DVDs $1 or 3 for $2; CDs 40 for $10), then wholesale-lot the rest (r/Flipping, 1,841 points: $598 from a $75 lot in six days, about 16 hours of work).
- **Services that attach:** alterations, gift wrap, engraving, delivery, vase upgrades, framing, appraisal.
- **Separately priced delivery:** a $20 delivery add-on was chosen by 39 of 140 fridge buyers; at $5 fuel that was $585 before labor, and the seller would charge $25 next time (r/Flipping, 712 points).

## Out of stock is a selling moment

- **Substitute matrix for heroes:** if A is gone, offer B and C. Write it down for staff before peaks (research synthesis 2026).
- **Endless aisle:** when a size or color is missing, staff place the web order for the customer at the till rather than "check our website". Shopify's FROM FUTURE case attributes +10% sales to cross-location stock visibility, ship-to-customer and click-and-collect (vendor case).
- **Waitlist on the spot:** phone or email, item, size. Text the minute it lands.

## Price talk on the floor

- **Hold price on items that sell at asking.** A seller declined $25 on a $29.50 item already selling at that price (r/Flipping, 1,983 points).
- **Check the competitor's real delivered price.** A buyer's "new $145" Mercari alternative was used with $40 shipping ($185 delivered); the $175 receiver sold at full price days later (same thread).
- **Small haggles get an explanation, big ones end the talk.** One repair owner claims 98% of $10 to $20 hagglers buy after the price is explained (r/smallbusiness, 1,175 points, self-reported).
- **Agreed price stays agreed.** Buyers arriving with a lower number usually pay when told no (r/Flipping, 1,293 points). Keep inspection to about 5 minutes for used goods.
- **Rare items can wait.** A Civil War item sat two years at about $1,000 despite "overpriced" messages and sold at full price to a museum (r/Flipping, 1,454 points). Only if the owner can afford the holding time.

## Complaints at the counter

Open with "I'm sorry, how can we fix this for you?". One operator with 25,000 customers says about 90% then state a reasonable fix; refund in full immediately if they want money or stay unpleasant, to protect owner time (r/smallbusiness, 485 points). For missing stock on an order, offer a comparable replacement before cancelling; one buyer liked the replacement better (r/Flipping, 1,418 points).

## Turnaround promises

For repairs, restoration, framing, alterations and custom orders, quote with slack. A repair manager moved quotes from 6 to 8 days to 16 to 18, delivered in 8 to 9, and angry customers became thrilled ones (r/smallbusiness, 2,216 points, 64-upvote comment).

## Tricks

- **Staff ATV and UPT leaderboard** by shift, shown weekly. Coach the bottom with the top's lines.
- **Count fitting-room entries** with a tag board; fitting-room-to-sale is the boutique's real conversion.
- **Use the till queue:** small, high-margin impulse items within reach, rotated monthly.
- **Gift cards in product displays,** not only at the register, during gifting peaks.
- **Close on time politely** with assisted service for late arrivals who know what they want.
