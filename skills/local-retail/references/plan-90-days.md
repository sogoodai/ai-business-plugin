# 90-day plan

For a new or stalled single-location shop. Order matters: instrument, fix conversion, build local distribution, then buy demand, then create the repeat loop. The sequence comes from the research synthesis (2026); timings are operating choices. Each phase ends with a short owner report: what changed, the scorecard, decisions needed.

If the 90 days cross a holiday peak, merge in [holiday-calendar.md](holiday-calendar.md) from 8 weeks before the peak and pause new tests during it.

## Days 1 to 15: instrument

**Week 1: baseline**

- Export 12 months of POS transactions with line items and customer IDs. Compute ATV, UPT, AUR, gross margin by category, identified rate, regular rate, second-purchase rate ([store-metrics.md](store-metrics.md)).
- Start door counts by hour (counter or hand tally) and three days of passersby sampling per daypart.
- Find the best sales day of the last 90 days and its cause.
- List hero SKUs (top 20 to 40 by gross profit with steady sell-through) and dead stock over 180 days.
- Florists: split last 3 months of orders by channel and compute contribution per order ([florists.md](florists.md)).

**Week 2: Google and capture**

- GBP: category, hours, holiday hours, photos, products, UTM link, review QR at counter and on receipts (audit depth in `local-seo`).
- Merchant Center and free local listings through the POS integration or Local Inventory app. Hand-check top 20 SKUs on Google and Maps.
- One capture prompt at every payment, one preference field, consent per channel for the shop's country ([capture-and-loyalty.md](capture-and-loyalty.md)).
- Weekly scorecard set up.

**Exit check:** two weeks of entries by hour; identified rate measured daily; GBP and listings correct; hero list agreed with the owner.

## Days 16 to 30: fix conversion before buying reach

- Two window tests and two merchandising tests (first table, price-point table), on matched dayparts ([foot-traffic.md](foot-traffic.md), [in-store-selling.md](in-store-selling.md)).
- Conversion by staffed vs understaffed hours; move staff to peaks.
- Three add-on lines for the highest-volume categories; attach rate tracked per staff member.
- Substitute matrix for hero SKUs; endless-aisle web orders at the till.
- Flows live: welcome, post-purchase with review ask day 3 to 5, back-in-stock or waitlist. Clicks and orders are the scorecard.

**Exit check:** entry rate and conversion each compared to week-one baseline; identified rate moving toward 50%; flows sending.

## Days 31 to 45: build local distribution

- List ten neighboring businesses with overlapping customers and different core purchases.
- Pitch three mechanisms: co-event, product collaboration, receipt or QR referral ([scripts.md](scripts.md#neighbor-partnership-pitch)).
- Run the smallest test with one partner: baseline, partner code, new identified target.
- Four weeks of social around commercial moments: delivery day, new arrivals, curation, behind the scenes, price comparison, event invite. Trial Reels for new hooks ([social-and-live.md](social-and-live.md)).

**Exit check:** one partner event run and scored; partner ranking started; at least one Trial Reel hook that beats the account's usual non-follower reach.

## Days 46 to 60: acquire demand selectively

- If free listings are accurate and producing impressions, test Local Inventory Ads on high-margin, high-intent SKUs only. Measure store visits, directions, calls or offline sales where eligible (setup in `paid-ads`).
- Promote only organically proven creative on social, in a radius matching the real catchment, with a concrete hook (product, event, drop, same-day availability), never "come visit us".
- Join or start one shop-local mechanism with neighbors (trail, passport, shared map).

**Exit check:** cost per store action and gross profit on promoted SKUs known; stop anything without a path to a sale.

## Days 61 to 75: create the repeat product

Pick the shortest credible return loop for the shop:

| Shop | Loop |
|---|---|
| Florist | Refillable vase, office or restaurant standing order, monthly home arrangement |
| Bookshop | Book club pickup night, signed-edition club, preorder pickups |
| Boutique | Clienteling texts, VIP preview hour per drop, alterations |
| Vintage and antiques | Wishlist alerts, first-look day, Live auction slot |
| Gift shop | Occasion reminders, seasonal collector series, maker launch nights |

Measure the share of first-time identified buyers who make a second purchase within 90 days.

**Exit check:** the loop has its first 20 to 50 participants and a weekly count.

## Days 76 to 90: cut losers, compound winners

- Rank every acquisition source by contribution margin from first purchase plus known repeat, not revenue or reach.
- Stop campaigns with no measurable path to a sale. Repeat the best partner event. Make the best window the default. Film the best hook with new products. Widen Google inventory in categories with local search demand.
- Owner report answers five questions with numbers: where new customers discover the shop, what share of passersby enter, what share of entrants buy, what share of buyers become identifiable, what share come back.

## Questions for the owner along the way

- Which discounts, gifts or events are acceptable, and the minimum dollar margin per order?
- Who may change prices and window displays?
- Which neighbors would they never partner with?
- Staff hours they can move to peaks?
- Florists: which wire memberships they would drop if contribution proves negative?
