# Social, live selling and local marketplaces

Content systems, batching and platform craft live in `organic-social`; ad accounts in `paid-ads`. This file keeps the shop angle: content that moves today's stock and brings people to the door.

## What changed in 2025 and 2026

- **Original content wins on Instagram.** 75% of US recommendations came from original posts after original-content prevalence rose 10 points in Q4 2025 (Meta, Jan 2026). Supplier imagery, memes and reposted inspiration are a weak default.
- **Trial Reels** show a Reel to non-followers first; results after about 24 hours, and a winning trial can auto-publish more broadly based on the first 72 hours (Meta, Dec 2024). Of 400k+ creators, 40% posted Reels more often after trying it, and 80% of those got more non-follower reach a month later (Meta, June 2025, not guaranteed).
- **Instagram Map** (2025, US first) can show location-tagged Reels, posts and Stories as local content. Tag the actual location, not only the city hashtag (Meta, Aug 2025).
- **Reposts** of an original keep attribution and can be recommended to the reposter's followers (Meta, Aug 2025). Ask partners to repost, not re-upload.
- **Posting more stopped working on TikTok:** brand views -23% YoY while posting rose 40% in 2025 (Socialinsider 2026).
- **Instagram ended live shopping** (product tags in Lives) on 16 March 2023; Facebook ended its live shopping in 2022 (TechCrunch, Feb 2023). Lives still work for attention; checkout must happen elsewhere.
- **TikTok Shop grew in Europe:** 100,000+ businesses across France, Germany, Italy, Spain and Ireland by 2026, expanding to Austria, Belgium, the Netherlands and Poland in June 2026; 31% of British online shoppers had bought on TikTok Shop (TikTok newsroom citing NielsenIQ, 2026, platform claims).

## Content that brings people to the door

The shop's edge is what competitors cannot copy: today's rack, the owner's buying logic, restoration, flower conditioning, curation, provenance, local happenings.

**Weekly rhythm for a small shop (2 to 4 posts plus Stories)**

1. Delivery day or new arrivals, shot on the actual shelf with prices.
2. One curation or "owner's pick" with a reason.
3. One proof post: customer styling (with permission), before and after restoration, what a budget buys.
4. Event or partner post, tagged location, partner reposts.

**Hooks by shop (test as Trial Reels, scale the winners)** (research synthesis 2026)

- Boutique: "Three things everyone tries on and puts back, until they see them styled."
- Bookshop: "If you liked [known title] but hated the ending, read these instead."
- Vintage: "Why this $40-looking chair is actually a $400 piece."
- Florist: "What $50, $75 and $100 actually buys from a local florist this week."
- Gift shop: "Five gifts under $30 that do not look like panic buys."
- Antiques: "How to tell real 1950s teak from a reproduction in 10 seconds."

**Procedure:** write 3 to 5 hooks around the same commercial goal, publish as Trial Reels, wait for non-follower results, then put organic push or paid budget behind the winner. Reuse the idea, not the asset: film the winning hook again with a new arrival every week.

**Attribution:** a store-only keyword ("say VINTAGE LIVE at checkout") or a POS code ties social to physical transactions. Five local visits beat a viral Reel watched abroad.

**Engagement health check only:** Instagram brand engagement 0.45% by followers, TikTok 3.90% by views (Socialinsider Q3 2026). Not commercial KPIs.

## Live selling

**Where checkout lives in 2026**

| Platform | Checkout in Live | Fits | Seller cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok Shop Live (US, UK, several EU markets) | Yes | Demonstrable, giftable, fashion, collectibles | Check current Shop fees in your market |
| Whatnot | Yes, auctions and buy-now | Vintage, collectibles, records, cards, toys, antiques | 8% commission (some categories lower) + 2.9% + $0.30 in the US; about 11 to 12% all-in (third-party 2026 guides; check Whatnot's page) |
| Instagram or Facebook Live | No product tags since 2022 to 2023 | Attention, drops, "DM to claim" or link to site | Payment fees only |
| Own site live tools (CommentSold-style, Shopify apps) | Yes | Boutiques with a loyal list | App subscription |

**TikTok's own operating guidance** (Seller Academy, platform recommendations, not independent data):

- Start with 1 to 2 hour sessions.
- Plan about 6 to 10 products per hour; 2 to 5 bestsellers often make 60 to 90% of Live sales.
- Pin a product about every 30 seconds.
- Post a teaser at least one day before and promote again about two hours before; keep teasers under 30 seconds.

**Shop rules on top**

- Bring the hero SKU back every 10 to 15 minutes; viewers arrive continuously (research synthesis 2026).
- Run Lives on delivery day so stock is new and plentiful.
- Price and condition said out loud and on screen. For vintage, show flaws on camera; it cuts returns.
- Hold a fixed weekly slot so regulars learn it.
- Live sales must decrement POS inventory at once. Selling a one-off twice is a customer-service problem, not a sale.
- Decline requests for unpaid custom content (squeeze videos, extra angles) from people with no buying history; sellers report they almost never buy (r/Flipping, 2,013 points).

## Facebook Marketplace and local listings (resellers with a storefront)

- **Repost every 3 days** when inquiries fall off; one seller moved 140 fridges in about 95 days this way (r/Flipping, 712 points).
- **List above your floor and hold it when demand is strong:** $65 OBO with a $50 floor sold 102 at $65 (same thread).
- **The price-drop button shows a strikethrough:** list slightly above target, then lower about 10% ($475 to $427.50) to show a visible discount (r/Flipping, 7,884 points, interface at that time; check current behavior).
- **Release two or three similar units at a time** with different specs, and refill as they sell, to keep messages manageable and not flood the market (r/Flipping, 3,449 points).
- **Proof before pickup:** a working video, photos from every angle and a link to the listing; five-minute inspection limit at collection; run the item in front of the buyer (one return happened only on the unit not demonstrated) (r/Flipping, 1,293 and 3,449 points).
- **Short cash:** "Cash only. If you need cash there is an ATM down the street." (r/Flipping, 1,293 points).
- **Always link the shop.** Every listing mentions the storefront address and hours; Marketplace buyers become walk-ins.

## Tricks most owners miss

- **Location tag every post** for the Instagram Map, plus the partner's handle on collab posts.
- **Collab posts with neighbors** reach both audiences with one original.
- **Save "sold" posts as a highlight** for antiques and vintage: proof that good things go fast drives "come early" visits.
- **Do not boost the feed at random.** Boosts gave one-time lifts while partner vending and community events built regulars (operators report, r/smallbusiness).
