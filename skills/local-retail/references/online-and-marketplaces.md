# Online sales, marketplaces and local delivery

Online should extend the shop's assortment and customer file, not become a second company unless the economics justify it. Store CRO, product pages, BFCM and Etsy or Amazon depth live in `ecommerce-brand`.

## KPI order

```
inventory accuracy -> fulfilled-on-time rate -> online conversion -> contribution margin -> repeat rate
```

A vintage shop that sells a one-off twice because stock did not sync has bought a complaint, not a sale (research synthesis 2026).

## Unified POS and online

- One inventory across shop and site (Shopify POS, Square, Lightspeed). Shopify's FROM FUTURE case (15+ French boutiques) attributes +10% sales to cross-location stock visibility, ship-to-customer and click-and-collect (vendor case). PAIGE reports inventory accuracy from 85% to 98% and omnichannel customers worth 44% more (vendor case, large brand).
- Shopify POS added future pickup orders (March 2026) and local-delivery orders created in POS (September 2026, eligibility conditions apply) (Shopify changelog).
- Staff place web orders at the till for missing sizes or colors. Credit the store, not the site.

## BOPIS and reservations

- 52% of US holiday shoppers planned to use buy online, pick up in store (ICSC 2025).
- "Order online, collect today" on high-intent product pages only where inventory is reliable.
- Pickup is a visit: put the pickup point past a new-arrivals table, and have staff ask the capture question.

## Local delivery

- Reserve it for products where convenience earns a fee: flowers, urgent gifts, bulky antiques and furniture, same-day occasions (research synthesis 2026). Free delivery on small orders is a margin transfer, not growth.
- Price it as an add-on and include fuel: $20 delivery chosen by 39 of 140 buyers, $5 fuel each, $25 next time as gas rose (r/Flipping, 712 points).
- Or require pickup for heavy, high-demand goods (60 to 80 lb units collected from the seller in peak season, r/Flipping, 3,449 points).
- Route density: group deliveries by area and day. Florists with standing commercial accounts gain most ([florists.md](florists.md)).

## Website basics for a shop

- Hours, address, map, parking, phone and "what's new this week" on the home page.
- Show shipping cost before checkout. A shopper bought from a small site despite higher shipping than Amazon because a ZIP-and-address-type checker showed the cost on the product page (r/smallbusiness, 1,077 points, shopper's view).
- For custom work (furniture, framing, floral design), show a few standard items with prices and a buy button; "custom" alone reads as unaffordable (r/smallbusiness, 708 points, 49-upvote comment, proposed).

## Marketplace fee math

**Rule:** list on a marketplace only when contribution after marketplace fees, fulfillment labor, returns allowance and shipping subsidy beats the owner's minimum dollar margin. Use marketplaces for discovery; capture repeat demand with compliant inserts, packaging and brand recall, without breaking off-platform rules (research synthesis 2026).

| Platform | Fees (check current terms) | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Etsy | $0.20 listing, 6.5% transaction, payment processing by country; Offsite Ads 15% of attributed orders under $10k trailing Etsy sales, 12% above; 2.5% currency conversion; regulatory fees in some countries (Etsy Help) | Vintage (Etsy policy: 20+ years old), handmade, gifts |
| eBay | About 13 to 13.6% final value in many categories, applied to shipping too (operators report, r/Flipping, 259 points) | Collectibles, antiques, parts, rare media |
| Whatnot | 8% commission (lower in some categories) + 2.9% + $0.30 processing, US; about 11 to 12% all-in (2026 third-party guides) | Live auctions of vintage, records, cards, toys |
| Facebook Marketplace (local pickup) | No fee for local cash or pickup sales; shipped sales carry fees | Bulky goods, furniture, fast local turns |
| Own Shopify site | Subscription + about 2.9% + $0.30 processing | Repeat buyers, the list |

**Worked comparison on a $50 sale** (operators report, r/Flipping, 259 points; verify rates): eBay $7.20, Etsy $5.20, Poshmark $10, Depop US about $2, Shopify $1.75 processing. Allocate the subscription: at $39 a month and 20 sales, Shopify costs $1.95 + $1.75 = $3.70 per order. At 5 sales a month it is $9.55.

```
marketplace contribution = price - item cost - marketplace fees (on item + shipping) - payment fee
                           - shipping cost + shipping charged - packing labor - returns allowance - ad fees
```

**Underwrite bulky and liquidation buys conservatively.** A $15 toy sold for $49.93 lost $4.45 after shipping and fees (r/Flipping, 735 points). A truckload of fridges forecast at 60% of retail sold at 42%: $5,567 profit before labor on $18,133 (r/Flipping, 306 points). Underwrite at the lower number.

**Bulky goods priced low locally can be the opportunity:** one seller bought $10 to $40 Ab Lounges locally and sold them on eBay at $169 to $199 for over $120 net per unit for six years, quitting when profit fell under $70 a unit (r/Flipping, 1,468 points). Set a minimum profit per unit and retire a product line under it.

**Promoted listings:** one eBay seller paid $394 in a year at a 1% promoted rate against $21,145 in attributed sales; sales roughly doubled after starting, increment unproven (r/Flipping, 735 points). Test at a low rate and compare against the prior period.

## Pricing one-off stock online

- Price from sold comparables in matching condition, never the highest sale on record. A $3,000 "ask" for 9 Beatles albums fell to $50 once condition-matched sold comps showed $8 to $12 each (r/Flipping, 1,114 points).
- For rare untested items, anchor to the closest working comparable and list "for parts or repair": a $10,000 ask got a $500 lowball, which the seller declined; the same buyer offered $5,000 twenty minutes later (r/Flipping, 1,049 points).
- Auctions: set the opening bid at your minimum or use Buy It Now; commenters say reserves scare buyers (r/Flipping, 1,418 points, 452-upvote comment for Buy It Now).
- Reserve unusual editions (imports, signed, sealed promos) for individual listings before clearing the rest cheaply.
- Accepting very low offers just to clear old stock brought 50% returns for one seller (r/Flipping, 2,013 points).

## Tricks most owners miss

- **Misspelled listings** on eBay are an under-bid sourcing channel (r/Flipping, 2,840 points).
- **Sourcing cards** naming the exact brands and pieces you buy got a vintage reseller 50+ garments' worth of photos; harassing follow-ups after a no got them blocked (r/Flipping, 922 points). One follow-up, then stop.
- **Pay staff a finder's fee for stock you buy** from their network; a dealership's $250 bounty plus 5% of resale gross turned its worst salesman into 13 acquisitions in two weeks (r/smallbusiness, 691 points; adapt for antiques and vintage buying).
- **Selling to the shop beats a higher private offer** when you can say why: "you'll get paid fast, it'll be painless, we do this every day, so your call" (same thread).
