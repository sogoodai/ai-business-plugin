# By niche

What changes by shop type. Everything else in the skill applies to all.

## Boutiques (clothing, shoes, accessories, home)

- **Conversion is the fitting room.** Count fitting-room entries and sales from them. Staff bring the second size and one companion. Fashion conversion runs 15 to 30% (Flame Analytics 2026); below that, look at sizes in stock and staffing at peaks.
- **Lost-size sales:** unified POS and online stock so staff can order the missing size at the till (Shopify FROM FUTURE case: +10% sales attributed to omnichannel features, vendor).
- **Clienteling** is the loyalty program: sizes, style notes, a personal text when their size lands.
- **Drops and season changes** create the reason to visit. A VIP preview hour before each.
- **Partners:** jeweler, salon, nail studio, photographer, alterations.
- **Live:** boutique try-on Lives on delivery day; checkout through the site or TikTok Shop where available.
- If the boutique is also building its own label online, use `fashion-apparel`. Accessories lines: `jewelry-accessories`.

## Gift shops and stationery

- **Price points sell gifts.** Tables and web collections under $25, $50 and $100; "five gifts under $30 that don't look like panic buys" as a hook.
- **Gift wrap and cards are UPT engines.** Put them next to the hero items, not only at the till.
- **Local makers on consignment or wholesale** bring their own audiences; give each a launch moment.
- **Tourist season** can dominate; capture tourists' email for online reorders and next-year visits.
- **Gift buyers are two customers:** capture the giver, offer a reminder for the recipient's date.

## Bookshops

- **ISBNs make local inventory easy.** Every title can appear in Google local listings with availability. Do this first.
- **Events are the core acquisition channel:** author talks, signings, book clubs, launch parties, school partnerships. Authors and publishers bring the audience.
- **Independent Bookstore Day** is the last Saturday of April (24 April 2027); exclusive merchandise is ordered months ahead from the American Booksellers Association catalog.
- **Return loops:** book club pickup nights, signed first-edition clubs, subscription boxes, preorders for big releases with in-store pickup.
- **Curation sells:** "If you liked [title] but hated the ending" shelves and Reels; staff pick cards with one sentence each.
- **Late crowds near theaters:** assisted purchases at close (r/retail, 1,439 points).
- **Companion line:** "the edition most people buy with this as a gift."

## Antiques and vintage stores

- **One-of-a-kind stock changes the playbook.** No replenishment; the trigger is new stock matching what they bought. Wishlist alerts and "first look" previews are the loyalty program.
- **Inventory sync is critical** across shop, Instagram, Etsy, eBay, Whatnot and Marketplace: one item sold twice is a refund and a bad review.
- **Pricing:** sold comps in matching condition, never the top sale on record (r/Flipping, 1,114 points). Rare items can sit at full price if the owner can afford the wait (Civil War item, two years, sold to a museum at about $1,000; r/Flipping, 1,454 points).
- **Check local sell-through before paying up:** Avon Cape Cod glass sat unsold on shopgoodwill, Marketplace and in antique malls despite a $450 collection purchase (r/vintage, 1,036 points).
- **Content:** provenance, restoration, "why this looks like $40 but is $400", how to spot a fake. Location-tag everything.
- **Mystery capsule machine** for small odds and ends: about 150 eggs a month at $0.50 cost (r/Flipping, 1,125 points).
- **Live auctions** on Whatnot for collectibles, records and toys; about 11 to 12% all-in fees in the US (2026 guides).
- **Partners:** interior designers, home stagers, realtors, film and photo shoots, tattoo studios. A reseller's listing photos led a realtor to hire them for property photography and staging at $75 to $150 an hour (r/Flipping, 1,994 points).
- **Sourcing:** sourcing cards naming exact brands and pieces (one follow-up maximum), finder's fees for staff, estate and move-out timing.

## Resellers with a storefront (thrift, secondhand, appliances, furniture, media)

- **Seasonal arbitrage** is the margin: buy off-season, release at first demand (AC $60 to $240; skis $50 to $200+; roof racks £20 to £100; student move-out furniture $50 to $90) (r/Flipping, 3,449 points). Release only 2 or 3 similar units at a time.
- **Condition rules before buying:** written damage thresholds (patio sets: pass on structural damage, shredded wicker, missing cushions; accept small rust, faded slings) (r/Flipping, 459 points).
- **Underwrite liquidation at 40% of stated retail,** and unwrap and test every unit before pricing (r/Flipping, 306 points). Never value an unmanifested mystery lot from its example photo (r/Flipping, 486 points).
- **Price by condition against a named retailer:** new 30% off, scratch-and-dent 40% off, damaged 60% off the Costco price, with expensive units needing about 50% off (r/Flipping, 306 points).
- **Demonstrate at pickup** and limit inspections to five minutes.
- **Every Marketplace listing names the storefront** and hours: online browsers become walk-ins.

## Other specialty shops (trophies, engraving, framing, repair counters)

- **Separate storefront brands by buyer** (corporate awards vs fraternity vs joke trophies) on one backend; commenters warned against mixing fraternity and HR positioning (r/smallbusiness, 927 points, proposed).
- **Packaged tiers for clubs and game stores** (club trophy plus top three, plaque plus top three, trophies only, medals only).
- **B2B work from the same equipment:** engraved electrical-panel labels and commercial signs won contracts for a family engraving shop (same thread).
- **Turnaround quotes with slack** (16 to 18 days quoted, 8 to 9 delivered; r/smallbusiness, 2,216 points).
