# Benchmarks

Read the cohort before the number. Most retail benchmarks come from vendors' own customers (traffic counters, POS, email platforms). They describe those customers, not the market. "n/a" means the source publishes no quartile; never fill it in. Sources with URLs: [sources.md](sources.md).

## No credible benchmark exists for

ATV and UPT for independent boutiques, gift shops or bookshops; window-display ROI; event ROI beyond single cases; loyalty program lift with a control group; Teleflora's current member economics (public numbers are pre-2024). Use the shop's own baseline and say the gap out loud.
