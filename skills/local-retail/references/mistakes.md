# Expensive mistakes

Two tables: what the data shows (measured, platform terms, or arithmetic on stated numbers) and what practitioners report losing.

## From the data

| Mistake | What it costs | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Buying or incentivizing Google reviews | Google can remove reviews, block new ones and show a warning (Google contribution policy) | One uniform ask with a direct link or QR, no reward |
| Running Local Inventory Ads before the feed is accurate | Paid clicks to unavailable stock; out-of-stock items lose local eligibility (Google Merchant Center) | Free local listings first, manual SKU checks, then LIA on high-margin heroes |
| Judging email on open rate | Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens (Top Growth Marketing 2026) | Clicks, orders and revenue per recipient; flows vs campaigns |
| Posting more because views fell | TikTok brand views -23% YoY while posting rose 40% (Socialinsider 2026) | Fewer, original posts; Trial Reels before scaling |
| Storewide discount without margin math | 20% off at 50% margin needs +66.7% units to stand still | Event-only add-on or gift with purchase over a threshold |
| Calling marketplace GMV revenue quality | Etsy 6.5% + processing + Offsite Ads 12 to 15% + other fees (Etsy Help) | Contribution per order after all fees and labor |
| Judging events by attendance | P.F. Candle's 17 to 34% lifts mean nothing without baseline and capture (Modern Retail 2026) | Baseline, partner codes, new identified buyers, 90-day value |
| Buying traffic while conversion is broken | Small gains downstream beat more visitors (Flame 2026 ranges) | Count entries and conversion before campaigns |
| Treating wire order value as owned revenue | FTD filler keeps about 71 to 73% before other fees (FTD 2025) | Channel P&L per order; wire only to fill idle capacity |
| Anonymous checkouts | Regulars spend about 6x transients and cannot be grown if unknown (Square 2026) | One capture prompt at every payment |
| One US-style consent checkbox everywhere | PECR, ePrivacy, CASL and Spam Act exposure | Localized consent per channel and country |
| Reporting Small Business Saturday forecasts or Amex totals as results | NRF forecast 67M, actual 62.7M (2025); Amex totals are survey projections | Report the shop's own sales vs baseline |
| Online orders on unsynced one-off stock | Same item sold twice: refund, complaint, review risk | One inventory across all channels before listing online |
