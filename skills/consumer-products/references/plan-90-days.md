# 90-day plans

One shared spine, then one plan per sub-model. The order comes from the research: freeze expansion and fix the economics, pick one hero and one offer, create proof, kill weak variants, package evidence, then scale only the mechanism that worked (deep research, Nov 2026).

Agent rule for every plan: **never authorize a scale action because a top-of-funnel metric improved.** Require the channel's downstream proof: contribution for DTC and Amazon, net Shop contribution for TikTok, reorder for subscriptions and wholesale, non-promoted velocity for retail.

Each phase ends with a short owner report: what changed, the scorecard, what the owner must decide.

## The shared spine

**Days 1 to 14: economics and claims.**

- Build the SKU by channel contribution model ([economics.md](economics.md)): landed COGS, waterfall per channel, Amazon, Faire and TikTok fee stacks, market-day break-even.
- Record current repeat (DTC cohorts by month), reorder timing, and store velocity if in retail.
- Kill any SKU and channel combination that is negative before paid acquisition.
- Build the claims ledger; remove every claim on pack, site, listings and ads that is not substantiated for that jurisdiction ([claims-labeling.md](claims-labeling.md)).
- Sync Merchant Center, GTINs, Product and Offer schema, shipping and returns, review feeds.

**Days 15 to 30: one hero, one offer, flows on.**

- Pick one hero SKU and one acquisition offer: a starter or variety bundle for food; one routine or outcome category for beauty and supplements, inside claims rules.
- Turn on welcome, first-purchase education, replenishment, review request and win-back flows (sibling `email-sms-loyalty`). If subscriptions exist, instrument churn by renewal number.

**Days 31 to 45: create proof.** Demos or events, controlled market tests, or creator seeding, depending on the sub-model below.

**Days 46 to 60: kill weak variants.** Rank retail SKUs by non-promoted $ and units per store per week; DTC offers by first-order contribution, 30-day repeat and blended CAC; creator assets by contribution after commission, refunds and ad spend. Ask every retailer for the category benchmark and next review date.

**Days 61 to 75: package the evidence.** Eight-slide buyer deck, one-page sell sheet, retailer-specific launch economics, 12-week test proposal ([scripts.md](scripts.md)). Submit only where a category review is open. Route self-sourced wholesale accounts to Faire Direct.

**Days 76 to 90: scale the proven mechanism only.** Retail velocity above benchmark: add stores. Creator content selling profitably: more seeding and paid amplification. Replenishment producing repeat: deepen segmentation. First-renewal churn high: fix cadence and education before buying subscribers. Nothing working: change product, offer or price before adding reach.

## Food and beverage (sauces, snacks, drinks)

| Days | Actions | Exit check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 14 | Waterfall for direct-to-independent and distributor routes; landed COGS under about 25% of shelf or a plan to get there; claims and label audit; liability insurance; GS1 barcodes; pH and process documentation for acidified products | Every SKU has a channel where contribution is positive |
| 15 to 30 | Choose 2 hero SKUs; 3-pack and gift bundle online; list 50 to 100 target independents within delivery range (Maps, adjacent-brand stockists); sell sheet and sample kit | Sell sheet done; bundle contribution positive |
| 31 to 45 | Weekly markets or events with one variable changed per day; 10 to 20 independents sampled per week (owner sets the number the sample budget allows); demos in current stores | Market break-even reached; first opening orders |
| 46 to 60 | Reorder prompts at day 30; shelf checks; rank SKUs by sell-through and UPSPW; ask current stores for category benchmarks | Second-order rate measured per account |
| 61 to 75 | Buyer deck and 12-week test proposal for one regional chain or a local program (Whole Foods local forager, co-op) timed to its review; RangeMe profile current | One submission inside an open review window |
| 76 to 90 | Add doors only where velocity beats the benchmark; distributor conversation only if a retailer requires it | Owner sees velocity-to-dollars table per store |

Owner questions: maximum free-fill and demo budget; delivery radius you will drive; which SKUs you will cut.

## Coffee and tea

| Days | Actions | Exit check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 14 | Wholesale price list with suggested retail and minimums; contribution per bag by channel; label descriptors simplified; fill weights checked | Wholesale contribution positive after delivery |
| 15 to 30 | Maps list of independent cafes, offices and restaurants within 40 miles; sample kits with catalog (photo, unit price, SRP, minimum); subscription with cadence and grind choices | 30+ prospects sampled |
| 31 to 45 | Follow-ups on day 3 and day 10; cafe tasting or office cupping events; corporate gifting outreach for Q4 | First accounts; subscription live |
| 46 to 60 | Reorder rhythm per account; subscription churn by renewal; re-pitch list at 12 weeks | Accounts reordering on schedule |
| 61 to 75 | Pitch one local grocer with account proof; plan the 12-weekly re-pitch | Grocer meeting or calendar entry |
| 76 to 90 | Add delivery days only where route density pays; cap any single account's share in the plan | No account above the owner's share cap |

## Beauty and skincare

| Days | Actions | Exit check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 14 | Cosmetic vs drug claim audit; MoCRA status (or small-business exemption records); EU or GB Responsible Person if selling there; DTC and Amazon contribution; review program audit (no gating, no staff reviews) | Every claim is cosmetic and documented |
| 15 to 30 | Hero product plus a paid mini or discovery set; product page with real before-and-after and shade range; education email sequence; Merchant Center feed with skin-type attributes | Discovery offer contribution known |
| 31 to 45 | Seed 30 to 50 category-matched creators on commission with the claims-safe brief; TikTok Shop with about five distinct videos at launch; own-product giveaway | Videos with order data |
| 46 to 60 | Authorize winning creator videos for GMV Max or Meta partnership ads; kill the rest; 90-day repeat and first-renewal churn by cohort | Net contribution per creator asset |
| 61 to 75 | Specialty retail or clean-beauty boutique pitches with DTC repeat and review proof; Faire Direct for self-sourced shops | 5+ stores pitched with proof |
| 76 to 90 | Scale only creators and offers with positive contribution after commission, fees, refunds and ads; add a set order cap for viral spikes | CAC under first-order contribution plus measured second-order contribution |

## Supplements

| Days | Actions | Exit check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 14 | Claims ledger per market; FDA structure/function notices filed; disclaimer everywhere; ingredient regulatory status (including hemp rules from Nov 12, 2026); cGMP documents from the manufacturer; Amazon shelf-life math; payment-token portability | No disease claim anywhere; notices filed |
| 15 to 30 | First order as a trial size or starter pack; post-purchase education sequence keyed to depletion; subscription offered after first use, skip and change-frequency before first rebill | Churn instrumented by renewal number |
| 31 to 45 | Micro-niche creators on performance pay with an approved claims bank; Amazon listing with S&S; check ad-policy status before spend | Approved ads running; no disapprovals unresolved |
| 46 to 60 | Kill creatives and offers on contribution; first-renewal churn read; fix education if churn is above the 26.5% median | First-renewal churn measured |
| 61 to 75 | Natural retail or pharmacy pitch with repeat proof; ask chains to co-fund launch costs | One retail conversation with terms |
| 76 to 90 | Buy subscribers only when first-renewal churn and payback fit the owner's window | Payback inside the owner's window |

## Cottage food

| Days | Actions | Exit check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 14 | Read the current state statute: allowed products, cap, venues, online and shipping rules, label wording; register or permit; label fixes | Every product and venue is legal |
| 15 to 30 | Weekly preorder menu with pickup windows; one or two markets booked after walking the lane; text or email list with consent | First preorder week sold out or measured |
| 31 to 45 | Market tests, one variable per day; flavor votes; premium tier; occasional free giveaway | Market break-even reached |
| 46 to 60 | Repeat rate from preorder list; drop slow items; add the easiest high-demand item | Repeat buyers per week rising |
| 61 to 75 | Local cafes or farm stands if the law allows; holiday presale menu | Holiday presale live |
| 76 to 90 | If the cap is in sight: commissary kitchen quotes and signed interest from accounts before leasing | Graduation plan with numbers |

## Weekly scorecard (all sub-models)

```
units and contribution by channel | doors carrying and UPSPW (retail) | opening orders, second orders (wholesale)
new customers, first-order contribution, 30/60/90-day repeat (DTC) | churn at renewal 1, 2, 3 (subscriptions)
market days: units, contacts, break-even | creator assets: orders and contribution | claims changes logged
```
