# Benchmarks

Measured and platform numbers first, then planning ranges, then operator reports. Vendor datasets describe their own customers, not the market; they are flagged. "Possibly outdated" marks data from before 2024.

Two traps (deep research, 2026):

1. **CPA is not CAC.** Platform CPA may include returning buyers and relies on platform attribution. Blended CAC = total acquisition spend / genuinely new customers.
2. **Retail repeat is not DTC repeat.** Household-panel repeat counts households buying again anywhere in retail; Shopify repeat counts direct reorders. Never compare them without the denominator.

There is no credible universal "good" velocity for emerging CPG. SPINS and Circana define velocity relative to distribution; compare against the same category, retailer, price tier and promoted state.

## What has no benchmark

No robust public number exists for: velocity "required" by a given chain, independent-store reorder rates by category, demo cost per incremental unit for small brands, market-day sales by product type in 2025 to 2026, co-packer COGS as a percentage of price, cottage food business revenue, creator fees for food brands, or supplement subscription LTV. Say so instead of inventing one, and build the brand's own denominator.
