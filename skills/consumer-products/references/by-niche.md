# By niche

What changes by product type: the first channel, the content that sells, the claims trap and the number to watch. Shared mechanics live in the other references.

## Sauces, condiments, pantry

- **First channels:** farmers markets, specialty food and gift shops, local grocers, hot sauce and specialty retailers (online and physical), then regional grocery.
- **Economics:** glass, caps and shipping weight make single-bottle DTC lose money (see [economics.md](economics.md#waterfall-3-dtc-why-single-jars-do-not-work)). Sell 3-packs and gift sets online; price singles for retail.
- **Process:** most shelf-stable sauces are acidified foods; know pH and water activity, the process authority letter and state rules (see [production.md](production.md#acidified-and-low-acid-foods-us)).
- **Content that sells:** enthusiast communities. A maker's market-prep post listing unusual flavors (cucumber lime jalapeño, dill pickle jalapeño, mango habanero, ghost reaper strawberry) hit 1,405 upvotes and drew buyers asking how to order (r/hotsauce). Heat ratings, food pairings, preparation shots.
- **Premium cues:** a distinctive cap let one brand charge $15 to $20 when most bottles were $5 to $8; commissioned label art won awards; limited collector releases in blown-glass bottles sell out and resell at up to 8x (operators report). Credit the label artist on the back.
- **Recovery is marketing:** Tabasco's lot-number check and twelve replacement bottles, Heatonist's same-day replacements with an extra bottle, Tapatio's free goodie basket to a fan asking about a sold-out hat each became highly upvoted posts (operators report).
- **Watch:** sell-through per account, UPSPW, breakage rate.
- **Trap:** shrinkflation framed as a saving (485-pt backlash thread).

## Snacks, sweets, baked goods (packaged)

- **First channels:** markets, cafes and coffee shops, offices and vending, corporate gifting, then grocery.
- **Gifting:** a commissary bakery founder reported about $150,000 a year in corporate gifting and about $350,000 in December gifting (operators report). Pitch office managers and event planners in September for Q4.
- **Event favors:** design showrooms and venues host events; branded snack favors with the host's sticker are a B2B channel (suggestion, 69-pt comment).
- **Novelty and impulse:** a candy gag gift priced at $19.99 delivered went viral after one shareable package was posted on Imgur, then sold through blog coverage (operators report). Make the received product self-explanatory in a photo.
- **Recipe choice:** paired taste tests with strangers before launch or reformulation (secondhand case).
- **Watch:** shelf life, breakage, seasonal concentration.

## Beverages (non-alcoholic)

- **First channels:** events and festivals, cafes and restaurants, gyms and venues that match the brand, then grocery.
- **Seed the scene, not the aisle:** Liquid Death first sold through bars and tattoo parlors with beer-style packaging (secondhand case). Pick venues where the pack fits the culture.
- **Production:** beverage co-packer minimums run from two pallets to 20,000 bottles or thousands of gallons (copackermatch, 2026); freight is heavy, so regional density matters more than reach.
- **Events:** music festivals and food events grow mobile drink businesses' sales and social reach (secondhand); premium event pricing works (about 20 people in line for $12 boba at a tournament).
- **Alcohol** (beer, wine, spirits, hard seltzer) runs under TTB label approval and state three-tier rules; out of scope here beyond this pointer. Check counsel.
- **Watch:** cases per account per month, cold-chain needs, deposit and recycling rules by state.

## Coffee

- **First channels:** wholesale to independent cafes, offices and restaurants; then subscription DTC; retail bags in grocery later.
- **Wholesale is the profit engine for small roasters:** one roaster with a single cafe and 8 wholesale accounts roasts about 65,000 lb a year, about $1.8M revenue at 19% profit after the owner's salary (operators report, 55-pt comment). Another roaster built 20 to 30 accounts in four months from a Google Maps list (1,885-pt thread).
- **Persist with grocers:** the 12-week re-pitch over two years that made one grocer equal to 25 other accounts (r/Coffee).
- **DTC fit:** coffee has the highest 365-day repeat of ecommerce categories at 31.7% (By the Numbers, 2026, via `ecommerce-brand`); subscriptions with grind and cadence choices work. One new coffee founder had $20,000 of inventory and a $30,000 SEO quote before a working store (operators report); start with wholesale accounts and a subscription offer, not paid SEO.
- **Labels:** plain flavor descriptors ("sweet," "lime, stone fruit, nutty") over "rock candy" or "kaffir lime"; leave cupping scores off retail bags unless the context is explained (Q grader, 207-pt thread). Fill to the stated weight.
- **Cafe tactic for converting palates:** ask sugar users to taste a flat white or pour-over before adding anything (operators report). Use it at events too.
- **Commodity risk:** green prices swing; build price-review dates into wholesale contracts.
- **Cafe operations** (menu, staff, delivery apps): sibling `restaurant`.

## Tea and herbal blends

- **Pick the less crowded category:** a founder who could not break even selling coffee to restaurants in a coffee-growing country switched to locally grown tea blends where few local suppliers competed, and won a fast-food chain's cafe line (operators report, 1,910-pt thread).
- **Content:** open with the moment of use in the first 2 to 3 seconds, then ingredients and preparation (a commenter's advice to a tea founder getting a few hundred views). Keep the moment non-medical: "my 9 pm wind-down," not "can't sleep? this fixes it."
- **Search:** long-tail descriptive terms ("decaf rosehip herbal tea") over "herbal tea" for paid search tests (commenter suggestion).
- **Claims trap:** sleep, digestion, immunity and "detox" claims on a food tea are drug-like in the US and unauthorized in GB and the EU unless on the register. See [claims-labeling.md](claims-labeling.md).
- **Formats:** sampler tins, gift sets, cafe and restaurant wholesale.

## Skincare and body care

- **First channels:** DTC with creators and TikTok Shop, Amazon, then specialty and clean-beauty retail; markets for soaps, scrubs and body care.
- **Content that sells:** visible results in one image. A half-face photo (treated vs untreated) for a color-correcting treatment drew 6,368 upvotes and a 1,274-upvote "want to try" comment (r/SkincareAddiction). Problem-visible creative over pack shots (agency practitioner). Split-screen "real customer results vs competitor review screenshots" lifted one skincare advertiser's ROAS from 2.1x to 2.9x at $150k+ a month (operators report).
- **Trial formats:** a paid mini ($15 against a $52 full size) and discovery sets with a full-size hero plus samples lower the risk of trying (consumer reports, r/SkincareAddiction). Check shade and white-cast range: consumers on medium and darker skin flagged white cast as the purchase barrier.
- **Expert founders:** a dermatologist-led brand turned keyword topics ("niacinamide benefits for skin," "bakuchiol vs retinol") into founder reels plus articles, targeted phrases like "fungal acne safe moisturizer," and reported growth from $2,000 to $48,000 a month in eight months (operators report, 323-pt thread).
- **Email:** a four-month education sequence, twice a week, 90% teaching and 10% selling, converted some leads five months after opt-in (operators report).
- **Giveaways:** give away your own line. One skincare giveaway drew 2,100 entries with 85% of new followers still there after 30 days, versus 8,900 entries and 12% retention for an iPad giveaway (operators report).
- **Reviews:** never rejected, gated, seeded or staff-written; the three biggest threads in this dataset are review scandals.
- **Claims:** stay cosmetic ("visibly," "looks," "feels"). Acne, SPF, eczema and "repair" claims are drug claims in the US. MoCRA listing is not approval. EU and GB need a Responsible Person and notification.
- **Watch:** first-order contribution vs CAC (€47.16 median beauty CAC, adtribute 2026), 90-day repeat (15.02% median), first-renewal churn (26.3%).

## Makeup and color cosmetics

- Same mechanics as skincare, more shade and swatch content: swatches on several skin tones, wear tests, the product name searchable on TikTok.
- Discovery bundles and minis carry trial; limited editions carry collectors.
- Before-and-after content must be real and unretouched.

## Supplements

- **First channels:** DTC subscription, Amazon; then natural retail and pharmacies; quick-commerce and specialty chains in Europe.
- **Economics:** median health and nutrition CAC €58.22 and 90-day repeat 12.12% (adtribute, 2026); first-renewal churn 26.5% (Recharge). One EU brand reported 74% repurchase in 2019 to 2020 (operators report). Use your own cohorts.
- **Ad friction:** expect Google and YouTube disapprovals; some agencies decline supplement accounts under $10,000 a month (operators report). Consider a 14-day-plus evaluation window for supplement and skincare ads, longer than impulse products (operator guidance).
- **Creative:** Hook, Promise, Solution worked for one EU brand from 2019 (operators report). Keep the promise inside structure/function wording. "This is not Ozempic" comparisons reportedly sold well for one account, but implied drug or weight-loss outcomes are high-risk claims: compare on format, taste or routine instead.
- **Creators:** micro-niche creators on mostly performance pay, with a shared video framework, produced daily content for an ACV gummy brand that its founder said beat Meta (operators report, disputed in comments).
- **Retail:** ask chains to fund part of a relaunch for their stores (one founder asked for about 70% of costs upfront for 68 stores).
- **Regulatory:** structure/function only, FDA notice within 30 days, disclaimer, cGMP manufacturer, ingredient status tracking; NPN before sale in Canada; GB and EU register wording only; TGA permitted indications in Australia; hemp-derived THC limits from November 12, 2026.
- **Ops:** portable subscription payment tokens; correct marketplace category; shelf life for Amazon is consumption time plus 90 days at check-in.

## Cottage food

- **First channels:** farmers markets, preorders with pickup, farm stands, pop-ups, local events; wholesale and shipping only where the state allows.
- **The law is the plan:** caps, allowed products, venues, online and shipping rules and label wording vary by state (see [markets-events.md](markets-events.md#cottage-food-what-the-law-allows-decides-the-marketing)).
- **What works:** weekly preorders plus a pickup window; a premium tier (inclusion loaves at $15 vs $12 standard); occasional free giveaways; holiday presales from a fixed menu (operators report).
- **Menu decisions:** add the item that is easy to make at volume first. Commenters advised a sourdough baker to add English muffins before bagels, which take about twice the effort and that customers expect to be cheap (operators report).
- **Graduation:** plan the commissary or co-packer move before the cap; signed interest first (see [production.md](production.md)).
- **Bakery storefront and cafe questions** go to sibling `restaurant`.
