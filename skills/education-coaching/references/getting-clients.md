# Getting clients: channels by sub-model

Pick two channels at most for the first 30 days: one with existing buyer intent (marketplace, Google, search ads, referral partner) and one owned or relationship channel (warm network, community answers, workshops, list). Judge each channel on paid customers and 90-day contribution, never on followers or leads.

## YouTube

- YouTube's recommendations use appeal (will people click), engagement and satisfaction; search adds relevance and quality. Tags matter only for misspellings, and publish time is not known to affect long-term performance (YouTube help, 2026). Stop tag and posting-time work; spend it on title, thumbnail promise and the first seconds.
- YouTube added AI-assisted search and an expanded "Ask" feature in 2025 and 2026: videos must answer a specific question cleanly enough to be retrieved.
- Titles built on a buying or learning job: "How to raise SAT Math from 650 to 750", "IELTS Writing Task 2: band 7 mistakes", "Grade 5 fractions diagnostic", "How to choose an executive coach".
- Structure: 0 to 10 seconds state the exact problem or result, show the output, teach one mechanism, demonstrate, then call to action to the diagnostic, template or waitlist. "Subscribe" is not the commercial call to action.
- Language teacher: students arrived by about the 3rd or 4th video on learning topics (channel later 1.2M subscribers).
- Course creators: split a lighter version of the course into a series and point each video to the paid course.

## TikTok, Reels and short video

- TikTok Creator Search Insights shows what people search and "content gaps"; search value is part of Creator Rewards. Workflow: pick a gap query, answer it directly, attach the matching lead capture.
- Volume worked for one life coach (3 to 7 TikToks a day in year one, 400,000 followers after years, $500K over 3 years; channel attribution not given). That is an audience business; most coaches need clients sooner.
- Social media in two 20-minute blocks a day, creating not scrolling, keeps it from replacing selling (r/lifecoaching, 60).

## LinkedIn

- Fits career, executive, business coaching and professional cohorts. Organic posting plus replies to comments: fully booked in 3 to 4 months at $250 a session, and $15,000 more in the two months after stopping posting from the built presence (operators report, 13).
- Staged DM sequence from engagement to call: connect, thank them for engaging with a specific post, share a useful article or webinar, ask their biggest challenge, give one piece of advice, ask if they want objective feedback from a coach (3 calls, 2 clients in a week; script in [scripts.md](scripts.md#linkedin-dm-sequence)).
- One coach dropped every network except LinkedIn and YouTube and targeted first-time business owners over 50: less time online, more clients (6).

## Reddit, Facebook groups, WhatsApp and WeChat

- Answer questions in groups where your buyer asks for help: 30 minutes a day; groups that forbid teacher ads have fewer competing teachers.
- Monitor immigration, study-abroad and exam subreddits for early questions; alert tools help.
- Adapt your best-performing short-form posts into long Reddit posts: journey videos become confession posts, most-saved educational posts become practical posts, transformations become case studies.
- Local Facebook groups (spiritual, business, women's groups) for openings and events from a personal profile, where rules allow (operators report, 6).
- WeChat parent groups and XiaoHongShu for Chinese families (see [tutoring-and-lessons.md](tutoring-and-lessons.md#language-and-esl-teachers)).

## Workshops, talks and events

- Coaches and relationship educators report workshops as the main channel: "a few workshops a year" replaced social media entirely for one ex-therapist earning twice her old income in 30 hours a week (74); a relationship coach earning $250K+ markets mostly through workshops (7).
- Networking groups (BNI, Connect W) produce workshop invitations; 20 attendees and 2 sign-ups kept one coach busy (5).
- Run the same webinar monthly when the pipeline is low (6).
- Music studios: recitals and showcases are marketing to siblings, grandparents and friends.

## Partnerships

- Music and tutoring: flyer swaps with enrichment centers (Kumon, RSM), music shops, sports clubs.
- ESL: commission-only referral deals with English-teaching YouTubers who have no time to teach.
- Coaches: therapists, doctors, HR leads, agencies, investors, nonprofits that serve your buyer.
- Courses: partner newsletters and joint webinars with creators serving the same audience; public playbooks default to a 50/50 revenue split on joint webinars (ideas from a proprietary source; uncited).

## Directories

- Psychology Today (for eligible coaches), coaching platforms with SEO (one coach recommends Vibly's search visibility, 5), and marketplace profiles. Do not pay until the profile paragraph names one person and their situation.

## Paid ads

- Run ads only once you know lead-to-paid rate and contribution ([economics.md](economics.md#acquisition-math)).
- Google: high-intent problem and location terms ("SAT tutor near me", "GCSE maths tutor", "IELTS tutor online", "career coach redundancy"), never broad "math help"; one page per query cluster with location, exam, age, format and availability in the copy.
- Meta: sell the diagnostic, assessment or mini-event to cold traffic, never the full package. Creative names the buyer and the symptom: "Year 11 parents: if your child keeps losing marks on algebra even after revising, the problem may be one of four prerequisite gaps. We'll find which one in a 25-minute maths diagnostic and send you the gap map." (synthesized hook, not a benchmarked ad).
- Track `spend -> clicks -> leads -> qualified -> booked -> showed -> paid -> revenue -> 90-day contribution` per campaign.
- Budget, structure, tracking and creative testing: sibling skill `paid-ads`.

## The one-question attribution habit

Put "How did you hear about us?" on every intake and booking form. Workshops, podcasts, parent groups, DMs and word of mouth never show up in analytics.
