# By niche

Short notes per niche: the engine, the trap, the first thing to check. Details live in the linked references.

## K-12 tutor (1:1, local or online)

- **Engine:** parent trust: diagnostic, gap map, progress report, end-of-block decision ([tutoring-and-lessons.md](tutoring-and-lessons.md)). Google profile and reviews for local search (sibling skill `local-seo`). School counselor and enrichment-center partners.
- **Trap:** selling hours with no outcome, then losing the family after the report card or exam.
- **Check first:** response time to parent inquiries; share of students who started from a diagnostic; whether parents get written progress.

## Tutoring center (multiple tutors)

- **Engine:** best closer runs diagnostics and trials (one online school pays trial specialists $26/h vs about $9/h for regular teachers, operators report); text-first lead response under an hour (vendor cases: 24% to 33% inquiry-to-enrollment; Mathnasium franchises 28% lead-to-student after adding texting).
- **Trap:** comparing tutors only on utilization. Compare retention and outcomes per tutor.
- **Check first:** inquiry-to-diagnostic and diagnostic-to-enrollment rates by staff member; month-3 retention by tutor.

## Test prep (SAT, ACT, AP, GCSE, A-level, IB, IELTS, TOEFL)

- **Engine:** sell to a test date with an official-format diagnostic, mocks and an error log; sell the second test date block before the first test.
- **Trap:** score guarantees; leaving students to churn at the test.
- **Check first:** the next 3 test dates and how many weeks of runway the current leads have.
- **Season:** Tutor.com's peak runs October to mid-November with spikes late August to September and April to May.

## Music teacher or studio

- **Engine:** studio policies (monthly prepay, slot framing, fixed makeup blocks), songs-first retention, recitals, flyer swaps with enrichment centers, outcome-based Google profile.
- **Price:** at or just below the local music school's family rate; UK median GBP 40/h (ISM). In-home lessons at a premium ($50/30 min, $60/45, $80/60 in a US metro, operators report).
- **Trap:** granting exceptions, teaching unpaid lessons, practice lectures to kids who want songs.
- **Check first:** payment timing, makeup policy, share of students with a performance this term. Opus1: 47% of new students still enrolled at 12 months; only 6% of cancellations cite cost.

## Language and ESL teacher

- **Engine:** one marketplace to start (Preply, italki, Superprof, AmazingTalker), then a job-specific niche ("IELTS for nurses") sold as 4 or 8-week packages off a free 15-minute check; Facebook, WhatsApp, WeChat and Reddit groups; YouTube on learning topics; weekly group session; niche guides.
- **Price:** platform $7 to $20/h; private $25 to $45/h reported; $40 per 50 min to Chinese families.
- **Trap:** selling "conversation" that AI voice apps now do for about $30 a month; ignoring rating thresholds on Engoo-type platforms.
- **Check first:** what share of income is "chat" vs correction, exam or job outcomes; rating trend; which platform rules allow direct clients.
- **China-facing:** XiaoHongShu marketing (app in English, camera translation), WeChat Pay via Stripe, parent groups via introductions.

## Outschool and online group classes for kids

- **Engine:** test many class concepts, add slots to the ones that fill (trivia 18 of 18 while others got 0); fun classes for younger kids in summer; repeat prepared classes for near-zero prep.
- **Price:** $18 to $21 per learner-hour guide; teacher keeps 70%.
- **Trap:** one-learner classes at low prices; funneling families off-platform (prohibited).

## Online course creator (self-paced)

- **Engine:** pre-sale, owned list, YouTube or search content that ends in a diagnostic, then a second offer (cohort, workshop, office hours). Median one-time price $150 (Ruzuku).
- **Trap:** building the full library first; one-offer catalogs; forecasts on old marketplace payouts.
- **Check first:** second-purchase rate and completion rate.

## Udemy instructor

- **Engine:** broad beginner courses as main products, niche courses as supplements; tech and trending topics; one long masterclass plus shorter versions; YouTube series with coupons; your own coupon links (97% vs 37%); blog page with email capture to sell off-Udemy products.
- **Trap:** subscription income falling (pool 15% in 2026); fake list-price discounts; piracy competition on price.

## Cohort course (Maven or own platform)

- **Engine:** 6-week runway, 100 page visits a week, live workshop in weeks -4 to -3, 1:1 conversations with engagers, proof and syllabus in weeks -2 to -1, push hardest in the last week ([courses-and-launches.md](courses-and-launches.md)).
- **Price:** $800 to $2,450 by live hours and projects (Maven).
- **Trap:** tapering promotion in the final week; pricing on video hours.

## Paid community (Skool, Circle, Discord, Heartbeat)

- **Engine:** attached to a course, cohort or coaching tier; first live event booked at checkout; one weekly core action.
- **Numbers:** $35/month typical, 23 members median; month-one event RSVP 51% vs 27% active at six months (Heartbeat).
- **Trap:** content-only memberships; launching to an empty room (hand-recruit 20 to 50 founders first).

## Teachers Pay Teachers seller

- **Engine:** catalog depth (sales jumps around 50 and 100 resources reported), no-prep resources with answer keys, Search Analytics by keyword, Pinterest, Substack or own list, bundles and seasonal units.
- **Trap:** choosing Exclusive while selling elsewhere; AI covers and descriptions under TPT's 2026 stance; reading reviews as demand (reviews fell to 1% or less of sales).
- **Check first:** Basic vs Exclusive break-even; share of revenue on TPT vs other channels; keyword conversion in Search Analytics.

## Life coach

- **Engine:** specific outcome positioning (consider "performance coach"), warm outreach, workshops, podcast guesting, referral partners; Psychology Today where eligible.
- **Price:** typical sold package $500 (Paperbell); one-to-one programs reported at $2,000 to $8,000.
- **Trap:** "I help people live their best life"; buying a launch formula before clients; heavy content with no conversations.

## Career coach

- **Engine:** LinkedIn content plus staged DMs; mine job-search subreddits for verbatim problems; tactical help blended with coaching.
- **Price:** typical package $900 (Paperbell); $250 per session full practice reported.
- **Trap:** pure question-led coaching for clients who want direction; matching only on last job when the client wants a pivot.

## Executive and business coach

- **Engine:** referrals from agencies, investors and HR; nonprofit boards; search ads on named services ("executive coaching") to a single-service page; quarterly contracts; assessments (DISC) as an entry product; employer-funded event coaching (board meeting prep reported at up to $2,000/h or $25,000 for six months).
- **Price:** typical package executive $1,250, business $1,000 (Paperbell); $850+/h reported.
- **Trap:** expecting fast cycles: one high-net-worth coach spends 4 to 6 months per client.

## Fitness and nutrition coach

- **Engine:** niche communities by life stage (men over 40), case-study posts with real timelines, comparison posts from client data, true failed-attempts stories; group programs.
- **Price:** typical health and wellness package $450 (Paperbell); offers over 2K reported for an established coach.
- **Trap:** before/after claims without permission or typical-result context; weight-loss guarantees.
- **Mindset coaches** working with fitness clients run a 90-day mindset phase before or alongside a trainer, and treat unwillingness to pay for specialist help as a fit signal.

## Relationship, parenting, grief and children's coaches

- **Engine:** workshops, local referral sources (pediatric offices, funeral homes, nursing homes), separate offers by audience.
- **Price anchor:** cost of the alternative (coaching vs divorce court).
- **Trap:** narrowing in a way that tells part of the market they are excluded.
