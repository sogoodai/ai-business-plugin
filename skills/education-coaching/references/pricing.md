# Pricing

Benchmarks with sources: [benchmarks.md](benchmarks.md). Break-even math: [economics.md](economics.md).

## Setting the first price

1. **Find the local or platform band.** List 15 to 30 competitors on the platform or in the town you sell in, for the same subject and level: their price, rating count and years. Wyzant's average range is $35 to $60/h; UK private music lessons GBP 40/h median and GBP 46/h top 20% (ISM 2024/25); Albanian teachers on italki $15 to $30 (operators report).
2. **Enter in the lower third, never at the bottom.** Practitioners agree both ways: a $5 lesson attracted students who did not respect the teacher (r/OnlineESLTeaching, 19), and a teacher who started very low got many matches that stopped when the price rose to a sustainable level (66). Preply says search does not simply favor cheap tutors.
3. **Hold firm on the stated price.** "Stay firm with your pricing. Don't negotiate because it will backfire" from a teacher who doubled to $45/h after leaving VIPKid and filled every slot (116-upvote thread).
4. **Benchmark against the employer you left:** music teachers can charge at or just below the music school's family rate and roughly double their pay (r/MusicTeachers, 20).
5. **Raise for new students when full,** keep current students for one cycle, then move them with notice. One coach priced high before having results, got few clients, lowered the barrier, filled the practice, then raised (r/lifecoaching, 14).

## Structure beats price

- **Blocks and monthly tuition over per-lesson.** Prepay at least 4 lessons; single lessons priced higher (r/MusicTeachers). Semester tuition (about 16 lessons over 18 weeks) in equal monthly payments for regulars; invoices every 4 lessons for occasional students.
- **No bulk discount by default.** Larger coaching packages kept roughly the same hourly price (Paperbell 2026); a blanket 15% discount on a $1,000 package gives away $150 with no evidence it was needed. Use the package to sell an outcome and a timeline, not a lower rate.
- **Exceptions that make sense:** italki's fee falls 6 points on 15 to 20-packs, so a small pack incentive is paid by the fee saving; referral partners who pre-vet clients can get a lower package rate (coach at $115 vs $150).
- **Session length does not halve the price.** Admin and support are the same (r/lifecoaching, 10).
- **Groups for capacity:** ESL speaking group at $15 a head; Outschool at $18 to $21 per learner-hour; coaching group at $2,000 next to $8,000 1:1 (r/lifecoaching AMA).

## Building a ladder

Each step solves the next problem the last one revealed:

- **Tutor:** paid diagnostic -> 6 to 8-session block -> exam sprint or next subject -> sibling diagnostic -> summer program.
- **Language teacher:** trial or evaluation -> 1:1 pack -> weekly group speaking -> niche guide or mini course.
- **Music teacher:** monthly tuition -> recital or exam prep -> ensemble or group class -> summer camp.
- **Course creator:** lead magnet -> self-paced course -> cohort or workshop -> office-hours membership or 1:1 review.
- **Coach:** assessment or workshop ($97 to $400) -> 3-month package -> renewal or maintenance plan -> group alumni community.
- **TPT seller:** single resource -> bundle -> related grade level -> seasonal unit.

## Payment plans

- 31% of coaching packages were bought on plans (Paperbell 2026). Offer one on anything above about a month's discretionary spend for the buyer.
- Price the plan above paid-in-full (public examples 12.5 to 20% more).
- No public data shows plans change refunds or completion. Track refund rate, completion and renewal by payment type in your own data.
- Collect by card on file with automatic retries; disable open-ended partial payments.

## Raising prices

- Break-even: you can lose p / (m + p) of students before contribution falls (worked in [economics.md](economics.md#price-change-break-even)).
- New students first, then current students at a term boundary with about two months' notice.
- Tie the increase to something visible: progress reports, recital, new materials, smaller groups, more feedback.
- Do not raise a provider or teacher whose calendar is not full; fix demand first.

## Guarantees and refunds

- Guarantee the controllable: sessions delivered, written plan, replaced session, completion support.
- Courses that need effort: conditional guarantee ("complete X by date, submit Y; if no result, refund").
- Coaching: "after the first two sessions, if you don't think this will deliver, we stop and refund the unused balance" (coreyhaines31/marketingskills, MIT).
- Never guarantee scores, income, weight loss or promotions.
- EU and UK online buyers often have a 14-day withdrawal right; Udemy-style "30-day guarantees" paid as platform credit generate public anger (r/Udemy, 27). Say exactly how refunds are paid.
