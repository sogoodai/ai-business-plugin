# Scripts

Adapt names, dates and prices. Short, plain, in the owner's voice. Messages never claim a person wrote them if they did not, never invent an offer, result or testimonial, and never promise a score or income. "Verbatim" marks wording quoted from a practitioner; the rest is synthesized from the sources and labeled as a template.

## Marketplace first reply

Template (research synthesis, not platform-endorsed):

> "I can help with [specific problem or exam]. Before we book, what is the deadline, your current score or level, and the one area that feels hardest? I'd use the first session to find the biggest gap, then send you a three-step plan for the next [time period]."

## Diagnostic offer to a parent

> "Thanks for reaching out about [child]. The fastest way to know what's going on is a [25]-minute diagnostic. I'll check the [3 to 5] skills [subject] depends on and send you a written gap map within 24 hours: what's missing, what fixes it, and how long that usually takes. I have [day time] or [day time]. Here's the short intake form so I can prepare: [link]."

## Free evaluation instead of a free lesson

Verbatim approach (r/OnlineESLTeaching, 15): a free 15-minute intro "to see whether or not the student and I do well together and to assess their level of speaking, reading, and reading comprehension. It's not a free class but more of an evaluation."

## Parent progress report

One screen, every 3 to 5 sessions:

> **Starting point:** [score or observed skill on date]
> **This period:** [what we worked on]
> **Evidence:** [quiz score, timed result, error types that disappeared]
> **Next target by [date]:** [specific threshold]
> **At home:** [one action, 15 minutes or less]
> **Recommendation:** [continue, change focus, intensify, or finish]

## End-of-block decision

> "We've finished the [8-session] block. [Child] went from [baseline] to [now]; [gap A] and [gap B] are fixed, [gap C] is still costing [marks]. My recommendation is [continue with a 6-session block on C before the (exam date)] / [stop here; they're ready]. If you'd like to continue, the same [day time] slot is held for you until [date]."

## Studio policy rollout

Verbatim (r/MusicTeachers, contract teacher, 3 upvotes), sent about two months before the term, with a couple of reminders and a policy to sign:

> "Hello music families! I wanted to let you know my studio has grown and my time will be tighter going forward. This is requiring me to be more structured with my teaching time. I need to institute new policies for fall that I will attach here. I have enjoyed working with all of you and look forward to our further musical adventures."

Policy frame, verbatim: "Students are not paying for lessons. They are paying to hold a regular time slot in your schedule. Your policy should make that explicit."

## Payment overdue

Template (one message, then pause):

> "Hi [name], [month]'s tuition hasn't come through yet. Lessons are paused until it's settled; once [amount] for [month] and the next lesson is paid here [link], we'll pick up at the usual time on [day]."

## Cancellation inside the window

> "No problem, I hope everything's OK. As in our policy, cancellations inside [24] hours are charged, so this one is billed. Your next lesson is [day time] as usual."

## Schedule change for a minor (to the parent, in writing)

> "Hi [parent], I'd like to move [child]'s lesson from [old] to [new] starting [date]. Please confirm by replying here; until you confirm, we stay at [old]."

## Marketplace or platform exit (only where allowed)

> "As [platform] is [closing / as we agreed], I'll keep teaching you directly from [date]. Same [day time], [price] per lesson, paid [monthly in advance] here: [link]. Your plan and notes carry over."

## Warm outreach (coach)

Reactivation, no pitch (r/lifecoaching, 21-upvote advice):

> "Hey [name], I was thinking about our chat about [thing they were working on]. How did that turn out?"

Openings announcement to a former-career network (LinkedIn post, template):

> "After [N] years in [field], I'm now coaching [specific people] on [outcome]. I have [3] openings starting [month]. If that's you or someone you know, message me."

## Community DM opener

Verbatim suggestion (r/lifecoaching, 3 upvotes; use only where DMs are welcome and with the person's own words):

> "Hey man, saw your comment about [specific struggle]. I actually help guys with exactly that. Mind if I ask you a few questions? No pitch, just curious."

## LinkedIn DM sequence

From a career coach reporting 3 consult calls and 2 clients in one week (46-upvote post):

1. Connect, verbatim: "Hi NAME. Thanks for engaging with my job search tips post, would be great to be connected!"
2. After acceptance: start a real conversation; share a relevant article or webinar invite.
3. When warm: ask their biggest challenge right now.
4. Give one small piece of useful advice.
5. Ask whether they would be open to objective feedback from a coach, or a call to learn about your services.

## Discovery call

Template questions (research synthesis):

> "What made you book today?"
> "What would need to be measurably different 90 days from now for this to have been worth the time and money?"
> "What have you tried already?"
> "Where does this cost you time, money, opportunity or confidence?"
> "What happens if nothing changes?"
> "Let me play that back: [their words]. Did I get that right?"
> "Based on that, I'd recommend [package]. It's [price] paid in full, or [plan]. We'd [delivery]. The result we'll measure is [metric]. Shall we decide whether that fits, or is there a specific risk you need resolved first?"

Fit statement, verbatim (r/lifecoaching, 58): "I only take on new clients when I'm confident it will be a win-win."

Outcome framing, verbatim example (r/lifecoaching, 3): "in eight to twelve sessions most clients can ask for the raise, or hand in the notice, without three weeks of working up to it." Only with a range your past clients support.

Voice-only call for a nervous prospect, verbatim (6): "the coachee's job (yours) really isn't to be eloquent. It's to show up and be you. So Google meet voice-only would be just fine."

## Post-call follow-up

> "Thanks for today, [name]. Here's what I heard: [goal in their words], [obstacle], [what it's costing]. The worksheet attached is the [first exercise] we talked about; it's useful whether or not we work together. If you'd like to go ahead with [package], here's the link [link]. I'm holding [day time] for our first session until [date]."

## Budget question from a buyer (help them ask, or answer it)

Verbatim buyer-side line (r/Coaching, 4): "my budget is $X for getting help with this and I understand there can be a big range - is this a budget you are able to work within?" Answer with your range in one line, then offer the call or a referral.

## Referral ask

Template (research synthesis):

> "You mentioned [specific result] has improved since we started. If you know one [parent / professional] dealing with [same problem], I have two assessment slots before [date]. I'm happy to run the same baseline assessment for them. If they join, I'll add [one-off benefit, e.g. a free session] to your next block."

## Review or testimonial request

Template:

> "Would you be comfortable writing 2 to 3 sentences on what was difficult before we worked together, what changed, and who you'd recommend this to? Numbers or a concrete before and after are especially helpful. Here's the link: [link]."

Monthly parent email line (quiet, every month): "If lessons have helped, a short Google review helps other families find us: [link]." Same request to everyone; nothing offered in return.

## Price increase

> "From [date, about two months out], tuition for new and continuing students will be [new price]. It covers [what is new: progress reports, recital, materials]. Your current rate stays until [date]. Thank you for being part of the studio."

## Positioning lines

- "I help corporate professional women in their 40s identify and secure a high-paying leadership role in 90 days or less." (verbatim example, r/lifecoaching, 38)
- "I offer healing from abusive relationships." instead of "I offer healing." (verbatim, 36)
- Google profile: "Our students have performed at [X], competed in [Y], gone on to study music at [Z]." (structure verbatim, r/MusicTeachers, 20)

## Content hooks that sell judgment

- "I tested 5 recovery methods on 30+ clients over 40. Here's what actually reduced soreness and improved performance." Closing: "The real magic is combining these, not doing one perfectly." (r/Coaching, 37: 8 DMs, 2 clients)
- Story closing: "Anyone else been down this road?" (same thread: 2 DMs)
- "The three signals I use to tell which of four problems you have."
- "Year 11 parents: if your child keeps losing marks on algebra even after revising, the problem may be one of four prerequisite gaps." (synthesized ad hook)

## Win-back

> "Hi [name], it's been a while since [child]'s last lesson. How are things going with [subject or goal]? No agenda, just checking in." If they reply with a complaint: stop the sequence and call.

Never: "You made Duolingo sad." (guilt copy that publicly cost a user, r/languagelearning, 32)

## Course launch: last 48 hours

> "Enrollment for [cohort] closes [day, time]. Here's exactly what happens in week one: [session 1], [first assignment], [first feedback]. This is for you if [fit]. It's not for you if [non-fit]. [link]"
