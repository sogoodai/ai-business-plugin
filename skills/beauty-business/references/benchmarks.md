# Benchmarks

Compare a business only with its closest category. A lash studio is a "specialty salon", not a med spa. Three kinds of numbers live here, keep them apart when you report:

- **Measured:** aggregated transaction data, platform terms, controlled trials. Zenoti is the main source: anonymized calendar-2025 North American data, published 2026. It is a vendor cohort skewed to multi-location, professionally run businesses, so its revenue figures overstate a solo operator. Its ratios (utilization, online booking share, ticket) transfer better than its revenue.
- **Vendor targets:** goals a software company publishes. Useful as aspirations, never as "the industry average".
- **Operators report:** Reddit practitioner numbers. One person's book, real but anecdotal.

"Year" is the year the data describes; the publication year follows when different.

## Cancellations and no-shows

| Category | Cancellation | No-show | Source | Year |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Salon | 8% | 3% | [Zenoti 2025 report](https://www.zenoti.com/reports/beauty-and-wellness-benchmark-report-2025_v1) | 2024 data, possibly outdated |
| Nails | 16% | 1% | Zenoti 2025 report | 2024, possibly outdated |
| Barber | 2% then about 4% | 4% | Zenoti 2025 report, then barber report | 2024, then 2025 |
| Waxing | 14% then 18% | 3% then 1% | Zenoti 2025 report, then waxing report | 2024, then 2025 |
| Med spa | 16% then 14% | 5% | Zenoti 2025 report, then med spa report | 2024, then 2025 |

Reading: cancellations, not no-shows, are the bigger leak almost everywhere. Optimize rescheduling and backfill before punishment.

Reminder evidence is from healthcare, not beauty: a 2026 review of 10 randomized trials (8,236 people) found reminders raise attendance, pooled RR 1.11 ([JHMH Policy](https://jhmhp.amegroups.org/article/view/10215/html)). A 2026 trial on 59,994 high-risk patients found adding an automated call to SMS cut no-shows from 11.3% to 9.6% ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42418615/)). Use as mechanism, not as a salon forecast. Beauty vendor case: a five-salon UK group reported no-shows under about 2% with day-before reminders ([Phorest case](https://www.phorest.com/us/case-study/beauty-secrets-rebooking-reminders/), selected case).

## Vendor targets (aspirations, not averages)

- Vagaro: utilization 70 to 85%, client retention 60 to 80%, retail 12 to 15% of service, rebooking 55 to 75% ([Vagaro](https://www.vagaro.com/learn/average-hair-salon-profit-margins)); 60%+ rebooking healthy, under 40% a warning ([Vagaro](https://www.vagaro.com/learn/salon-multi-location-management-guide)), 2026.
- Salon Today operators use 13% retail-to-service as a coaching trigger and 17% as a higher-commission threshold ([Salon Today](https://www.salontoday.com/articles/building-careers-not-chairs-the-systems-behind-sustainable-growth), 2026). Internal rules from selected salons.
- Brazil salon pack (MIT, uncited): chair occupancy 65 to 75%, monthly repurchase 30 to 40%. Do not load as facts.

Observed median utilization is under 50% for most categories. A 70 to 85% "target" is a top-decile outcome, not normal.
