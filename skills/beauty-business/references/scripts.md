# Scripts

Adapt names, dates and prices. Keep the tone warm and short. Messages from the business never claim a person wrote them if they didn't, and never invent an offer the owner has not approved.

## Booking and policies

- **Voicemail:** "For faster service, please book online at [link], 24 hours a day."
- **Deposit policy (testing default):** "We reserve your appointment specifically for you. A [30%] deposit is applied to your final bill. You can cancel or move the appointment free until [48 hours] before the start time. Cancellations after that forfeit the deposit; no-shows may be charged up to [amount]. If an emergency comes up, contact us as soon as you can."
- **Confirmation with prep:** "Reminder for your appointment on [date] at [time] for a color and cut. Please arrive with prewashed hair, or we can add a clarifying service ([price], [minutes] extra), whichever is easier for you."
- **48-hour reminder:** "See you [day] at [time] with [provider]. Need to move it? Change it here free until [time]: [link]."
- **First cancellation, one freebie:** "No problem, you can reschedule whenever it suits you. Just so you know, everyone gets one freebie, and future appointments follow the cancellation and late policy." Then reply only about scheduling.
- **Recurring no-show slot:** "It doesn't seem like Fridays work out for you. Would you like to cancel all Friday appointments going forward?"
- **Same-day only for unreliable clients:** "You can call Friday morning and we'll see if we have room the same day."
- **Late arrival:** "Due to my schedule, we'll be ending at the regular time." If they want to leave: "That's your choice, the no-show fee will apply."
- **Rescheduled transformation:** "Looks like we'll need to make this a 2 to 3 step process, or reschedule you for a day when you have more time. I'll need a 50% deposit on the next booking."
- **Unexpected arrival (booking mix-up):** "Welcome, I'm just finishing setting up, I'll be with you shortly." Sort the system out after; don't open with the error.
- **Second service after the first is done:** "That sounds great, when would you like to book another appointment? I'd be happy to do it then."
- **Male therapist booking:** "We can get you in with John, he has availability on [day]." Never "Are you OK with a male therapist?" Neutral version: "Are you comfortable with either gender of therapist, or does anyone have a strict preference?"
- **Mobile massage screening:** "Do you have any preferences when it comes to shoes on in your home?"
- **Massage boundary on the booking page or call:** "I'm a licensed massage therapist providing therapeutic massage only. Any sexual comment or behavior ends the session immediately, full payment is kept, and it is reported to the police." Then ask whether they still want to book.

## Consultation

- **Unrealistic inspiration:** "The picture you showed me is a different hair type than yours. Let's grab the iPad and find a look we can achieve today."
- **Filtered influencer photos:** "Show me a picture of a time you loved YOUR hair, not someone else's. Let's do what's possible for you."
- **Massage goals:** "What are our goals today? Are we here to relax or to work on an issue?" Then "Any areas you want me to avoid? Scalp, hands, feet, pecs, glutes?"
- **50-minute choice:** "Do you want full body, or should I focus on waist up or waist down?"
- **Quiet style:** "I'm not super chatty, so apart from checking in on pressure, I won't interrupt your zen."
- **Deep pressure warning (call before):** "I see you asked for very deep pressure. Fair warning, I go firm and deep, but not super deep. If you want more, I can recommend someone."
- **Pressure reset with a regular:** "Last session I gave you the pressure you like, but I was extremely sore afterward and can't keep doing that. I can still work with you at a pressure I can sustain, or I can recommend [name] for deeper work."
- **Kids:** coach parents to say "We're going to get your hair trimmed", not "cut". Cut the area the parent cares about most first.
- **Politics:** "Don't politics feel stressful? Do you really want your heart rate up during your treatment?" (a 20-year spa pro says it works 90% of the time)
- **Religion:** "Spirituality is deeply personal to me."
- **Between-appointment boundary:** "I appreciate our conversation, but I can't talk for 30 minutes after your session like last time. I need that time to prepare for my next client."
- **Workload question:** "As many as I can get, some weeks are busier than others."

## Rebooking and retention

- **At checkout:** "Your [color/fill] will start shifting around [6 to 8] weeks, that's the best time to come back so it still looks fresh. I can hold [option A] or [option B] now, and you can move it later."
- **Due soon (not prebooked):** "Hi [first name], you're coming into your usual [service] window. [Provider] has [Tuesday 3:30] or [Thursday 11:00]. Want either held for you? [link]"
- **Overdue, no offer:** "Hi [name], it's been a while since your [service] with [provider]. Everything OK? [Provider] has [two options] if you'd like to come in: [link]"
- **Value add (second touch only):** "We haven't seen you for [service] in a while. If you come back by [date], we'll include [low-cost add-on] with your appointment."
- **Gap fill:** "Two openings with [provider] tomorrow: [time] for [service] and [time] for [service]. Book here: [link]. First booked, first held."
- **Short-notice list invite:** "Want first dibs on last-minute openings? Reply YES and I'll text you when a slot frees up. Reply STOP anytime."
- **Moving salons:** "I'm moving to [new salon], [address], from [date]. Your formula and notes come with me. Book your next visit here: [link]."

## Reviews and referrals

- **Review request (same for every client, no gating):** "Thanks for visiting [Business]. Would you share an honest review of your experience? Mentioning which service you had helps others know what to expect: [Google review link]."
- **Never:** "If you loved it, leave us five stars", or a discount or entry for a review.
- **Tip redirect (tip-included pricing):** "Only hugs, referrals or reviews accepted."
- **Referral ask after a great visit:** "I have a couple of openings next week if you have friends who need [service]. Here are a few cards, most of my best clients come from referrals."
- **Public reply to a bad review:** "I'm sorry for any miscommunication, as I felt we nailed it at your appointment. I'd love a chance to improve the areas that aren't working for you. Please call me to schedule."
- **Reply to a review about a problem:** "Thanks for telling us about [specific issue]. We'd like to look at what happened and make it right. Please contact [channel] so we can sort it out privately."

## Money

- **Price change:** "Beginning [date], your [service] with [provider] will be [new price]. This lets us keep [specific product, time or training standard]. Appointments before [date] stay at the current price. If you'd like a different price point, we can match you with [provider tier]."
- **Price increase reason:** "Would you want to work at a job for years where the only way to make more was to work more hours?"
- **Inflation pushback (only with rapport):** "You know how your groceries cost more now? Mine do too."
- **Cheaper elsewhere:** "You should go there." No discount.
- **Ending a friend discount:** "I'm updating my pricing and can't keep the long-term 50% friend rate. Starting after your next appointment, I'll be charging my regular rates."
- **Friends and family price change:** "Hi! I'm restructuring my prices for friends and family. The new prices begin [date], here's the list for your next booking. Thank you for your support!"
- **Upgrade instead of extra pressure:** "Are we upgrading to deep tissue? No? Then we'll keep the pressure at a nice 6, and you can upgrade anytime."
- **Prep charge going forward:** "Clarifying and detangling used to be complimentary. From [date] it's [price] and [minutes] extra, or free if you arrive with clean, detangled hair."
- **Included retail:** "This service today is [price] and includes a shampoo and conditioner as a bonus, so you can maintain your hair and I can keep giving you the best results."

## Fixes and complaints

- **Post-appointment change request:** "We agreed on this look and you liked it yesterday. Which area would you like to fix?" If it is a change of mind: "Fixing is complimentary; changing the whole result is another service at an additional price."
- **Ending a session that isn't working:** "I feel this isn't a good fit. I'm going to end the session now so we won't have to charge you."
- **Dissatisfied client discovery:** "When did you start getting worried?" and "How does this differ from what you expected?" instead of "What's wrong?"

## Ending relationships

- **Chronic canceller:** "I think you need someone with more flexibility than me. I understand life comes up, but I've lost [amount] in income from these cancellations and I can't take that hit anymore." (Some prefer to drop the amount.)
- **Schedule mismatch:** "Unfortunately it looks like I won't be able to meet your scheduling needs. I wish you luck on your hair journey."
- **Unresolved retention issue (lash):** "I'm sorry you're experiencing that. You might have more luck with another lash tech. Thanks, and good luck!" Only after troubleshooting and aftercare education.
- **Service-related reason, never vague:** "Due to the nature of our services, which involve personal care for both of us, I can't continue to [service]."

## Growth and ads

- **Model call agreement:** "This is a model appointment at product cost, and I choose the style for my portfolio. Still interested?"
- **Opening post:** "Two spots open this week: Wed 2pm (cut + gloss), Fri 10am (full set). Book: [link]"
- **Ad hook, transformation services:** "Thinking about [balayage / lash extensions / brow correction] in [city]? See three results on clients who started with [specific problem]. New-client consultation and a personal plan. Check real availability here."
- **Form lead auto-text:** "Got your request, thanks! Sending prices and next openings now. Or book directly here: [link]"
