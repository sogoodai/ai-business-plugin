# Grand opening and the first 90 days

Two tracks: a new opening (start 4 to 6 weeks before the doors open) and a stalled existing business (start now). Each phase ends with a short owner report: what changed, the scorecard, what they need to decide.

## A. Opening countdown

The 2025 to 2026 launch cases all started before opening: 381 reservations, 1,155 covers and $96,303 tracked before day one (Breadth Marketing 2026); 1,200 signups at about $0.50 over a 45-day prelaunch, then $40,000 of web takeaway in 51 days (Debox 2025); 1,109 RSVPs for a cafe launch party (BILLIONMONSTERS 2026). They are selected agency cases: proof the queue can be built, not forecasts.

**Weeks -6 to -4: entities and list**

- Claim the Google Business Profile as early as Google allows (verification can take weeks); exact category, address, planned hours marked as opening date, a few real photos as soon as there is something real to show. Apple Maps, Yelp, Tripadvisor, OpenTable or the chosen reservation tool, Facebook. Depth: `local-seo`.
- Mobile landing page: opening date, neighborhood, hero items, one signup form with postcode and intent (dine-in, coffee, catering, private events).
- Signup wording: [scripts.md](scripts.md). No review promises and no "first 100 get free food for a review".
- Decide the direct ordering and reservation stack before launch, so Google's preferred links point to it from day one ([delivery-and-direct.md](delivery-and-direct.md)).

**Weeks -4 to -2: content and neighbors**

- Reveal hero dishes one at a time (a dish, a technique, a person), not a generic brand campaign.
- Recruit 5 to 15 hyperlocal micro creators for preview services with written usage rights and their own codes ([demand-and-events.md](demand-and-events.md)).
- Walk the neighborhood: offices (catering), boutiques (receipt deals), real estate agents (welcome packs), hotels and concierges, schools and teams, the business association. Bring a card with a code: "Here, this one's on me whenever you get a chance."
- Plan the selfie spot or mural before the fit-out ends: logo visible, no tables in front.
- Small paid push to the trade area only: Meta or TikTok to the signup page, geo-limited. Track cost per signup by postcode.

**Weeks -2 to 0: preview and first access**

- Friends-and-family and neighbor previews to train staff (no review asks from friends; Google and the FTC ban insider reviews without disclosure, and Google bans asks tied to perks).
- Open reservations or preorders to the list before the public. Bakeries: a preorder opening box. Trucks: publish the first two weeks of locations as text on the website.
- Opening event with a cap and an RSVP list; tag attendees as "launch" so they don't pollute the trade-area list.

**Weeks 1 to 4 after opening**

- Neutral review ask to every real guest from day one, and reply to all of them. Ratings in the first months set the tone: one owner pair hit 4.9 by month 4 by talking with every table and using tap stands.
- First-visit welcome and second-visit nudge live from the first order.
- Shift opening spend from awareness to the creatives that produced matched bookings or orders.
- Daily notes on what guests ask for; adjust the menu with specials, not reprints.
- Keep posted hours exactly. A closed door in the first month ends a guest's second try.

## B. Days 1 to 90 for a stalled or existing business

The order matters: economics and data, then conversion surfaces, then retention, then acquisition (research report 2026).

**Days 1 to 7: economics and instrumentation**

- Pull 90 days of item sales by daypart and channel, the last 3 P&Ls, marketplace statements and reservation invoices.
- Compute prime cost, contribution per order by channel, direct vs marketplace share, RevPASH by daypart, first-to-second visit rate, list size and consent, loyalty 90-day active rate ([economics.md](economics.md)).
- Record Google rating, review count, last-90-day reviews, reply rate, and the same for 3 nearest competitors.
- Set UTM conventions and map every book, order and call path.
- Exit check: you can state contribution per order by channel and the actual conversion endpoint for each channel. No acquisition spend before this.

**Days 1 to 14: Google entity and reviews**

- Categories, hours and special hours, attributes, text menu with prices, preferred menu source, preferred order and reservation links, current photos including the entrance. Same facts in website HTML.
- Official review link and QR on receipts, thank-you messages and tables; ask every guest; reply queue for 1 and 2 stars within 48 hours.
- Platform audit: unauthorized listings, OpenTable referral fees, DoorDash campaigns you didn't approve.
- Exit check: every money query leads to accurate hours, menu and a working direct path.

**Days 5 to 21: triggers and direct ordering**

- Welcome, second-visit, lapse and visit-4 milestone flows; holdout where the list allows ([retention-and-loyalty.md](retention-and-loyalty.md)).
- Direct-ordering break-even and checkout-total comparison; a tested $10 or similar direct-only first-order code in every marketplace bag; preferred order link on Google.
- Exit check: one flow live with an exit condition, one coupon tested end to end.

**Days 10 to 30: menu**

- Matrix per category, plate costs with yield, one Plowhorse rewrite, one Puzzle repositioned, Dogs cut, add-ons priced ([menu-and-pricing.md](menu-and-pricing.md)).
- Pick the first price moves item by item; monitor 4 to 6 weeks.
- Exit check: the owner approved a dated list of changes with expected contribution.

**Days 15 to 35: two demand products**

- One slow-night product (named, dated, capped, own link) and one high-value funnel (catering page, private events, preorder drop or group bookings).
- Shoot 8 to 12 short assets: hero dish, preparation, owner or staff, an occasion, the neighborhood, catering or private offer, the event.
- Exit check: both products have a price, capacity, booking path and contribution target.

**Days 25 to 45: paid intent and local B2B**

- Google on brand, cuisine + area, order and reservation, catering and private dining; dining Local Services Ads where available. Meta and TikTok only on concrete offers in the true trade area. Depth: `paid-ads`.
- B2B list: offices, venues, schools, hotels, coworking, planners; short pitch and sample order where the economics allow. Track lead, quote, booked revenue, repeat buyer.

**Days 35 to 60: cohort review**

- New guests from month one by source: first-order contribution and second visit within the format's window. Pause sources that cannot pay back after repeat behavior.
- Creators judged on matched bookings and codes.

**Days 45 to 75: regulars and marketplace test**

- Visit-2 and visit-4 objectives in loyalty or CRM; staff recognition of known regulars; early access for regulars instead of discounts.
- If volume allows, test a lower marketplace tier or pause in-app promotion for comparable weeks; compare incremental orders and contribution.

**Days 60 to 90: repeat what worked, then allocate**

- Repeat the winning event or drop on a predictable cadence; retarget past attendees.
- Review price and menu changes after 4 to 6 weeks: keep what raised total contribution, reverse what lost units without paying for itself.
- Rank channels by incremental contribution per dollar, then payback time and repeat quality. Separate acquisition, retention and capacity-filling budgets for the next quarter.
- Exit report: channel P&L, cohort retention table, Google and review health, direct-order share, CRM lift test result, next quarter's experiment list.

## Questions to put to the owner at each phase

- Which items and prices are untouchable, and why?
- How much can we spend before seeing matched results, and on what?
- Who answers catering and event inquiries, and how fast?
- Which offers are acceptable (comped items, threshold offers, bundles) and which are not (percent off everything)?
- Who may reply to reviews in the business's name?
