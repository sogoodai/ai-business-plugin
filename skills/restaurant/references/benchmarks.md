# Benchmarks

Compare a business only with its closest format. A coffee shop is not a full-service restaurant, a truck is not a caterer. Keep three kinds of numbers apart when you report:

- **Measured:** association surveys (NRA), transaction data (Toast about 189,000 US locations, Square), platform fee schedules, controlled studies. Vendor transaction data is a customer cohort, not a census.
- **Vendor and agency figures:** loyalty, CRM and ad results attributed by the company that sells the tool. Attributed is not incremental.
- **Operators report:** Reddit practitioner numbers. One person's business, real but anecdotal.

"Year" is the year the data describes; the publication year follows when different. A blank "strong" cell means no credible 2025 to 2026 source publishes one. Do not fill it in.
