# Scripts

Adapt names, dates and prices. Keep it short and human. Messages never claim a person wrote them if they didn't, never invent an offer the owner has not approved, and never make a dietary or allergen promise the kitchen has not confirmed. "Verbatim" marks wording a practitioner reported using; the rest is drafted from the sources and should be tested.

## Review requests (Google and Tripadvisor only; never ask for Yelp reviews)

- **Receipt or next-morning message:** "Thanks for visiting [Restaurant]. If you'd like to share your honest experience, you can leave a Google review here: [link]. Positive or critical, we read every one." (research report 2026)
- **Table QR or receipt line:** "How was your visit? Scan to leave an honest Google review. No purchase or reward is tied to your rating." (research 2026)
- **In person, every table, same words:** "If you have a minute later, we'd love an honest Google review. The QR on the stand takes you straight there."
- Never: "If you loved us...", "mention [server]'s name", "show us your review for a free dessert", a happy-or-unhappy survey that only sends happy guests to Google.

## Review replies

Under 60 words for most. One specific detail from the review, a person's name, a real offline route. No arguing, no private details, no public offers of free food (anthropics small-business, Apache-2.0; ai-restaurant-claude, MIT).

- **Positive, staff named:** "Thanks so much, [first name]. [Staff] will be over the moon, we'll share this at pre-shift. Next time save room for the [dessert]. [Owner name]"
- **Positive, dish named:** "Thank you, [first name]. The [dish] is [chef]'s favorite too, it takes [real detail, e.g. two days]. See you soon. [Owner name]"
- **Mixed, 3 stars:** "Thanks for the honest note, [first name]. Glad the [thing they liked] hit the mark, and you're right about [issue]. We've [specific change, only if true]. I'd like to hear more: [email]. [Owner name]"
- **Negative, service or wait:** "Thanks for flagging this, [first name]. A [45-minute wait] is not the experience we aim to deliver. I've shared this with the team and we're correcting [specific process]. If you're willing, please contact me at [email/phone] so I can understand what happened." (research report 2026)
- **Negative, food:** "Thanks for telling us, [first name]. The [dish] should have [standard]. [Chef] has [corrective action]. I'd like to make it right: [owner name], [email]."
- **Price complaint:** "Thanks, [first name]. Our costs have gone up and we've raised some prices this year; we work to keep [entry item] at [price] so there's always a way to eat with us. I'd welcome your thoughts: [email]." Never cite your expansion or compare yourself with a Michelin restaurant.
- **Delivery-app review:** 2 to 3 sentences about the order, with the refund or credit route in the app, not an invitation to the dining room (ai-restaurant-claude).
- **Retaliatory, rejected job applicant (calm version):** "Hi, I'm sorry we were not able to hire you for the open position. Good luck in your future endeavors." Then report it with screenshots of their messages. (A blunter reply, "this review is from an applicant that we did not extend an offer to. Looks like we made the right decision", got the review taken down for one owner, 874 upvotes, but reads as hostile to other guests.)
- **One banned visitor framed as a group:** "We recently barred a student from entry for the first time, because they visited regularly to harass staff, and never purchased anything. Other young people from the neighboring high school have been great, and are valued customers." (verbatim)
- **False claim about your supply:** name one real ingredient you buy from that distributor and how you prepare it: "Yes, we get our cage-free eggs from them; our sauces and [dish] are made here from scratch." (verbatim pattern)
- **Disputed complaint, in person or by message:** "I sincerely apologize that your meal wasn't up to the quality you expect. Thank you for bringing it to my attention. I'm going to investigate with the kitchen and take steps so it doesn't happen again." Only promise a callback you will make. (adapted, operators report)
- **Owner's public apology after an outburst:** "Hi, I screwed up. In a moment of pressure and frustration, I said something stupid about our team. In public no less. I'm sorry to the team. There's no excuse, it's not true, and I shouldn't have said it. I'm also sorry to our customers who read it, and you are right to be angry. This is never going to happen again. I hope that based on our [XX] years serving [town], this can someday be seen as an isolated incident from someone making a huge mistake on a very hard day." (proposed, operators report)
- Draft emotional replies, then edit for facts only, several passes, before posting.

## Influencer and creator outreach

- **First DM (local creator):** "Hi [name], I run [Restaurant] in [neighborhood]. Your [specific video] is exactly how our guests eat here. Would you like to come in as our guest for [the new menu / the Tuesday pasta night] in the next two weeks? We'd host [2 people] for [fixed dishes]. If it fits your audience, we'd love a post, and if a video works well we'd like to pay to run it as an ad. Happy to share details."
- **Before confirming:** "Could you send a screenshot of your audience's top cities and last month's average views? We focus on guests within [X] miles."
- **Paid deal terms (email):** "Here's what we'd propose: [1 Reel or TikTok + 3 Stories], posted by [date]; we host [2 guests] for [fixed tasting menu]; fee [amount]; your code [CREATORNAME] gives followers [offer] through [date]; paid usage rights for [60 days] to run the video as a [Spark Ad / partnership ad] within [5] miles, fee [amount]; please tag it as a paid partnership. Does that work?"
- **Creator brief:** "Open with where you are: 'I'm in [neighborhood], and this is the thing to order at [Restaurant].' Show [hero item] within the first few seconds. Mention [specific differentiator]. End with '[event/menu] runs through [date]; booking/order link is [location].' Please do not claim it is 'the best in the city' unless that is your genuine opinion." (research report 2026)
- **Unauthorized promo accounts:** "Hi, we're [Restaurant]. Your post lists specials we don't offer and uses our logo. Please take it down today; we're happy to talk about working together properly."

## Catering and private events

- **Inquiry reply (within the hour):** "Thanks [name]! For [date], [headcount] guests, we'd suggest [Package A: description, $X a head] or [Package B, $Y a head]. Both include [dietary handling, setup, delivery by time]. Minimum is [amount], lead time [days]. Want me to hold the date while you check?"
- **Quote follow-up, day 2:** "Hi [name], checking the quote for [date] reached you. I can still hold [date] until [day]; after that it opens to other bookings."
- **Follow-up, day 7:** "Last note on [event]. If the plan changed, no problem. If headcount or budget moved, tell me the number and I'll build a menu around it."
- **Agreement:** "Here is the LOA for you to review. Once it's signed and returned, I can get the event staffed." (verbatim, truck operator)
- **After the event:** "Thank you for having us at [event]. How did it land with your team? If you run [monthly lunches / client meetings], I can set up a standing order so you don't have to think about it."
- **Next-meeting reminder (buyer's cadence):** "Hi [name], your [quarterly review / team lunch] is usually around [date]. Want the same as last time for [headcount], or something new?"
- **B2B cold outreach:** "Hi [name], I run [Restaurant] two blocks from [office/venue]. We cater groups of [range] with [two concrete package examples], and we can deliver by [time]. Here's the one-page menu: [link]. If you organize team lunches or client meetings, I'm happy to set up a sample order for your next one." (research report 2026)
- **Conference organizer:** "Hi [name], saw [conference] is coming to [venue] on [dates]. We're [distance] away and host private groups of up to [capacity] for dinners and receptions. Want our event menu?"
- **Price objection (truck):** "For 100 guests it's $24 a head: entree, side, dessert and drink, cooked fresh on site, served by our staff, setup and cleanup included. Delivered fast food for the same group comes to about $23 a head once fees, delivery and tip are added. It is a licensed restaurant that comes to you." (adapted, truck operator)
- **Social post, holiday season:** "Book us for private parties: [room/capacity], menus from [$] a head, dates open in December: [link]." (verbatim core, "book us for private parties")

## Event and organizer terms (trucks and pop-ups)

- **Minimum guarantee:** "Our minimum for a [2]-hour service is [$1,200], plus [$400] for each extra hour. Guest purchases count toward it; the host covers any shortfall at the end with the card on file." (operator figures)
- **Walk-up events:** "We need a [$500] guarantee to show; sales are credited against it and the deposit comes back when they cover it." (verbatim core, "$500 to show, guaranteed")
- **Before accepting:** "How many food vendors are booked, and how many last year? Where exactly will we park relative to the entrance and stage? What's the expected attendance and how long do people stay?"
- **Withdrawal after late vendor additions:** "We were booked as the only [savory] truck. With [two more] added, there isn't enough demand for us to cover costs, so we'll step back this time. Let us know next time you need one good truck." (adapted from a 49-upvote reply)
- **Office preorder:** "Lunch on [day]: [3 to 4 meals], pickup [11:45 to 12:15 / 12:15 to 12:45]. Pay here by 9 am the day of: [link]. We come if we reach [N] orders."
- **Fundraiser:** "On [date], the [Mascot] Burger is $5 more than our regular burger and every extra $5 goes to [school PTA]."

## Wholesale pitches

- **Bakery or roaster:** "We bake [product] locally on [days] and currently have capacity for [quantity] additional units per week. Wholesale is [price/minimum], delivery is [days/area], shelf life is [verified period]. Can I drop off a sample set Tuesday or Wednesday?" (research report 2026). Re-pitch good prospects every 12 weeks.
- **Event host snack (popcorn, cookies):** "We do branded snack bags for designer and client events: white bags with your sticker, [N] bags for [$]."

## Direct ordering and offers

- **Bag insert:** "Next time, order direct at [short URL]. Use [CODE] for $10 off your first direct order, no minimum. Direct orders earn [loyalty benefit] and get our online-only [bundle]." (built from the report's insert and the $10 no-minimum tactic; offer for ordering direct, never for a review)
- **App bundle description:** "Family meal for 4. Call the store for a $50 discount." (verbatim, operators report; check platform terms first)
- **Slow night:** "Tuesday Pasta Night: three courses, $39, 6 to 9 pm, 40 seats only. Book: [link]." / "Thursday Wine Table: four pours + four small plates, one seating at 7 pm. Reservations required." (research report)
- **Bakery drop:** "Saturday croissant box: 6 pastries, preorder by Thursday 6 pm, pickup Saturday 8 to 11 am. We make only the preordered quantity plus a limited walk-in batch." (research report)
- **Event email series** (ai-restaurant-claude): 21 days out the full menu and price; 7 days out "8 seats left" (only if true); day before start time, welcome drink 15 minutes early, dress code.
- **Opening list:** "We open [date] in [neighborhood]. Join the first-access list for opening reservations, menu previews and our first [special event/drop]." (research report)
- **Return voucher chain:** "Hope you enjoyed the sandwich. You've got to try our cookie, here's a card for a free one when you come back." At redemption, give a sandwich card and suggest another item. (proposed)

## Regulars and service moments

- **Teaching the order:** "I know all the barista speak can be really confusing if you aren't used to it. Do you want me to tell you exactly what to ask for next time so that it's easier for you to get what you want and I don't have to bug you with all my questions?" (verbatim)
- **Unsure order:** "I want to make you the drink you want, but let me ask some questions so that I can make what you're expecting to get." (verbatim)
- **Macchiato:** "Do you want a traditional macchiato, which is a small unsweetened drink [hold up the tiny cup] with just espresso and some milk foam, or a latte style more similar to what you get at Starbucks?" (near verbatim)
- **Americano complaint:** "Ah, traditionally an Americano is just water and espresso! I can add some milk, cream or sugar if you'd like?" (verbatim)
- **Coffee-hesitant guest:** "Do you like cinnamon toast? Then try a cinnamon dolce latte. If you don't like it, we can try something else." (verbatim)
- **Moving a lingering regular:** "Hey, we have a few families waiting for a table, any chance we can move you to the bar for a while?" (verbatim)
- **Laptop guest before the rush:** "Just a heads up, we're going to get slammed tonight, so we'll need this table in two hours for the dinner rush." (verbatim)
- **Outside drinks at the door:** "If you'd like, you can dispose of that here or we can seat you once you're finished." (verbatim) Sign option: "Any outside beverage brought into this establishment will have a $1 carry-in fee applied to the bill. Thank you." (verbatim)
- **Off-menu requests:** "We have two salads I think you'll love, and they both have the option of adding grilled or fried chicken, along with avocado." / "We can do your burger in a lettuce wrap, or deconstructed on your plate so that you can put it together as you'd like. Will that work?" (verbatim)
- **Off-menu but possible:** "We can do that, it's outside tonight's setup, so it adds at least 25 minutes. Still want it?"
- **Repeat send-backs, before they order:** "Nice to see you back. Just so you know, from now on we are unable to remake any dishes for you, and you will need to pay for all food served to you. Would you like to go ahead and order?" (proposed, 30 upvotes)
- **Persistent comp-seeker who escalates:** "I'm sorry, we can't meet your standards, and you'd be happier somewhere else." (adapted)
- **Soda in a water cup:** "I'm so sorry, I thought you wanted water. That drink will be $2.59. I apologize." (near verbatim; works best from the cashier who rang it)
- **Silent phone call:** "Hello? If you can hear me, I can't hear you. Please call back so I can better assist you." (verbatim)
- **Food ready (trucks):** text "Your food is ready at [truck]" to guests who left the line.

## Neighbors, vendors and platforms

- **Hostile neighboring owners:** "We are hearing a lot of hate from some of our local businesses, which we support. We invite the staff and owners from these businesses to come eat for free. We want to create a collaborative and industry-driven relationship that is positive for all of us." (proposed)
- **Donation requests in person:** "Oh wow, that sounds so cool! So the process for donations is this..." and point to the form. (verbatim)
- **OpenTable renewal:** "Our promotional rate ends [date]. We're comparing Resy and Toast Tables; we'd need the bill down to [amount] to stay." (adapted from "you're going to have to switch to Resy if you can't get your bill down")
- **Unauthorized marketplace listing (call support):** "We have no agreement with you. This listing takes orders we can't receive at prices we don't charge. Remove it tonight and confirm in writing."
