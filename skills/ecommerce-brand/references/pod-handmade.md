# Print on demand and handmade playbook

Two businesses with one shared constraint: thin margin per unit (POD) or capped labor hours (handmade). Both usually start on Etsy; Etsy search mechanics are in [etsy.md](etsy.md).

## Print on demand

### What sells

- **Micro-niche = identity + occasion + emotion** (electrician + retirement + pride). A niche passes if you can write 10 phrases in 10 minutes, it fits 3 products, and one community will clearly recognize itself (r/printondemand, 59-pt thread).
- **Inside-joke filter.** Collect 20 to 50 audience phrases by hand from Etsy and Amazon titles and reviews, Reddit and Facebook groups; split the audience into 3 to 5 angles (cat moms: work from home, rescue, wine); put the first 10 to 20 designs into one angle. "All designs must feel like an inside joke between cat mom and cat, not for everyone else." (43-pt thread, 12-year seller).
- **Write for the gift buyer.** Enthusiasts rarely buy for themselves. Plain profession words on the design ("AEROSPACE ENGINEER") and keywords like "gift for pilot husband".
- **Lead products:** T-shirts, art prints, mugs and phone cases were over half of one seller's first 1,000 sales; the top 10 product types were 77.6% (44-pt thread).
- **Only 43 to 57% of designs ever sell** and expected winners often do not (208 designs: 42.8% sold; 242 designs: 56.6% sold). Launch narrow, then keep adding steadily.
- **Launch 3 to 5 products in 1 to 3 niches.** 45 of 50 abandoned Etsy POD shops launched about 23 listings in two weeks and stopped; shops with 500 to 5,000 sales launched 3 to 5 and expanded what sold (173-pt thread).
- **Crowdsource the copy:** the $15 boosted "You know you are a [X] if..." post (see [first-100-sales.md](first-100-sales.md)).
- **Bestseller-badge gap:** buying keyword + bestseller badge + shop under 100 reviews = a niche a new shop can enter. One seller redrew a ~$30 wedding mug cleaner and reached 10+ orders a day and $18k over a few months, until cheaper copies arrived.
- **Seasonality:** September to January plus Mother's Day, Father's Day and Valentine's carry most POD revenue. Prepare seasonal designs 4 to 6 weeks before the spike; page one reflects gaps from 3 to 6 months earlier.
- **Keep finding the next niche while the current one sells.** One seller's best niche died; monthly sales fell to $10k to $12k and took months to rebuild to $40k to $60k at about 35% profit.
- **Public-domain art on one product type** (NASA and Navy ships on puzzles) avoids IP risk.

### Pricing: the $8 rule

- **Leave at least $8 to $10 profit per sale after every cost.** 38 of 50 abandoned shops were underpriced: median $17.99 against $12 to $14 production plus shipping, leaving about $1.79 a sale (280 sales to make $500). Surviving shops priced at a $26 to $32 median (173-pt thread).
- **Price backward:** `(production + shipping + $8 target profit) / 0.75` = $32.67 for a $12 shirt with $4.50 shipping, rounded to $32.99. The 0.75 is a fee and Offsite Ads allowance; replace it with your real fee rate.
- **Itemize Etsy fees per order** (listing $0.20, 6.5% transaction on the total including shipping, 3% + $0.25 processing, 15% Offsite Ads when charged, regulatory fees by region; operators report, verify). One seller found an $18.99 bestseller lost $0.80 per sale; raising prices $3 to $6 gave $6.50 to $9 per product.
- **Fold part of shipping into price.** Shipping shown at $4.50 instead of $6.99 with $2.49 added to the item reduced cart abandonment for one seller.
- **Premium tier:** a $42 hoodie with Stanley/Stella blanks from a European provider sold at $65; $40 to $42 premium tees with free worldwide shipping held a 4.8 rating.
- **Drop the blanket coupon.** A daily-renewed 10% coupon made no visible difference when removed; a "10% off 3 items" bundle offer was kept.
- **Move proven designs to inventory.** Test with POD, then stock what sells consistently from a local printer (a Gildan hoodie at $8 to $10 wholesale vs ~$40 landed through POD). Stickers and bookmarks especially: batch print and ship yourself.

### Platforms and providers

- **Etsy plus Printify or Printful** is the usual start. Redbubble's excess-markup fees took 73 to 84% of earnings in several reports (one $19.79 sale paid $3.07); avoid raising markup above the standard there.
- **Check Amazon Merch and Etsy competition separately:** niches with 500+ listings on Amazon can have fewer than 20 on Etsy.
- **Add one marketplace at a time with about 30 listings.**
- **Screen providers:** order samples of the same product from several providers (do not trust auto-selection); 100% cotton blanks for DTG (blends print worse); wash-test (one side-by-side after three washes favored Printful over Printify on softness and cracking); check the production country (some products ship from overseas even for US orders).
- **Budget the complaint rate:** 5 to 7 issue emails a week at 50 to 100 sales a week (one Printful seller). Set a custom Etsy production window of 1 to 3 weeks so a backup provider fits; a synced 7-day default caused refunds.
- **Do not use POD for TikTok Shop SLAs** without testing real dispatch times (see [tiktok-shop.md](tiktok-shop.md)).

### Listings and ads

- **Featured image:** the phrase fills 70 to 80% of the frame, four lines max, one clean background, readable at 1-inch thumbnail size; one small true cue ("Ships in 48h").
- **Real people over default mockups;** lifestyle mockups in ads, the provider's exact item image on the product page.
- **Use the common keywords** buyers already use; "original" keywords waste the slot (one seller's year-one lesson).
- **$5 Meta engagement filter before a product gets budget:** one ad set at $5 a day, one broad interest, copy "Love this shirt? Click here: [link]", Shop Now button. After 24 hours it passes only with 1 to 2+ comments and 3 to 5+ shares; reactions alone fail (r/printondemand, 26-pt thread; 6 years of use).
- **Geotarget identity clusters:** aviation designs to areas around air bases and aviation employers.
- **A $1,000 revenue day can need $500+ a day in ads and leave $100 to $150 profit.** POD profit depends on organic Etsy rank and repeat buyers more than ads.

### Risk

- **IP:** never use brand names, characters, celebrity names or protected phrases; audit tags as well as titles. "Redesign the bestseller" tactics work briefly and attract copies.
- **AI designs:** Etsy allows seller-prompted AI creations when disclosed; AI prompt bundles are not "seller-designed" (Etsy Creativity Standards 2026). Use humans for audience language and AI for visuals; disclose.
- **AI product images in Google feeds** should carry the IPTC TrainedAlgorithmicMedia label (AgriciDaniel harvest, MIT).
- **Vacation mode can cost rank;** one seller lost page-one positions after under 24 hours.

## Handmade and small-batch

- **Price for labor first.** A handmade knife maker kept raising the price while it still sold and reached "several hundred dollars". A $20 to $60 jewelry range grows too slowly; add fine pieces at a few hundred dollars.
- **Publish fixed-price standard builds with Buy Now** so "custom" does not read as unaffordable; charge for every change.
- **Make rare combinations to order instead of stocking them** (12 colors x 12 colors = 144 combinations, each made in 1 to 2 hours when ordered).
- **Separate quality tiers clearly** (sterling silver and Swarovski vs simpler pieces); mixing materials such as chain on bead-woven pieces reads as costume jewelry.
- **Paid add-ons instead of costly freebies,** with the best bonus reserved for top tiers.
- **Sell your designs to other makers** as digital patterns or files ($1 to $27 depending on effort).
- **Markets and cons:** buyers arrive planning to spend; individual $4 units sell where a $25 family size does not at outdoor events.
- **Educate first:** about 20 educational posts per pitch grew one candle-supply business's Facebook group from 52k to 105k and YouTube from 62k to 108k, and its free-content classes still sold out at 25 seats.
- **Do not reveal how simple the equipment is** to a high-spending customer who values the finished custom piece (a craft-cutter disclosure preceded a big buyer's orders stopping).
- **Wholesale** gives makers predictable volume but caps labor: see [wholesale.md](wholesale.md). Check hourly earnings first: one couple filling a 5,000-piece subscription-box order netted $2 to $3 an hour.

## 90-day plan: POD

**Days 1 to 14:** pick 1 to 3 micro-niches with the identity + occasion + emotion test; build the phrase bank; sample 2 to 3 providers; price with the $8 rule; 3 to 5 products live.
**Days 15 to 45:** 5 to 20 new listings a week inside the winning angle; $5 engagement filter before any paid push; Etsy Ads on everything at low spend.
**Days 46 to 75:** expand winners to more product types and occasions; turn off ads on page-one listings; seasonal designs 4 to 6 weeks ahead.
**Days 76 to 90:** move consistent winners to stocked inventory if margin improves; add a second marketplace with about 30 listings; exit check: $8+ profit per sale, 40%+ of designs selling in the winning niche.

## 90-day plan: handmade

**Days 1 to 14:** hourly rate target; fixed-price standard builds; photo set and making-of video; shipping quotes for large pieces.
**Days 15 to 45:** Etsy plus one in-person market or fair per month; community posts with process content; collect emails at markets.
**Days 46 to 75:** raise price on the bestseller until it slows; add one premium tier; test one wholesale or consignment partner on paper terms.
**Days 76 to 90:** made-to-order for rare variants; holiday processing windows; exit check: hourly earnings above target, a waitlist or backlog on the bestseller.
