# Expensive mistakes

Two tables: what measured data or platform rules show, and what practitioners report losing. Sources in [sources.md](sources.md).

## From the data and platform rules

| Mistake | What it costs | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Judging ads on revenue ROAS | US online returns were 19.3% in 2025 (NRF); platform ROAS ignores returns, fees and shipping | Break-even ROAS from contribution, on the same order value the platform reports |
| Buying traffic into a weak checkout | 70.22% average cart abandonment; checkout UX alone could lift large sites 35.26% (Baymard 2026) | Checkout and PDP audit before scaling paid |
| Comparing to "2 to 3% conversion" | Category averages run from 0.63% (jewelry) to 5.70% (pet care) (Dynamic Yield 2026) | Compare to the category |
| Ending A/B tests early | 19% of tests ended within a day, only 33% ran two weeks; ~14,000 visitors per variant needed at 3% baseline for a 20% lift (Replo 2026) | Pre-calculate sample; test big structural changes |
| Newsletters before flows | Automated email $2.87 per send vs $0.18 for campaigns (Omnisend 2025) | Welcome, cart, post-purchase, back-in-stock first |
| Skipping back-in-stock | 6.72% conversion and $9.14 per email, used by 0.6% of brands (Omnisend 2025) | Turn it on for every SKU that goes out of stock |
| Deep BFCM discounts | ~2.05x gross-profit growth near 15% off vs ~1.75x near 40% off (Lebesgue 2025) | Threshold gifts and bundles; model incremental contribution |
| Etsy SKU that cannot absorb Offsite Ads | 15% (12% above $10k) on attributed orders, 30-day window, mandatory once over $10k (Etsy 2026) | Price for the attributed case |
| Renewing Etsy listings as SEO | Etsy says the boost lasts hours to days and renewal for it is not effective (2025) | Improve intent match and conversion |
| Incentivized or gated Amazon reviews | Violates Amazon policy; account risk | Vine, Request a Review, neutral insert only |
| Optimizing Amazon for cheap CPC | Top-of-search CPC +56% but CPA -40% vs product pages (Perpetua Q3 2026) | Bid by placement CPA and contribution |
| Reading GMV Max ROI as incremental | It counts organic orders of promoted products while running (TikTok 2026) | Blended view and pause tests |
| Copying a guru's TikTok commission | 1% to 80% allowed; TikTok recommends from your category's last 30 days | Solve commission from contribution and creator supply |
| Judging PMax in days | Google: six weeks for PMax, 4 to 6 week experiments with week 1 discarded | No structural changes during learning |
| Paying creators without usage rights | 80% of single-post deals include paid usage or whitelisting (Kolect 2026) | Price organic, content, affiliate and usage separately |
| Building llms.txt for Google | Google says it does not use it (2026) | Fix Merchant Center, product data and page facts |
| One global email RPR target | Klaviyo cart RPR p75 is $5.86 at $83 to $112 AOV but $16.40 at $163 to $291 AOV ($1M to $5M brands, 2026) | Compare to the matching revenue and AOV row |

## From practitioners

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Reordering 5x after one good day | $10,000 lost; 1,000 units after a single 7-sale day (r/FulfillmentByAmazon, 318-pt) | Reorder on 28-day velocity |
| Huge first order to cut COGS | Over a year to sell; next launch blocked (2026 AMA) | Small first order, funded reorder |
| Stocking out on a differentiated product | 5 exact copies on page one within a month, using the seller's images (122-pt) | Reorder at lead time plus buffer; never cut orders when lead times stretch |
| Not tracking all-in Amazon costs | True costs 38% of gross vs 26% tracked; prices needed +50 to 60% in another brand | Quarterly all-in SKU audit |
| Same SKU at Target and on Amazon | One Target discount removed the Amazon Buy Box: "six figures" in a weekend (r/ecommerce, 115-pt) | Separate SKUs, packaging or GS1 barcodes per channel |
| Loose wholesale screening | 10 rogue sellers cut a $79.99 price to ~$65; Buy Box share tanked | Screen accounts, enforce MAP |
| Resold UPC codes | Whole Amazon store removed (r/Entrepreneur, 553 up) | Buy GS1 |
| Accepting Etsy's AI title suggestion "Basquiat-Style" | 3 violations, legal letter, all listings removed (346-pt) | Never use artist or brand names |
| Brand names hidden in tags | Listing flagged a year later (578-pt) | Trademark audit on tags too |
| Offering a refund for a better review | Buyer dropped 4 stars to 2 and quoted the offer (240-pt) | Never trade anything for a review |
| Cancelling an order to dodge Offsite Ads | Buyer reported it publicly (1,482-pt) | Price for the fee |
| Launching 23 POD listings in 2 weeks then stopping | 45 of 50 abandoned shops did this (173-pt) | 3 to 5 products, expand winners |
| Underpricing POD | $1.79 profit per sale; 280 sales to make $500 | $8+ profit per sale |
| Redbubble excess markup | 73 to 84% of earnings lost to fees | Standard markup there, or another platform |
| POD provider on TikTok Shop | 30 orders, 100% late, 30% cancelled or faulty, account violations | Test real dispatch times first |
| Gifting creators with no selection | 15 creators, $300+ shipping: one story each (59-pt) | Select by recent sales and fit; track the sample funnel |
| Influencer spend ahead of margin | Revenue +100% in a year, zero profit (101-pt) | Contribution per order or proven repeat first |
| One creative carrying a store | $10k a day revenue to zero when the last of three creatives burned out (2,332-pt) | Next creative always in production |
| Extending product tests "one more day" | Up to $800 per test vs a $100 plan (r/dropship, 147-pt) | Write the kill rule before launch |
| Product-hopping weekly | $4,000 spent, $600 back over 8 months | Commit to one product for about 3 months |
| $3,000 on a $20 product with no sale | Total loss (r/shopify, 300-pt) | Reconsider at about 10x price spent with zero sales |
| Changing the ad account's payment card at scale | Meta ban: $120k to $20k a month in a week | Backup ad account and card set up early |
| Shipping identical bulk orders to one address | $3,013 in chargebacks (r/shopify) | Hold identical-quantity orders to one address |
| Untested discount code across markets | ~€18k lost on 400 orders (r/ecommerce) | Test every code in every market |
| "Continue selling when out of stock" left on | 30 oversold units in 12 minutes | Off unless preorders are intended |
| Desktop-only store | "Hundreds" spent, zero add-to-carts | Build and test on mobile first |
| Fake social proof ("23,000+ happy customers") | Flagged as a scam by shoppers | Real numbers or none |
| Partial refund on your own mistake | $2.75 of $4 refunded on a failed gift wrap led to a public complaint (r/Etsy, 583-pt) | Fix your errors in full and fast; a Priority replacement turned one complaint into 5 stars |
| Not quoting shipping before custom work | $460 shipping on a blanket; cancelled, negative review | Packed quote before accepting |
| Letting a viral spike outrun cash | Personal money used to ship 300+ orders while payouts were held | Cash buffer, order caps |
| Borrowing for unvalidated ads | $80k borrowed, staff laid off (709-pt) | Validate on one channel first |
| Hidden fees at checkout | Itemized tariff fee "tanked" sales in 2 days | Show costs before the cart or fold them into price |
