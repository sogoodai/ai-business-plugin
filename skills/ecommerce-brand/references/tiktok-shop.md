# TikTok Shop playbook

For products that show their value on camera in 15 seconds, usually $10 to $60. TikTok Shop is an affiliate and LIVE business with an ad optimizer attached. Reddit evidence here is thinner than for Etsy or Amazon (mostly 3 to 22 upvote comments and a few 50 to 300 point posts), so treat operator numbers as direction, and verify fees and rules in Seller Center: they change often.

## How the money works

- **Shop button beats link in bio.** One seller saw about 5 sales a day through a bio link vs 50 to 100 a day from a video with the Shop button (r/TikTokshop; one seller).
- **Three engines:** affiliates (creators earn commission), your own videos and LIVE, and GMV Max (TikTok's automated Shop Ads). Most small sellers who report $10k+ months credit samples and affiliates or LIVE, not ads.
- **GMV Max is the default and only Shop Ads type since July 2025.** It attributes **all paid and organic orders** of promoted products to the campaign while it runs, even if the buyer never saw an ad (TikTok Ads Help, 2026). Never call its ROI "incremental ROAS".

## Unit economics first

Fee stack operators itemize (r/TikTokshop, 2026; verify): referral about 6%; Smart Promotion 3.5% of sales outside campaigns and 4.5% in campaigns if enabled; affiliate commission (15%+ common); ad spend; free-shipping subsidy ($8+); samples. Worked example in [economics.md](economics.md): a $25 item with free shipping leaves about $5 before samples and ads.

- **Track net profit per SKU daily** (today, yesterday, month to date) including COGS, shipping, affiliate payouts, promotions, campaign service fees and samples. Promote or delist on that, never on GMV.
- **Audit Smart Promotion.** A shop doing $500k+ a month paid $22k to $28k a month for it and could not tell what it drove (17-pt thread).
- **Payout lag is cash you must hold.** Reports of 31 days plus delivery, $25k+ held over a week, $20k frozen and never recovered. Withdraw often; keep enough cash to ship a spike.

## Affiliates and samples: the main engine

TikTok rules (Seller University, Sep to Nov 2026): commission 1% to 80%; Target Collaboration rate overrides Open Collaboration for that creator; free-sample creators need Shop sales in the prior 120 days; review sample requests within 7 days, ship within 7 days of approval; free-sample content is typically due within 14 days of receipt. TikTok's recommended commission uses top performers in your category over the last 30 days: use that, not a guru's "15 to 20%".

Operating system:

1. **Open Collaboration** on every product at a commission your contribution supports, for discovery.
2. **Target Collaboration** at a higher rate for creators whose recent Shop sales and content fit.
3. **Sample volume is what moved sellers.** One credited 10 to 30 samples a day as the breakthrough; another runs about 50 a week, vetted for product fit, at $2 flat per unit sold (r/TikTokshop, 51-pt thread). Smaller creators made the best videos for a $14 to $19 product seller doing about $13k a month mostly through affiliates.
4. **Score creators on recent Shop sales and relevant views, not followers.** Pick by likes and comments on recent posts.
5. **Cold outreach: about 100 creator emails a week** offering a small flat fee for a 30 to 45 second video; most do not reply, but one good collaboration brought inbound sample requests (51-pt thread). Script in [scripts.md](scripts.md).
6. **Always keep a commission on flat-fee deals,** so the creator's video can later run in GMV Max (same OP).
7. **Onboard every approved creator:** ask for an email and send a short instruction video on using the product and making the content; otherwise send written notes plus "search TikTok for [product name]" to see what works.
8. **Retainer for the best creator:** one month, 2 videos a week.
9. **A handwritten thank-you note in the sample box.** One sub-5k creator posted a video that reached 250k views in 1 to 2 days (sales unknown).
10. **Track the sample funnel:** requests, approved, delivered, posted within 14 days, qualified views, orders, repeat creator orders. Cut creators by contribution per sample dollar, not by whether the video "looked good".

Tagged vs untagged: one seller saw an untagged tutorial get about 10x the views of the same creator's tagged video and now pays flat rates for untagged content (37-pt thread; 12-up corroboration). Tagged links still convert. Use untagged for reach and tagged for sales, and measure both.

## Own content

- **Test hooks, not products.** Same footage, change only the first 2 seconds; post 3 focused variants a day. One operator reports 200 vs 20,000 views from a hook change (low evidence).
- **Diagnose against the best video.** Find the top affiliate video for your product (Kalodata or similar), run both through an LLM with "analyse the hook CTA and pacing", compare how early the product appears.
- **Product in use in frame early;** one video a day minimum while learning. An affiliate posting 15 videos a day earned little for months, then $10k to $20k a month after about 6 months.
- **Recent winners only:** if every top video for a product is 3 months old, it is past its peak (affiliate heuristic).

## LIVE

LIVE has more support than "videos only" in the data:

- One seller went live 4 to 8 hours a day for $100 to $300 per live hour and $500 to $2k a day; videos alone brought about $500 a month (309-pt thread).
- Sales were 50% higher on live days for another seller; a seller with 2 hired hosts and 1 shipping helper reported $95k GMV in a month.
- **Structure every product pitch the same way:** hook, problem, demo, price, urgency. A repeatable 30-second script; swap 3 to 4 lines per product and keep them on a screen behind the camera. Tight close-up demos if the host is quiet.
- **Closing sequence:** hold the product to the camera, ask who in chat is buying next, ask the remaining viewers what is holding them back, run a 5-minute flash sale, aim to convert 3 of the roughly 10 who stay, repeat.
- **Faceless LIVE works:** one hour broke a $0 streak with 4 sales; another reports $80 to $200 an hour, weekends better.
- **Restarting a LIVE spikes viewers, not sales** (200 to 300 viewers back to 10 within minutes; 1 sale in 4 hours).
- **Split sessions** (2 sessions of 2 hours) and end when energy drops.
- **Live auctions** for collectibles with a start price that cannot lose money: one seller's first $4k day came from 2 hours of auctions.
- **Go live during a brand's flash sale** on an already-viral product.

## GMV Max

- **Build the creator video library first,** then run GMV Max and keep feeding it fresh creator content. One seller at $1.5k to $2k a week reported $1,871 spend, 660 orders, $11,255 attributed sales in 7 days, about 6x revenue ROAS (51-pt thread; no profit figure).
- **Exclusion cycle (operator method, unverified):** Smart Promotion off, GMV Max on with an ROI target of 20, exclude the videos that eat budget, let TikTok find a new one that holds the target for about a week, repeat.
- **Judge on profit, not GMV.** A brand made viral by a top creator lost money on GMV Max for 4 to 5 straight months including Q4; an affiliate manager reports another at zero profit for 4 months.
- **Measurement guardrail:** report TikTok's GMV Max ROI next to a blended view (total shop contribution vs a pre-period or unpromoted control SKUs). Pausing GMV Max for a week on one SKU is the cheapest incrementality read.
- For learning budgets on TikTok web conversion campaigns (not Shop), Smart+ wants 10x CPA a day minimum and 30x with targets (TikTok, 2025). Depth in `paid-ads`.

## Fulfillment SLAs and account health

- **Acceptance scans.** Drop off daily at a fixed time; under 20 parcels wait for counter scans, over 20 bring a manifest with one master barcode. Unattended drop-offs can take 24 to 48 hours to scan (51-pt thread, 100% ship rate). Confirm manifests actually update tracking: for one seller they did not, causing about 215 automatic refunds on 1,500 orders.
- **Cap orders before anything goes viral.** A candle video at 400k views brought 800 orders in 48 hours; packing ran to 2 a.m., messages went unanswered, quality fell, bad reviews followed. Set maximum orders per product.
- **Made-to-order:** check processing-time extension eligibility for your category before listing; one seller had 2 days to process items that took 4 hours each.
- **POD on TikTok Shop is fragile.** Provider delays caused severe late-dispatch violations (Gelato UK mugs: 30 orders, 100% late, 30% cancelled or faulty). Test the provider's real dispatch time first.
- **Have warehouse-address proof and business license ready before orders;** later document requests caused violations and throttling.
- **Category must match how creators promote it.** A pet product promoted as a supplement triggered regulated-goods violations, a ban and $20k+ frozen commissions.
- **Do not swap the main image to new packaging** on an existing listing; one seller lost all reviews. Screenshot reviews regularly.
- **Limit inventory sent to TikTok's own fulfillment** until you trust it (100 of 300 units lost for one seller).
- **Escalation:** account manager first; for frozen funds after every channel failed, a detailed dated post in r/TikTokshop got the official account to restore withdrawal for one affiliate.
- **Never** open replacement accounts under someone else's identity, repost others' videos, or run follow-for-follow schemes.

## 90-day plan: TikTok Shop

**Days 1 to 14: setup and economics.**
- Fee stack per SKU with samples; price so a 15% affiliate commission still leaves contribution; Smart Promotion off until measured.
- Documents ready, shipping SLA tested, order caps set, 3 to 5 hero products listed (one seller credits listing the full catalog for extra visibility; start with heroes if cash is tight).
- Open Collaboration on all products at the category-recommended rate.

**Days 15 to 45: creator engine.**
- 10 to 30 samples a week to vetted creators, scaling to daily; 100 outreach emails a week; onboarding video; sample funnel tracked.
- Own content: one product-in-use video a day, 3 hook variants per concept.
- First LIVE: 1 hour, 3 times a week, scripted.

**Days 46 to 75: double down.**
- Target Collaboration and a retainer for the top 3 creators by contribution; cut creators with zero orders after 2 posts.
- GMV Max on the hero SKU once 10+ creator videos exist, with a blended-profit readout weekly.
- LIVE up to 4 to 6 hours a week if live days outsell.

**Days 76 to 90: protect.**
- Daily SKU profit check; withdraw weekly; inventory and cash sized for a 10x spike.
- Reuse winning creator videos on Meta and the PDP with usage rights (see [creators-ugc.md](creators-ugc.md)).
- Exit check: contribution positive after samples and ads, more than one creator producing orders every month (no single-creator dependency), late-dispatch rate near zero.
