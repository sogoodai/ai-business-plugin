# Etsy playbook

For handmade, personalized, vintage, craft supplies, digital downloads and Etsy print on demand. POD-specific margin and provider notes are in [pod-handmade.md](pod-handmade.md).

## How Etsy search works (Etsy Seller Handbook, Aug 2025)

Two stages: **matching**, then **ranking**.

- **Matching** reads title, tags, attributes, categories, description, the first photo and reviews. Use all **13 tags** (20 characters each), the most specific category and every attribute.
- **Ranking** weighs relevance, engagement and conversion (clicks, favorites, purchases), listing and shop quality, customer service, shipping, recency and personalization.
- **Shipping is a ranking input.** US domestic listings with shipping under **$6** get search priority in relevant cases (since Oct 2024).
- **What Etsy says does not work:** renewing listings for the recency boost (it lasts hours to a few days), buying Etsy Plus for ranking, expecting Etsy Ads to lift organic rank, and checking your own rank by searching (results are personalized).

Agent rule: optimize **orders per qualified impression**, not impressions. A keyword that brings low-intent clicks lowers conversion and can hurt ranking.

## Listing build, in order

1. **One dominant purchase intent per listing.** Do not stack "wedding gift / birthday / dog lover" in one title.
2. **Title:** the clear human phrase first, then attributes, material or style, occasion. Etsy has moved toward shorter readable titles; the old 80 to 140 character stuffing target is dated (harvest reviewer note). POD template that operators use: "[Occasion] gift for [Identity] - "[Key Phrase]" [Product], [Material], [Audience]".
3. **13 tags:** distinct multi-word phrases, synonyms, occasions and recipients. No near-duplicates, no single generic words ("bracelet").
4. **Trademark audit on tags too.** A listing was flagged a year later for "Onesies" in hidden tags; use "infant bodysuits", "hook and loop" (not Velcro), "plastic air packaging" (not Bubble Wrap). Check tmsearch.uspto.gov (r/EtsySellers, 578-pt thread).
5. **Reject Etsy's AI title suggestions that name an artist or brand.** Accepting "Basquiat-Style" brought 3 policy violations, a legal letter and removal of all listings (r/EtsySellers, 346-pt thread).
6. **Photos carry the facts, because Etsy hides the description behind an expander.**
   - Image 1: instant recognition, scale and difference. For small items an open-palm shot first; buyers who misjudge size never swipe further.
   - Image 2: in-hand or on-body scale. Avoid props like letterboards that distort scale.
   - A ruler shot in inches and cm (a coin fails across countries).
   - An image that names materials ("Czech glass beads and rhinestones").
   - Use restrictions as icons (crossed-out car with rain and sun for non-UV stickers).
   - For patterns and printables, a screenshot of part of the actual file.
   - Keep real shadows on white backgrounds; perfectly isolated white reads as mass-produced to some buyers (r/Etsy, 1,224-pt thread).
7. **Video slot:** a 10-second making-of clip proves you make it (76 up, 226 up across threads). Put a sign with the shop name inside the frame if copies are a problem.
8. **About section and provenance.** Buyers who spend $200 to $1,000 a month check it and skip empty ones. "Guaranteed hand made", "not drop shipped" in description and tags where true.
9. **Personalization field wording:** "Type name exactly as you would like it printed. Double check for spelling errors and grammatical mistakes. To keep production flowing as quickly as possible a mockup will not be sent prior to production." Sell a mockup as a $4 add-on (r/EtsySellers). State formatting in text ("names will be lowercase"); photos alone do not communicate it.
10. **Digital downloads:** add a required field "I understand this is a digital download" (imperfect, buyers still mis-read). Turn off instant download on digital bestsellers if catalog theft appears: one buyer took 1,200+ designs.

## Variations and price display

- **Search shows the lowest variation price.** A $9.99 PNG variation on a $25 shirt gets excluded when a buyer filters at an $18 minimum, and bait pricing reads as misleading (162 up). Make the lead photo match the cheapest variation, or split into separate listings.
- **Move cheap samples or swatches to their own listing** linked from the main one.

## Testing what sells

- **5 subniches x 5 listings.** One seller tested 25 listings across 5 subniches; the winner became 90% of sales from about 20% of listings, then they added matching pieces (earrings for the necklace) (r/EtsySellers, 232-pt thread).
- **Target a recipient, not a product.** Name bracelets aimed "hardcore" at gifts for girls 6 to 12: 9,000+ sales (r/EtsySellers).
- **Mine your favoriters.** Open profiles of people who favorited your item and study what else they favorite (75 up; endorsed by an 8,000-sale seller).
- **Use the gap filter for POD:** search a buying keyword, filter bestseller badge, keep shops with under 100 reviews. That combination means a small shop can rank (r/printondemand, 114-pt thread).
- **Read Etsy Stats search terms,** then rewrite weak listings around the terms that convert.

## Etsy Ads and Offsite Ads

- **Etsy Ads:** operators advertise a subset at about $1 a day per 3 listings, hold budget 30 days, wait 90 days before raising (r/EtsySellers; low evidence). POD operators advertise everything at low spend, then switch ads off for listings that reach page one; they keep selling organically (r/printondemand, 197-pt thread). Expect 2 to 3 loss-making months.
- **Judge on orders.** $121 bought 26,000 impressions and 276 clicks ($0.44 CPC) and zero sales in one shop (r/Etsy). If CTR is fine and orders are zero, the listing, price or photos are the problem.
- **Offsite Ads:** 15% under $10k trailing sales (opt-out possible), 12% above and mandatory, $100 cap, 30 days after a click (Etsy Help 2026). Model every SKU at the attributed rate ([economics.md](economics.md)). Never cancel an ad-attributed order to dodge the fee: it is fee evasion and buyers report it (r/Etsy, 1,482-pt thread).

## Pricing and shipping

- **Price for the attributed-order case and for your labor.** A $12 rose taking an hour per piece, with priority upgrades absorbed, returned about $1,000 on 93 orders before materials (r/EtsySellers, 657-pt thread).
- **Raise prices on the labor-heaviest bestseller.** Reports: price doubled and sales doubled; sharp raise and sales tripled (252 up); a four-year shop at +46% sales and +99% revenue year on year. No controlled test, but the direction is consistent.
- **Shipping:** stay under $6 domestic where possible for ranking. Charge separately with combined-shipping profiles for items bought in multiples; free shipping only above a threshold ($35 or $50 proposed). Charge for priority upgrades.
- **Custom and large items:** full payment including shipping upfront; quote a slightly high shipping estimate and refund the overage; quote 2x your expected turnaround. One seller does $800 to $1,000 pieces this way with all 5-star reviews (1,025 and 69 up). Get a packed shipping quote before accepting oversized work: a 90x100 blanket cost $460 to ship and was cancelled (r/EtsySellers).
- **Coupon hygiene:** Etsy's abandoned-cart coupons can fire within an hour while the shopper is still browsing; a flat $5 coupon created when everything cost $30+ later let a $6 item sell for $1; thank-you codes leak to coupon extensions. Audit codes quarterly.
- **No permanent sale.** Some buyers read a forever-50%-off as fake pricing.

## Reviews and cases

Etsy sends its own review reminders. Do not offer anything for a review or a review change: a seller offered 10% back to move 4 stars to 5, and the buyer dropped it to 2 stars and quoted the offer (r/Etsy, 240-pt thread).

- **Report first, reply later.** A public reply locks the review against buyer edits; Etsy can still remove it. Reply first only to lock a good or improved review.
- **Removal grounds sellers report working:** duplicate reviews from one order (3 of 4 removed), reviews about Etsy itself, reviews for the wrong shop, competitors advertising their shop in your reviews, size complaints from buyers who did not read the listing (hit or miss, 1 to 2 days).
- **Evidence habits:** video every packing (a "missing item" was "found" after the buyer heard about the footage); screenshot and screen-record the buyer's profile before any case; search the buyer's public social posts for the "undelivered" item.
- **Escalation when Etsy rules against you:** ask for a second mediator, ask which policy and unmet listing requirement the ruling rests on, resubmit dated delivery proof. One seller got every dollar credited back after a month; another got $60 credited "within minutes" on a closed case. A BBB complaint resolved a $1,400 case.
- **Orders above $250 carry protection-gap risk** even with signature and insurance (a $1,400 watch was refunded to the buyer). Price the risk into high-ticket listings.
- **Screen buyers before custom production:** match repeat orders by address, not name; ship only to the order address (cancel and reorder for changes); check review history with lookup tools.
- **Gift-proof every order:** a printed card saying what the item is and how to care for it. A missing explanation on an unusual gift produced a 3-star review. DTG care card: inside out, max 30°C, no fabric softener, no ironing the print.
- **Do not ship custom items in reused Amazon boxes;** the packaging was used to win a "used item" not-as-described refund paid from the seller's funds.

Scripts for every one of these conversations: [scripts.md](scripts.md).

## Off-Etsy and the second channel

- **Diversify before you need to.** A 12-year six-figure shop was suspended over a card belonging to a person with a suspended account, while its own site made 1/12 of Etsy revenue (r/Etsy, 577-pt thread; later reinstated after a second, specific appeal).
- **Instagram as proof of making** (sketches, layers, process) with the Etsy link in bio; buyers check it.
- **Wholesale terms that work for makers:** 30% off retail with a $500 minimum got one seller into galleries (r/EtsySellers). See [wholesale.md](wholesale.md).
- **Partner cards with a tracked code:** doll-clothes maker cards inside a doll maker's orders, with a partner-specific discount code to measure it (75 up).

## 90-day plan: Etsy

**Days 1 to 14: economics and listing hygiene.**
- Fee model per SKU including the Offsite Ads case; kill or reprice anything under target contribution.
- Trademark audit of every title and tag.
- Rebuild the top 5 listings: one intent, 13 distinct tags, photo order (scale, in-hand, ruler, materials, use icons), 10-second making-of video, About section.
- Shipping profiles: domestic under $6 where possible, combined shipping, paid upgrades.

**Days 15 to 45: find the winning subniche.**
- 5 subniches x 5 listings, or 3 to 5 products in 1 to 3 micro-niches for POD.
- Etsy Ads at about $1 a day per 3 listings on the test set; read Etsy Stats search terms weekly.
- One niche community post per week with proof (see [first-100-sales.md](first-100-sales.md)).

**Days 46 to 75: expand the winner.**
- Matching pieces, colors and occasions for the winning subniche; a bundle listing for items bought together.
- Raise price on the labor-heaviest bestseller by 10 to 20% and watch conversion for 3 weeks.
- Turn ads off on listings that reach page one; keep them on new listings.

**Days 76 to 90: protect and diversify.**
- Packing video habit, insert cards, holiday processing times (extend twice as Christmas nears, update the shop announcement and auto-message together).
- Capture buyers you are allowed to reach: an insert card to a non-Etsy channel (Instagram, newsletter via your own site) where Etsy policy allows.
- Exit check: one subniche clearly carrying sales (in the 25-listing test the winner became 90% of sales), contribution per order above target even in the Offsite Ads case, no unanswered review or case.
