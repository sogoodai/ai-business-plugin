# Creators, UGC and whitelisting

Buy creator work as four separate things and price each: **organic posting** to their audience, **content production** (the files), **affiliate performance** (commission on sales), and **paid usage** (whitelisting, Spark codes, partnership ads, edit rights, duration). In a 120,000+ collaboration dataset, 80% of single-post deals included paid usage, whitelisting or ad-code rights (Kolect, Aug 2025 to Oct 2026). A mediocre organic post can still be a strong paid ad, so track creator organic sales and paid-ad performance as separate fields. TikTok Shop affiliate mechanics are in [tiktok-shop.md](tiktok-shop.md).

- Half of collaborations cost $300 to $900; median cost per 1,000 views $22.83 overall, $23.11 on TikTok, $44.54 on YouTube (Kolect 2026, vendor dataset).
- These exclude product-only gifting. Treat them as a negotiation reference, not a rate card.
- Operator anchors: niche pages around 300k followers charged $100 per shoutout or $75 each for 5 (r/ecommerce); meme pages $75 to $150 a post (r/Entrepreneur).

## What worked, ranked by evidence

1. **Gift first, pay only the responsive creators who decline gifting.** A backpack brand emailed and DM'd nano and micro creators in adjacent niches (photography creators for a camera backpack), offered free product with a mention "only if they actually like it", and paid sponsorships only where needed. Reported 7x average return on product plus fees; then fixed the one con a creator raised (r/Entrepreneur, 2,730-pt thread).
2. **License the footage and recut it.** The same brand bought commercial usage rights and had editors reorder, cut and add voiceovers to make many variants: ads went from unprofitable above $100 a day to $500 a day at 3.5x ROAS.
3. **Many micro creators in one window.** 5 to 10 creators at 5k to 50k followers posting within the same 7 days, each doing at most 2 to 3 promotions a week; ask "have you promoted this exact product recently?" to avoid saturation (r/dropship, 183-pt thread).
4. **Affiliate invite instead of a fee.** Offering an affiliate link plus 30% off their own purchase brought 10+ sales with $0 in fees (r/dropship).
5. **Collect UGC from customers:** a free product for a video, or a chance at a full refund, asked in post-purchase email. One brand collected 300 videos in under 60 days; not worth it under about $15k a month in sales (r/ecommerce, 103-pt thread).
6. **Creator LIVE with revenue share.** A creator's live promo for a new candle store did $15k the first night and $50k+ in 4 days (r/ecommerce, 93-pt thread; repeatability disputed).

What failed:

- **Gifting without selection:** 15 creators (2k to 50k followers), $300+ in shipping, 15% commission: one story each, nothing more (r/ecommerce, 59-pt thread).
- **Paying for reach without a click path:** a $1,000 post to a 150k page did not recoup and the photos could not be used in ads; six paid LinkedIn posts drew 3,500 likes and zero site visits.
- **Influencer spend ahead of margin:** revenue up 100% in a year with zero profit (r/ecommerce, 101-pt thread). Creators need repeat purchases or high contribution per order to pay.

## Selection

- **Rank by** recent sales of similar products (TikTok Shop shows this), relevant views on recent posts, comments that ask where to buy, content completion history, and reuse rights. Followers are secondary.
- **Score:** `expected qualified views x expected CVR x contribution/order - fee - sample - commission` ([economics.md](economics.md)).
- **Vet** with Social Blade or a similar tracker for bought followers; check whether they reply to their comments; for bloggers, cross-check claimed traffic against comment counts.
- **Recent ad and paid post history:** if they promoted three competitors last month, skip.

## Paid usage and whitelisting

- **Get in writing:** platforms (Meta partnership ads, TikTok Spark codes, YouTube), duration (30, 60 or 90 days, renewal price), edit rights (cut, caption, voiceover), raw files, exclusivity window and category, and who pays for boosting.
- **Spark Ads preserve the original post identity** and attribute paid engagement to it (TikTok, 2026). Run Spark when an organic creator post already shows resonance.
- **Keep winners' post IDs** when restructuring Meta campaigns so social proof carries over; one agency that skipped this burned about $1,400 at 0.2 ROAS vs a normal 1.9+ (r/ecommerce, 92-pt thread).
- **Recycle viral micro-creator videos as ads until they fatigue,** and refresh UGC ads every 2 to 4 weeks.

## The brief

Research template (2026):

> "The goal is not a polished commercial. Show the product naturally in {{specific situation}}. Open on the problem or surprising result in the first beat. Demonstrate {{mechanism/proof}} visibly. Please do not make claims outside the approved claim sheet. Required: product shown in use, one clear reason to believe, one objection answered, and a direct CTA. Avoid scripted brand-history intros."

60-second beat sheet (coleschaffer harvest, MIT): 0 to 3 s hook; 3 to 12 s problem; 12 to 20 s discovery; 20 to 40 s experience; 40 to 50 s result with a number or timeframe; 50 to 60 s casual CTA. Give 3 hook options and 4 to 6 B-roll shots. Format by awareness: testimonial for retargeting, unboxing for launches, day-in-life for cold lifestyle audiences, problem-solution for crowded markets.

Short UGC brief that operators use: selfie mode, one real use situation, include the shipping and arrival experience, one minute or less (r/ecommerce, 7 up).

Hook lines for ads and creator openings (research report 2026):

- "I bought this because {{pain/problem}} kept happening."
- "The difference between {{common alternative}} and {{product}} is this."
- "If you use {{category product}} for {{use case}}, check this before you buy."
- "Three things I wish I knew before buying {{category}}."
- "This looks like {{obvious assumption}}, but the reason it works is {{mechanism}}."

## Compliance

- Creators disclose paid and gifted posts (#ad or platform paid-partnership label). The brand is responsible for claims creators make about the product: give a claim sheet.
- Never script fake testimonials or present paid creators as unpaid customers.
- Never ask creators or customers for reviews on Amazon in exchange for anything.
