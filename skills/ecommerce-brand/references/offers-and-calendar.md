# Offers, AOV and the promotion calendar

Every offer is judged by incremental contribution, not revenue. Formulas are in [economics.md](economics.md). The 2025 evidence says deeper discounts do not maximize gross profit: Klaviyo's BFCM cohort spent 11% more while discounts fell 10%, and Lebesgue's 500+ stores with cost data grew gross profit about 2.05x around 15% off vs 1.75x around 40% off (both observational, 2025).

## Offers ranked by how much margin they keep

1. **Threshold gift:** "Spend {{threshold}} and choose {{gift}} free through {{date}}." Keeps the hero price intact.
2. **Bundle that solves a problem:** starter kit, routine, size set, matching pieces. Show "Value: $149 | Bundle price: $119, Save $30 (20% off)" with component images (finsilabs, MIT). 2 to 5 components; bundle discount 5 to 10% when bought together.
3. **Quantity break:** "buy 2 get 10% off, buy 3 get 20% off"; show the next tier on the PDP ("Add 3 more to get 10% off") and savings in dollars. In one dropship case 1 in 3 buyers took the multi-unit option.
4. **Free-shipping threshold** (below).
5. **Post-purchase one-click offer** (below).
6. **Subscriber or replenishment incentive** for products that run out.
7. **Slow-moving add-on discounted, attached to a full-margin hero.**
8. **Percentage off sitewide:** last resort, with an end date that holds.

Rules:

- **Automatic discounts over codes.** Removing code entry stops coupon-site leakage (finsilabs; the 10 to 15% conversion claim is uncited).
- **Single-use codes for targeted sends, capped in dollars** ("20% off up to $50"); deactivate rather than delete codes.
- **Keep discount-only buyers out of full-price launches and VIPs out of discount blasts.**
- **Frame a component as free** rather than discounting the bundle: "boat with a free trailer" sold in a week after months unsold (Amazon anecdote).
- **Be careful with "free bonus" framing that just hides a lower price:** one commenter showed "Get A for $45 & we'll include B & free shipping" collected $4.99 less per order than the plain offer.

## Free shipping and thresholds

- **Model it, do not copy it.** 94% of shoppers say free shipping influences purchase and 56% of retailers use a minimum (Shopify 2026, survey data).
- **Threshold candidate = median AOV + one sensible add-on,** or 20 to 30% above AOV. Check: the added gross margin must cover the shipping (an $8 shipment at 40% margin needs $20 more basket).
- **Test thresholds on conversion x contribution per order,** not AOV alone.
- **Show the exact dollar gap** ("Add $12.50 more for FREE shipping") in the mini-cart and cart; suggest gap-filler products when $5 to $15 away; compute on the post-discount subtotal (finsilabs, MIT).
- **Folding shipping into the price often beats a separate fee.** $10 + $4 shipping became $14 free shipping and sales "skyrocketed" (r/ecommerce); a $30 item with $20 shipping became about $60 with "free shipping" and sales tripled (r/smallbusiness). But a 21-up commenter found free shipping lowered AOV in their store: test it.
- **Etsy:** keep domestic shipping under $6 for search priority; combined shipping profiles for multiples.
- **Show any surcharge before the cart.** An itemized tariff fee at checkout "tanked" sales in 2 days for one seller (66 up); the fix was to fold it into price or disclose it on the PDP.

## Upsells and cross-sells

Placement order (finsilabs, MIT): PDP below Add to Cart and cart first; post-purchase next (zero risk to the order); checkout last, limited to 1 to 2 add-ons under $30.

- **Price cross-sells under 50% of the main item** and show them in cart or thank-you page so they do not distract before commitment.
- **Order bump at 20 to 40% of the front-end price.**
- **Post-purchase one-click, card not re-entered, "No thanks" always visible.** Rebuy case studies report +27% AOV (MaryRuth) and +18.93% (Feals), vendor-selected. Operators: a complementary product at 15% off added $349.56 in a month for a small store; "So your travel partner doesn't feel left out" (a second identical backpack, deep discount) took 6%.
- **Show a discounted post-purchase version only to buyers who declined the PDP upsell.**
- **Cart ladder capped at 13% pre-purchase** ("Add 1 more product for 10% Off", then free shipping + 10%, then + 13%), 15 to 20% off declined complements right after purchase, a 10-minute code on the thank-you page: AOV $176.40 to $203 (r/dropship, 1,564 up).
- **A one-more-item popup** ("If you purchase one more item, you can get 10% off") moved AOV from $20 to $45 for one low-AOV store (748-pt thread; OP sells a tool).
- **The upsell never implies the main product falls short;** it solves an adjacent problem.
- **BNPL** fits AOV $100 to $3,000; it costs 2 to 3x a card fee, so check margin.

## The promotion calendar

**BFCM 2026: Black Friday is November 27, Cyber Monday November 30.** BFCM 2027: Black Friday November 26.

If BFCM is under three weeks away (as of mid-November 2026), do only this:
- Confirm stock and fulfillment capacity for a 3 to 5x day; set Etsy and TikTok order caps and processing times.
- One offer that protects contribution (threshold gift or bundle), set with real end dates; compare-at prices reverted after.
- Early access email to buyers, launch-morning email, one evening reminder, Cyber Monday email only to non-buyers.
- SMS 1 to 2 sends only, to consented subscribers, excluding anyone who bought in the last 3 days.
- Holiday shipping cutoffs in every listing, auto-message and confirmation email.

Full BFCM plan when there is time (finsilabs and nexscope harvests, MIT; adapted):

| When (T = Black Friday) | Action |
|---|---|
| T-8 weeks | Offer chosen from contribution math; creative and email templates built; inventory ordered |
| T-6 weeks | Suppress 90-day non-openers; ramp send volume gradually; SMS opt-in push (start 3 months out if possible) |
| T-21 days | Teaser to all subscribers |
| T-14 days | "Save the date" with the exact offer |
| T-7 days | VIP early access announced (VIPs get the deal 24 h early) |
| T-1 | Launch eve |
| T-0, morning | Launch email and SMS; evening reminder |
| Weekend | Bundles and gift angles |
| Cyber Monday | New angle or last chance, non-buyers only |
| Tuesday | Thank you; optional 24 h extension |

Cap each person at 4 to 5 emails in BFCM week; more than 2 texts in 4 days spikes opt-outs (finsilabs, uncited).

One operator's November pacing (r/dropship, 181-pt; challenged): week 1 a free gift with orders to start a second purchase within 30 days; week 3 free shipping on full-price bestsellers with real low-stock messaging; week 4 rotating short-window discounts on single products, never deep cuts on bestsellers.

A DTC store that discounts only once a year (25% off everything on BFCM) did $12,098.96 in a day vs a $7,500 prior record at 15.34% conversion, with a same-day fulfillment timer beside Add to Cart (r/ecommerce).

## Holiday operations that protect reviews

- **Extend processing times at least twice as Christmas nears** and update the shop announcement and auto-message together.
- **Work back from the carrier deadline** (one seller used December 17 for December 24 delivery) to an earlier store cutoff and repeat it in every confirmation; 15 years of use.
- **Offer only faster shipping methods from Black Friday to mid-December** if slow methods cause late-gift complaints (one seller: zero complaints after doing this).
- **Do not trust supplier holiday cutoffs blindly;** POD orders placed before a provider's cutoff were still unshipped after Christmas.
