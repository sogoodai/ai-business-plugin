# 90-day plan

For a new or stalled jewelry or accessories business. Order: economics and product data, then conversion, then owned demand, then paid and B2B scale. Skip blocks that do not apply (permanent jewelry, bridal, wholesale) and say which were skipped. Every exit check is a yes or no the agent can verify.

## Owner questions before day 1

1. What do you sell, at which price bands, and how is it made (your bench, casting house, factory, sourced, vintage)?
2. Metals and their supplier price per gram; stones and their origin; plating construction.
3. Hours per piece (or agree to log five pieces per design this month).
4. Channels today and monthly sales per channel for 12 months; Etsy Offsite Ads status.
5. Return and refund rate and reasons; insurance and shipping method for pieces over $250.
6. Countries you ship to (UK hallmarking, EU GPSR and REACH, Canada marking).
7. Email and SMS list size and consent source.
8. Your floor: minimum hourly pay and minimum profit per order. Offers you will and will not run.
9. Wholesale, consignment or event accounts and their terms.

## Days 1 to 14: economics and instrumentation

- SKU sheet with unit cost, replacement cost, MSRP, wholesale, contribution per channel, `wholesale_eligible`, max CAC ([economics-and-pricing.md](economics-and-pricing.md)).
- Reprice triggers for gold and silver SKUs.
- Etsy Offsite Ads orders split out; payment, fulfillment and return reserve per order.
- Purchase tracking with product IDs verified on the site.

Exit: every active SKU has current cost, replacement cost, contribution by channel and max CAC; channel profit can be computed per order.

## Days 1 to 21: product data and compliance

- Structured fields on every SKU: metal and fineness, plating construction, stone and origin, lab-grown status, treatment, dimensions, care, lead time, return eligibility.
- Fix titles: karat before "gold", "laboratory-grown diamond" before "diamond", treatments in marketplace titles.
- UK, EU and Canada checks if shipping there.

Exit: no SKU can publish without the required fields; lab-grown origin appears in the title.

## Days 8 to 30: photography

- Reshoot the top 20% of SKUs by traffic or gross profit first using the shot list ([product-pages-and-photography.md](product-pages-and-photography.md)).
- 8 to 12 s video per hero SKU; making-of clip with the shop name in frame.
- Turn the top 10 pre-sale questions and return reasons into images.

Exit: every hero SKU answers scale, thickness, clasp, materials and gift packaging visually.

## Days 15 to 35: PDP, feed and marketplace reset

- Factual first screen, personalization preview text, returns line by Add to cart.
- Merchant Center feed clean; Product and Merchant Listing markup.
- Etsy: 3 to 5 query clusters per hero listing, all 13 tags, correct attributes, replace weak hero photos, stop routine renewals, prune listings with views and no sales.
- About section states who makes the pieces.

Exit: Merchant Center diagnostics clean; Etsy listings with impressions and no clicks are in a creative queue, not an ad budget.

## Days 22 to 45: organic creative lab

- 20 to 30 short concepts across six angles: scale, styling, construction, personalization, gifting, maker process.
- Instagram Trial Reels for testing; TikTok with product proof in the first 3 seconds.
- One gift-story post in the "price contrast" format.

Exit: at least five concepts beat the account baseline on non-follower reach, retention, saves or qualified site sessions.

## Days 30 to 50: flows and post-purchase

- Welcome, browse and cart abandonment, post-purchase care, matching-piece cross-sell, review request (product-specific), birthday or anniversary date capture (`email-sms-loyalty` for build).
- Insert card inside the tissue; care card; brand-colored mailer.

Exit: flows are live and measured by placed-order rate; reference 1.77% for jewelry flows (Klaviyo 2026).

## Days 35 to 60: creator test

- One hero product. 20 to 30 tightly matched nano and micro creators. Separate no-obligation gifting from 5 to 10 paid deliverables. Raw files and paid usage priced separately.

Exit: known cost per delivered asset, cost per 1,000 views, assisted orders, and how creator assets perform as ads; fees compared with the $400 micro median (Kolect 2026).

## Days 40 to 65: paid acquisition

- Only on SKUs whose economics allow acquisition. Several genuinely different creatives. Product sets by margin, AOV and gift use. Catalog retargeting at most about 10% of budget. Google Shopping after feed hygiene.

Exit: budget rises only when marginal contribution and new-customer CAC stay inside the limits.

## Days 45 to 70: next gifting event

- Pick the next peak by lead time; gift edits by recipient, occasion and price; bundles; shipping and personalization cutoffs; gift card fallback; creator seeding dates ([channels-and-creators.md](channels-and-creators.md)).

Exit: dated plan with merchandising, creator, paid ramp and cutoff dates.

## Days 50 to 75: wholesale pilot (if any SKUs are eligible)

- Six to twelve-SKU buyer edit and line sheet; 30 highly matched shops; pitch from [scripts.md](scripts.md); prepaid first orders; consignment only on written terms.

Exit: 10 qualified buyer conversations or enough rejections to name the pricing or assortment problem; reorder timer set on every opening order.

## Days 55 to 80: permanent jewelry pilot (if applicable)

- One partner-venue event and one private group, both with minimums. Log booked, show rate, walk-ins, gross and contribution per hour, attach rates, contacts.

Exit: the better format per labor hour is known.

## Days 60 to 85: custom and bridal funnel (if applicable)

- Intake form, budget tiers, deposit rule, design fee, insured shipping. Bridal education content and the three-option follow-up.

Exit: leads are segmented by budget, timing, stone preference; every quote is written with validity date and deposit terms.

## Days 75 to 90: prune and scale

- Rank SKUs and channels by contribution, repeat and operating burden. Write scale, maintain or stop for each channel. Feed winning hooks into PDP images, Etsy thumbnails and creator briefs.

Exit: a written decision per channel and a next-90-day budget built from unit economics.

## Weekly dashboard

Net sales, gross profit, contribution, new-customer CAC, blended CAC, AOV, CVR, add-to-cart, checkout completion, return rate, flow revenue, creator cost per delivered asset, wholesale reorder rate, event contribution per labor hour, gold and silver price vs last reprice.
