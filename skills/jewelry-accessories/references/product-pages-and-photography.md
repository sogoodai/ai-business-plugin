# Product pages, photography and listings

Jewelry is a merchandising and evidence problem before it is a traffic problem. The category converts at about 0.63% (Shopify citing Dynamic Yield, June 2026) because shoppers cannot judge scale, thickness, sparkle, clasp or finish through a screen. Every photo and every line of copy should answer one of those doubts. General PDP structure, popups and checkout live in `website-conversion` and `ecommerce-brand`; this file keeps the jewelry and accessory specifics.

## The shot list per SKU

Score each asset against a specific doubt (scale, sparkle, thickness, flexibility, clasp, capacity, color, stacking), not against a photo count.

| Slot | Shot | Doubt it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clean hero, single finished piece, well lit, about 2,000 px+ for Etsy | What is it |
| 2 | On body at true scale: ring on an adult hand, earring on an ear, necklace on a neckline with the neck size stated, bag carried on a body | How big |
| 3 | Second on-body angle or styled stack | How it wears |
| 4 | Side or profile view (ring band thickness, pendant depth) | How thick |
| 5 | Macro of stones, texture, setting | Quality |
| 6 | Back, clasp, post, bail, hinge, strap attachment | Will it hold |
| 7 | Dimension diagram with mm and inches | Exact size |
| 8 | Materials frame: "Sterling silver, Czech glass beads, rhinestones" written on the image | What it is made of |
| 9 | Packaging and gift view | Is it giftable |
| Video | 8 to 12 s: front, profile, movement in natural light, clasp, then on body | All of the above |

Bags: interior and capacity views with everyday items inside (Etsy seller handbook). Watches and watch accessories: attachment, removal and fit close-up instead of the clasp shot.

Evidence:

- Baymard's jewelry and watch research (470+ guidelines, 2026) and its 2026 product-page review single out jewelry, watches and bags as products that need human context to judge scale.
- A Dutch jewelry retailer reported +9.7% conversion across about 150 products from virtual on-model imagery (Bright River case study; vendor, test design undisclosed).
- Shoppers say they will not buy without on-person photos; real rings on real hands beat polished light-box shots, and ring side views matter (r/Etsy, 158 pts; 22-pt reinforcement).
- Scale photo: bracelet on a wrist or a quarter beside earrings, at least one per listing (r/EtsySellers, 276 pts).
- Materials named in an image because buyers skip descriptions (r/EtsySellers, 15 pts on a 566-pt "does my jewelry look cheap" thread, after two buyers were disappointed by size and perceived cheapness on a $78 necklace).
- Estate and vintage: a photo of the piece on a gram scale plus measurements; sellers who skip it get "bombarded" with questions (r/jewelers, 76 pts).

**Tricks most owners miss**

- Shoot one ruler-calibrated master image, then derive a consistent scale diagram for every variant.
- Use pre-sale questions and return reasons as the photo backlog: if buyers ask "how big?" or "does it sit flat?", that answer belongs in image 2 or 3.
- An off-spec batch can still sell if photographed honestly. When 35% of a container arrived in the wrong color and finish, the brand photographed the real inventory and it sold out within weeks with a few complaints (r/Entrepreneur, 2,581 pts).
- Design details read as price signals. A boutique seller advises replacing exposed chain with beadwork on bead-woven pieces because chain reads as costume jewelry; smaller, rounder wire loops with three wraps look finished (r/EtsySellers, 4 to 37 pts on a 566-pt thread).

## First-screen copy

Facts first, story second. Template:

> **14k solid gold • 6.5 mm pendant • 16 to 18 in adjustable chain • natural sapphire, color varies slightly by stone • shown on a 15 in neck.** Ships in 2 business days. Gift box included.

Required fields on every SKU (store them as structured data, not prose):

- Metal and fineness (14k solid, sterling 925, gold-filled, vermeil, plated and over what base, stainless steel).
- Finish and coating, and how long it lasts with wear.
- Stone name, natural or laboratory-grown, treatment, origin if known.
- Dimensions in mm and inches; chain length and clasp type; ring sizes and resizing policy.
- Weight for gold and estate pieces.
- Care, lead time, return eligibility (personalized or not), country of manufacture.

Answer styling, occasion and care on the product page itself, not in separate blog posts. One jewelry store rewrote PDPs with "how to style this piece", "what occasions is this for" and "care instructions" and moved from position 10 to 5 in six weeks alongside other changes (r/DigitalMarketing, 268 pts; not isolated).

Personalization fields: surface the irreversible decision before Add to cart:

> **Personalization:** Enter exactly the characters you want engraved. We copy capitalization and punctuation as entered. Preview carefully before ordering.

Gemstone meaning copy: a compact paragraph with birth month, symbolism and origin, with beliefs clearly attributed: "for those who believe, turquoise..." (r/jewelrymaking, 7 pts; the seller could not tell whether it helped). Specimen-specific notes sell better than metaphysics: "most of the labradorite I come across has a yellow or blue flash, this one's special because it has an orange/pink/purple flash" (r/jewelrymaking, 19 pts).

## Provenance and the About section

Buyers burned by resellers check the About section before buying and skip shops without one. State whether you make the pieces, source vintage, or design and outsource production (r/Etsy, 944-pt thread; one shopper spending $200 to $1,000 a month on Etsy). If production is outsourced (casting house, made-to-order factory), say so plainly in the listing; one made-to-order precious-metal seller discloses it in every listing and reports positive reviews (r/printondemand, 27 pts).

## Etsy listing craft for jewelry

Full Etsy mechanics (search, ads, Star Seller, fees) live in `ecommerce-brand` (etsy.md). Jewelry specifics:

- **Order of work:** query cluster, then category and attributes, first image, price and shipping, title and tags, conversion proof, and only then ads. Etsy says engagement and conversion feed ranking and that renewing listings for the new-listing boost is not an effective strategy (Etsy Seller Handbook, 2025).
- **Tags:** use all 13 as multi-word phrases. "gold charm bracelet" covers "gold bracelet" and "charm bracelet"; spend repeated words only on exact phrases you want (r/EtsySellers, 276 pts).
- **Title separators:** put spaces around dashes: "Gold Charm Bracelet - Gift for Her - Emerald Stone" (thread claim, not verified current behavior).
- **Recipient beats aesthetic.** A plain alphabet bracelet seller with 9,000+ sales "hardcore" targets gifts for girls aged 6 to 12 (r/EtsySellers, 14 pts).
- **Find the subniche by testing:** 5 subniches x 5 listings; two listings in one niche sold enough to pick it; that niche became 90% of sales from 20% of listings; then add matching pieces (earrings to the necklace) (r/EtsySellers, 235 pts).
- **Prune:** purge old listings with views and no sales; turn off ads on listings that eat budget without orders (r/EtsySellers, 311 pts).
- **Do not use dummy cheap variations.** A $20 complex bracelet "from $1.50" through an always-sold-out simple option drives shoppers away (r/Etsy, 162 and 58 pts).
- **Reject Etsy's AI title suggestions that add an artist name.** A seller accepted "Basquiat-Style" for original graffiti wearables and got 3 policy violations, a legal letter and every listing removed (r/EtsySellers, 345 pts).
- **Mine favoriters:** open the profiles of people who favorite your items and look at what else they favorite for design ideas (r/EtsySellers, 75 pts from a 10,000-sale seller; an 8,000-sale seller called it "genius").
- **Etsy Ads:** advertise a subset (about "$1 per 3 listings"), judge after 30 days; when impressions come but clicks do not, fix thumbnail, title and price instead of raising budget (Etsy Seller Handbook; r/EtsySellers).

## Copies, Temu and stolen photos

Jewelry is among the most-copied categories on AliExpress and Temu (r/EtsySellers). The defense is evidence the copy cannot fake:

1. **Making-of video with the shop name physically in frame** (a handwritten sign on the bench). A watermark can be cropped; a sign in the scene cannot (r/EtsySellers, 231 pts for process video, 9 pts for the sign).
2. **Searchable signature watermark** on images: a buyer traced a stolen design back to the original Etsy artist through the watermark and bought from her (r/EtsySellers, 12 pts).
3. **Side-by-side comparison image:** buy one knockoff, photograph it next to yours, label both (r/EtsySellers, 8 pts; proposed).
4. **Review accusing you of reselling:** report it to Etsy before replying, because a public reply locks the review (thread claim, 663 pts, plus 255 and 203). Privately send process photos and ask the buyer to amend it. Reply script in [scripts.md](scripts.md).
5. **Do not answer a cheaper-copy report with 20% off.** A seller tried; the shopper bought the Temu copy anyway (r/EtsySellers, 399 pts).
6. **Turn copying into a portfolio** for design work: screenshots of copied designs and their sales prove you design things buyers want (r/EtsySellers, 10 pts; proposed).

Keep process and equipment details out of customer chats. A custom-decal seller told a big repeat customer which cutter they used and the spending stopped (r/EtsySellers, 30 pts).

## AI shopping and Google

- Google needs no special schema for AI Overviews or AI Mode; clean Product and Merchant Listing markup plus a current Merchant Center feed is the route (Google Search Central, 2026). Google added Merchant Center attributes and Universal Commerce Protocol in January 2026 for conversational shopping.
- Poetic copy ("an heirloom for your story") never replaces attributes: "14k solid yellow gold", "7 mm", "laboratory-grown diamond", "fits Apple Watch 45 mm".
- Build subcategory collection pages by intent. A jewelry store started SEO with lower-difficulty engagement-ring subcategories, one page each, lowest difficulty first (r/DigitalMarketing, 154 pts). Depth: `seo-content`.

## Checkout and contact

- **Callback button instead of a phone number** (call, SMS or WhatsApp): $420 extra sales in the first week, about $50 per request; most chose a call; log the page URL with each request (r/Entrepreneur, 1,120 pts; not jewelry, high-ticket fit).
- **Shipping cost visible on the product page** with only a ZIP code, remembered across pages (r/smallbusiness, 1,072 pts; buyer account).
- **Returns line next to Add to cart:** "Free 30-day returns" or "Made to order: final sale except for defects" (harvested MIT skill).
