# Channels, creators and the gifting calendar

Platform depth lives in sibling skills: `organic-social` (content systems), `paid-ads` (account structure, bidding, measurement), `email-sms-loyalty` (flows, consent, deliverability), `ecommerce-brand` (Etsy, TikTok Shop and Shopify mechanics, creators-ugc.md). This file keeps what changes for jewelry and accessories.

## Channel fit by business

| Business | First channel | Second | Usually a trap early |
|---|---|---|---|
| Handmade silver or beaded | Etsy search, local markets | Instagram Reels, wholesale to a few shops | Cold Meta ads below about $60 AOV |
| Demi-fine DTC ($40 to $200) | Creators plus Meta and TikTok | Email flows, TikTok Shop affiliates | Wholesale before margins support keystone |
| Fine and custom ($500+) | Instagram portfolio, Google, referrals | Appointments, bridal content 6+ months ahead | Etsy for $1,000+ pieces without insurance and signature |
| Permanent jewelry | Host venues, private parties, Instagram and TikTok local | Google Business Profile for a studio | Public events with no minimum |
| Bags, watch straps, hair accessories | Creators showing use, TikTok Shop, Etsy | Pinterest, Amazon if search demand | Follower-count influencer pricing |

## Instagram

- 75% of US Instagram recommendations came from original posts by Q4 2025, up 10 points (Meta, January 2026). Reposted or aggregated content loses distribution.
- **Trial Reels** go to non-followers first. In Meta's analysis of 400,000+ creators, 40% posted Reels more often after using them and 80% of those raised non-follower reach (Meta, June 2025; internal before-and-after, not randomized). Procedure: publish 3 to 5 trial hooks per hero SKU, read results after about 24 hours, promote the best retention, share and save concepts to normal Reels and to ads.
- Jewelry hooks that put evidence in the first frames (synthesized, not platform copy):
  - "This is what a 6 mm pendant actually looks like on a neck."
  - "Gold-filled vs plated after daily wear: here is the construction difference."
  - "Three ways this chain sits with a crew neck."
  - "The ring looks delicate, but here is the band thickness from the side."
  - "I made this from her grandmother's handwriting."
- **Gift story with a price contrast** is the highest-scoring jewelry post in the corpus: "Couldn't afford the $15,000 bracelet wife wanted so I made it myself" (11,358 pts, r/jewelrymaking). Comments asked for the Instagram and a matching set; no sales reported. Use the format: a person, a reason, a retail anchor, your piece.
- **Single sensory benefit positioning** for accessories: a sunglasses brand markets only its lens tint, "makes everything look happier!" (r/Entrepreneur, 133 pts).

## TikTok and TikTok Shop

- TikTok creative guidance (2025): 9:16, at least 720p, proposition in the first 3 seconds, hook within 6 seconds, captions on, native feel.
- **Check the country first.** TikTok Shop launched in France, Germany and Italy on 31 March 2025 after Spain and Ireland in late 2024; it reported 100,000+ European businesses by 2026. Canada and Australia availability differs (TikTok Newsroom; TikTok Shop seller university).
- US referral fee: several precious-jewelry subcategories 5%, many other categories 6% (fee schedule, 8 October 2026). Jewelry categories can require qualification documents.
- Model affiliate commission as CAC, not "free organic". Affiliate pays only on a sale, which suits small brands with no budget for fixed fees.
- Performance tricks from accessories: test opening visuals separately; one ad had 3x the three-second view rate because of an odd-looking elbow in the first frame (r/Entrepreneur, 27 pts). Cut from a creator's spoken experience to footage of the exact function being described (same thread).

## Pinterest and SEO

- One ecommerce owner reports about 6 million organic Pinterest impressions a month, nearly all routed through blog posts that link to products (r/Entrepreneur, 39 pts on a 2,581-pt thread; category undisclosed). For jewelry, pin styled on-body images to gift-guide and "how to layer necklaces" posts.
- SEO depth: `seo-content`. Jewelry-specific: build one collection page per buyer intent (lab-grown oval engagement rings, men's signet rings, birthstone necklaces for mom) and start with the lowest-difficulty subcategory.
- **Do not buy links.** A link seller reports agencies asking for paid links to be removed after Google detected them (r/Entrepreneur, 31 pts). A $6M a year accessories store got 99% of its traffic from Google; a manual link penalty a week before Black Friday cut traffic 99% overnight, mid-morning sales fell from about $6,000 to $150, 50+ staff were laid off and the penalty took six months to lift (r/Entrepreneur, 1,532 pts). Build a second channel before scaling on one.

## Paid ads for jewelry

Account structure and bidding: `paid-ads`. Jewelry rules:

1. **Run the economics first** ([economics-and-pricing.md](economics-and-pricing.md) section 5). Scale on contribution after returns, never on platform ROAS. Apparel and accessories median ROAS 2.70 and Meta CPA $36.98 (Triple Whale, Aug 2025 to Jul 2026) tell nothing about your metal cost or return rate.
2. **Broad or Advantage+ once the catalog and creative are strong.** One jewelry advertiser ran interests at $60/day vs Advantage+ at $60/day plus $30/day retargeting for a couple of weeks; Advantage+ won and the account moved from the agency's 0.77x to a 3.03x week in under eight weeks without deep discounts (r/FacebookAds, 83 pts; not isolated).
3. **Cap catalog retargeting at about 10% of daily budget** and use it only for larger catalogs (r/FacebookAds, 329 pts).
4. **Creative matrix = angle x proof x format.** Angles: gift, self-purchase, craftsmanship, scale and fit, personalization, durability, story, stackability, scarcity, value. Proof: macro construction, on-body demo, customer review, maker process, wear test, comparison, packaging, certificate. Formats: founder to camera, hand demo, creator UGC, macro loop, slideshow, catalog.
5. **Kill on the bottleneck metric.** Weak thumb-stop means the hook failed. Good engagement with weak PDP sessions means the offer or CTA failed. Sessions without add-to-cart means merchandising, price or trust failed. Add-to-cart without purchase means checkout, shipping or returns failed. Never call every weak ad "an audience problem".
6. **Mine reviews of comparable products for copy.** Paste 20 to 50 detailed Amazon reviews into an LLM, build funnel-stage profiles, then rewrite by hand. The jewelry example surfaced married women who already own an engagement ring and want affordable, non-tarnishing everyday pieces (r/FacebookAds, 5 pts).
7. **Test concepts as images, produce winners as video** when creative capacity is thin (r/FacebookAds, 16 pts).
8. **Google Shopping and AI Max for Shopping** (launched April 2026) only after the Merchant Center feed is clean.
9. **Platform claims are vendor evidence.** Meta's "22% ROAS improvement" from its AI tools is self-sponsored.

## Creators and seeding

- **Two pipelines, never mixed.** Gifting with "no posting obligation" is for relationship discovery; a contract is for deliverables. 90.5% of marketers say ghosting is worse in gifting (Modash, 74 marketers, 2026).
- **Gift first, pay the ones who decline.** A photography backpack brand cold-emailed and DMed nano and micro creators, gifted to anyone who would post only if they liked it, and paid those who wanted a fee. It reports 7x attributed revenue over gifted COGS plus fees and scaled ads from a $100/day ceiling to $500/day at 3.5x by recutting the licensed footage into 50 to 100 variants (r/Entrepreneur, 2,729 pts; commenters questioned profit, so track contribution, not that ratio).
- **Brief for use and honesty:** "Just make stuff your audience will watch & enjoy." The creator took the bag on a trip and named one con; the owner fixed it (same thread).
- **Price four things separately:** the post, raw files, organic usage term, paid usage or whitelisting. Kolect 2026: $500 median agreed fee, $400 for 10K to 100K followers, Instagram median ask $1,120 vs agreed $450. Price on the creator's last dozen actual views, not followers.
- **Seed early for gift windows:** packages out 5 to 7 weeks before the peak (see calendar).
- **Component and maker partnerships:** a maker sells handmade earring drops to another seller who adds beads and hooks and credits and tags her on every pair (r/EtsySellers, 15 pts). A meteorite jeweler made thousands of pieces for a science brand's webshop (r/jewelers, 208 pts). Look for audiences that already love your material or motif.
- **Recipient-generated content goes viral more than brand posts.** A gift product reached Imgur's front page through the recipient's own post: about 500,000 views, about 50 orders that day, $30,000+ in sales after blogs picked it up (r/Entrepreneur, 1,067 pts). Make the unboxing and the gift card note worth photographing.

## Email and SMS for jewelry

Flows before campaigns: jewelry flows place orders at 1.77% vs campaigns at 1.02% (Klaviyo, 2026). Jewelry-specific flows on top of the standard set in `email-sms-loyalty`:

- **Matching piece cross-sell** after the first order (earrings to the necklace, a second stacking ring): matching pieces were part of the winning Etsy subniche expansion above.
- **Care and tarnish flow** at day 14 and day 60: care steps, polishing cloth, plating refresh or re-dip offer.
- **Date capture for gifting:** ask "who was this for and when is their birthday or anniversary?" at checkout or in the thank-you email, then remind 3 weeks before.
- **Birthstone and month flows** for birthstone shoppers.
- **Bridal nurture** over months (see [custom-and-bridal.md](custom-and-bridal.md)).
- **Permanent jewelry:** capture phone and email at booking, not after welding; add-a-charm and re-weld reminders.
- Repeat is modest: 16.9% 365-day repeat for jewelry, 12.9% for bags (By the Numbers, 2025 to 2026). Do not fund first-order losses with hoped-for repeat.

## Post-purchase and packaging

- **Business card inside the tissue, not loose:** one seller switched to bright white mailers at no extra cost, purple tissue with a logo sticker and the card inside the bundle, and saw a marked rise in reviews (r/EtsySellers, 311 pts; no count).
- **Brand-colored outer mailer** so a misdelivered parcel is easy to find (r/EtsySellers, 380 pts).
- **Time the packaging against AOV:** 15 minutes of twine and wax seal on a $20 average order is a cost, not a brand (same thread).
- **Small, earned extras:** a seller added free matching studs when a customer said the earrings were for her daughter's wedding; the customer then ordered bridesmaid earrings (r/Etsy, 784 pts; causation not shown).
- **Second identical item post-purchase** for gifts and couples: 6% take on "So your travel partner doesn't feel left out" (r/Entrepreneur, 2,729 pts; backpacks). Jewelry version: "Add a matching one for your best friend."
- **Drop non-brand inserts.** Religious conversion cards in unrelated orders cost one shop a repeat buyer and a planned 3-star review (r/Etsy, 1,365 pts; 504-pt agreement).
- **Care kit instead of a discount** for negotiators (script in [scripts.md](scripts.md)).

## The gifting calendar

Build every event backward from the peak, using the owner's real lead times:

| Weeks before peak | Action |
|---|---|
| 8 to 6 | Merchandising done: gift edits by recipient, occasion and price ("Mother's Day necklaces under $150", "gifts for daughter", "personalized gifts that ship by [date]"); bundles and gift boxes priced |
| 7 to 5 | Creator seed packages shipped |
| 6 to 4 | Gift landing pages and Etsy listings live; Etsy promotion moves to the seasonal SKU, not just new wording |
| 4 to 3 | Paid creative ramps |
| last 7 to 10 days | Shipping-deadline ads and emails; personalization cutoff earlier than stock cutoff |
| after cutoff | Gift card, e-gift or "printable gift certificate with a photo of the piece" fallback |

Demand anchors: US Valentine's jewelry spend $7.0B (NRF, January 2026); Mother's Day jewelry $7.5B, 33% shopping online and 29% at specialty stores (NRF, April 2026). On Etsy, Mother's Day searches climb from about April 7 and peak the week of April 21 at 460,000+ (Etsy, 2026). Add graduation (May to June), wedding season (bridesmaid and groomsmen gifts), back to school for hair accessories, and Q4.

Inventory: a gift product went from about 5 to 100+ Amazon orders a day after one viral repost in late November and nearly ran out before Christmas (r/Entrepreneur, 2,483 pts). Hold a contingency plan for chain, findings and packaging before Q4.
