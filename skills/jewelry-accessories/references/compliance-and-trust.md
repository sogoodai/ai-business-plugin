# Compliance and trust

In jewelry, the claims are the conversion copy. Wrong metal or stone language costs refunds, marketplace removals and regulator letters; precise language raises trust. This is marketing guidance, not legal advice: for unusual constructions, mixed metals or cross-border sales, the owner should confirm with an assay office or counsel. Several rules below predate 2024 and are flagged; the legal frameworks are still in force.

## Store claims as structured fields

Metal, fineness, finish or plating construction, stone name, natural or laboratory-grown, treatment, dimensions, country compliance, return eligibility. One field set feeds Etsy, Google Merchant Center, TikTok Shop, line sheets and PDPs, so claims never drift between channels.

## US: FTC Jewelry Guides (16 CFR Part 23; revised 2018, flagged as pre-2024)

Metal terms:

| Term | Requirement | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Gold below 24k | Say the karat immediately before "gold", equally conspicuous ("14k gold") | 23.3(b)(2) |
| Gold Filled | Plating of at least 10k, at least 1/20 of the weight of all metal in the item | 23.3(c)(2) |
| Gold plate / Rolled Gold Plate | At least 10k, at least 1/40 of metal weight; rolled gold plate is shown with the fraction ("1/40th 12 Kt. Rolled Gold Plate") | 23.3(c)(2) |
| Gold electroplate | At least 10k, minimum 0.175 microns of fine gold | 23.3(c)(3) |
| Heavy gold electroplate | At least 10k, minimum 2.5 microns | 23.3(c)(3) |
| Vermeil | Sterling silver base, gold of at least 10k, minimum 2.5 microns | 23.4(b) |
| "Solid" gold | Not for hollow items | 23.3(c)(1) note |
| Karat tolerance | 3 parts per thousand without solder, 7 with solder (National Stamping Act) | 23.3(d) |
| Platinum | "Platinum" unqualified needs 950 ppt; 850 to 950 may be marked with the number ("900Pt."); below 500 ppt never "platinum" | 23.6 |

Agent rules:

- "18k gold plated brass" is legal wording; "18k gold" for a plated piece is not.
- "Gold-filled" and "vermeil" are durability claims: describe how they wear compared with flash plating, which is a strong ad angle ("Gold-filled vs plated after daily wear").
- Sterling: use "925 sterling silver" only for 92.5% silver. Confirm silver terms in 16 CFR 23.5 before writing "coin silver" or "silver plated" claims.

Stones:

- **Laboratory-grown diamonds:** disclose immediately before the word "diamond", equally conspicuous: "laboratory-grown", "laboratory-created" or "[manufacturer]-created" (FTC, "In the Loupe", 2019). FTC warning letters in 2019 stressed that disclosure must sit close to the product claim. Title pattern:
  - Right: **1.0 ct Laboratory-Grown Diamond Solitaire Ring, 14k Gold**
  - Wrong: **1.0 ct Diamond Solitaire Ring*** with "lab-grown" only in the description.
- **Treatments:** disclose treatments that affect value or care, in the title for marketplace listings (see the returned treated diamond in [custom-and-bridal.md](custom-and-bridal.md)).
- **Simulants:** name the material ("cubic zirconia", "moissanite"), never "diamond-like" without the material name.

Reviews and endorsements: never condition an incentive on a positive review; disclose any incentive; creators disclose gifted and paid posts. Buying reviews fails: one commenter's 20 purchased Google reviews all disappeared within about seven weeks and rankings ended lower than before (r/smallbusiness, 70 pts on a 10,236-pt thread).

## UK: hallmarking (Hallmarking Act; law predates 2024)

Items described as gold, silver, platinum or palladium need a UK hallmark above these weights: 1 g gold, 7.78 g silver, 0.5 g platinum, 1 g palladium (GOV.UK guidance, current). Online sellers must display the Dealer's Notice information where applicable. A US maker shipping a 10 g sterling cuff to UK consumers must check whether the description triggers hallmarking.

## Canada

Applying a precious-metal quality mark is not mandatory in every case, but any mark used must comply with the Precious Metals Marking Act and Regulations and be accompanied by a registered trademark where required (Competition Bureau Canada, 2025). Do not copy US "14k" stamping and listing practice without checking.

## EU

- **GPSR** (General Product Safety Regulation) applies to online and distance sales since 13 December 2024: an EU responsible person where required, product identification, safety information and risk analysis. A US or UK maker selling directly into the EU needs this workflow, not just EU shipping rates.
- **REACH Annex XVII:** lead in any individual jewelry part at or above 0.05% by weight is prohibited, with exemptions (ECHA). Nickel release limits: 0.2 µg/cm²/week for post assemblies in pierced ears and piercings; 0.5 µg/cm²/week for items in prolonged skin contact (earrings, necklaces, bracelets, rings) (REACH entry 27, via compliance guides; confirm in EUR-Lex). Cadmium at most 0.01% by weight in metal jewelry parts (entry 23, via compliance guides). Ask suppliers for test reports on plated base-metal pieces before selling into the EU.
- **Withdrawal right:** 14 days for distance sales, with the personalized-goods exception ([custom-and-bridal.md](custom-and-bridal.md)).

## Australia

Gemstone advertising guidance retrieved predates 2024 and may be outdated; consumer guarantees under the ACCC framework still apply. Use local review for origin terminology rather than copying FTC phrasing.

## Marketplace and platform traps

- **Artist and brand names in titles or tags** (Etsy's AI suggested "Basquiat-Style"; all listings removed, r/EtsySellers, 345 pts). Audit tags, not just titles.
- **Amazon title wording** can remove a listing in one marketplace for months: an accessories brand's EU listing was pulled over title wording, cases took 3 to almost 4 months, and weekly profit fell from $5,700 to $3,800 (r/Entrepreneur, 19 pts on a 2,408-pt thread).
- **Jewelry category gating** exists on TikTok Shop US and Amazon: expect document requests before launch.
- **AI-generated product images:** Merchant Center expects the IPTC "TrainedAlgorithmicMedia" label on AI-generated images (harvested MIT skill). An AI-rendered design must be reproducible by the factory before listing; one made-to-order seller paid several factories to test reproduction first (r/printondemand, 26 pts).

## Trust signals that also sell

- About section that states who makes the pieces and how (r/Etsy, 944-pt thread).
- Hallmark and fineness close-up photo; assay or lab report for stones above the owner's threshold.
- Care card and written warranty (repairs, re-plating, re-welds).
- Making-of video with the shop name in frame.
- Specific, product-level review requests that ask about scale, finish and occasion, with an on-body photo.
