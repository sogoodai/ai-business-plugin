# 30/60/90-day plan

For a business with zero to about 10 customers, or one that launched and stalled. A business moves to the next phase when the evidence gate is met, not when the date arrives. Each week ends with a short owner report: numbers, what changed, what you need them to decide.

Before day 1, route: if a segment skill exists, run its first-customers section in parallel ([by-business-type.md](by-business-type.md)). This plan sets the order and the gates.

## The first-100 log (start on day 1)

One row per prospect and customer, kept forever. The first 100 customers are the best acquisition dataset the business will ever have (research report recommendation).

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Customer number | 7 |
| Source and first touch | Nextdoor post, 12 March |
| Trigger (why now) | Moving out, deposit at risk |
| Workaround before you | Did it themselves |
| Exact words they used | "I just need it gone before Friday" |
| Objection | Price vs a friend with a truck |
| Days from first touch to payment | 2 |
| Price paid, discount | $180, intro 20% |
| Activated or repeat | Booked again in May |
| Referred anyone | Yes, customer 11 |

Add "where did you hear about us?" to every booking or onboarding form. Give every physical channel its own phone number, QR or link: one founder's QR on postcards showed zero hits in week one, which revealed the mail had never been sent ([r/sweatystartup, 97 upvotes](https://www.reddit.com/r/sweatystartup/comments/xvq8o9/update_i_posted_before_how_started_a_tankless/)).

## Days 1 to 30: demand evidence and the first 5 to 10 customers

**Week 1: one buyer, one problem, one offer.**

1. Write one sentence: "[Specific buyer] who [trigger] pay us to [outcome] instead of [current workaround]." If you cannot, stop and interview first ([validation.md](validation.md)).
2. Ask the owner the first-session questions in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) and compute the outreach math ([manual-acquisition.md](manual-acquisition.md#time-budget-by-customer-count)).
3. Build the founding offer: cap, price, end date, what you ask in return ([offers.md](offers.md)).
4. One page or one booking link with one call to action and a way to pay or deposit. No brand project.
5. Local business: create and start verifying the Google Business Profile today (`local-seo`).
6. Set up the first-100 log and source tracking.

**Week 2: talk to 20 to 30 people.**

- Warm network: 100 individual messages ([scripts.md](scripts.md#warm-network-announcement)).
- B2B: list 50 to 100 named prospects with a trigger; send in batches of 10 to 20 a day, under 100 words.
- Local: three 2-hour door-to-door sessions in one dense area, or handwritten house-specific quotes.
- Consumer: answer questions in the 3 to 5 communities where the use case lives; DM only people who described the problem.
- Log every conversation in the five fields: trigger, workaround, pain in numbers, objection, exact words.

**Weeks 3 and 4: close and serve by hand.**

- Ask for the deposit, pilot or purchase in every warm conversation.
- Deliver the first jobs personally and fast. Fix anything that goes wrong for free, the same day.
- After the first visible result: review ask (local) or referral and case-study ask (B2B) ([word-of-mouth.md](word-of-mouth.md)).
- Write a weekly voice-of-customer note: repeated phrases, outcomes, workarounds, objections, why they bought. Rewrite the page from it.

**Gate to move on (any one):** 3+ paying customers or deposits from strangers of the same type; or 1 paid pilot plus 5+ booked sales conversations in one segment; or a smoke test above its preset kill rule plus 3+ calls describing an urgent workaround. Survey enthusiasm and signups never pass the gate.

**If the gate fails at day 30:** 20+ conversations and no payment means the offer or the buyer is wrong. Change price, packaging or segment and rerun weeks 2 to 4. Fewer than 20 conversations means not enough outreach: fix the volume first.

## Days 31 to 60: channel tournament, then concentrate

**Days 31 to 45: test 3 to 6 channels.** Score candidates in [channels.md](channels.md), write pass rules, run each test small and time-boxed. Always include warm network and one "go where they ask" channel. Typical sets:

- B2B: founder outbound on a trigger, partner or referral, high-intent search test, one niche community.
- Consumer app: community launch posts, creator seeding, a launch surface, a capped paid social test.
- Local service: reviews engine plus Google profile, neighbor referrals and density, one lead marketplace, a capped search test.
- Ecommerce: marketplace listing or hobby community, creator seeding, a boutique or market stall, a capped Meta creative test.

Score every channel on paying customers, cost per paying customer including founder hours, activation or repeat, and hours spent. Cheap leads that do not pay lose.

**Days 46 to 60: concentrate.** Put most effort into the winner and keep one secondary test. Standardize the winning offer, page, qualification questions and follow-up cadence (1 hour, then days 1, 2, 3, 4 for quotes). If outbound won, segment by the trigger with the best positive-reply rate. If local search won, automate the neutral review request at the right moment. If referrals won, find people who referred twice and give them an ambassador tier.

**Launch events belong here, not earlier:** Product Hunt, Hacker News, a big Reddit post, press, podcasts, an opening event. Only once the page converts and someone can follow up with every signup the same day ([launches-and-press.md](launches-and-press.md)).

**Gate to move on:** one channel produced at least 5 paying customers in two weeks at a cost per customer below first-order gross profit, or within one payback period you can afford.

## Days 61 to 90: proof, repeatability, then scale or iterate

**Days 61 to 75: manufacture proof.**

- 2 to 3 case studies: before, what we did, measured after, time, caveat. Use names only with written permission.
- Publish one piece of firsthand evidence (local data, a comparison table, a calculator, a customer result). Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode rely on normal indexing and reward original, non-commodity content; no special markup is needed ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)).
- Press: pitch only journalists whose recent work matches the data, one exclusive at a time ([scripts.md](scripts.md#press-pitch)).
- Local: reach 20 to 25 reviews that name specific services.

**Days 76 to 90: decide.** Classify the business in one of four states (research report framing) and tell the owner which:

| State | Evidence | Next 90 days |
|---|---|---|
| Validated and repeatable | One channel keeps producing paying customers with acceptable payback | Double down; add automation to the manual motion; paid scale via `paid-ads` |
| Validated, channel unclear | Customers pay and stay, but each came from a different place | Run a second channel tournament; read the first-100 log for patterns |
| Attention, weak payment or retention | Signups, traffic, launches, but few pay or come back | Fix offer, onboarding or product; stop acquisition spend |
| Weak demand | Few conversations convert, no pull | Change segment or problem, or stop. Do not prescribe "more content" or "more ads" |

## Weekly owner report (keep it to one screen)

- Conversations started, conversations held, paying customers this week and total.
- By channel: hours, spend, paying customers, cost per paying customer.
- Top 3 phrases customers used this week; top objection.
- Reviews, referrals and case-study permissions collected.
- One decision needed from the owner (approve a message, a price, an offer, a spend).

## What the agent asks the owner during the plan

- Week 1: approve the one-sentence target, the founding offer and price floor, and the outreach list.
- Before any message goes out: approve the exact text and recipients.
- Before day 31: which channels are off-limits (brand, time, budget, comfort with door-to-door or calls).
- Before any spend: a hard cap per test.
- Day 60: which case-study customers may be named.
- Day 90: which state they agree the business is in.
