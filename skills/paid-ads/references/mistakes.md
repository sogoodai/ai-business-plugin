# Expensive mistakes

Two tables: what platform data and arithmetic show, and what practitioners report losing (self-reported, one account each unless noted).

## From the data and platform rules

| Mistake | What it costs | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Optimizing to cheap leads | $20 CPL at 2% close = $1,000 CAC vs $60 CPL at 15% = $400 (arithmetic); med spa $12.18 CPL became $251 per booked appointment (MDConsultingNY 2026) | Optimize and report on qualified, booked or won; import outcomes |
| Fragmenting a small budget | Four $25/day ad sets at a $50 CPA each get about 3 conversions a week, far from Meta's about 50/week per ad set | One campaign, one ad set until the floor is met |
| Launching TikTok Smart+ below 10x CPA a day | $50/day at a $30 CPA is 6x under TikTok's own minimum; learning never completes | Wait for budget or use Meta and organic TikTok |
| A $100 Pinterest A/B test | Pinterest recommends about $825 to $18,000 per test version | Big-swing tests, or none |
| Judging Search on a $25/day budget across broad themes | $5.42 average CPC buys 4 to 5 clicks a day (WordStream 2026) | 5 to 15 exact keywords on the highest-intent theme |
| Comparing traffic-objective CPC with lead CPA | Meta traffic CPC $0.70 vs lead CPC $1.92 and CPL $27.66 (WordStream 2025) | Compare inside the same objective and conversion definition |
| Celebrating PMax ROAS with brand inside | Plumbing PMax: $11 CPL on 9 leads, only 1 non-brand, vs $35 historical | Brand exclusions; compare total account conversions |
| Trusting Ad Strength | "Excellent" RSAs: $28.68 CPA vs $12.43 for "Average" (Optmyzr, about 20,000 accounts) | Judge on CPA and lead quality; ignore the badge |
| Full RSA pinning | $32.57 CPA vs $13.68 with 1 to 3 pins (Optmyzr) | Pin only what must stay fixed |
| Setting a cost cap or tCPA below the clearing price | Meta: $9 cap on a $9 to 11 CPA gave $45 to 50 CPA on $700 (operators); platform docs warn low caps block learning | Highest volume until CPA is stable, caps at or above actual |
| Daily edits to automated campaigns | Each significant edit restarts exploration (TikTok 7 days, Pinterest about 2 weeks) | Edit windows: Meta weekly, Google 2 major changes a month |
| Adding up platform conversions | Meta 100 + Google 80 when the store had 120 | Business data for totals |
| Long forms with no qualification logic | 1 field 18.2% vs 5 fields 8.1% CVR (NP Digital 2024); only 51.7% of starters finish (Zuko) | Multistep form, 1 to 3 qualifiers, measure qualified per visitor |
| Slow mobile pages | Over 4 s: 22% lower CVR (Yottaa 2025) | Under 3 s; a separate lightweight landing page |
| Unanswered calls | Only 52% of home service calls reach a person (Invoca 2026) | 90%+ answer rate before more spend |
| Generic stock or AI imagery for local ads | Real imagery +10% CTR, location copy +17% (Nextdoor 2024) | Real crew, real street, city in the copy |

## From practitioners

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Letting Google's xWF team or a rep optimize | Spend +300%, sales +0% at $1,150/day; another: CPC $50 to $425, $10,000 in 30 hours (half refunded); rep's broad switch took cost per conversion from 3.99 to 48.99 | Written approval for every change; roll back, don't add budget to rescue |
| Leaving auto-apply on | 500 irrelevant keywords added as active; "upgrade to broad" replaced exact and phrase; a reverted bid strategy was re-applied days later | Turn off the auto-apply rule first, then revert |
| Forgetting to reset daily budgets on the 1st | $104,000 overspend, $10,000 courtesy credit | Account-level budgets, reset checklist |
| Search Partners and Display left on | $3,000 over 6 months, 2,500 clicks, 1 lead | Search network only; test partners deliberately |
| "Presence or interest" location | 10x clicks from an excluded country on B2B | Presence only |
| Broad match with no negatives | About $30,000 a year on arts-and-crafts clicks for a tech company | Exact and phrase plus starter negatives first |
| Adding negatives one by one, daily | $5 clicks on "chicken soup" for a tattoo ink seller, half the budget in hidden terms | Negative lists before launch, rule-based scripts |
| PMax for a local lead-gen business | $6,900 for 50 reported leads, 0 real, plus $2,250 fees; told "learning takes 3 months" | Search first; PMax only with offline quality |
| Accounting firm Search with informational terms | $6,000, 650 clicks, 0 leads in 8 weeks ("what is job costing", templates, PDFs) | Informational negatives; owner keeps account access |
| Display at $0.01 CPC replacing converting Search | 1-second average time on page | Display for remarketing only |
| Paying click-fraud tools before fixing setup | No improvement; Google refunded $0.54 on 1 invalid click of 2,500 | Network, geo, form and speed fixes first |
| Jumping Meta budget $100 to $250 on a winner | Sales fell from about 10/day to 1 to 2 and never recovered | +20% steps or horizontal duplication |
| Doubling budgets and duplicating into separate ad sets | Both copies got worse | One change at a time |
| Relaunching campaigns every 3 to 4 days when they plateau | Constant relearning at 10 to 15 purchases | Add new ads inside the live campaign |
| Putting 40 old winning post IDs into one ad set | Profitable for a month, then burned out | Fresh concepts plus a few proven ads |
| Selecting "Instagram only" and not checking | 43% of $600 went to Facebook and Threads, zero sales | Read the placement breakdown |
| Relaunching winners without their post IDs, wrong links | 0.2 ROAS vs the usual 1.9 on about $1,400 | Promote by post ID; QA every link |
| Instagram boosts as the ad strategy | $40 of boosts, 0 sales; Facebook audience testing gave a couple of sales a day | Conversion campaigns in Ads Manager |
| Landing page CTAs pointing to an external site | $50k+ of Google and Meta spend with no leads | Click every CTA before launch |
| Spending a week on one polished ad | It flopped; 10 quick ads with auto subtitles beat it | Low-fi first, produce only winners |
| Judging Meta lead forms nobody followed up | "Junk leads" that were never called within the hour | Speed-to-lead system, then judge |
| Optimizing soft retargeting off traffic or engagement campaigns | Low-quality audiences | Purchase or lead optimization through the funnel |
| Recreating a banned account's campaign in the same Business Manager | New account banned immediately | Fix and appeal first |
| Meta funded by a credit line | $112,311 of fraudulent spend in 30 minutes, no refund after 26 days | Prepaid top-ups, card dispute route |
| AI agent with write access | Permanent ban after a week (cause disputed); best keyword paused at 2 am | Read-only agents, human-approved diffs |
| Selling only through Facebook groups | 90% of orders gone when groups were banned; bankruptcy | Own the email and SMS list |
| Pre-creating ad accounts for franchisees | About 15 accounts disabled, no reactivation | Each franchisee creates its own and grants access |
| Scaling ChatGPT ads on 1 to 2 conversions | No signal to act on | Wait for volume like any other channel |
| Copying a famous "turn off all ads" test | A retailer restarted after 4 to 5 days each time | Plan a holdout with a metric and an end date |
| Hard-coding a $40/day SaaS budget with $25+ CPCs | Under 2 clicks a day; client left in 6 months | Refuse budgets below the learning floor |
| Raising tROAS to slow PMax spend | CPCs rose further; spend kept doubling | Control spend with the budget |
| Raising PMax tROAS from 450% to 700% to match actual | Orders -28%; budgets only 72 to 84% spent | Move 10 to 15% per step, watch volume |
