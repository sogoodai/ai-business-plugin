# Scripts: exact wording

Fill the brackets from the owner's real facts. Never invent a result, a client name or a quote. Draft only: the owner confirms text and recipient before anything is sent. "Research" marks a synthesized template from the research pass (not a tested script); thread scores mark practitioner wording.

## Warm network and referrals

**Availability post (LinkedIn, after a contract ends)**
> "I've just wrapped [project type] for [client type]. I have room for [one or two] new [specific offer] projects starting [month]. Best fit: [ICP] dealing with [trigger]. If that's you or someone you know, message me."

**Referral request (research)**
> "I'm opening two slots for [specific offer] for [ICP] this month. Best fit is a company dealing with [trigger]. We recently [specific proof] for a similar team. If someone comes to mind, would you be comfortable introducing us? I can send a two-sentence note you can forward."

**Forwardable blurb (research)**
> "[Name] helps [ICP] get [outcome] by [mechanism]. They recently [proof]. They're offering [bounded diagnostic or pilot] for teams dealing with [trigger]. Worth a short conversation if that's on your roadmap."

**Referral ask at the win** (r/copywriting, 359 pts; r/agency, 64 and 92 pts)
> "Hey, do you know anyone else who might need this kind of work?"
> "Do you know 1 to 2 people who'd benefit from what we did for you?"
> "I'm helping companies solve [problem X]. Do you know anyone currently dealing with that?"

**Past client check-in** (r/copywriting, 246 pts)
> "Hey, it was great working with you on [X]! I've noticed your updated website could be improved by adding [Y]. I'd be glad to work on that with you if it's useful."

**Former client cadence** (r/agency, 51 pts): a plain "Hey [name], saw [their news], hope it's going well" at 30, 90, 180, 365 days. Never "when are you coming back?"

**Referral partner terms (research)**
> "A referral is attributable when [partner] makes a direct introduction and the prospect was not an active opportunity in our CRM during the prior [period]. Referral compensation is [percentage or flat fee] of collected revenue from [scope or time period], paid within [period] after client payment. The receiving party owns delivery and contracting unless otherwise agreed. Neither party may represent the other without written permission."

**Partner agency opener** (r/digital_marketing, 96 pts)
> "Hey [name], I see that you run a [type] agency. Have you ever thought about offering [service] to your clients?"

## Cold outreach

**Email, trigger plus observation (research)**
> Subject: [company] + [specific problem]
> "Hi [Name], noticed [specific trigger]. For [similar client type], we [measurable result] by [mechanism]. I spotted [specific gap] in [their asset]. I can send a 90-second teardown showing the two changes I'd test first. Want it?"

**Follow-up that adds information (research)**
> "One more thing I noticed: [second observation]. If [business consequence] is on your roadmap, I can send the teardown. If not, no need to reply."

**Close the loop (research)**
> "I'll close this out so I don't keep filling your inbox. If [problem] becomes a priority later, reply 'teardown' and I'll send the notes."

**Audit email close** (r/digital_marketing, 78 pts; 2% to 18% replies)
> "...thought you'd want to see this, happy to walk through what I'd fix first if you're curious."

**Speculative redesign** (r/agency, 32 and 84 pts)
> Subject: "We redesigned one of your pages. Want to see it?"
> "We mocked up a redesigned version of one of your [product/service] pages, purely speculative, but we believe it could increase both conversions and search performance. Should we send it over?" (Only state a lift figure you can back with your own past results.)

**Three-line problem email** (r/agency, 61 pts)
> "Your booking page errors out on mobile. Anyone coming from Instagram can't book. Want me to send a 2-minute video showing the fix? Free either way."

**Outcome with a deadline** (r/copywriting, 359 pts)
> "Can I revive your dead list in the next 14 days?"

**Old job post** (r/copywriting, 359 pts)
> "Hey, did you ever find a copywriter for this project?"

**Loom permission ask (research)**
> "I spotted two things on [asset] that may be costing [specific outcome]. I recorded a 70-second walkthrough. Want me to send it?"

**LinkedIn connection note, 200 characters (research)**
> "Saw your point about [topic]. It maps closely to work we're doing with [ICP] around [problem]. No pitch, I'd like to compare notes."

**After acceptance (research)**
> "Thanks, [Name]. The thing I'm curious about from your [post, change, hiring signal]: are you already solving [problem] internally, or is it still on the backlog?"

**Editor pitch on LinkedIn** (r/freelanceWriters, 113 pts)
> "I came across [company] on LinkedIn and was wondering if you were looking for any additional freelance writers. I've written for publications like [x, x and x]. Here are a few samples of my recent work: [link]."

**Cold call opener** (r/Entrepreneur, 480 pts; 30% past 30 seconds vs 14% for a pitch)
> "Hey, this is a cold call, you can hang up, but give me 18 seconds first."

## Marketplace proposals

**Lead with what they asked** (first 148 characters show in the preview)
> "[Answer to their main question]. Fixed price $[X] for [deliverables], delivered [date]."

**Preview attached** (r/Upwork, 190 pts)
> "I reviewed your brief and already designed the first [asset] concept for your [event]. Quick preview here: [link]. I can deliver all 3 initial directions within 24 hours, built to your [platform] specs and editable for revisions."

**What I need to start** (r/Upwork, 260 pts)
> "If you say go ahead, I would need the following: 1. [login] 2. [API key] 3. [content or assets]."

**Prerequisites, honestly** (r/Upwork, 170 pts)
> "Yes I can run ads for you, but to increase the chances of success: in similar campaigns [X, Y, Z] were in place. I don't want to waste your money without these, and if you don't go with me, I think you should put them in place before running ads with someone else."

**Post-interview mini-build** (r/Upwork, 133 pts)
> "Hey, I got curious about what [feature] might look like, so I did a quick little implementation. Here's a [screenshot, video, link]."

**Close** (r/Upwork, 129 pts)
> "I'm free for the next few hours if you want to hop on a quick call."

## Discovery and closing

**Open the call** (r/Entrepreneur, 1,199 pts)
> "Should we dive right in? I'll start by asking a few questions to get a really clear picture of your business. If it sounds like I can definitely help, I'll explain how I work. Sound good?"
> Inbound: "What prompted you to book this call today?"
> Outbound: "When I reached out, something in my message must have clicked. What was it that made you decide to take this call?"
> "Have you worked with another freelancer or agency on this? What was that like?"

**Budget** (r/freelance, 172 pts)
> "I can craft a $5,000 solution or a $50,000 solution. Obviously, you get a lot more with the $50,000 one. So I need a ballpark idea of what resources you have before I can recommend an approach."

**Anchor** (r/freelanceWriters, 88 pts)
> "For a project like this, I'd normally charge a flat fee of $[X]. Is that something we could work with?"
> "Full price would be $[X], but if we dropped a couple of options we could work with your budget."

**Price on the call** (r/Entrepreneur, 1,199 pts)
> "I've done similar projects for around $[X]."

**DIY honesty** (r/Entrepreneur, 485 pts)
> "Before we talk price, do you have 5 to 10 hours over the next month? Because if you do, you should DIY it. [Free resource] covers it. You'll spend 5 to 10 hours either way, doing it yourself or explaining your business to me."

**No guarantee, a stop-loss** (r/agency, 990 pts)
> "There's no guarantees. Here's what we'll do to make sure it works as early as possible, so we can cut our losses short if it doesn't."

**Small agency as access** (r/agency, 56 pts)
> "We're pretty small: [revenue], just me, my partner and [N] people. So I'd be your daily point of contact and personally handling your strategy."

**Discovery close (research)**
> "Based on what you've said, the expensive part is [problem], because it's creating [consequence]. You want [outcome] by [time], and [decision process] is involved. I think there's a fit. I'll send a proposal built around [approach], not a generic menu. Is there anything that would prevent you from moving ahead if the scope and economics make sense?"

**"Pick your brain"** (r/Entrepreneur, 190 pts)
> "Happy to help. My consulting rate is [€150] an hour. First 15 minutes free to see if I can actually add value."

**Free sample request** (r/freelance, 146 pts; r/freelanceWriters, 121 pts)
> "I'm happy to make you a sample, but I don't make free samples. Please send over the contract and I'll make the sample."
> "Sorry, I'm not offering pro bono work at this time. My normal rate is [X], in case you decide to move forward."

**Prospect asks for a taster of your advice** (r/startups, 277 pts)
> "I can think of [X] different solutions to try out. Here's a case study from a similar client showing how we approached it."

**Price shopper** (r/freelance, 437 pts)
> "Best go with those guys, then!"

## Delivery and money

**Onboarding dependencies (research)**
> "To hit [30-day milestone], we need the following access before [date]: [systems]. Anything outstanding by then moves the milestone date one-for-one. Your first success review is on [date], where we'll compare [baseline] with [target]."

**Deposit** (r/freelanceWriters, 88 pts)
> "I work on a 50% deposit and 50% on completion basis."
> "For projects under $[50], I need payment up front before I start work."

**Scope change** (r/freelance, 67 pts)
> "Absolutely, it'll cost an extra [X hours or dollars]." (Get written approval first.)
> "That request is outside the scope of work. If you'd like to add it to the current contract, I can send you a change order with the updated fees, or we can do a follow-up project once this one is done."

**Labelled freebie** (r/freelance, 67 pts)
> "This is extra, but I'll do it for you, this time."

**MVP upsell** (r/freelance, 385 pts)
> "I could also add a feature that does [x], but it falls outside the MVP scope. Let me know if you'd like me to add it down the road."

**Scope cap clause** (r/agency, 40 pts)
> "The Client can submit up to two feature requests per month, subject to scoping agreement from the Contractor."

**Change clause** (r/freelance, 132 pts)
> "Any requests for changes must be made by the client in writing. Any change to the scope of the work after acceptance may be subject to additional charges. Should such changes negate any part of the work already completed at the time of the changes, the client accepts responsibility for payment for any work completed to date."

**Handover exclusion** (r/freelance, 112 pts): add a paragraph that later changes to brand information, prices or product data, "including appearances in ChatGPT" and other AI answers, are a new project.

**Silent client** (r/freelance, 174 pts)
> "Because I haven't heard back from you since [date], I'm assuming your priorities have changed. I can either close the project with payment for the work completed, or we can agree a new timeline."

**Bad day** (r/freelance, 118 pts)
> "Got this, I'll come back to you [Thursday]."

**Weekly update (Loom, 2 minutes, Fridays)**: what we sent this week, what the data shows, what we test next week.

**Pushback with evidence** (r/agency, 56 pts)
> "Look, it's your site, but I wouldn't be doing my job if I didn't give you my advice." Then the data.

## Price increases

**Existing client, anchored** (r/freelanceWriters, 118 pts; accepted in under an hour)
> "From [date, 60 days out], my rate for new clients is [new rate]. Because we've worked together for [time] and I value it, your rate will move from [old] to [old + 20%]. Happy to revisit together in [season]."

**Annual raise** (r/freelanceWriters, 110 pts)
> "My base rates are increasing to reflect my current experience and market value. Starting [date], my new rate will be [amount]. I'd love to discuss how we can make this work within your budget."

**Legacy tier** (r/agency, 55 pts)
> "If you're happy with the way things are, you're welcome to stay at your current tier. We also have a new tier that includes [new things]."

**Capacity framing** (r/PPC, 563 pts; only if true)
> "New clients now start at [rate] and I have a referral waitlist. I'd like to keep working with you first, at [rate] from [date]. If that doesn't work, I'm happy to refer you to someone more affordable."

**Rate ask on an offer** (r/freelanceWriters, 122 pts)
> "As for the rate, can you do $[X] per word?"

## Expansion and proof

**Next bottleneck (research)**
> "We've moved [metric] from [baseline] to [result]. The constraint has now shifted to [next bottleneck]. I see three options: leave it as is, have your team handle it, or let us run [next phase]. Want the one-page economics?"

**Testimonial at the win (research)**
> "We hit [milestone]. Would you be comfortable writing two or three sentences on what was difficult before, what changed and the result? If easier, reply with bullets and I'll draft something for your approval."

**Strategy through delivery** (r/agency, 72 pts)
> Client: "What should we do next?" You: "Let's run a workshop and help decide."

**Win back via the incumbent** (r/agency, 51 pts)
> "Let me hop on a call with your agency and maybe give them some pointers."

## Ad and post hooks for services

- "Here's $[4,000] a month this account was burning." (teardown, 76 pts)
- "A VP of Marketing costs $250k a year. Ours costs less and starts this week." (431 pts)
- "$500 site vs $15,000 site: what you actually get." (adapted from price-extreme hooks, 2,228 pts)
- Name the buying moment: "Dropping music this month and need an album cover that actually gets clicks?" (44 pts)
- Bid on pain searches: "google ads account suspended", "my google ads stopped converting", "google ads audit" (76 pts).
