# Benchmarks

Compare the business with the closest dataset and say which one you used. Three kinds of numbers live here; keep them apart when you report:

- **Measured:** platform terms, aggregate vendor datasets, agency surveys with a stated sample. Almost all agency data is self-selected (agencies that answer surveys or use a given tool). Promethean surveys are 74 to 78% US and Canadian, median 13 FTE. Treat their gaps as associations, not causes.
- **Vendor datasets:** cold email and LinkedIn tools measure their own customers, with their own definitions. Never average them together; compare the business with one dataset and keep its definition.
- **Operators report:** Reddit practitioner numbers. One person's business, real but anecdotal. Score is the thread's upvotes.

"Year" is the year the data describes; publication follows when different. Full URLs: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Known gaps

No robust current source for: discovery-call close rate, cost per agency client by channel, freelancer churn, a standard agency referral fee, community-to-pipeline conversion, a safe LinkedIn daily invite count, or a measured lift from productizing. Treat those as parameters to measure in the business.
