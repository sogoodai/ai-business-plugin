# By niche

Each section: who buys, the entry offer, price anchors, the channel that works, what to watch. Rates: Clutch and Upwork 2026 unless a thread score is given. Use the closest section and say which.

## Marketing and paid media agencies

- **Buyers:** local services, ecommerce, B2B with budget for media. Screen with BuiltWith: no Analytics, pixel or email platform can mean too early (r/PPC, 538 pts). Budget must cover the niche's cost per result.
- **Entry:** account audit with a dollar figure of waste; "Here's $4,000 a month this account was burning" as content (76 pts).
- **Price anchors:** local menu $1,250 a month for one ad channel, $2,250 ads plus SEO, website $1,500 one-off (55 pts); $1,500 to $2,000 a month plus per-lead fee (r/PPC, 284 pts); percentage of spend with lower marginal rates above thresholds, or minimum plus 10 to 15% of ad revenue (r/PPC, 563 pts). Enterprise: about $120,000 a year average client at one 8-figure agency (155 pts).
- **Channels:** cold calls and audits for local; pain-keyword search ads for the agency itself ("google ads audit") (76 pts); TikTok and LinkedIn teardowns.
- **Keep clients:** agreed cost-per-lead target including your fee; patience rules in writing; creative refresh; "paused ads = paused work"; one client per state with reused templates (53 pts); attribution tier (call tracking, CRM) as the reason for a higher price.
- **Watch:** rev-share on volatile niches (dropshipping roster collapsed in a quarter, 303 pts); the founder-sells, junior-delivers trap.

## SEO and content agencies

- **Entry:** technical recovery audit; local visibility audit (website, "service + neighborhood" ranking, AI answer presence, GBP, schema) as cold email (78 pts); AI answer audit.
- **Price anchors (MIT harvest, uncited):** GBP audit $200 to $500; local visibility $500 to $1,500; site SEO $800 to $2,500; AI visibility $750 to $2,500; single-location retainer $500 to $3,000 a month. Results take 3 to 6 months: say so up front.
- **Demand shift:** 66% of agencies see client demand for AI search work; 59% say AI search disrupts SEO (AgencyAnalytics 2026). Sell non-commodity content (data, first-hand evidence, named experts) and AI citation tracking separate from rankings (28 pts).
- **Proof to quote:** 3 to 4 finance articles a month for 18 months at $300 to $350 each took organic visits from 100 to 6,500 a month (80 pts); value framing of about $0.25 per organic visitor vs a $3.44 paid-search benchmark (80 pts).
- **Watch:** "no guarantees, here's our stop-loss" (990 pts); link buying and blind disavows; thin city pages.

## Web design and development

- **Buyers:** profitable local firms with 25 or fewer staff and 2 to 3 locations (194 pts); funded startups; agencies needing dev capacity.
- **Entry:** one redesigned page built after a yes (84 pts); speed and conversion audit; "$2,500 five-page website" (1,655 pts).
- **Price anchors:** US, Canada, Australia agencies mostly $100 to $149 an hour; Upwork $15 to $50. Three tiers $2,999 / $3,999 / $4,999+ (437 pts). About €1,000 no-code one-pager vs €10,000+ acquisition-focused build (55 pts). Subscription: $0 down and $175 a month, or $3,800 plus $25 a month hosting (55 pts).
- **Terms:** 40% to start, 40% at design approval, 20% before go-live; launch and handover only when paid in full (134-pt thread comment). 60-day small-edit window. 3 reference sites before design.
- **Channels:** cold calls (50 a day took one developer to about $120,000 a year, 1,843 pts); defects spotted as their customer; footer credits; partners (marketing agencies without dev).
- **New demand:** rescuing AI-built apps that cannot ship (broken payments, auth) (358 pts).
- **Watch:** $200 to $500 sites for cash-poor owners; revision loops.

## Design, brand and creative studios

- **Entry:** logo or brand sprint (easiest to sell cold, 53 pts); pitch-deck design; 3D product mockups for agencies' pitches (2,556 pts).
- **Price anchors:** US, Canada, Australia $100 to $149 an hour, UK $50 to $99; a former Nike designer set $100 an hour as a floor (3,410 pts).
- **Channels:** daily public work (Instagram design a day for two years led to a Nike campaign, 3,410 pts); spec work for real or invented brands, labeled; book covers for existing books led to university press work (2,962 pts); the retained media agency's account manager for big brands.
- **Portfolio:** niche-specific account per vertical; "graveyard" of unused concepts (2,678 pts).
- **AI context:** 88% of companies use AI design tools, 18% say it cut their need for designers, 47% raised budgets (Clutch 2026).
- **Watch:** free spec for strangers; mixed portfolios with paid ads.

## Video production and editing

- **Entry:** three-video founder package; a free video for a business owner whose customers are business owners (271 pts).
- **Price anchors:** agencies mostly $100 to $149 an hour (Clutch); Upwork editors $10 to $60. AI video generation demand +329% on Upwork, but low-complexity generation is where pay per contract falls.
- **Delivery:** AI-assisted editing per style with a brand layer: first video in a new style about 12 hours, second about 2 (27 pts). Equipment packages rentable at 4 to 10% of retail (281 pts).
- **Channels:** LinkedIn is where 8 in 10 teams share video (Wistia 2026); local UGC creators per city for restaurant clients (321 pts).
- **Watch:** creator retainers without validated demand: testimonials from 350K and 23M-subscriber creators still produced 0 subscription clients from DMs (47 pts).

## Writers and copywriters

- **Rate floor:** about 250 finished words per total working hour; per-word floor = target hourly / 250 (126 pts).
- **Price anchors:** Upwork writers $30 to $59 an hour; copywriter menu: email $100 to $200, landing page $600 to $2,500, full site $2,500 to $4,000, consulting $200 to $300 an hour (215 pts); B2B SaaS blog $1,200, long-form $2,200, white papers from $2,000 (110 pts); specialist legal and finance $0.80 to $1 a word (132 pts); sales letter $15,000 plus 3% royalties (187 pts).
- **Channels:** funded startups under 10 employees (from about $20 to $100+ an hour, 246 pts); trade journals (153 pts); agencies as clients (a $135 article with brief supplied, 215 pts); LinkedIn editors via the People tab (113 pts); r/forhire and Facebook job boards.
- **Survive AI:** ship more than words (CTA embed code, branded infographics, diagrams, expert interviews) (186 and 188 pts); name AI-assisted and human offers separately.
- **Raise:** each new prospect at 20% above the top current rate (110 pts); +25% if the client blocks portfolio use.
- **Watch:** content mills, flat fees on unbounded word counts, "great for beginners" listings.

## AI automation and agents

- **Buyers:** 10 to 50 person service firms (accounting, real estate, recruiting, legal) still moving data by hand; agency owners; MSP and accounting partners.
- **Entry:** workflow opportunity map; one pilot built with the client's own API keys for a testimonial (383 pts); a public "how I built X" post with a form (two $5,000 implementations in a month, 1,115 pts).
- **Price anchors:** Upwork AI automation engineer $35 to $60 an hour; Zapier developer $40 to $90, projects $300 to $800 simple to $3,000 to $10,000+ cross-functional; $500 a month for a small hosted CRUD app (40 pts); sales calculators and evaluators $5,000 to $10,000 each (202 pts); setup fee plus 20% rev share (112 pts, challenged).
- **Positioning:** sell the outcome (time from call to approved proposal, hours saved, errors avoided) and maintenance; complex AI work earns 45% more while simple generation pays 13% less per contract (Upwork 2026).
- **Partners:** no-code automators whose clients outgrow no-code (43 pts).
- **Watch:** selling "AI" as the product; unmaintained automations; data handling (DPA in the EU).

## Consultants and fractional executives

- **Entry:** paid diagnostic; prepaid 1-hour session, then 2, then 4-hour sprints (zero refunds and unpaid work for years, 114 pts); paid discovery €3,000 to €5,000 before a retainer (59 pts).
- **Framing:** "A VP of Marketing costs $250k a year. Ours costs less and starts this week." (431 pts). Employee cost is 2 to 3x salary (808 pts).
- **Channels:** personal network (over 70% of one consultant's revenue, 68 pts); advisor networks; workshops; LinkedIn expertise posts. High-growth firms activate experts 2.5x more (Hinge 2026).
- **Proposal:** their data under NDA before the first proposal; no generic market slides (984 pts).
- **Watch:** free "pick your brain" sessions (190 pts); 1-hour free consults that leak the plan (277 pts).

## Social media managers

- **Price anchors:** $500 to $1,000 a month single channel; packages rose from $500 to $1,500 a month with a clear process (134 pts). A social manager who raised 22 clients by 40% lost 7, gained 12% revenue and cut work 35% (offers-pricing, 1,239 pts).
- **Delivery:** monthly calendar approved in a shared sheet, reports 15 days after month end (319 pts); holiday coverage scoped as paid add-on (organic-social).
- **Watch:** local service clients paying $2,000 to $3,000 a month for social with about 800 followers when search would sell more (131 pts); be honest about which channel fits.

## Recruitment and other service agencies

- Tracked referral link after every placement: about 18% of new candidates via referral in 3 months; three-metric weekly report (67 pts).
- Lead-gen agencies police the client's sales reps and cut non-compliant reps off leads (50 pts).
