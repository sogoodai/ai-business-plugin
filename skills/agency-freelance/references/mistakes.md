# Expensive mistakes

Two tables: what the data shows (measured or arithmetic on measured numbers) and what practitioners report losing. Scores are thread upvotes; URLs in [sources.md](sources.md).

## From the data

| Mistake | What it costs | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Discounting instead of fixing positioning | Rate cutters -6% revenue vs raisers +8% (Promethean 2025, observational) | Fix offer, proof and risk before price |
| Adding services when the pipeline slows | Growth 9.8% vs 13.0% for agencies that cut services; net margin about 10% vs 30% (Promethean 2026) | One flagship offer; others as upsells |
| Ignoring past clients | 43.6% acceptance for new vs 71.8% for past-client proposals (Nusii 2025 data) | Reactivation as its own tracked channel |
| Treating replies as pipeline | 4.9% median replies but only 0.79% positive per prospect (Smarter Outbound 2026) | Judge on positive replies and meetings |
| Cold email on pre-2024 deliverability habits | Gmail bulk rules (about 5,000/day), spam under 0.3%, stronger enforcement from Nov 2025 | SPF, DKIM, DMARC, clean lists, low volume per inbox |
| Marketplace without a P&L | $20,000 of every $100,000 on Fiverr; $15,000 at Upwork's 15%; $5,000 via Direct Contracts | Compute effective take and CAC per win |
| Writing proposals for unqualified buyers | 37.6% of proposals get no answer (Nusii) | Six qualification fields before any proposal |
| Desktop-only proposals | 31.9% first opened on a phone | First screen: problem, result, price, next step |
| Loose onboarding | 62% of agencies say access delays damage confidence (Promethean 2026) | Access and baseline before kickoff, in the contract |
| Selling commodity AI output | Contracts +90%, pay per contract -13% (Upwork 2026) | Sell workflow, integration, judgment |
| Automating LinkedIn | Account restriction; no published safe limit | Manual sending; agent drafts |
| Reporting activity, not revenue | 55% of clients ask whether marketing ties to revenue (AgencyAnalytics 2026) | Attribution built into delivery |
| Recommending "value pricing" as a label | Use fell 31% to 18%; users grew less (Promethean, association) | Concrete structure by risk |
| Full money-back guarantee on platform-dependent results | $52,000 refunded to 20 clients when Google filtering changed (r/startups, 270 pts) | Stop-loss and milestone gates |

## From practitioners: money and payment

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit by card on a big job | $15,000 deposit charged back at completion despite a contract (53 pts) | Bank transfer or staged milestones; hold files until paid |
| Agency-hopping, haggling prospect with "our contract" | Paid $1,000 of almost $15,000 owed, access removed (134 pts) | Decline the trifecta |
| Publishing before the last payment cleared | $250 unpaid, site live (72 pts) | Launch after final payment |
| Equipment and production before a deposit | A drone and a month of work never paid (58 pts) | Deposit before any project-specific spend |
| Expanding scope with a good payer, no new deposit | Client refused to pay until done and demanded source files (38 pts) | Prior balance plus new deposit first |
| Repeat client at last year's rate, plus infrastructure their sysadmin avoided | Card reversed, about $6,000 lost (155 pts) | Current rate; decline work their specialist refuses |
| Evidence only inside client email | Chargebacks split 3 to 3 with identical evidence; $4,100 lost (21 pts) | Copies of deliverables and approvals outside client tools |
| Accepting before the milestone was funded | €90 fee on a €900 job lost; another freelancer ended at -$1 (195 pts) | Never start before funding |

## From practitioners: scope and pricing

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Absorbing small additions on a fixed quote | 43 hours for a $2,000 page quoted at 20; about $2,300 unpaid (67 pts) | $0 quote and written OK for every addition |
| Retainer with drifting scope | Margin 28% to 11% by month 3 (47 hours vs 30, 15 out of scope) (59 pts) | Quarterly scope review; change orders |
| Retainer unchanged while the client's business boomed | About 80 hours a week for "below McDonald's" pay while the client reached $2.2M revenue (81 pts) | Monthly effective hourly per client |
| Starting with 10% of content | Projects stalled weeks or months (243 pts) | All content before production |
| No small-edit window | 8-hour sites stretched past 2 months (243 pts) | 60-day small-edit window; new features are new projects |
| $250 a month intro retainers | 30 clients, chaos; $12,000 new MRR offset by $12,000 churn in one month (55 and 134 pts) | Floor price; fewer, bigger clients |
| $200 Upwork websites with endless revisions | Quit after the 20th change of mind on a $100 site (1,854 pts) | Cap revisions; leave revision-heavy low-fee markets |
| Six years without a raise | Catch-up +30% (some +100%) lost 20% of clients at once (47 pts) | Yearly raise clause |
| Discount for a referral friend | 10x normal client time, launch moved 7 times (54 pts) | Firm scope before any discount; reschedule fees |
| Working "for exposure" or a testimonial | Client stuck at the low rate (88 pts); $5 an article for a year (106 pts) | Paid trial at full rate |
| Subsidizing a first client's ads | $300 a month cash loss before labor (62 pts) | Price any case-study subsidy and cap its length |
| Client budget below the niche's cost per result | Roofing and solar lead gen at $500 to $700 a month; agency closed in a year (154 pts) | Screen budget against cost per appointment |

## From practitioners: acquisition

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Stopping prospecting while busy | 3 multi-year clients lost in one month, restart from zero (212 pts) | Fixed weekly acquisition block |
| One acquisition source | $30K+ a month to $2K when contacts were laid off (132 pts) | Two live channels minimum |
| Free consults | 500+ in 5 years, about €50,000 of time, zero clients (190 pts) | Paid hour with 15 free minutes |
| 9-hour custom proposal with samples and travel | Ghosted (74 pts) | Paid discovery; cap unpaid proposal time |
| Buying a cold email stack before the offer worked | About $2,000 for 2 clients; manual got 3 (84 pts) | Prove the offer by hand, then scale |
| Cycling new offers on fresh infrastructure | 10 to 15% opens, almost no replies vs 50 to 60% for the proven offer (32 pts) | Scale what already wins |
| Outsourced "20 leads a month" | About $10,000, zero meetings; replies counted as leads (30 pts) | Lead = booked meeting |
| Boosting LinkedIn posts | About $720, 1 conversation, 0 clients (76 pts) | Conversations before boost spend |
| Instagram ads with a mixed portfolio | $200, 8 DMs, 0 clients (44 pts) | Niche portfolio account first |
| Bark leads for a PR agency | $2,500 a month; clients churned in 1 to 2 weeks or stopped paying (61 pts) | Judge on 90-day collected revenue |
| Facebook-ad sourced retainers | 2 of 3 quit after month one, third card declined (108 pts) | Channel retention, not lead count |
| Website before clients | Many hours, zero leads (several threads) | Sell first; build the site later |
| Upwork volume and boosts | Proposal-to-contract 20% to 2%; nearly $1,000 a month in Connects (473 pts) | Quarterly re-measure; selective bids |
| Launching an agency brand with no repeat freelance clients | 7 months to the first agency client (66 pts) | Repeat clients before the agency brand |

## From practitioners: delivery and trust

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Founder sells, junior with 14 accounts delivers | $5,000 a month retainer fired (227 pts) | Name the operator; cap accounts per person |
| Three weeks of silence while testing | €2,000 a month client lost though campaigns performed (340 pts) | Friday 2-minute Loom |
| Extra zero on a daily budget | $10,000 in 3 days, account manager replaced (44 pts) | Second-person launch check |
| Same creative for 3 months | Client gone after $15,000 (121 pts) | Planned creative refresh |
| Partner's editor used their own email domain | 4+ year client went direct (35 pts) | Subcontractors on your domain |
| Doubling the agency in a year | Broken systems, a year of repair (37 pts) | 10 to 25% growth, waitlists |
| Selling 100% of team hours | Burnout, departures (80 pts) | Plan at 60 to 80% utilization |
| Unvetted outsourced writers | 34% plagiarized or spun, 18% AI nonsense across 359 articles (1,579 pts) | Check every vendor's work |
| Engagement bots on client accounts | Action block; reach near zero for months (178 pts) | No automation that breaks platform rules |
| Hiding AI-edited work as human | Writers quit, clients complained (109 pts) | Separate, disclosed offers |
| Disavowing links a tool called "toxic" | Client traffic -40% (comment, 222 pts) | Verify manually |
