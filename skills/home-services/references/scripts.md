# Scripts

Verbatim wording that operators or sources report using. Replace brackets, keep the owner's voice, keep texts under 160 characters where possible. Before sending anything as the owner, run the slop test (adapted from anthropics small-business, Apache-2.0): would the owner say this out loud; could it go to another customer with two words changed; does it open with flattery; is there a phrase the owner never uses? Delete "I hope this finds you well", "just checking in", "reach out", "touch base".

## Missed calls and first replies

**Missed-call text (research pass, 2026):**
> Hi, this is [Business]. Sorry we missed your call. Are you looking for [service]? Reply with your ZIP and a one-line description and I'll tell you the next available time.

**Form reply (research pass):**
> Hi [Name], [Business] here. I saw your request for [specific service] in [area]. I can help. Two quick questions so I quote it correctly: [question 1]? And [question 2]?

Better still, one question: replies naming the job and asking one clear question got 60.5% 24-hour reply rates vs 50.3% (LeadTruffle 2026).

**Form submitted, quote going by email (wedding photographer, r/WeddingPhotography 66):**
> hey received your inquiry, sending pricing to your email now!

**Qualified reply, under 100 words (adapted from anthropics speed-to-lead, Apache-2.0):**
> Hi Dana,
> Yes, we cover multi-building portfolios on one service plan, and six buildings is well inside what we handle.
> Straight answer on pricing: it depends on unit age and access, but for a portfolio that size it usually lands cheaper than per-building contracts.
> I could walk the sites Thursday morning or Friday after 1. Either work?
> Thanks, Ray

**Hot lead reply, then an owner alert ending in one instruction:**
> Hi Marcus, no cooling on a 40,000 sq ft floor is a same-day problem, and yes, we can help. I can have someone there this afternoon. Calling you in the next few minutes to confirm the address and access. Ray
>
> HOT: Marcus Ilo, no cooling, 40,000 sq ft, needs same day. I said you'd call in minutes. Call 503-555-0142 now.

**Unclear:**
> Happy to help with this. One thing I need first: is this for a house or a commercial building? It changes who I'd send and what it runs.

**Out of scope, referred by name:**
> Commercial kitchen refrigeration isn't something we're certified for. Basin Cooling handles it; ask for Teri and tell her I sent you.

**Phone range for size-based work (r/sweatystartup 18):**
> Most houses your size land between [X] and [Y]. I confirm the exact number when I measure.

**Arrival window (r/sweatystartup 200):**
> We'll be there between 8 am and 12 or 12 and 4 pm, which works best? Great, we can send you a text or give you a call before we head over. Which would you prefer?

## Quote follow-up

> Hi [Name], wanted to make sure you had everything you need to compare the [service] options. Is the main question price, timing, or what's included? Reply with one and I'll clarify it.

**Day 7:**
> We have [Tuesday AM] and [Thursday PM] open in [area]. If you want me to hold either slot while you decide, reply TUE or THU.

**Day 14, close the loop:**
> Hi [Name], I'll close out the [service] quote for now so it doesn't clutter your texts. If anything changes, reply here and I'll pick it straight back up.

## Objections and negotiation

**"I'll think about it" or "getting other quotes" (r/sweatystartup 222):**
> I completely understand. Normally, whenever my customers tell me this, it has to do with price. May I ask, what was your expectation?

**Price came in above expectation, conceding one tier for a decision today (r/sweatystartup 262):**
> I understand this was a little more than you were expecting. May I ask, what was your expectation?
> Thanks for sharing that. The good news is, we're not that far away. I'll tell you what, I'll lower my price to [$3,636] if you allow me to earn your business today.

**Budget objection, smaller scope at the same rate (r/smallbusiness 321):**
> I understand. What budget range are you working with? Sometimes I can adjust the scope to fit.
> I can definitely work within that budget. At [$X], I'd recommend we focus on [specific piece]. You'd get [outcome] without the [removed elements]. Would that work?

**Cheaper competitor (r/smallbusiness 321):**
> Does their quote include [specific thing]? What's their warranty if something goes wrong?
> I'm confident in my pricing because [one sentence of value]. But if they're a better fit for your budget, I completely understand.

**"My friend got it for half" (repair shop, r/smallbusiness 1,175):**
> The lowest I've ever done it is [$120], and that was because they brought several items. This one is [$150].

**Firm refusal when haggling continues (r/pressurewashing 2,164):**
> No, that's the best I can do.

**Leave the door open (r/smallbusiness 276):**
> Well, if you change your mind or ever need something else, please give us a call.
> I'll be here when you need me.

**Preventive repair (handyman, r/smallbusiness 1,419):**
> I can fix that hole in the siding today for [$200]. Before the next big rain gets behind it, that's a lot cheaper than what it turns into.

**Wear found on a maintenance visit (HVAC, r/smallbusiness 535; show the photo):**
> Your capacitor is at about 70%. It'll probably last another season, but I'd plan on replacing it next spring.

**Extra request outside the agreed scope (r/WeddingPhotography 141; reported 9 of 10 decline):**
> Sure, I'd be happy to accommodate your request. To make that happen it will cost an additional [$X].

**Batching later changes (r/smallbusiness 876):**
> Whenever you want them, put them all together on a list and I'll give you a quote based on what you want.

**Contract edit requests (photographer, r/WeddingPhotography 339):**
> Any edits to our contract will be presented to our attorney at a rate of $400 per hour. That fee is paid upfront so our attorney can properly advise us, but it does not guarantee acceptance of the suggested changes.

**Intro pricing, said out loud (r/smallbusiness 2,215):**
> This is our new client pricing.
> Right now I'm not too busy as we're just starting out, so I can get to this right away.

**Portfolio discount tied to photo use (r/WeddingPhotography 213):**
> The use of your photos for marketing offsets the $900 I discounted from my normal fees. If you wish to deny the use of the photos for marketing, I will have to charge my normal rate.

**Discount request from a business client (cleaner, r/smallbusiness 276):**
> The price is based on the work, not the size of my office.

**Withdrawing from work beyond your skill (adapted from commenters, r/AutoDetailing 1,427):**
> I could make the interior look acceptable for $600, but the problem underneath would still be there. After looking into it, I don't think that would actually fix it for you, and I'd rather not take your money for that.

## Scope and expectations

**Water supply (pressure washing, r/pressurewashing 411):**
> We are more than happy to provide the water so we don't have to use yours. There will be an additional $200 charge for us to bring water, due to the extra labor of filling tanks and trips back to the shop. Or we can use your water for $250.

**Deck cleaning disclaimer lines (r/pressurewashing 124):**
> Cleaning does not constitute refinishing work.
> Power washing may reveal uneven wood tones due to long-term UV exposure.

**Booking rule for date-based work (photographer, r/WeddingPhotography 252):**
> The first person to pay the deposit and sign the contract gets the date.

**Ending a session on time (photographer, r/WeddingPhotography 333):** warn at 30 minutes, remind at 10, offer a paid extension before staying.

**Customer ends key work early:**
> That's perfectly fine. Just so you know, I won't have [photos as a couple / the back patio done] then.

## Neighbors, doors and walk-ins

> We're working for one of your neighbors on [street] today and have room for one more [service] stop in this area on [day]. If you want a quote while the crew is already nearby, text [number] with "[street]".

**Same street, on the spot (r/sweatystartup 578):**
> Need your gutters cleaned? I'll do it for [$XX] right now.

**Door knock with real proof, only with that customer's permission (r/sweatystartup 188):**
> I'm taking care of your neighbor Linda down the street tomorrow.

**Handwritten door card (weeding, r/sweatystartup 514):** index card with business name, your first name and phone; sticky note with "[exact address], [price], Front weeds only."

**Resident who doesn't need it (r/sweatystartup 30):**
> No problem. If you know a friend or family member looking for a cleaner, would you mind passing this on?

**Commercial walk-in (r/sweatystartup 17):**
> Hey, I own a local office cleaning company. Who usually handles janitorial here?
> (If out:) Could I get their name and best email, and roughly when you review vendors or contracts?

**Commercial survey opener (r/sweatystartup 17):**

> I own a cleaning company and I'm doing a survey on what businesses like about their current cleaning companies and what they wish they did better.

**Builder visit (r/sweatystartup 20):**

**Partner proposal for property managers, realtors, HOAs (research pass):**
> We handle [service] in [area]. We can reserve two priority slots a week for your clients, send photo completion reports and invoice by property. If that would remove a headache for your team, I can send the exact turnaround and pricing.

## Plans, referrals and price changes

**Frequency offer (research pass):**
> Most customers in your area choose every 2 weeks because it keeps the price per visit lower and locks the time slot. One-off is [$X]; every two weeks is [$Y] a visit.

**Referral ask with a real opening (r/pressurewashing 338):**
> I have a couple of openings next week if you have any friends who need [service]. Here are a few cards. Most of my best customers come from referrals, so pass on my info.

**Calling a referrer when their referral is going badly (r/smallbusiness 828):**
> First, thank you again for the referral to Joe. It means a lot that you felt confident enough in my work to make that introduction. Can I ask your advice about Joe? I'm concerned I'm not quite hitting the mark with him. I'm serving him the same way I serve you, and yet something feels off.

**Price increase notice (r/smallbusiness 1,239; use a smaller step for homeowners):**
> Hey [name], I wanted to give you a heads up that starting [month] my rates will be [new rate]. This reflects [the growth in my team and costs] and an adjustment to market rates. I completely understand if this doesn't fit your budget and I'm happy to recommend someone else if needed. If you'd like to continue at the new rate, no action is needed on your end.

## Reviews (compliant)

**After a completed job, same text to every customer (research pass, Google-compliant):**
> Thanks for choosing [Business], [Name]. If you'd like to share your experience, here's our Google review link: [link]. Honest feedback helps people know what to expect.

**In person while the customer is praising the work (r/GoogleMyBusiness 40), then text the link before you leave:**
> Thanks for saying that. Would you mind putting it in a review? I just texted you the link, it takes a second. It's how we get work these days.

Do not copy practitioner versions that mention "5 star reviews" or offer anything in return; asking for a rating or giving incentives breaks Google's rules. Never ask for Yelp reviews. Reply templates for good, bad and fake reviews: `local-seo`.
