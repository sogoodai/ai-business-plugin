# Recurring revenue and retention

Recurring routes (cleaning, lawn, pest, pool, pet waste, grooming, windows, detailing, fleet washing) amortize acquisition over many visits. Trades (HVAC, plumbing, electrical) use maintenance agreements to own the next big replacement. Email, SMS flows and loyalty mechanics live in `email-sms-loyalty`; this file covers the service side.

## Where recurrence fits

| Service | Natural interval | Plan shape |
|---|---|---|
| House cleaning | Weekly, biweekly, monthly | Frequency pricing, same team each visit |
| Lawn mowing, fertilization | Weekly in season; 5 to 8 treatments a year | Seasonal agreement paid monthly |
| Landscaping maintenance | Season | All services for the year, divided into monthly payments |
| Pest control | Quarterly, or monthly in heavy pressure | Annual contract with re-treatment included |
| Pet waste removal | Weekly or twice weekly | Monthly subscription by dog count, no contract |
| HVAC | Twice a year | Two inspections, priority service, waived call-out fee |
| Windows, gutters, house wash | 1 to 4 times a year | Annual schedule, prepay for a discount |
| Detailing | Biweekly or monthly | Maintenance wash after a full detail; office routes |
| Commercial cleaning, floor care | Nightly to quarterly | 1-year auto-renew contract, flat monthly price |
| Grooming | 4 to 8 weeks | Pre-booked next appointment before the dog leaves |
| Dog training | Course-based | Package then group classes or refresher sessions |
| Photography | Life events | Annual family session, mini sessions, referrals |

## Converting one-off jobs to plans

- **Offer at the relief moment:** right after the problem is fixed and the customer is visibly pleased, or when you have just shown wear ("your capacitor is at about 70%"). Offer rate target: the plan offered on 90%+ of eligible jobs (research pass, 2026).
- **Sell frequency as a price and scheduling benefit:** "Most customers in your area choose every 2 weeks because it keeps the price per visit lower and locks the time slot. One-off is $X; every two weeks is $Y a visit." (research pass script).
- **Make the first visit the reset and price it separately:** cleaners do one thorough initial clean, then routine maintenance; refuse clients who book less than monthly but expect a full reset each time (cleaner since 1992, r/sweatystartup 197).
- **Same team, same routine** wins in small towns: one cleaner in a town under 6,000 people sold weekly, biweekly and monthly flat-rate plans with a fixed two-person team (r/sweatystartup 19).
- **Biweekly is the core cohort** for one cleaner in a high-income city (r/sweatystartup 1,014).
- **Trades targets (aspirational, partly pre-2024):** 30% service-call to membership, 80% tune-up to agreement, 25% new-member and 70% renewal minimums (ServiceTitan playbook and operator case). Housecall Pro's 2025 survey: 51% of homeowners expect a provider to offer a service agreement.
- **HVAC agreement economics** (operators report, r/smallbusiness 535): 200 to 400 contracts at $15 to $25 a month is $3,000 to $10,000 monthly before service calls; members convert to replacements at 3 to 4x cold leads; building the base takes 2 to 3 years.
- **Sticker the equipment:** a large sticker on the furnace or appliance with the company number and next service date (r/sweatystartup 48).
- **Recurring commercial from a one-off:** one truck wash became a customer needing washes five days a week (r/pressurewashing 254); a floor strip-and-wax ($1.20/sq ft) sells with monthly scrub-and-recoat ($0.35/sq ft), weekly burnishing ($0.10/sq ft) and daily mopping (r/sweatystartup 351).
- **Fleet and dusty businesses:** pitch monthly washing to trucking firms, construction companies and rock yards (r/sweatystartup 956).

## Plan design

Three tiers that change scope, not just price (research pass, 2026):

- **Basic:** scheduled preventive visits and reminders.
- **Priority:** Basic plus priority scheduling and reduced or waived call-out fee.
- **Protection:** Priority plus consumables or inspection and a defined discount or service credit.

Rules:

- Price from a timed visit plus drive time at the target hourly rate, plus vehicle and materials margin; cut tasks, not the rate (r/sweatystartup 16).
- Define exactly what the visit includes and quote discovered repairs separately. Otherwise "leak inspection" becomes a free two-hour repair (r/sweatystartup 16).
- Quarterly beats monthly for home maintenance: same revenue, a quarter of the drives (r/sweatystartup 16).
- Pet waste: $16 for one dog and $19 for two weekly (one operator at $4,000 a month adding 8 to 12 clients a month); or $45 a month entry weekly, more per dog, twice-weekly upgrade (over $2,000 subscription revenue by month three); weekly $18 to $25 vs biweekly $25 to $35, so less frequent costs more per visit (r/sweatystartup 431, 529). Price by dog count, not yard size.
- Seasonal pause has a cost: charge more for the first spring cleanup after winter (one yard had 217 piles, r/sweatystartup 431).
- Landscaping: bundle the year into one agreement paid monthly at about $100 per labor hour plus materials; one operator reached $100k take-home on $300k gross by year four (r/sweatystartup 383).
- Commercial cleaning: flat monthly price on a 1-year auto-renew contract 98% of the time; square-foot pricing only when the client asks (r/sweatystartup 1,990).
- Report plan gross margin separately from recurring revenue; members' discounts on repairs and waived fees are costs.
- Route members into low-demand weeks to flatten seasonality.

## Keep them: service habits that retain

- **Inspect your own work** before the client finds a problem. A commercial cleaner with 101 contracts lost two in a year and names "hire cleaners and never go back to inspect" as the classic loss (r/sweatystartup 1,990).
- **Reply to client issues within one hour; visit the same day for critical problems** (commercial cleaning, r/sweatystartup 428).
- **Leave proof of the visit:** a note on the step plus two dog treats kept a pet-waste customer for years and willing to accept a price rise (r/sweatystartup 431). A process-and-FAQ sheet handed over on arrival (gutters, r/sweatystartup 200).
- **Arrival windows, not times:** 8 to 12 or 12 to 4, with a text before heading over; give customers a wider window than you give cleaners (8 to 11 for the customer, 8 to 9 for the cleaner) (r/sweatystartup 200, 201).
- **Pickup and fast return** as a differentiator: knife sharpening returned in 2 to 3 hours within 3 miles vs 7 to 14 days for mail-in rivals (r/sweatystartup 622); same-day computer pickup and return earned three referrals from the first customer (r/smallbusiness 2,215).
- **Quote realistic turnaround:** a repair shop moved its quoted time from 6 to 8 days to 16 to 18 days; finishing in 8 to 9 days turned angry customers into thrilled ones (r/smallbusiness 2,215).
- **Premium homeowners over price-shopping corporates** for premium window cleaning: one operator keeps corporate under 1% of customers because they switch on price (r/sweatystartup 453).
- **Do not let one account become the business:** one maintenance customer worth $200,000 a year and 90% of income for 15 years left when a new manager took the work in-house (r/smallbusiness 928). Cap any single account's share and keep selling.
- **Fire clients who cost more than they pay.** One owner refunded a full $800 month to end a boundary-pushing client (r/smallbusiness 438).

## Service recovery

- Fix your own mistakes fast and at your cost. An HVAC installer who undersized a system swapped to the right one at no charge and won a customer for life (r/smallbusiness 481). A detail shop that missed spots re-did the car, rechecked everything, and added a free waterless wash product (r/AutoDetailing 1,584).
- Ask what recovery the customer wants before offering one. A photographer who missed a wedding refunded fully and offered a free bridal session; the couple still wanted photos of the actual day and considered a bad review (r/WeddingPhotography 1,641).
- Missed cleaning: backup coverage first; else a discounted reschedule; else a gift card for a future clean (r/sweatystartup 201).
- Document before and after: photograph pre-existing damage close up (etching, cracks, stains) and film a walk-around with the customer present; customers who refused to pay paid after seeing the video and signed work order (r/sweatystartup 956, r/pressurewashing 179).
- Water bill complaints (pressure washing): minutes on site x machine gallons per minute x the local rate gives the maximum possible usage. One such calculation exposed a city meter running 10 times fast (r/pressurewashing 411).

## Cross-sell and add-ons from existing customers

- Gutter season customers become the spring list for house washing and gutter guards (50 to 60 customers, r/sweatystartup 200).
- Paver sealing plus gutter cleaning or soft washing on the same visit, with tight routes, were one operator's biggest wins (r/sweatystartup 18).
- Pet services: waste removal clients cross-sold to dog walking, sitting, in-home grooming and training; one operator averaged over $6,000 a month by end of year one at $60 to $100 an hour (r/sweatystartup 431). Yard deodorizer as a summer add-on.
- Overnight in-home pet sitting for pets that cannot board: a "big money maker" for a sitter business profiting $150,000 a year (r/smallbusiness 901).
- Carpet cleaning as the loss leader for apartment complexes, then air ducts, odor fogging, 24/7 water extraction and dryer vents (r/sweatystartup 22).
- Drain cleaning: sell the camera inspection that explains why the clog keeps coming back (r/sweatystartup 17).
- Detailing after mold remediation: recommend a six-month maintenance treatment (r/AutoDetailing 1,427).

## Win-back by each customer's own rhythm

Adapted from anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins small-business reactivate and churn-signals (Apache-2.0):

- For customers with 3+ jobs, normal interval = median gap between their own jobs. Quiet = current gap over 2x normal; gone = over 3x. An annual tune-up customer at 13 months is normal. Fewer than 3 jobs: compare with the business median and label it an estimate.
- Rank by past gross profit, then frequency, not by how long they have been silent. Fifteen names the owner will actually call beat two hundred in a blast.
- Top of the list: anyone who left a negative review and then stopped. Call, do not text.
- Sequence: 3 messages over 4 weeks, each under 90 words. Message 1 acknowledges the gap and asks what happened, no pitch. Message 2 says what changed (new service, crew, hours). Message 3 gives a specific time-bound reason (a route day in their area, a seasonal slot). Any reply stops the sequence; a complaint goes to the owner.
- Offers only if the owner authorized them; high-value customers with a complaint get an apology and a call, not a coupon.
- Gratitude is not a pricing policy: a lawn operator still charged his second customer $55 a week four years later, when larger companies quoted about $120 (r/sweatystartup 445). Move legacy rates up at the plan renewal.

## Tricks most owners miss

- Report churn reason and route for every recurring account.
- Time referral thank-you cards to slow weeks if you want a controlled bump (20-year owner, r/sweatystartup 405).
- Recurring availability is part of the product for pet services and grooming: book the next visit before the current one ends.
- Do not run a membership if peak capacity cannot serve members: a court facility charges per booking because members could not reserve at peak (r/smallbusiness 291). Same logic for HVAC priority promises in a heat wave.
