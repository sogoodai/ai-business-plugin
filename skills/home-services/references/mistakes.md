# Expensive mistakes

Two tables: what the data and platform rules show, and what practitioners report losing.

## From the data and platform rules

| Mistake | What it costs | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Paying for leads nobody answers | Only 52% of home-service calls reach a person (Invoca 2026); LSA bills an in-hours missed call held over 20 seconds | Missed-call text, answering service, pause ads in hours you cannot answer |
| Ranking channels by cost per lead | Cleaning CPL $46.99 at 15% booking = $313 a job; plumbing $129 at 55% = $235 (LocaliQ 2025, Invoca 2026, illustrative) | Booked and completed CAC, then GP CAC |
| Buying traffic before following up quotes | Leaves 1 to 15% of revenue on the table (ServiceTitan 2025, self-reported) | Fixed follow-up cadence; recovered revenue as its own line |
| Counting on LSA disputes to fix targeting | No credits for wrong job type or area; no automated credits in EMEA (Google 2026) | Tight service areas and job types; weekly reconciliation |
| "Google Guaranteed" on trucks and scripts | Outdated since Oct 2025; the money-back guarantee ended | "Google Verified" only if verified |
| Review automation that includes Yelp | Yelp's filter can hide solicited reviews | Google ask only; Yelp claimed and left alone |
| Review gating, incentives, staff quotas | Google can remove reviews, block new ones, show a warning; FTC rule since 21 Oct 2024 | Same neutral ask to every customer |
| Fake or virtual-office locations | Suspension; LSA blocked | One real location; hide a home address on service-area profiles |
| Promo texts without consent | TCPA damages $500 to $1,500 per message (US); PECR, CASL, Spam Act elsewhere | Consent records; service replies only (see `email-sms-loyalty`) |
| Testing three marketplaces at once | No attribution, no capacity to answer | One at a time, 20 to 30 leads, kill rule |
| Expanding the service area for "more leads" | Drive time eats margin; out-of-route stops cost more | Densify first; price remote stops separately |
| Membership discounts larger than the margin | Recurring revenue with negative gross profit | Price from timed visits; report plan margin |
| Websites missing service areas, hours, FAQ | 43%, 65% and 74% of home-service sites (Jobber 2026); 54% of consumers visit the site after reading reviews (BrightLocal 2026) | Service area, hours, prices or ranges, FAQ on the page |

## What practitioners report losing

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Bought Google reviews | 20 bought, 12 vanished in 3 weeks, the rest a month later; rankings fell below the start (r/smallbusiness 10,223) | Real requests only |
| Free product for reviews | 50+ reviews deleted, suspension, 30-day review block (r/GoogleMyBusiness 17) | No incentives of any kind |
| Discount in exchange for a Google review | Suspension risk flagged by commenters (r/sweatystartup 201) | Remove it from the process |
| Virtual office to verify a service-area profile | Suspended on the third video; LSA for a $150,000-a-month advertiser at risk (r/GoogleMyBusiness 37) | Real address, hidden if customers do not visit |
| Home address shown on a moving company's profile | Two suspensions in three months (r/GoogleMyBusiness 35) | Hide the address |
| Generic city street as a photographer's address | Suspended after five years of reviews and top-3 placement (r/GoogleMyBusiness 18) | Real address, service-area settings |
| Listing 24/7 without coverage | Two 1-star reviews seen from callers who found it closed (r/localseo 95) | List only answered hours, or staff them |
| Yelp ads without written terms | Charged $130 before the promised free period ended; no refund (r/smallbusiness 1,269); $176 after one week, fake-looking leads (r/smallbusiness 476); $300 credit plus $165 gone in 16 days after keywords and a 15-mile radius were re-added | Terms in writing; weekly settings check; cancel fast |
| Agency call reports taken on trust | A few thousand a month for six months; 2 real calls among dropped calls with dead numbers (r/localseo 27) | Listen to calls; own the call tracking |
| Thousands on Angi | Painter "left broke" (r/sweatystartup 16) | Bounded test with kill rule |
| $500 of Facebook ads in month one | Zero leads for a new pressure washer (r/pressurewashing 94) | Google profile and neighbors first; ads with a capped test |
| Borrowing to raise ad spend after sales dropped | $30,000 loan ended up covering payroll; layoffs (r/smallbusiness 707) | Prove a channel converts before scaling |
| One EDDM drop | $200 spent, $0 back (r/sweatystartup 200) | 3 to 4 rounds to the same homes, or skip |
| Blanket door hangers | 300 hangers, 0 leads and a complaint call; 150 hangers, 0 calls (r/pressurewashing 338) | Only near jobs, with tracking |
| 100 yard signs with no tracking | No calls (r/pressurewashing 94) | Busy stops, readable number, tracked line |
| A full summer of door knocking on commission | 16,000 doors, 82 contracts, $800 net (r/sweatystartup 188) | Measure net per door and per conversation early |
| 1,000 cards printed before testing the design | A mascot that read as aggressive and suggestive (r/pressurewashing 278) | Show 5 people before printing |
| $4,000 website and marketing bundle | About $2,000 lost on a cookie-cutter build (r/sweatystartup 197) | Cheap first site, upgrade from earnings ($450 site, first job on launch day, r/sweatystartup 1,014) |
| Underquoting for reviews or recurring work | Lost money on early cleaning jobs (r/sweatystartup 201) | Labeled intro price with an end date |
| $80 for three hours of pressure washing | About $27 an hour before costs; peers suggested $220 to $350 (r/pressurewashing 581) | Minimum charge and hourly target |
| Friendship rate on a paint correction | $1,000 for about 40 hours plus 4 hours of daily driving vs a $2,000 market rate (r/AutoDetailing 2,813) | Normal price; any discount on a separate line |
| $600 quote for full mold remediation | Specialists charge $4,000 to $7,000; PPE alone $100+ (r/AutoDetailing 1,427) | Scope or decline |
| Verbal scope | Agreed $100 for three hours, customer added a patio and threatened a review, paid $60 (r/pressurewashing 2,164) | Signed, surface-by-surface estimate |
| Skipping the test patch | $269 house wash, discoloration, two days in and another visit (r/pressurewashing 976); blue siding turned green (r/pressurewashing 129) | Finger-rub for chalking; test hidden spot |
| Wand on metal-roof screws | A rival's job caused $500,000 in roof and content damage (r/pressurewashing 260) | Low pressure, protect washers |
| Rushing a gutter job | Mud blown onto siding, 1.5 hours of extra cleanup (r/sweatystartup 200) | Hand-remove wet debris first |
| Wrong ladder, no hose | Dismissed from the job (r/sweatystartup 200) | Job checklist per address |
| Unseen lawn quoted at $40 | Almost 3 hours of work (r/sweatystartup 43) | Inspect or quote a range |
| Commercial HVAC quote missing callback, refrigerant, drive and rental | 8% margin on a 3-day job (r/sweatystartup 41) | Line-item checklist for every cost |
| Out-of-trade add-on (tile for a painter) | Tile took longer than the paint job, plus damages (r/sweatystartup 274) | Refer it out |
| Gratitude pricing for early customers | $55 a week for years when rivals charged $120 (r/sweatystartup 445) | Annual price review for everyone |
| One customer at 90% of revenue | $200,000 a year lost overnight when a new manager went in-house (r/smallbusiness 928) | Cap concentration; keep selling |
| A one-time discount not labeled one-time | Client expected $250 for a $600 project and complained to the referrer; lost both (r/smallbusiness 612) | "One-time" in writing |
| Discounts for promised future volume | Promised repeat orders almost never came (r/smallbusiness 276; r/pressurewashing 338) | Normal price; volume pricing after volume |
| Taking holiday bookings without asking crews | Crews cancelled the night before; angry customers (r/sweatystartup 252) | Confirm availability before opening dates |
| Accepting a wedding with incomplete details | 11-hour, three-location itinerary sent 72 hours before (r/WeddingPhotography 1,150) | Collect venues, hours, contacts at signing |
| Paying review-removal "services" | Another attack a week later; scammers vanish (r/GoogleMyBusiness 24, 61) | Report through Google's tools with evidence |
| Asking a competing coach for bid advice | The coach underbid and took the job (r/WeddingPhotography 232) | Never share live bids |
