# By trade, with the seasonality calendar

Each trade: the growth engine, the numbers we have, and what is specific. Data gaps are stated: r/lawncare and r/Dogtraining threads in the input were homeowner and hobbyist posts with no business tactics, so lawn and pet-service notes lean on r/sweatystartup operators and the research pass.

## Residential cleaning

- Engine: recurring biweekly plans, Google profile, neighborhood cards, Care.com for solo cleaners, move-out work from realtors.
- Numbers: search CPL $46.99 (LocaliQ 2025), LSA about $33 (99 Calls 2026), cleaning was the fastest trade to reply (26% within an hour, Jobber 2026) yet the slowest to raise prices (55%).
- Specific: price per visit by bedroom count or a flat rate, not hourly; first clean priced as a reset; refuse less-than-monthly clients expecting a full reset; same team each visit; customer arrival window wider than the cleaner's; confirm holiday availability of each team before taking bookings (cancelled crews the day before Halloween, r/sweatystartup 252). Mine competitors' reviews for recurring complaints and make the opposite your promise ("hassle-free booking, no walkthrough required", r/sweatystartup 187).

## Commercial cleaning

- Engine: one person doing direct sales (walk-ins, calls, emails), permits and bid lists, buying books of business. See [commercial-and-partnerships.md](commercial-and-partnerships.md).
- Specific: flat monthly 1-year auto-renew; inspect your crews; single-tenant buildings; quote glass and high dusting separately; post-construction priced per phase.

## Lawn care and landscaping

- Engine: density. Door knock one subdivision, park the trailer and mow house to house (30 to 40 yards a day at $50, r/sweatystartup 445); 25 to 40 dense fertilization stops a day (Glacier Lake Partners 2026).
- Numbers: search CPL $117.92 (LocaliQ 2025); LSA landscaping about $37 (99 Calls 2026); lawn and tree phone leads convert 54% on the call (Invoca 2026).
- Specific: recently sold homes on small lots are the best door-knock list; seasonal agreements paid monthly; referral rewards by adjacency; inspect before quoting (a $40 lawn that took 3 hours, r/sweatystartup 43); pre-sell the season in late winter; leaf cleanup and snow (where relevant) bridge the off-season. Gratitude pricing to early customers is a common leak.

## Pressure washing, soft washing and window cleaning

- Engine: Google profile (90 to 95% of new customers for some, r/sweatystartup 477, r/pressurewashing 338), neighbor saturation, visible before/after, Instagram and Facebook video, winter pre-selling.
- Numbers: window cleaning search CPL $66.69 (LocaliQ 2025).
- Specific: minimum charge $130 to $250; target $100 to $200 per working hour; quote by surface and get it signed; test-patch every house (chalking finger-rub, bleach on organic-pigment paint turned blue siding green, r/pressurewashing 129); protect metal-roof screw washers (a rival's mistake cost $500,000 in roof and content damage, r/pressurewashing 260); small gutter jobs lead to house washes ($100 gutter job became an $800 wash, r/pressurewashing 94); one fence-cleaning video reached 843,000 views and kicked off the first $20,000 month (r/pressurewashing 160). Commercial: gas stations overnight before inspections, fleets, apartment complexes. Book a whole Saturday and rent a machine before buying (r/smallbusiness 919). Window cleaners: a $1,500 ladder and water-fed pole meant refusing no jobs (r/sweatystartup 16); premium homeowners retain better than corporates.

## Auto detailing

- Engine: workplace and office routes, repeat maintenance washes, Google profile, before/after.
- Numbers: Search conversion rate 15.51% for automotive repair and service (WordStream 2026).
- Specific: video walk-around with the customer and a signed work order listing declined services before starting; scope heavy jobs before quoting (a $600 mold quote vs $4,000 to $7,000 for proper remediation, r/AutoDetailing 1,427); never a "friendship rate" for 5 to 6 hours of work; office wash days at $50 to $75 a car; fleets and RV parks.

## Pest control

- Engine: annual or quarterly contracts, direct mail repeated to the same streets, door-to-door with real neighbor proof, LSA.
- Numbers: pest phone leads convert 55% on the call (Invoca 2026); annual contracts of $500 to $800 where price cuts rarely saved a sale (r/sweatystartup 188); mailers at about $0.70 per address pulled calls from 3 to 5% of homes (r/pressurewashing 338).
- Specific: launch seasonal treatment plans before pressure peaks; convert the first incident into quarterly protection; one mosquito business reached about 200 customers and CAD 100,000 in six months (GorillaDesk case, single vendor story).

## Handyman

- Engine: Google, Thumbtack or TaskRabbit for small jobs, realtor punch lists, door knocking at houses with visible faults.
- Numbers: search CPL $54.05 (LocaliQ 2025); LSA $35 median (AdGenius 2025).
- Specific: minimum of a couple hundred dollars; focus on visible, hard-to-DIY exterior repairs; preventive framing; decline out-of-trade add-ons (a painter's tile job cost more than the paint job, r/sweatystartup 274); furniture assembly near college move-in.

## Painting

- Engine: quoting and follow-up discipline (considered purchase), Google, referrals, property managers.
- Numbers: search CPL $138.38 (LocaliQ 2025); LSA about $33 (99 Calls 2026).
- Specific: three margin tiers and the "what was your expectation?" script; line-item estimates with warranty in labor; interior work in winter, exterior in season; niche by vertical (medical, apartments).

## Junk removal

- Engine: yard signs, truck visibility, realtors, estate cleanouts, property managers, LSA.
- Numbers: LSA about $33 (99 Calls 2026); one operator from $5,500 a month to $10,000+ with yard signs as the main source (r/sweatystartup 227).
- Specific: paid appliance haul-away plus resale; partnerships around moves and inspections.

## Pet services: waste removal, walking, sitting, grooming, training

- Engine: Nextdoor, vets and shelters, yard signs, recurring subscriptions, cross-selling across pet services; Rover or Wag for first reviews.
- Numbers: pet waste $16 to $25 weekly, or $45 a month entry; one operator $200k first year with Facebook and Google at $40 to $65 a day combined (r/sweatystartup 529); overnight in-home sitting a top earner (r/smallbusiness 901). Rover keeps about 20% of sitter fees (secondary sources, 2026).
- Specific: price by dog count; text-only lead handling works; visit-completion notes; pre-book the next groom before the dog leaves; dog training is a course sale (package, then group classes or refreshers). Data gap: no business-side benchmarks for grooming rebook rates or training conversion in the inputs.

## Photographers (wedding, portrait, family)

- Engine: planners and venues, Google for niche searches (courthouse weddings were nearly 40% of one photographer's inquiries, r/WeddingPhotography 447), Instagram with browsable stills (a bride left profiles with only reels, r/WeddingPhotography 225), venue reviews with photos, fast inquiry replies.
- Specific: date goes to whoever signs and pays the retainer first ("the first person to pay the deposit and sign the contract gets the date"; 30% retainer, r/WeddingPhotography 252); collect every venue, hours and both partners' contacts before accepting; reconfirm the timeline in the final days; weekday elopements protect weekends; minimum coverage; delivered-vs-shot ratio explained at consultation ("I don't throw away good pictures. You get every good shot I take."); attorney-review fee for contract edits ($400 an hour, prepaid); post the couple the day before the wedding so guests find you. Lead platforms are weak economics here unless ticket and upsell justify shared leads (research pass).

## Plumbing

- Engine: LSA, Google Search, maps, vehicles, emergency availability, rural and dirty jobs rivals avoid (one plumber did $69,000 in his first four months and $110,000 the next quarter, r/sweatystartup 373).
- Numbers: search CPL $129.02 (LocaliQ 2025); LSA $57 (SearchLight Feb 2026); plumbing phone leads convert 55% on the call (Invoca 2026); ServiceTitan average-ticket target $500+ (dated).
- Specific: $99 drain-cleaning offers only work if camera inspections and replacements follow; drain-only franchises reportedly failed on lead cost (r/sweatystartup 17); camera inspections sell the explanation.

## HVAC

- Engine: maintenance agreements, LSA, seasonal shoulder-month tune-ups, financing, Good-better-best.
- Numbers: LSA $51 (SearchLight Feb 2026); HVAC converts 43% on the call (Invoca 2026), slowest replies of any trade (11% within an hour, Jobber 2026), 83% raised prices; repair share of revenue rose to 31.3% by Q4 2025 (Housecall Pro); average-ticket target $375+ (ServiceTitan, dated).
- Specific: tune-ups and plan sales in spring and fall, emergency capture in heat and cold waves; photographed wear and replacement timelines; include every cost in commercial replacement quotes (callback allowance, refrigerant, drive time, rentals: one 18-tech firm cleared 8%, r/sweatystartup 41); sticker every unit.

## Electrical

- Numbers: search CPL $93.69 (LocaliQ 2025); LSA $39, the cheapest trade in SearchLight's Feb 2026 data; ServiceTitan ticket target $575+ (dated).
- Specific: LSA first; license number visible on profile and site; panel and EV-charger work as high-ticket options; generator maintenance plans where storms are common.

## Seasonality calendar (Northern Hemisphere; shift six months south)

| Trade | Peak | Shoulder: pre-sell and plans | Trough: what to sell |
|---|---|---|---|
| HVAC | Cooling Jun to Aug; heating Nov to Feb | Mar to May and Sep to Oct: tune-ups, agreements | Indoor air quality, replacements with financing |
| Lawn and landscaping | Apr to Sep | Jan to Mar: door knock, sell season agreements | Leaf cleanup, holiday lights, snow, commercial bids |
| Pressure washing, windows, gutters | Apr to Jun, Sep to Nov (gutters in fall) | Jan to Mar: winter door knocking with deposits | Holiday light installs, commercial, interior windows |
| Pest | Spring to late summer | Feb to Apr: annual plans before pressure | Rodent work, renewals |
| Painting | Exterior May to Oct | Feb to Apr: book exteriors | Interior work, commercial repaints |
| Cleaning | Spring cleaning, move season May to Aug, holidays | Before holidays: book deep cleans | Recurring conversions, commercial |
| Junk removal | Spring and moving season | Estate and realtor partners | Commercial cleanouts |
| Detailing | Spring and summer | Pre-summer ceramic and packages | Interior and maintenance plans, fleets |
| Pet services | Holidays and summer travel (sitting) | Book holiday sitting early, with deposits | Training courses, grooming plans |
| Photographers | Wedding season, fall family sessions | Engagement and holiday mini sessions | Weekday elopements, courthouse weddings, events |
| Plumbing | Freezes, holidays (drains) | Water heater and winterizing | Remodel and repipe quotes |

Rules:

- Budget from last year's booked jobs by month, not leads or impressions.
- Publish seasonal content and GBP posts 4 to 6 weeks before the season; raise LSA budgets for the peak only if answer rate holds (harvest: localseoskills, unifapi, MIT).
- Pre-sell with deposits before the peak; route members into low-demand weeks.
- Compare ranking and lead data like season to like season.
- In the peak, protect answer rate before anything else; spend on high-intent capture.
