Never pool specialties: dental case acceptance, psychotherapy session economics and PT visits per case measure different businesses. Where a source publishes no true 75th percentile, the table says so. Store each KPI's definition and denominator with the number. URLs in [sources.md](sources.md).

Bias key: (V) vendor customer data or vendor survey, (P) platform self-report, (S) self-reported industry survey, (G) government or regulator, (O) operator report from Reddit with upvotes.

## Paid search (US)

LocaliQ, 3,542 US healthcare search campaigns, Oct 1, 2024 to Sep 30, 2025, via Medvisibility. "Lead" = ad-platform conversion, not an attended visit. Medians, no quartiles published.

| Specialty | CPC | Conversion rate | CPL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physical therapy | $4.95 | 15.35% | $32.79 |
| General dentistry | $7.03 | 7.74% | $84.77 |
| Mental health | $4.22 | 1.85% | $141.17 |

Arithmetic, not a benchmark: if half of dental leads attend, attended CAC is about $169.54 before staff or agency cost.

Meta lead ads, operator case: a Zurich dental clinic spent CHF 3,500 (about $3,800) for 166 leads, CHF 21 (about $23) CPL, 58 booked (35%) after 5 automated follow-ups (O, 131). Health and fitness Meta and Search rows live in `fitness` and `paid-ads`.
