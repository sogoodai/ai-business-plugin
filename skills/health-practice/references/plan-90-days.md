# 90-day plans

For a new, stalled or leaking practice. Order: instrument, fix access and conversion, fix discovery, fix retention, then add paid acquisition. Each phase ends with a short owner report: what changed, the scorecard, what they need to decide. The order follows the evidence (unanswered demand, wait time, plan completion), not a validated formula.

## Shared spine (every sub-model)

**Days 1 to 14: instrument and audit.**
- Inquiry record and stage taxonomy ([intake-and-conversion.md](intake-and-conversion.md#stage-taxonomy-set-up-on-day-1)); capture source before staff overwrite it.
- Baseline: answer rate (hours and after hours), median two-way reply time, qualified to booked, booked to attended, new-patient lead time per clinician, cancellation and no-show split, recovered cancellations, PVA, OVA, plan completion, CAC by source ([economics.md](economics.md)).
- Call the practice as a patient: count rings, check voicemail, ask about insurance. Book online on a phone and count taps.
- Tracking audit: every tag, every payload on booking and intake pages; remove pixels from booking, intake and portal ([reviews-and-compliance.md](reviews-and-compliance.md#4-pixels-analytics-and-tracking)). Freeze any patient-list upload or condition audience.
- Payer table: net rate by plan and code, days to pay, denials.
- Ask the owner: approved offers and clinical claims, fee policy limits by payer, who answers after hours, consent held.
- Exit: three biggest gaps written in money.

**Days 15 to 30: access and conversion.**
- Overflow phone path, missed-call text-back, one owner per inbox, 5-minute SLA in hours.
- First-reply and intake-call scripts live ([scripts.md](scripts.md)); "ask for the appointment" on every call; four end states only.
- Protected new-patient slots so lead time drops under 7 days; waitlist with first-confirmed-wins.
- Two-way reminders with one-tap reschedule; policy updated per payer ([no-shows-and-policies.md](no-shows-and-policies.md)).
- Exit: answer rate over 80% in hours, median reply under 15 minutes (target 5), no inquiry older than 24 hours without a state.

**Days 31 to 45: discovery.**
- Canonical practice record pushed to site, GBP, directories, EHR booking ([acquisition.md](acquisition.md#1-the-canonical-practice-record)).
- GBP: categories, services mirrored from site pages, booking link, hours, photos, plans in the description where allowed (depth: `local-seo`).
- Service and condition x location pages with insurance, price range, first-visit details and availability; clinician pages.
- Neutral review request to every eligible patient (not therapy clients); reply template on every review.
- Claim Healthgrades and profession directories; fix NPPES.

**Days 46 to 60: retention.**
- Book-the-plan rule at visit 1; re-exam dates; missed-visit triggers.
- Reactivation queues by reason ([retention-and-recall.md](retention-and-recall.md#reactivation-queues)); dormant list run once.
- Dropout curve by visit number; fix the visit where it drops.

**Days 61 to 75: referrals and paid acquisition.**
- Referrer capacity sheet to the top 20 referrers or likely referrers; closed-loop notes.
- Paid only if access KPIs hold: Google Search on high-intent terms with call tracking under a BAA; Meta only with authorized real video and neutral events.

**Days 76 to 90: allocate.**
- Attended CAC and 90-day contribution ratio by source; cut cheap but low-fit channels.
- If lead time drifts toward 14+ days, move money from acquisition to capacity.
- Payer review: keep, renegotiate, or exit with notice.
- Owner report: scorecard (inquiries, answer rate, reply time, booked, attended, lead time, cancellation, no-show, recovered cancellations, PVA or reappointment, plan completion, OVA, CAC by source, utilization).

## Talk therapy, solo

- Week 1: reply-speed fix, autoresponder with consult link, voicemail greeting with crisis line.
- Week 2: Psychology Today rewrite (problem-first opening, true filters, availability current, booking link). Add one more directory relevant to the niche (Zencare, Inclusive Therapists, Open Path for sliding scale, TherapyDen).
- Week 3: payer decision with the contribution model; if insurance, start credentialing or a platform now (3 to 6 months direct).
- Week 4: cancellation policy in informed consent; standing weekly slots.
- Days 31 to 60: peer referral list (10 colleagues with openings in other specialties), 5 physician or school counselor introductions with a capacity note, one group program concept if a population is unserved.
- Days 61 to 90: Google profile and a one-page site with niche pages; Search Ads only if cash caseload is short and reply speed holds; never request client reviews.
- Exit check: consults booked per week, consult to intake rate, sessions per week vs target from the caseload formula.

## Talk therapy, group

- Days 1 to 14: intake coordinator metrics, routing rules by clinician availability, profile for every clinician.
- Days 15 to 45: weekly fill report per clinician; targeted promotion of specific open clinicians; payer mix per clinician.
- Days 46 to 90: clinician retention review (split, admin load, referral fairness); the cost of a departing clinician's caseload is the benchmark for retention spend.

## Chiropractic

- Days 1 to 14: NP, PVA, OVA for the last 12 months; collections process; dropout curve.
- Days 15 to 30: report of findings rebuilt (goal, finding, plan, measure, money, booking); first block booked at visit 1.
- Days 31 to 45: reactivation of every past patient; GBP and reviews; neighbor walk-ins.
- Days 46 to 60: start one networking group (20 calls a day) and three partner lunches (massage, trainer, stylist or doula).
- Days 61 to 90: video ads with authorized patients and a low paid exam for cash patients (federal beneficiaries excluded); wellness membership for discharged patients if state rules allow.
- Exit check: PVA up, OVA stable or up, NP from free channels before paid.

## Physical therapy

- Days 1 to 14: referral log (received, contacted, scheduled, attended) by referrer; same-day call to every new referral.
- Days 15 to 30: book-the-plan at visit 1; capacity sheet built.
- Days 31 to 60: capacity sheet delivered to 20 referrers with a named contact; eval and discharge notes back to each; gym and trainer partnerships at fair market terms.
- Days 61 to 90: direct-access pages and Google Search on condition plus location (median CPL $32.79 as sanity check); cash niche page (pelvic health, running, vestibular) if the clinician has it.
- Exit check: referral to attended eval rate, visits per case vs plan, lead time.

## Dental

- Days 1 to 14: call audit (answer rate, missed calls not returned, booking asked), lead time, hygiene reappointment, unscheduled treatment list size, collection rate definition.
- Days 15 to 30: phone scripts with insurance answer in minute one; missed-call callbacks within 15 minutes; two emergency slots daily.
- Days 31 to 45: pre-appoint hygiene at checkout, target +10 points; unscheduled treatment list worked weekly; plan phasing and financing in writing.
- Days 46 to 60: insurance and membership plan pages; GBP services mirrored; neutral review flow.
- Days 61 to 90: Search Ads for emergency, insurance and procedure terms; Meta for one high-value procedure with real clinic video and authorized patients; dormant list reactivation.
- Exit check: new patients per month, reappointment, acceptance and completion, lead time.

## Dietitian

- Days 1 to 14: decide insurance vs cash vs platform; program structure.
- Days 15 to 45: physician and prescriber referral sheet to 20 practices; niche landing pages; telehealth booking.
- Days 46 to 90: one content series with clinical review; employer or gym partnership; track referral to first visit and visits per program.

## Acupuncture and cash-pay clinicians

- Days 1 to 14: rebook rate at checkout, visits per course, price and package review.
- Days 15 to 45: course-of-care booking at visit 1; GBP and reviews; space-share or referral swap with a chiro or PT.
- Days 46 to 90: fertility, pregnancy or pain-clinic partnerships with compliant claims; reactivation.

## Optometry

- Days 1 to 14: recall rate, optical capture by definition, contact lens auto-ship share.
- Days 15 to 45: recall cadence by text and email; same-visit optical hand-off; unused-benefit reminders.
- Days 46 to 90: medical services pages (dry eye, myopia control); diabetic exam reports to PCPs; Search Ads on "eye exam [city] [plan]."
