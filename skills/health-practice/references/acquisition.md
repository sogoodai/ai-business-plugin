# Getting patients

Order of operations: answer and convert what already arrives ([intake-and-conversion.md](intake-and-conversion.md)), then free listings and referrers, then paid directories, then ads. GBP depth lives in `local-seo`; ad account mechanics in `paid-ads`.

## 1. The canonical practice record

Keep one record and push it everywhere (site, GBP, directories, EHR booking, referral sheet): legal name, specialties and services, clinician roster with credentials, phone, address, parking, hours, accepted plans by exact name, Medicare and Medicaid status, self-pay prices or ranges, languages, telehealth, typical new-patient lead time, booking URL. Google builds profiles from the business, crawled pages, third parties and users (Google 2026): conflicting data is competing evidence.

Tricks:
- Plan names as visible text on the site, never only a logo strip or PDF.
- One page per clinician. AI and branded searches increasingly resolve to a person ("Dr. X physical therapist reviews").
- Publish answerable facts: "We accept Aetna, Cigna and Medicare," "New patients are usually seen within 5 days," "First visit is 60 minutes," "No referral needed in [state]."

## 2. Google Business Profile for clinics

- Primary category = the revenue-defining specialty (Chiropractor, Physical therapist, Dentist, Psychotherapist, Dietitian, Acupuncturist, Optometrist). Up to 9 secondary categories that describe the business, not keywords (Google 2026).
- Real business name only; keyword-stuffed names risk suspension.
- Services list mirrors the site's service pages word for word. A dentist reports their local ranking score moving from 5.66 to 2.68 after aligning GBP service descriptions 1:1 with landing pages and reusing snippets as posts (r/localseo, 39; tool score, not traffic).
- Booking link goes to real availability, not a contact form.
- Practitioner listings: in multi-clinician clinics, Google allows individual practitioner profiles; decide with the owner, because a departing clinician's profile can keep pulling calls.
- A chiro clinic reports showing up in "near me" searches after fixing hours, services, photos and holiday hours and asking every patient for a review, replying to all (operators report, 16).
- Reviews at volume do not fix a dead funnel: a 13-year chiro with 200+ five-star reviews gets about one inquiry a week in a 5-mile radius holding 50 chiropractors (operators report, 23). Check answer rate, booking path and differentiation before more reviews.

Review requests and replies under HIPAA: [reviews-and-compliance.md](reviews-and-compliance.md).

## 3. Psychology Today (therapists, psychiatric NPs, some dietitians and coaches)

- $29.95 a month, no contract, no premium placement. Sorting uses relevance, completeness, user preferences, engagement and rotating exposure (Psychology Today terms 2026).
- The search card shows the photo and only the first lines of the personal statement (estimates vary from about 190 to 250 characters; vendor blogs). Those lines decide the click.
- First lines formula: who you help + the problem in their words + how + availability. Example: "Burned-out professionals with ADHD who are tired of starting over. Practical, skills-based therapy, evenings available, in person in [city] or online across [state]."
- Fill every truthful filter: insurance, issues, modalities, age groups, languages, faith, telehealth, sliding scale. Do not tick 20 issues; a short, true specialty list reads as expertise (blog claim, untested).
- Keep "accepting new clients" and availability current. A stale "full" flag silently kills inquiries.
- Add a direct booking or consult link if offered. Zencare data: bookable consult intake completed 73.7% vs 40.4% for message intake (2025).
- Operators: an Alberta psychologist says a niche-focused paid profile (ADHD, burnout, executive functioning) plus peer referrals filled 15 to 25 clients a week (873). Coaches with a counseling degree report most clients from Psychology Today and referrals, zero from social (r/lifecoaching, 59 and 22).
- Measure monthly: views (diagnostic only), calls and emails, fit inquiries, consults, intakes, clients still attending at session 4.

## 4. Zocdoc

- New-patient discovery booking fee $30 to $140 by specialty and location, charged at booking even if the patient cancels or no-shows; existing-patient, branded, GBP and website-widget bookings can be free (Zocdoc 2026).
- Sponsored Results is a separate auction on top of the booking fee; rank = relevance + bid. Relevance inputs Zocdoc names: reviews, accepted insurance, availability and visit reason.
- Before any bid: every accepted plan listed, every visit reason enabled, near-term slots open (availability is part of matching), reviews flowing.
- Bid by visit reason using the ceiling in [economics.md](economics.md#5-zocdoc-bid-ceiling). Multi-visit reasons (PT eval, new-patient dental exam with likely treatment) can carry a bid; one-off reasons often cannot.
- Reconcile the monthly invoice against attended patients. Dispute existing-patient charges; Zocdoc credits confirmed existing patients.
- Use the Zocdoc widget on your own site and GBP to keep those bookings fee-free where the contract allows.

## 5. Healthgrades, Vitals, WebMD and vertical directories

- Every US clinician with an NPI has a Healthgrades profile; claim it, correct NPI-derived specialty and address, add photo and care philosophy (Healthgrades 2026).
- Sponsored profiles appear above organic results and on competitors' profiles, syndicated to Healthline, Sharecare and Medical News Today. "Completed profiles get twice the appointment requests" is a platform claim with no method.
- Fix the NPPES record first; directories copy it.
- Country equivalents: Doctolib (France, Germany), Jameda (Germany), Healthengine and HotDoc (Australia), Rate My Treatment and Doctify (UK). Same rule: claim free, pay only with source tracking.
- Profession directories: APTA Find a PT, ACA and state chiro associations, ADA Find-a-Dentist, Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics Find a Nutrition Expert, NCCAOM, AOA Doctor Locator. Free or member-included, and they feed AI answers.

## 6. Insurance platforms and volume marketplaces

**Therapy insurance platforms (Headway, Alma, Grow, Rula, Sondermind):** they credential you with payers, bill, and sometimes send referrals. Headway says joining is free and it negotiates rates; Alma charges a membership fee; Grow is paid through a share of reimbursements (vendor pages, 2026). Credentialing claims of 2 to 4 weeks are Headway marketing; direct payer credentialing commonly takes 3 to 6 months.

Agent checklist before joining: net rate per CPT code vs direct contract, who owns the client relationship if you leave, whether you can also list privately on Psychology Today, referral volume promised, cancellation and no-show rules, documentation requirements.

**Dietitians (Nourish, Fay, Berry Street and similar):** insurance-billed telehealth networks. Nourish lists $145 self-pay and says 94% of in-network patients pay $0 (2026). Useful for volume; your own practice still needs a direct channel.

**Volume therapy platforms (BetterHelp model):** a 30-year therapist reported $30 an hour rising $5 per 5 weekly hours to a $70 cap at 35+ hours, a $650 insurance stipend only at 120 logged hours a month, $10 paid for late cancels, short 30 to 45 minute sessions, and referrals that stopped after a year (operators report, 739). Treat as bridge income.

Trick: test your own listing as a patient every quarter. The same therapist found referrals had been switched off on the platform's side only when a family referral could not reach her (739).

## 7. Referrals from professionals

**PT and chiro:** 90% of recent PT patients were physician-referred and 88% of PCPs want to refer more (APTA 2025; 2024). The referral is not the patient: 28% of prospective PT patients held a referral they had not used (APTA 2025).

The referrer capacity sheet (one page, refreshed monthly): conditions and populations you see, accepted plans, current new-patient lead time, direct-access rules for your state, how to refer (fax, eReferral, secure email), a named contact, what you send back and when. Script in [scripts.md](scripts.md#referrer-outreach).

Closed loop: acknowledge every referral within one business day, call the patient the same day (they hold the referral, not you), send an eval summary and a discharge note with patient authorization. Report referral received to attended eval by referrer, monthly.

**Who refers to whom:**
- PT and chiro: PCPs, orthopedists, podiatrists, OB-GYNs (pregnancy, pelvic health), personal trainers, massage therapists, gyms, running clubs. A trainer with a paid PT network reports about 95% close on PT and naturopath referrals (operators report, 89).
- Therapists: PCPs, pediatricians, psychiatrists, school counselors, EAPs, divorce attorneys, OB-GYNs (perinatal), other therapists with full caseloads (keep a referral list of peers with openings and they will keep yours).
- Dentists: physicians, pediatricians, sleep physicians (oral appliances), specialists who refer back after treatment.
- Dietitians: PCPs, endocrinologists, GI, bariatric programs, obesity medicine and GLP-1 prescribers, eating-disorder therapists, OB-GYNs.
- Acupuncture: fertility clinics, OB-GYNs, chiro and PT, oncology support programs, pain clinics.
- Optometry: PCPs and endocrinologists (diabetic eye exams), pediatricians, schools.

**Cross-referral rules:** no payment, gift or rent arrangement tied to referral volume (anti-kickback rules in the US). Sharing space at fair market rent is fine; "free room in exchange for referrals" is not.

## 8. Local networking (chiro, PT, acupuncture, cash practices)

From a chiropractor who built a practice this way (operators report, 27):
- Run your own 20-minute coffee groups for local business owners: everyone says what they do and how others can help, exchange cards, email the next date, each person invites one more. Run several time slots (mornings, lunches, after work). Groups ran 4 to 16 people.
- Fill them by calling the professions that attend everything: insurance agents, realtors, bankers, photographers. Quota: 20 calls a day, or until 20 no's.
- Walk into neighboring businesses with food and a card, a 20-second introduction, and your planner so people can book on the spot.
- Lunches with natural referral partners: massage therapists, trainers, podiatrists, doulas (pregnancy and pediatrics), hair stylists (neck, shoulder, wrist and headache complaints). Go to learn, not to pitch.
- Workplace entry through existing patients: ask for the HR, morale or wellness committee contact (script in [scripts.md](scripts.md#workplace-introduction)).

Massage therapists report renting space inside chiro offices as their best referral source (operators report, 199); flip it: rent a room to an LMT or acupuncturist and build a two-way flow, at fair market rent.

Avoid what fails: fishbowl lead-capture marketing done off the clock by staff and scripted "spinal screenings" read as pressure (operators report, 45).

## 9. Content, website and AI search

- Pages answer concrete intent: one page per service or condition x location where clinically appropriate, each with price range or insurance, referral or direct-access rule, first-visit logistics and current availability (Google AI Mode query fan-out, 2025 to 2026).
- Low-authority sites win long-tail first: "chiropractor for scoliosis in [small city]" or "best stretches for SI joint pain," not "chiropractor near me." A marketer reports sites with DR/DA 8 to 20 get posts indexed and trafficked within weeks, sites with none do not regardless of age (operators report, 22).
- Answer the exact question patients ask AI: "which dentist in [town] accepts [plan]" pages (r/localseo, 34; unmeasured).
- Show the clinician: face, voice, a 60-second "what your first visit looks like" video, office walk-through, parking. A chiro rebuilding a clinic puts the doctor's presence and "why" at the center of the site (operators report, 52).
- Dental FAQ addresses fear directly: how the practice keeps visits comfortable, sedation options, what happens at visit 1 (Moz AMA on r/bigseo, 43).
- Therapist bios: "Psychologist teaching you the exact CBT techniques I use in session" beats "psychologist helping with anxiety and depression" (operators report, 225).
- Schema: `Dentist`, `Physician`, `MedicalClinic`, `Optician`, or `MedicalBusiness` subtypes with `availableService`, `medicalSpecialty`, `isAcceptingNewPatients`.

Content system depth: `organic-social`. Pages and conversion: `website-conversion`. AI answer checks: `local-seo`.

## 10. Google Ads

- Only after answer rate, speed to lead, lead time and tracking are fixed.
- Campaigns by intent: brand (defend), urgent (dental emergency, "same day"), service + location ("physical therapy for back pain [city]"), insurance + service where allowed ("Aetna dentist near me"). Keep research terms ("what is cognitive therapy") out or in a separate low-budget campaign.
- Medians to sanity-check: PT $32.79 CPL at 15.35% CVR, dental $84.77 at 7.74%, mental health $141.17 at 1.85% (LocaliQ 2025). Mental health converts poorly on broad terms; lead with modality and niche terms.
- Call tracking with recording only under a BAA; call is often the conversion in dental and PT.
- Optimize to qualified booking or attended visit fed back offline, through a privacy-reviewed setup with no health data in the payload. Never Customer Match, remarketing lists or first-party segments for sensitive health conditions (Google personalized advertising policy, 2025 to 2026).
- Certification: addiction treatment, some telehealth and prescription services need Google healthcare certification (often LegitScript). Check before launch.
- Local Services Ads exist for some health categories in some markets; check availability per category and city.

## 11. Meta ads

- Works best for cash-pay services with visible results and a low-barrier first step: chiro, dental cosmetic, PT for specific problems, acupuncture, nutrition programs.
- Real clinic video beats stock. Dental: real staff and premises lifted CTR 29%; procedure-specific USP (premium veneers, best All-on-4 price) tripled conversion vs generic (operators report, 131). Chiro micro practice: filmed sessions with willing patients plus a very low exam fee drove 56 then 80 new patients a month (operators report, 65). Written HIPAA marketing authorization for every patient on camera.
- Health and wellness data limits: since January 2025 Meta restricts some health businesses' lower-funnel events (Purchase, Schedule and similar) and URL parameters, with severity by category; check Events Manager for your restriction tier (agency and vendor reports, 2025). Since September 2, 2025 Meta flags custom audiences and conversions implying health conditions (Meta).
- Use neutral event names (`Lead`, `QualifiedLead`, `AppointmentBooked`) sent server-side without health data, and Meta lead forms with immediate follow-up. Depth: `paid-ads`.
- DIY risk: a solo chiro lost money on two months of self-taught Google and Meta ads; four agencies each "started good and faded" (operators report, 23). Another pays $2,500 a month to an agency including $1,000 Meta spend and still struggles to get patients in (17). Own the account, measure attended CAC monthly, and cut at the first month that misses.

## 12. Community and outreach

- Therapy groups: a therapist built a wheelchair-accessible gardening therapy group around local loneliness; the beta filled and members asked to return next year (operators report, 889). Groups lower price per client and fill a niche nobody else serves.
- Employer and school talks: one talk, one sign-up link, one offer (free screening for cash patients, lunch-and-learn).
- Professional courtesy: chiropractors treat colleagues and students free and give a relocated colleague's family member a week of care, then a discount (operators report, 28). Cheap, builds referral goodwill.
