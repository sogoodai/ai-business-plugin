# Expensive mistakes

Two tables: what the data shows (measured, or arithmetic on measured numbers) and what practitioners report losing.

## From the data

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Buying leads while the phone leaks | 46% of healthcare calls never reached a person; 45% of qualified phone leads booked (Invoca 2026). Dental: 1.45M of 11.55M calls ended without a booking, 890,000+ never called back (Patient Prism 2025) | Answer rate, missed-call text-back and callbacks before any spend |
| Not asking for the appointment | 43% of organizations never asked callers to book (Invoca 2026) | Two concrete times on every call |
| Slow replies | Under-5-minute human reply booked over 3x the rate of 24h+ (2026, 9 practices); average therapist reply 28 hours (Zencare 2025) | 5-minute SLA in hours, self-booking after hours |
| Long new-patient waits while spending on ads | About 70 vs 6 bookings per 100 searches at under 7 vs 30+ days wait (DexCare 2026); dental lead time 25 days vs 4.5 top 10% (HS1 2026) | Protected new-patient slots; shift budget to capacity when lead time passes 14 days |
| Optimizing to form fills | Mental health median CPL $141.17 at 1.85% CVR (LocaliQ 2025) looks fine and can lose money per retained client | Report attended CAC and 90-day contribution by source |
| Treating cancellations as final | 14.4% cancellation + 6.2% no-show = about 206 lost visits per 1,000 scheduled (Planet DDS 2025 arithmetic) | Reschedule offer, waitlist backfill, recovered-cancellation KPI |
| Counting acceptance, not completion | 56% accepted vs 46% completed (Planet DDS 2025) | Schedule every phase; weekly unscheduled-treatment list |
| Review replies that confirm a patient | $30,000 (Manasa 2023), $23,000 (New Vision Dental 2022) HHS settlements | Non-confirming template for every reply |
| Testimonials and photos without authorization | $25,000 (Complete P.T., HHS OCR) | Signed HIPAA marketing authorization per patient |
| Pixels on booking and intake pages | HHS guidance exposure; OAIC breach findings against Medmate and Monash IVF (2026); Meta cutting health conversion signals (2025) | Tag audit; no pixels on booking, intake, portal; neutral server-side events under a BAA |
| Condition-named audiences or events | Meta restriction from September 2, 2025; Google prohibits condition-based personalized targeting | Neutral names, contextual and location targeting |
| Assuming cash pay is more profitable | Cash-only therapists' profit only about $3,000 above insurance-accepting despite $15,000 more revenue (Heard 2026) | Model contribution per hour by payer after CAC and vacancy |
| Comparing collection rates across definitions | HS1 80% vs Planet DDS production on expected collections: not comparable | Store each KPI's definition with the number |
| Fixed visit targets regardless of outcome | Payer scrutiny and dissatisfaction; WebPT warns its 10 visits per case needs outcome context | Plan by goals, re-exam on dates, report dropout and early success apart |
| Free exam ads that reach Medicare patients | Beneficiary inducement exposure above $15 per item, $75 a year (OIG 2016) | Exclude federal beneficiaries in the offer or drop it |
| Charging Medicaid patients no-show fees | Prohibited under long-standing CMS policy (reconfirmed 2015) | Policy wording without fees for Medicaid |

## From practitioners

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Chasing new patients with low PVA and OVA | $5,000 a month in marketing for $3,600 of revenue (chiro series, 52) | Fix visits per case and collections first |
| DIY Google and Meta ads from YouTube | Two months, lost money; four agencies each faded after a good start (chiro, 23) | Own the account, measure attended CAC monthly, cut fast |
| Agency retainer without outcome tracking | $2,500 a month incl. $1,000 Meta, still struggling to get patients in (chiro, 17) | Contract on reporting of attended patients, not leads |
| Giving the exam and advice free | Rearranged the schedule, came in early, patient left untreated, nothing billed (first-year DC, 25) | State that the evaluation is billable; low paid exam for cash patients |
| Presenting a long plan before trust | "This all feels very sales and not very medical"; patient left to "think" (DC, 25) | Goal first, measure, short first block, re-exam date |
| Scripted volume model | Staff off-the-clock fishbowl marketing, 1,000 visits a week targets, staff churn (CA in an AMPED office, 45) | Patient-centered visits; staff time paid |
| Location without parking | Caseload "very difficult" to build inside a great downtown gym (PT, 268) | Choose access and parking over prestige |
| Percentage rent for too long | $15 a session at 15 sessions a week is about $1,000 a month (PT, 268) | Fixed rent around $700 for a room; percentage only 3 to 4 months |
| Living on a volume therapy platform | $30 to $70 an hour, $10 per late cancel, referrals shut off after a year with a 75-client caseload (therapist, 739) | Platform as bridge, own directory and referral channels |
| No cancellation policy | 4 cancellations in a day, 4 more over two days, one client referred out (therapist, 886); $8.50 effective hourly on a bad day (agency clinician, 904) | Written policy, fee where allowed, waitlist |
| Courtesy discounts on dental work | $1,200 off four crowns, still accused of overcharging (dentist, 393) | Standard fees, written estimate, no ad hoc discounts |
| Doing procedures at money-losing insurance rates | $30 to $35 kept on a $90 extraction vs $115 for an exam and PA (dentist, 164) | Review per-procedure contribution; refer out or renegotiate |
| Staying on a bad plan too long | Years of denials; plan raised only 3 to 6% on a few codes; Medicaid paid better (dentist, 271) | Annual payer review; exit with notice and patient communication |
| Lots of reviews, no differentiation | 200+ five-star reviews, one inquiry a week among 50 local chiros (23) | Fix booking path and positioning; niche service pages |
| Not telling patients before a move | Patients tracked the clinician down, upset (massage, beauty threads) | Tell regulars where you are going, before you go |
