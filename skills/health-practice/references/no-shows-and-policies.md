# No-shows, cancellations and policies

Rule: build cancellation recovery, not punishment. A patient who cancels early is inventory you can resell; a no-show is inventory lost. Template policies here are not legal advice; check payer contracts and local law.

## Baselines

- Dental: 14.4% advance cancellation, 6.2% no-show (Planet DDS H1 2025, 5,200+ practices); 4% average no-show, under 1% top 10% (Henry Schein One 2026, different cohort).
- UK chiro: 11.9% cancellation, 8.2% DNA, the highest of MSK specialties because of 2 to 3 visits a week (survey posted by its author, operators report, 15).
- Therapy: no neutral benchmark. Whole days of cancellations are common in r/therapists (886 and 966 upvote threads); one clinician at an agency with no cancellation fee earned $8.50 an hour on a day with two no-shows and a cancellation (904).
- Telehealth: missed-appointment odds ratio 0.61 vs in person, stronger in mental health (2025 meta-analysis, confounded). Offer a telehealth switch instead of a cancellation where clinically fine.
- Track cancellation and no-show separately, by clinician, weekday, lead time and new vs established.

## Reminder system

- Two-way reminders with one-tap reschedule. 69% of patients want to reschedule without calling (Tebra 2026).
- Cadence that worked for a dental clinic: 24 hours before, morning of, 1 hour before; no-shows fell 30% within a month (operators report, 131).
- Healthcare RCT evidence: reminders raise attendance (RR 1.11, 10 RCTs) and adding an automated call to SMS cut high-risk no-shows from 11.3% to 9.6% (2026; see `email-sms-loyalty`).
- Extra touch for new patients, long lead times (booked 3+ weeks out), and anyone with a prior no-show.
- Reminder content: date, time, clinician, address or video link, how to change it, what to bring. No diagnosis, procedure detail or condition in the text.

## Cancellation recovery

1. Every cancellation offers two new times or a self-book link immediately.
2. The freed slot goes to the waitlist by text, first confirmed wins.
3. Each cancellation ends as rescheduled, waitlisted or lost; report the recovered cancellation rate weekly.
4. Track by lead time: if far-out bookings cancel most, shorten booking horizons or add a confirmation step 7 days out.

## Fees: what is allowed

| Payer | Can you charge a missed-appointment fee? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cash and self-pay | Yes, with a signed policy | Card on file or deposit for new patients makes it collectible |
| Commercial insurance | Usually, if the contract allows and the policy applies to all patients | Read each contract; bill the patient, never the plan |
| Medicare (US) | Yes, if the same policy applies to all patients and they were told in advance | CMS Claims Processing Manual ch. 1, 30.3.13; MLN MM5613 (2007). Bill the patient, not Medicare |
| Medicaid (US) | Generally no | CMS has held for decades that Medicaid members cannot be charged for missed or cancelled appointments, reconfirmed 2015 (Colorado HCPF; Kentucky, Texas and others) |
| Volume platforms | Platform rules | One platform paid the therapist $10 for a late cancel (operators report, 739) |

Common amounts reported by operators: $50 for cancellation under 24 hours (PT office) and $60 for no-shows with the balance due before rescheduling (former practice manager), both from one r/smallbusiness thread (571). A dentist charges a fee for under 24 hours' notice and dismisses habitual hagglers (209). Set the fee as a missed business opportunity, not the full session fee, where you want it to feel fair.

## Policy wording

Template ([scripts.md](scripts.md#no-show-and-cancellation-policy)): 24 hours' notice, fee up to [amount] where law and payer agreement allow, how to reschedule, emergencies reviewed individually. Avoid punitive language that discourages clinically necessary care.

Rollout: tell all patients before the policy changes, collect signatures at the next visit, apply to everyone equally, waive the first instance once with a note on the account.

## Therapy specifics

- Late-cancel fee for the full or partial session fee is common for cash clients; insurance clients pay the fee themselves (never billed to the plan), Medicaid clients cannot be charged.
- Recurring same-slot clients who cancel repeatedly: offer a different standing slot or move them to an as-available schedule.
- A therapist with four cancellations in two days ended up referring one client out for inconsistency (operators report, 886). Write the attendance expectation into informed consent.

## Dental, PT, chiro specifics

- Pre-appointed hygiene booked 6 months out cancels more: confirm 2 weeks out, again 2 days out, and keep a short-notice list.
- PT and chiro plans at 2 to 3 visits a week: book the whole first block, send the weekly schedule in one message, and move rather than cancel.
- Dental chair time for crowns: deposit for long procedures on cash patients.

## Dismissal

A dentist's rule: one warning for abusive behavior; immediate dismissal for threats, mentions of lawsuits or board complaints, or chronic haggling (operators report, 209). Follow the profession's abandonment rules: written notice, interim emergency care window, help transferring records. Never dismiss in a public reply.
