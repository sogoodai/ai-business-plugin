# Expensive mistakes

Two tables: what the data and platform terms show, and what practitioners report losing.

## From the data and terms

| Mistake | What it costs | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing Etsy goods on the 6.5% fee alone | Offsite Ads add 15% (12% above $10,000 trailing sales) on attributed orders; a $35 mug drops from 26% to 11% margin ([pricing.md](pricing.md), Etsy 2026) | Price every SKU so the Offsite Ads case clears the owner's floor |
| Letting Faire claim shops you found yourself | 15% commission plus $10 on the opening order versus 0% via Faire Direct; on a 12-candle order, $1.32 versus $4.25 a unit (Faire 2026) | Send every self-sourced shop your own Faire Direct link |
| Choosing next-day Faire payout by habit | 3.5% + $0.30 versus 1.9% + $0.30 at 60 days: 1.6 points on every order (Faire 2026) | Pick the slowest payout cash flow allows |
| Copying US TikTok Shop math to the UK | 6% versus 9% commission: 3 points before other costs (TikTok 2025 to 2026) | Separate margin model per market |
| Newsletters only, no flows | 0.16% campaign versus 2.11% flow placed-order rate (Klaviyo 2026) | Welcome, cart, post-purchase, replenishment or collector preview flows first |
| Hiding art prices | 43% of online art buyers cite a missing price as an obstacle; 48% missing information (Artsy 2025) | Price, size, medium, framing and shipping on every work |
| Commercial rights at a personal-commission price | Broad, long, worldwide licenses carry materially greater value (AOI) | Quote usage, territory and duration separately |
| Gallery discounts with no clause | The artist learns of the discount after the sale; model agreements cap shared discounts around 15% (research report 2026) | Write discount authority and who absorbs it |
| Small Patreon tiers | 10% plus 2.9% + $0.30 for new creators after 4 Aug 2025: the fixed fee is 10% of a $3 tier on its own (Patreon 2026) | Tiers with real repeat value; avoid sub-$5 tiers |
| AI-mocked product photos on Etsy | Since Oct 2026, at least one unedited real photo is required; enforcement for misleading AI use (Etsy) | Real item, real background, then mood shots |
| Therapeutic soap claims | Moves a soap into drug regulation (FDA) | Cleaning, scent and look only, or comply as a drug |
| Assuming handmade kids items are exempt | No small-business exemption from CPSIA tracking labels (CPSC) | CPC, tracking label, small batch registration, testing where required |
| Selling a "limited" print you can reprint | California treats a stated edition size as an express warranty for $100+ multiples (Civil Code 1740 to 1745) | Open editions are called open editions |

## From practitioners

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| No deposit on a large custom painting | Client approved progress photos of a $900 24x36 painting, then offered to cover supplies only (r/ArtBusiness, 56 pts) | 50% non-refundable deposit, balance before delivery |
| Accepting a custom without a shipping quote | 12 lb crochet spider: $200 order lost to $60 shipping; chunky blanket: 48+ hours, $460 shipping, refunded with a bad review (r/EtsySellers, 1,744 pts) | Pack, weigh and quote before accepting; refund overage |
| Absorbing priority shipping in a $12 item | 93 rose orders, about $1,000 before materials and gas, close to no profit (r/EtsySellers, 657 pts) | Charge for upgrades; end free priority |
| Underpricing to get first reviews | First 15 orders at almost zero profit; a $128 stone tray left $46 after shipping, customs and fees before labor; later price rises stalled sales (r/Etsy, 195 pts) | Price for real costs from order one; earn reviews with photos and service |
| Paying a $75 fee at an empty venue | $40 in sales plus surprise coupon obligations (r/ArtBusiness, 93 pts) | Visit or ask past vendors; get obligations in writing |
| Paying an "art fair representation" agency | $2,000 lost, five fake sale contracts (r/ArtBusiness, 89 pts) | Check official exhibitor lists; call listed artists |
| Prestigious gallery with travel costs | Very little income and debt from commissions plus show travel (r/ArtBusiness) | Model net per piece; try local low-fee spaces |
| Trusting a gallery to display | 16 framed oils left in the back during a 50,000-visitor festival (r/ArtBusiness, 103 pts) | Visit during the event; written display commitment |
| Trusting a consignment shop's "zero sales" | Stock never put on the shelves for a month (r/Entrepreneur, 1,115 pts) | Visit, photograph, reconcile monthly |
| Solo show at a venue without an audience | Four Friday evenings, invitations to about 100 people, nobody came (r/ArtBusiness, 84 pts) | Verify the invitation list size; your own list does the inviting |
| Instagram ads to Redbubble prints | Sales at a net loss, the only Instagram income in 5 years (r/ArtBusiness, 77 pts) | Ads only to own listings with margin; or none |
| Etsy Ads judged on clicks | $121, 26,000 impressions, 276 clicks, zero sales (r/Etsy, 195 pts) | Judge on orders; fix listing conversion first |
| Ignoring stolen photos | Etsy deactivated a seasonal bestseller for months; a few thousand in lost sales (r/Etsy, 2,434 pts) | Monitor copies before peak season; keep making-of proof |
| Accepting Etsy's AI title suggestion with an artist's name | 3 violations, a lawyer's letter, all listings removed (r/EtsySellers, 346 pts) | No artist or brand names anywhere in listings |
| Going viral with no order cap | 800 orders in 48 hours, unanswered messages, bad reviews (r/TikTokshop, 67 pts) | Max order quantity and a stated production window |
| Low-priced PDF pattern with heavy support | $6 crochet pattern, 6 months to write, constant "where are my booties" messages, a handful of sales (r/EtsySellers, 239 pts) | "Digital pattern, not a finished item" in the first photo; price for support |
| Starting with too many scents | Operators advise 5 to 6 scents in 2 sizes; perfumer advice caps the range at 10 SKUs (r/candlemaking) | Small core, rotate seasonal |
| Chasing every new fragrance release | Sales concentrate 80/20; new scents peak for a few months then flatline (r/candlemaking, 213 pts) | Core library plus a few seasonal tests |
| Going full time on one good month | A $5,000 month followed by a slow summer; back to a day job and still paying debt (r/ArtBusiness) | Reserve for slow seasons; reinvest early profits while employed |
| Withholding a paid order to bundle an unpurchased custom | A week's delay and an angry buyer (r/Etsy, 1,259 pts) | Ship what is paid; quote the custom separately |
| Blocking a buyer who asked about a digital file | The buyer bought a similar print elsewhere (r/Etsy, 943 pts) | Answer politely; say what you do sell |
| Partial refund on a failed $4 gift wrap | Public complaint and a review over $1.25 (r/Etsy, 583 pts) | Refund small failures in full |
| Booking a fixed-date order on "fast shipping" from abroad | 35 days was not enough after a customs paperwork gap (r/ArtBusiness, 85 pts) | Order event stock with weeks of slack |
| Saying yes to a giant custom wholesale order | Net 60, custom labels and returns would have demanded cash the maker did not have (r/smallbusiness, 925 pts) | Prepaid trial first |
