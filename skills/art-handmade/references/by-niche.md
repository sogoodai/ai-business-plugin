# By niche

Each niche lists what is different: the engine, the numbers that matter, the traps and the tricks. Shared mechanics live in the other references.

## Fine artists (originals)

- **Engine:** collector list, juried shows, open studios, private showings, then galleries. Direct-from-artist is 20% of HNW collector spend (Art Basel/UBS 2025).
- **Price:** one method per body of work (square or linear inch from a sold anchor), studio price equals gallery price, raise after a series sells through ([pricing.md](pricing.md)).
- **Show transparency:** price, dimensions, medium, framing, shipping and an in-room photo on request. 69% of collectors hesitated over transparency (Artsy 2025).
- **Small originals as the entry ticket:** $100 to $150 small works and framed 4x6 or 5x7 drops (operators report).
- **Local subjects to local and visiting buyers:** landscapes of the place where the market is held sold well enough to be a part-time job for one painter (operators report).
- **Hybrid income is normal:** grants and fellowships ($40,000 in one painter's year), workshops, commissions. One painter made $0 from social media in two years and still neared $100,000 (operators report).
- **Agent on royalties:** a relative handling the business for perpetual royalties took one painter to about $1M a year (operators report). Rare; get any royalty deal in writing.
- **Traps:** pay-to-show agencies, prestige galleries with travel costs, venues without an audience ([mistakes.md](mistakes.md)).

## Illustrators, print sellers and commission artists

- **Engine:** markets and cons with prints, cards and stickers; commissions; licensing; Patreon; Etsy.
- **Quantity ladders lift baskets:** the 562-sale, $5,000-profit market ([pricing.md](pricing.md)).
- **Usage-based quotes for business clients** (AOI); floors per format for personal commissions.
- **Low-cost handmade merch:** draw directly on leftover sticker paper with Posca markers, cover with glitter or cracked-glass laminate offcuts, sell as tiny originals (operators report). Printed bookmarks with cut corners and hand-beaded tassels sell at markets with "huge margins" at real labor cost (operators report).
- **Batch your own stickers** rather than dropship them; cheap to order and ship (operators report, five years).
- **POD as test and long tail:** InPrnt pays artists 50% of profit; chase late payouts directly (operators report). Redbubble fees took 79% of one artist's margin (r/printondemand). Move proven designs to stocked prints.
- **Prints versus AI:** one Etsy artist is phasing out on-demand prints and selling mostly originals because competing with AI prints is unrealistic (r/Etsy, 16-pt comment). Physical, signed, embellished or original work is the defensible end.
- **Stock and books:** Adobe Stock Premium from leftover concepts; coloring books on Amazon at $1,000 to $1,500 a month for one artist ([commissions-and-licensing.md](commissions-and-licensing.md)).

## Candle makers

- **Engine:** markets first (98% of one maker's first-season sales), then Etsy, wholesale to boutiques, refills and subscriptions.
- **Range:** 5 to 6 scents in 2 sizes to launch; 10 SKUs total including seasonal; 8 to 10 oz sizes sell best; a vanilla-type staple; florals for positioning (operators report and perfumer advice).
- **Price examples:** 6.5 oz $16, 10 oz $24 (one first-year maker); landmark-shaped candles €29.99 unscented, €34.99 scented (operators report). Worked cost and channel math in [pricing.md](pricing.md).
- **"I won't buy a candle I didn't smell first"** is the top online objection: sample tins as a gift-ready discovery kit, a scent quiz built from the questions you ask in person, tea-light samples (operators report, planned).
- **Repeat:** refills at about $10 for a premium vessel; a $25 monthly two-refill subscription concept; a surprise 2 oz tin of the next collection in large orders (operators report, planned).
- **Testing:** burn-test the nearest competitor before obsessing over wet spots; one maker found the competitor worse on burn time, spillage and throw (operators report).
- **Label and product ideas that drew attention:** beer-can candles with plantable seed-paper labels; typewriter labels on cotton rag paper; awareness-month colors and molds (operators report).
- **Compliance:** CPSC and ASTM labeling, EN 15494 and CLP in the UK and EU, no "non-toxic" claims ([compliance-and-labeling.md](compliance-and-labeling.md)).
- **Micro-influencers:** 30 emails, 2 yeses (operators report). A creator's live stream with a cut of sales did $50,000+ in four days for a new candle store (r/ecommerce, 93 pts; repeatability disputed).
- **Education audience:** a candle maker grew a Facebook group from 52,000 to 105,000 and YouTube from 62,000 to 108,000 with about 20 educational posts per pitch, then opened a supply store (r/Entrepreneur, 1,888 pts).

## Soap makers

- **Engine:** markets, gift sets, farmers-market partners, wholesale to gift shops.
- **Claims decide regulation:** cleaning-only copy for true soap ([compliance-and-labeling.md](compliance-and-labeling.md)).
- **Display:** bars on thrifted dishes, $1 Goodwill baskets for gift sets, paper wrap for sniffing (r/soapmaking).
- **Signature designs carry the table:** rainbow and "Intergalactic" designs sell out first in maker posts; a provocatively named novelty soap is on its third loaf, though it got the maker removed from a Facebook soap group (r/soapmaking). Know the audience at each venue.
- **Partners:** barter with honey or produce vendors for ingredients and gift-basket placement (r/soapmaking, 358 pts).
- **Subscriptions:** a shaving soap box expanded from soaps only at $12 a month to a fuller box at $29, then sold annual prepay (r/Entrepreneur, 1,754 pts). Grandfather early subscribers.

## Ceramics and pottery

- **Engine:** studio and atelier markets, holiday markets, Instagram waitlists, commissions, wholesale of a simplified line.
- **Separate production ware from decorated work.** Hand-decorated pieces with 1.5 to 8 hours of decoration need their own price tier; the wholesale line is simpler ([pricing.md](pricing.md)).
- **Low price points struggle at fairs:** a table with nothing over $35 barely covered the fee (r/Pottery, 3,104 pts). Add a few $60 to $90 statement pieces and gift sets.
- **Sell-outs signal underpricing:** sold out in 3 hours at a first market with a waitlist (r/Pottery, 3,842 pts); market buyers ignored prices after a raise (r/Pottery, 2,204 pts).
- **Accessibility and story:** mugs with handles made for people who struggle with ordinary mugs became a viral TikTok story for Made with Mud (TikTok newsroom, 2024 data, possibly outdated).
- **Proven-post amplification:** Donovan Pottery's Spark Ads case (TikTok, vendor-selected).
- **Food safety:** glaze testing before "food safe" claims; decorative pieces marked per 21 CFR 109.16 ([compliance-and-labeling.md](compliance-and-labeling.md)).
- **Shipping:** quote packed weight and breakage allowance into price; insure.

## Woodworkers

- **Engine:** markets for boards and small goods, commissions for furniture, institutional orders, plans and videos.
- **Touch sells finish:** unfinished-versus-finished board demo (r/woodworking).
- **Unusual woods and large sizes first;** compliments are not sales (headphone stands: lots of pointing, one sale).
- **Pricing:** $45 boards undercut finishing time; $75+ with $10 engraving add-on (operators report). Furniture and builds priced on hours plus materials; a woodworker's teardrop trailers at $20,000 to $30,000 with a $500 refundable deposit and a waitlist over a year out (r/woodworking, 38,138 pts).
- **Institutions:** a $10,000 county-court conference table (r/smallbusiness). Cabinet makers who tested caskets one week in four ended up making only caskets (r/smallbusiness, 96-pt comment).
- **Content:** cultural-moment builds and constraint stories go viral (47,550 and 44,865 upvotes) but convert only with plans, a list or a link attached. "Do you sell plans?" is the most common reply: have them ready.
- **Kids items:** wooden toys trigger CPSIA; untreated, unfinished wood avoids lead testing but finishes and paints do not ([compliance-and-labeling.md](compliance-and-labeling.md)).

## Fiber artists (crochet, knit, embroidery, quilting, textiles)

- **Engine:** small finished pieces and gifts, patterns and kits, Lives, commissions priced as art, state fair competitions.
- **The hour ceiling:** a crocheted wedding dress is $14,000+ at $30 an hour; most listings sit near $3,000 ([pricing.md](pricing.md)). Sell big pieces as art or commissions at full rate, and build the business on patterns and smaller items.
- **Patterns:** 10% of one embroidery artist's income; label digital clearly; show a screenshot of the pattern's writing style so buyers know what they get (a crochet seller added one after a 1-star review, r/EtsySellers, 960 pts). Depth in `digital-products`.
- **Unusual applications launch businesses:** embroidered shoes went from one post at 800 followers to 32 overnight orders and a full-time business (r/ArtBusiness, 632 pts).
- **State fairs and competitions:** cash prizes and exposure to commission buyers; a fair art-department organizer recommends entering (r/crochet). Ribbon-winning pieces make strong hero content (17,973 and 15,115 upvotes).
- **Lives:** three crochet Lives a week, 2,000+ views each, used to grow an email list rather than for gifts (operators report).
- **Kids items:** CPSIA applies to toys and kids' clothing; safety eyes and small parts are the risk ([compliance-and-labeling.md](compliance-and-labeling.md)).
- **Shipping:** chunky blankets can cost hundreds to ship; vacuum-sealing damaged one blanket's shape (operators report). Quote before you start.
