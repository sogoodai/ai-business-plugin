# Scripts

"Verbatim" marks wording a practitioner or source used. "Template" marks wording written for this skill from the cited mechanism. Fill brackets with the owner's real terms; never invent a price, policy or testimonial. Get the owner's approval before anything is sent.

## Commissions

**Intake form or first reply (template, built on AOI's four variables and Illustrators Australia's guidance)**

> Thanks for thinking of me for this. To quote properly I need a few details:
> 1. What is the piece for: your home, a gift, or business use (print, packaging, web, merchandise)?
> 2. Size or format you have in mind, and where it will hang or be used.
> 3. Subjects: how many people or pets, and any accessories or outfits.
> 4. Reference photos (clear, well lit, the angle you want).
> 5. Your deadline, and the delivery address so I can quote packed shipping.
> 6. A budget range, so I can suggest the best size and format for it.

**Quote and terms (verbatim structure from the research report, 2026)**

> Your quote is $___ and includes the finished work, ___ revision round(s) at sketch stage, and personal display rights. A ___ deposit reserves the project slot. The remaining balance is due before shipment or delivery. Changes after sketch approval, additional subjects, rush delivery and commercial reproduction rights are quoted separately.

Add (template): "Estimated completion: [2x your honest estimate]. Shipping, packed and insured: $___ (if it comes in lower I refund the difference)."

**Commercial license line (verbatim, research report 2026)**

> The fee includes [specific use], in [territory], for [duration]. Any additional media, territories, products or extensions can be licensed separately.

**Before shipping a custom piece (template, from a crochet seller's practice)**

> Here it is, finished. I've attached photos from a few angles plus one next to a ruler for scale. Take a look and let me know if you'd like anything adjusted before I pack it up tomorrow.

**Pausing a demanding repeat client while you reset terms (template, from an artist who kept the client)**

> I've loved working on these with you. To keep giving each piece the time it deserves, I'm pausing new commissions and changes to finished pieces for a couple of weeks while I update my pricing and revision policy. I'll finish the edits you've already paid for by [date] and send you the new terms first.

**Cheaper competitor quote (verbatim, r/smallbusiness)**

> Does their quote include [specific thing]? What's their revision policy?
> I'm confident in my pricing because [one sentence on value]. But if they're a better fit for your budget, I completely understand.

**Digital portrait product line (template, from a 5-upvote suggestion)**

> A custom portrait of your pet, delivered as a print-ready file. Pay once, print it as many times and at as many sizes as you like.

## Licensing

**Inquiry reply (verbatim, research report 2026)**

> Thanks for reaching out. Before I quote, could you send the product(s), expected wholesale/retail price, first production quantity, territory, sales channels, launch date, license duration, exclusivity requested, and whether you need adaptation work?

## Galleries

**Gallery pitch email (template; Artsy 2025 transparency findings and CAA practice)**

> Subject: [Your name], [medium], new series "[title]" for [gallery name]
>
> Hello [director's name],
>
> I've followed [gallery]'s program since [specific show or artist you saw]. My work sits close to [artist they show] in [subject or approach], and I think it would fit your [collector base, season, or theme].
>
> "[Series title]" is [number] works in [medium], [size range], priced $[low] to $[high] retail. [One sentence on the idea.] Recent: [one show, award or sale fact that is true].
>
> Ten images with titles, sizes and prices are here: [link]. I'd be glad to bring two pieces by or arrange a studio visit.
>
> Thank you for your time,
> [Name] | [website] | [Instagram]

Ask in writing before agreeing: commission rate, display period, who pays shipping both ways, insurance on premises, discount authority and who absorbs it, payment timing, price parity terms.

## Wholesale and consignment

**In person at a fair or shop (verbatim, research report 2026)**

> I'd love to send you our wholesale line sheet. What email should I use? I'll include bestsellers, opening minimum and lead time so you can see in two minutes whether it fits your shop.

**Wholesale terms line (verbatim structure, research report 2026)**

> Wholesale: $___ per unit / MSRP $___ / opening minimum $___ / reorder minimum $___ / lead time ___ / payment terms ___ / shipping charged separately.

**Cold email to a boutique (template)**

> Subject: [Product] for [shop name]: sells at $[MSRP], $[wholesale] wholesale
>
> Hi [owner's name],
>
> I make [product] by hand in [town]. I noticed you carry [adjacent brand or category], and my [bestseller] tends to sell alongside it: [one true fact, such as "it was our best seller at the [market] three seasons running"].
>
> Opening order is $[minimum], lead time [x] days, prepaid for the first order, and you can order through my Faire link with no commission on your side: [Faire Direct link]. Line sheet attached.
>
> Happy to drop off samples on [day] if you're open to it.
> [Name, phone]

**Reorder nudge, day 30 to 40 after delivery (template, timing from `ecommerce-brand`)**

> Hi [name], checking in on the [product]. If you're down to the last few, I can ship a reorder by [date] so you're not empty for [season or weekend]. Same terms as before. Want me to send the usual?

**Counter to oversized terms (template, from the candle maker who declined net 60 and returns)**

> Thank you, this is a big compliment. As a small studio I can't carry net 60 or returns of unsold stock yet. I'd love to start with a smaller prepaid order of [quantity] at [price], and grow it as soon as it sells through.

**Consignment proposal terms (template; full agreement in `jewelry-accessories` scripts)**

> Consignment terms: [60/40] split, [maker/shop], on the retail prices listed on the attached inventory sheet. Work stays my property until paid. You insure it against theft and damage on your premises. No discounts without my written OK. Payment and a sales statement by the [10th] of each month. Work displayed on the floor at all times. Either side can end with [30] days' notice; unsold work returned in the condition received.

## Collectors and email

**Preview email (verbatim, research report 2026)**

> You're seeing this before it goes public. I'm releasing [collection] on [date]. There are [edition/quantity details where relevant]. Reply if you want measurements, detail shots or a first option on a particular piece.

**Release email (verbatim, research report 2026)**

> The collection is live. Prices, dimensions, materials and shipping are listed on each work; reply if you need an in-room photo or payment/shipping question answered.

**Follow-up to people who clicked one piece (verbatim, research report 2026)**

> You looked at [piece]. If the only thing stopping you is a practical question (scale, framing, shipping or payment), reply and I'll answer it directly. No pressure.

**Market signup line (verbatim, research report 2026)**

> Want first access to new drops and event dates? Add your email here; I send [frequency/type], and you can unsubscribe anytime.

**Craft-secret askers at a booth (verbatim, r/EtsySellers)**

> Oh, some day I will write a book. Would you like to sign up for my email list?

## Online messages and reviews

**Request for your techniques or suppliers (verbatim, r/EtsySellers, 939 pts)**

> It's cool you're interested in [craft]! I've been perfecting my skills for [x] years. I'm not much of a teacher though, here's a link to help you get started.

Follow-up, two to three days later (verbatim, 157 pts):

> Hi again, I'm sorry, I don't have time to write up a tutorial, but here's a link to a youtube search for what you are looking for. Hope this helps!

Supplier fishing (verbatim, 39 pts):

> I get my supplies wholesale, and there's a $5k minimum purchase. Give me your resale certificate number and I'll pass it on to my sales rep to reach out to you.

**Buyer asks for a format you don't sell (template, from a buyer who was blocked and bought elsewhere)**

> Thanks for asking! This one is only available as a physical print right now, [size] on [paper], ships in [x] days. If a digital version would help, tell me how you'd use it and I'll let you know if I can make one.

**Negative review, factual mismatch (verbatim structure, r/Etsy, 943 pts)**

> This is what the listing states: [quote]. I've sent you a message to resolve this further.

**Review accusing human art of being AI (verbatim, r/Etsy, 74 pts)**

> Hi, I just wanted to clarify that your order was for a bad doodle, which is a fun little project that takes a fraction of the time I spend on my other pieces. I have never used AI in my artwork and am committed to producing art made by humans, for humans (and sometimes dogs!). I hope this can reassure you and that the doodle can put a smile on your face!

Before replying publicly to a review accusing you of reselling, ask Etsy to review it and message the buyer privately with process photos; a public reply locks the review (commenters in r/EtsySellers, 653 pts).

**False non-delivery claim with proof (verbatim, r/EtsySellers, 720 pts)**

> Upon further investigation I can confidently say that you definitely have my one-of-a-kind, handmade item in your possession. I want to thank you for supporting my shop and my passion. I was honoured to see it make an appearance on your Instagram account. If you could please tag my shop in the future I would be so grateful!

**Refunded buyer whose late parcel arrived and who offers to pay (template, from r/Etsy, 887 pts)**

> That's so kind of you. The simplest way is to repurchase the same listing; I'll mark it as complete on my side so nothing ships twice.

**Personalization mismatch: replace, don't patch (from r/Etsy, 729 pts).** Send a new piece with faster shipping and let the buyer keep the first one when a fix risks damage. The seller netted $3 on the sale and got five stars and a promised repeat order. Then state capitalization and spelling rules in the listing text.
