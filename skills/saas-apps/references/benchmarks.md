# Benchmarks

Compare a product only with its closest segment: B2B self-serve SaaS, AI-native tool, consumer subscription app, or sales-assisted B2B. A $9 Chrome extension is not a $5M ARR B2B company. Keep three kinds of numbers apart when you report:

- **Measured:** large platform or billing datasets (RevenueCat, ChartMogul, High Alpha, Baremetrics) and platform documentation (Apple, Google, Product Hunt, Hacker News). Most are vendor customer cohorts, not a census; say so when it matters.
- **Agency or vendor aggregates:** useful ranges, with a selection bias (PipeRocket, Kiin, Userpilot, Appcues).
- **Operators report:** Reddit founder numbers. Real, one product each, often unverified.

"Year" is the period the data describes; the publication year follows when different. Full URLs in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Launch platforms (rules, not revenue benchmarks)

No credible 2025 to 2026 cross-platform dataset gives a median Product Hunt or Hacker News launch-to-paid conversion (research, November 2026). Platform facts:
- Product Hunt: launches start 12:01 a.m. Pacific; points system since 1 October 2025 weights engagement and established members' votes over new accounts; asking for upvotes can disqualify; relaunch allowed after a major update, launches under 2 months apart need a request.
- Hacker News Show HN: users must be able to try it, ideally without signup; landing pages and waitlists are not Show HNs; no coordinated voting.
- AppSumo claims a 1.3M audience (its own marketing). Case studies: Pretty Prompt 5,200+ purchases, 170 reviews, 4.86 stars; Mootion from a few thousand monthly subscribers to $100K to $150K MRR after three campaigns since October 2025 (vendor-selected, 2026).
