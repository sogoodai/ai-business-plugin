# 90-day plan

For an existing or stalled small manufacturer, supplier, distributor or industrial service firm. A brand-new firm uses "First customers" in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) for its first month, then joins here at day 31. Order matters: measure, then fix qualification and quoting, then capture active demand, then add account coverage. Advertising does not start in month one.

Each phase ends with a short owner report: what changed, the scorecard, what the owner must decide.

## Days 1 to 30: instrument and remove friction

**Week 1: the scorecard.** Export 24 months of invoices, quotes and RFQs. Compute ([accounts-and-retention.md](accounts-and-retention.md), [quoting-and-pricing.md](quoting-and-pricing.md)):

- revenue and gross profit by customer; top-1, top-3 and top-5 shares; concentration index
- reorder interval per repeat customer and part; lapsed accounts
- RFQs received, declined, quoted; median quote turnaround; quote-to-PO by count and by dollars; loss reasons where known
- source of every customer won in 24 months (ask the owner when the CRM is empty)
- receivables by customer and days to pay; worst-case credit loss for the top 5

**Week 1: owner questions.** See "First session" in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md). Get the list of certifications with expiry dates, the equipment list, the "we do not do" list and the capacity picture by machine or line.

**Week 2: one pipeline.** One CRM pipeline with fields: source, account, contact role, first touch, RFQ date, qualified date, quote date, quote value, expected margin, PO date and value, loss reason, campaign IDs (GCLID, UTMs). Add "how did you find us?" as free text on the RFQ form and on the first call.

**Week 2 to 3: the website audit.** Score each capability page against the block list in [buyers-and-website.md](buyers-and-website.md): capabilities, envelope, equipment, materials, certifications with numbers, documents, volumes and MOQ, lead-time logic, applications, files, one CTA. Fix first the pages that already get Search Console impressions and the categories that produce real RFQs. Rebuild the RFQ form (file upload, NDA line, quantity and date) and the automatic acknowledgement.

**Week 3: the quote SLA.** Publish the A/B/C classes internally, the same-day decision rule, the loss-reason email and the open-quote follow-up. Start measuring turnaround. Set the baseline quote-to-PO rate before touching price.

**Week 4: directories and catalogs.** Claim and complete Thomasnet and the two or three directories that matter in the sector, with UTMs. Check distributor catalogs and CAD platforms for presence. Register on SAM.gov if government work fits.

**Exit check at day 30:** CRM capturing every RFQ with source; RFQ form and acknowledgement live; quote turnaround measured; top capability pages rebuilt; concentration and credit risk reported to the owner.

## Days 31 to 60: capture active demand

**Google Search** ([paid-ads.md](paid-ads.md)): one or two tight campaigns on process and product terms with qualifiers, negatives from day one, GCLID capture, qualified RFQ as the primary conversion. Budget from the allowable cost per qualified RFQ, not from a round number. Compare against $5.87 CPC, 8.20% click-to-conversion and $75.19 CPL (WordStream 2026), but decide on qualified RFQs.

**Deep pages:** build 10 to 30 commercial and technical pages from the nouns and questions in real RFQs and sent quotes: process plus material, tolerance band, industry, certification, part family, cross-reference. No generic AI articles.

**Named accounts:** score accounts, pick the top 25 to 100, map the buying group, write one trigger per account ([accounts-and-outbound.md](accounts-and-outbound.md)). No ABM software.

**Outbound in small batches:** 10 to 15 researched contacts a day across email and phone, samples to the best-fit accounts. Track positive reply per first contact, conversations per account, qualified RFQs per account.

**Existing customers:** reorder check-ins on each account's rhythm, open-capacity emails by machine, the next-job question on every order, two referral asks a week after good deliveries.

**Channel partners:** if a territory or category is uncovered, shortlist reps or distributors through customers' buyers and non-competing reps. Draft the agreement checklist in [reps-and-distributors.md](reps-and-distributors.md) before any call.

**Exit check at day 60:** Search producing qualified RFQs at or under the allowable cost (or cut); 10+ new deep pages indexed; named-account list worked; reorder and capacity emails running; partner shortlist ready.

## Days 61 to 90: add coverage, scale only what survives downstream

**LinkedIn,** only if one new customer is worth tens of thousands in gross profit and the segment is big enough for a 300-member matched audience (LinkedIn recommends 1,000+ companies). Start with Thought Leader Ads from the founder or engineer and a document ad with a technical guide, Lead Gen Form against landing page, qualified-lead feedback through the Conversions API. If the market is too small, do not widen the ICP to feed the platform; put the money into outbound and samples.

**Next trade show:** decide go or no-go with the cost-per-qualified-meeting math; if go, start the 120-day pre-show plan, book the engineer and pick the demonstration ([trade-shows.md](trade-shows.md)). If no booth, attend with booked meetings and host a breakfast.

**Pricing moves** from the quote data: classes where win rate is very high and the queue is long get a price review; paperwork and portal work get a line item; long-term agreements get an index or review clause ([quoting-and-pricing.md](quoting-and-pricing.md)).

**Concentration plan:** for any customer over 20% of gross profit, the next-three-accounts list, five named contacts inside the big account, and the contract protections to raise at renewal.

**Day-90 ranking:** score every channel:

```
channel score = (gross profit won + probability-weighted qualified pipeline - channel cost) / channel cost
```

Then three buckets:

- **Scale:** repeatable profitable economics.
- **Repair:** good lead quality but weak conversion or broken measurement.
- **Stop:** attention without qualified opportunities.

Never promote a channel because CTR, traffic, downloads or raw leads improved. The bar is qualified RFQ, quote, PO, margin and repeat potential.

**Owner report at day 90:** scorecard then vs now (RFQs, qualified RFQs, quote turnaround, quote-to-PO, gross profit won, top-customer share), channel buckets with spend, the next 90 days, and the decisions only the owner can make (price moves, new rep or distributor, show budget, credit limits).
