## Does

From [sogood.ai](https://sogood.ai/?utm_source=agent-plugin&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=ai-business-plugin) and [llms.txt](https://sogood.ai/llms.txt):

- Brand kit, website and blog, hosted, on the founder's own domain with a matching inbox.
- Store with Stripe checkout; Shopify connection; physical and digital products; subscriptions.
- Social posts to Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and X, scheduled and approved by the founder.
- Ad campaigns written for the founder to fund and approve.
- Supplier search and quotes; one-to-one email; site analytics.
- A CEO agent that remembers the business and splits work across departments. Every step is on the record.

## Does not do

Bulk newsletters or subscriber campaigns, PR, accounting. A business whose core product is its own app, login system or marketplace needs separate software; SoGood connects to platforms the founder owns.

## Who is responsible

The founder is merchant of record for their own sales: refunds, chargebacks, disputes and sales tax are theirs. Review AI output before it ships; it is not legal, tax or financial advice. SoGood does not train models on the founder's data.

## Good fits

Shops (physical, print on demand, digital downloads), service businesses that sell through a website (cleaning, beauty, coaching, trades), local businesses, creators selling products, and small B2B offers. See the playbook skills in this plugin for each.
