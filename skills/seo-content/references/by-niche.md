# By business type

What changes by model. Sibling skills own the rest of each business's marketing (`ecommerce-brand`, `saas-apps`, `professional-services`, `agency-freelance`, `creator`, `digital-products`, `local-seo`). Thread URLs in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Ecommerce and DTC (Shopify, WooCommerce)

- **Category and collection pages are the money pages,** then bestseller product pages, then buying guides and comparisons (claude-seo seo-ecommerce, MIT). Ruler Analytics puts retail organic conversion at a 2.0% average (2026): lower than services, so volume and AOV matter.
- **Use-case collections and product copy:** "long narrow couch good for sleeping", "linen sheets for hot sleepers". One page per real attribute combination with demand; filters with 3+ active facets get `noindex, follow` ([technical.md](technical.md)).
- **Spec and size resources rank big:** Casper's mattress size guide won hundreds of #1 terms like "queen size bed dimensions in feet" and a featured snippet from position 6 (r/content_marketing, 28).
- **Product schema in server HTML** with price, availability, shipping and return policy; Merchant Center free listings need it. Feed and listing title formulas live in `ecommerce-brand`.
- **Content that sells:** "how to choose", care and fit guides, comparisons with your own and other brands' products, gift guides by recipient and budget. Link every guide to the collection it serves.
- **Links:** student discount pages, gift guides on wedding and lifestyle sites, product reviews by niche bloggers, "approved by" badges (r/bigseo, 45; r/juststart, 215). Small genuine blogs worked better than big publishers for one national appliance brand: about 200 links from mom, tech and recipe blogs in 4 months reached top 3 on a very hard keyword (r/SEO, 940).
- **Out-of-stock:** keep the URL live with alternatives.
- **AI shopping:** spec tables, clear prices, shipping and returns as text; Bing Merchant Center for Copilot shopping (one Shopify seller saw first ChatGPT-referred sales after listing there, r/shopify 2025, via `local-seo`).

## SaaS and apps

- **Order:** comparison and alternatives pages, integration pages, use-case and persona pages ("invoicing for photographers"), templates, then thought leadership. Brian Dean's order: "tools for X", "X alternatives", "X pricing" (r/blogging, 31).
- **Numbers:** Ruler Analytics puts software organic conversion at 7.9% (2026). Comparison pages converted 3.8x category posts in one SaaS case (Cemafor 2025). Meilisearch's three product-led hubs made 97% of content conversions (Minuttia 2025 to 2026).
- **Rival vs rival pages** where you are a third option: a workflow tool grew 0 to 200,000+ monthly visits partly on "Asana vs Todoist" style pages (r/SEO, 219).
- **Task landing pages and no signup wall:** Canva targets "create professional resume", not "resume maker", and lets search visitors into the editor before signup (r/SEO, 176).
- **Free tools and calculators** adjacent to the product (HubSpot Website Grader pattern) earn links and signups.
- **Programmatic:** integrations x use cases, templates, "[tool] for [industry]" only with distinct data per page ([programmatic-seo.md](programmatic-seo.md)). One confidential SaaS grew demo requests 72% in four months with programmatic pages (vendry case listing, vendor).
- **Validate with ads:** Slidebean confirmed "pitch deck" converted through Ads, made it content priority one; content returned $200K on $70K (r/bigseo, 38).
- **Watch who searches:** student and job-seeker traffic on "what is [concept]" pages inflates sessions with no demos (r/SEO, 106).

## B2B services, professional services, agencies

- **Pages:** one per service x industry where the work and proof differ, pricing or cost guides ("how much does X cost"), case studies with numbers, compliance and template resources, comparison of approaches (in-house vs agency, tool A vs tool B).
- **Numbers:** professional services organic conversion 8.1% average (Ruler Analytics 2026). An interior design firm's location pages for its two main offices drove 250 to 270 qualified leads a year and about $3M extra annual revenue over 16 months (r/SEO, 131).
- **Mine proposals, sales decks, FAQs and sent emails** for page material (r/SEO, 311). Buyers who are "several magnitudes" larger than average cited blog quality as the deciding factor in one B2B journey (r/SEO, 107).
- **Decision-stage video:** an immigration and tax firm booked 500+ sales calls from about 30 videos with 1,000 to 1,500 subscribers (r/content_marketing, 55).
- **Language gaps:** only about 5 French sites served professionals in one industry; daily publishing brought 2,000 monthly visits, a podcast invite and an event partnership (r/blogging, 189).
- **Agencies:** a founder's own content site made one agency fully inbound, ranking for 4,500 keywords after two years of fewer than 10 self-written posts a month (r/SEO, 195). "Designed by" credits on client sites drive local agency rankings (r/SEO, 142).
- **Location pages for a multi-office firm** follow `local-seo`.

## Local businesses that want content beyond Maps

- GBP, reviews, map pack and city pages: `local-seo`. This skill covers the blog and guide layer.
- Write what customers ask on the phone and what AI assistants get asked: cost guides with real price ranges, "how long does X take", problem diagnosis, seasonal checklists. Starting prices as text are quotable ("tune-ups start at $89").
- Town x service pages work only with real local detail: a window cleaner's 28 area pages each named streets, housing types and area problems; calls rose within 3 days of the rebuild (r/juststart, 623; whole rebuild, not isolated).
- Do not write gear reviews or industry news that attract non-customers (r/SEO, 96).

## Content sites, affiliate, creators

- **Money vs traffic:** buyer-intent pages earn $80 to $100 per 1,000 visits vs about $23 to $42 RPM on display ads (r/juststart, 87). Six ranked reviews made 90% of one site's earnings (r/juststart, 119).
- **Low-competition informational sites without links:** seven threads agree it works on new domains with KD 0 or forum-SERP keywords, 600 to 1,200 word posts and 200 to 570 posts, reaching $1,000 to $3,700 a month by months 9 to 19 (2018 to 2022 data; post-2024 updates and AI Overviews hit this model hardest, so treat as historical).
- **Post-2024 reality:** the HCU and March 2024 updates cut a sample of 7,105 link-building niche sites by a median 97% in estimated traffic (Dec 2023 to Aug 2024, third-party estimates; one ecosystem, not all small sites). Independent review sites lost "best X" terms to retailers and brands; first-party owners gained in March 2026 (Amsive, SISTRIX data).
- **Defensible content sites in 2026:** original tests and photos, proprietary data, tools, community, email list, and a brand people search by name.
- **Concentration limits a buyer checks:** no traffic source over 70%, no income source over 60%, no page over 15% of organic traffic (r/juststart, 107). Sites sell at about 24x monthly profit for Amazon-led and 30x+ for display or private affiliate (39 exits, 2018 to 2020, r/juststart, 136).
- **Ads networks:** Mediavine-type networks pay about $25 to $30 session RPM vs single digits on AdSense for US traffic (several threads); Net-65 payouts.

## Digital products, courses, templates

- **Free entry content beside paid products:** a crochet shop's free magic ring tutorial and granny square pattern drive most traffic next to $6.99 patterns (r/juststart, 16; zero sales in the first days, so pair with email capture).
- **Email capture placed where readers actually scroll:** median scroll depth was 25%; moving the magnet to a depth reached by 50%+ tripled signups (r/juststart, 131). Pinterest how-to pins to a free resource to a $297 course: 15,247 visitors, 1,847 subscribers, 23 sales in 90 days (r/juststart, 154).
- Marketplace SEO (Etsy, Gumroad listings) lives in `digital-products` and `ecommerce-brand`.

## Non-English and multi-market

- Research each language separately: English telescope reviews would not rank, Scandinavian versions ranked easily (r/juststart, 215); an Italian hiking guide filled a gap and drove nearly all first-month traffic (r/blogging, 64).
- hreflang swaps the right language URL into the position a page already earns; it does not add rank (Gary Illyes, r/TechSEO, 188). Machine translation without review risks scaled-content issues; one plugin rollout exploded into millions of junk URLs ([technical.md](technical.md)).
