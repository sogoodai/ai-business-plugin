# Links, mentions and digital PR on a small budget

Links still matter for competitive commercial terms; mentions matter more for AI answers (unlinked brand mentions correlated about 3x more with AI visibility than backlinks, Ahrefs December 2025, 75,000 brands, correlation). For small budgets the evidence favors earned-link assets, reactive PR and direct relevance over bought guest posts (deep research 2026). Local link sources (chambers, sponsorships, local press) are in the `local-seo` skill. Thread URLs in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Policy line (Google spam policies, current)

Link spam = buying or selling links for ranking, excessive exchanges, automated link programs, low-quality directories, guest posts with optimized anchors made for links. Paid placements are fine when marked `rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow` ([Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)). A sponsorship bought mainly for a followed link is technically a scheme: worth it for referral traffic, mentions and AI citations, never promise an SEO lift from it.

## What to build links to

- **Money pages directly, not only link bait.** One affiliate site earned about 350 referring domains, 90% from skyscraper content; its commercial pages gained far less than from direct links (r/juststart, 73). With 1,000+ referring domains, about 95% pointed at skyscrapers or the homepage while new competitive pages needed their own links (r/juststart, 86). Measure links to money pages, not homepage DR.
- **Optimize the target page first,** then build links (r/SEO, 173). Then pass equity with internal links from the pages that earn links (r/SEO, 239).
- **Concentrate:** when the niche has little keyword room, put the link budget on 2 or 3 pages with the highest commercial value (r/juststart, 89). Pages already at positions 5 to 20 respond fastest (r/juststart, 155).
- **Realistic targets:** filter a competitor's backlinks to dofollow, in-content, DR 20+ and 2,500 links shrank to 95, about 8 a month for a year instead of 208 (r/SEO, 99). Use referring domains, not backlink totals: one "spike" from 690 to 6,700 backlinks was one sitewide source (r/bigseo, 39).

|---|---|---|
| Partners, suppliers, customers, integrations | Time | Supplier and partner pages, testimonials to suppliers, joint posts with non-competing peers (3 threads agree). Partner anchors that describe the service ("our Rhode Island domestic plumbing partner") (r/SEO, 136). LottieFiles became the default animation library inside Avada (650,000 customers) (r/content_marketing, 78) |
| Official listings and resource pages that already send clicks | Time | Getting listed on official sites and wiki pages that send traffic; one buyer later found the software through a ChatGPT recommendation (r/SEO, 78) |
| Unlinked mentions and image reuse | Time | Ask sites that mention you or reuse your charts to add a link; a designer reverse-searches the team's research graphics (r/content_marketing, 144) |
| Journalist request services (HARO successors such as Qwoted, Featured, Help a B2B Writer) | Time | 365 days of answers took an affiliate site to DR 35 with no bought links (r/juststart, 119); 40+ links in five months answering in 1 to 2 paragraphs with selection criteria and specific picks (r/juststart, 94). Reply within the hour; quotable, specific, within word limits |
| Data-led digital PR | Hours plus data | Applied Toyota's $1,800 tariff math to 10 other top-selling cars, 50 to 100 short emails, links from CBS News (r/SEO, 111). Practitioners average 15.58 links a month; 87.3% never pay for placements (BuzzStream 2025) |
| Expert quotes before publishing | Time | Survey of 1,117 bloggers plus expert quotes on the findings: 100+ links and over 2x baseline traffic on a 6-month-old blog (r/blogging, 77). Quoted experts share and link (2 threads) |
| Skyscraper and resource replacement | Content plus outreach | About 50 links from 1,000 prospects (5%) (r/SEO, 441); 400+ referring domains for $473 lifetime spend (r/juststart, 84). Broken or outdated resources with 100+ referring domains are the best targets (r/content_marketing, 42) |
| Free tool or calculator | Build cost | Niche calculators reached 13% search CTR over 1.2M impressions (r/juststart, 74); a free tool replacing a removed Google feature drew 50,000 impressions on launch (r/bigseo, 63) |
| Discount and gift-guide placements (ecommerce) | Discount | Student discount pages produced 12 dofollow .edu links for Casper; wedding gift guides on The Knot and Brides (r/bigseo, 45). Review "approved" badges that vendors display, linking back (r/juststart, 215) |
| Paid guest posts and niche edits | $50 to $300+ each | Operators report $59 per link in placement fees, about $111 once outreach labor is counted (68 links, r/juststart, 364); $300 per paid link (r/SEO, 209). Against Google policy unless marked sponsored. Same pages often get resold to spam sites (r/bigseo, 39) |

Outreach yields to expect: 2% (68 links from 3,500 prospects), 4 to 6% (2 to 3 from 50), 5% (50 from 1,000); replies run higher than links (17.6% replies, about 2% links on 500 emails) (r/juststart, r/bigseo). Budget accordingly: 1,000 prospects for a meaningful campaign.

## Digital PR procedure (one asset per quarter)

1. **Find a finding only you can produce:** customer data, a price index, a test, a survey of 300+ people, a teardown of 100 pages, public data recalculated for a timely story.
2. **Write the method page:** sample, dates, method, limits, a downloadable table and 2 to 3 charts journalists can embed.
3. **Build a list of 30 to 100 people who cover this beat now.** Read their last 5 pieces; skip anyone silent for 6+ months. Score beat match, reach and recency (coreyhaines31/marketingskills, MIT).
4. **Pitch the number in the subject line** ([templates.md](templates.md)). Plain text, no attachments, one follow-up after 3 to 5 days. Some links appear 2 to 3 weeks later.
5. **Newsjack:** when a story breaks in your category, publish the data angle within 24 to 48 hours and pitch it while the story is live.
6. **Log every mention,** linked or not, and ask unlinked ones for a link a week later.

Mechanics that raise coverage: one genuinely new statistic in the first sentence, a defensible method, a timely hook, a chart, and something the journalist can verify (deep research 2026). The failed version: "we've published an exciting new study" with no finding up front. Operators also report 3 days of work on assets that got zero coverage (r/SEO, 111): test the hook on 10 contacts before building a big asset.

## Expired domains and redirects

Operators use expired domains to skip the early lull (two threads claim 6 to 12 months; unmeasured). Rebuilding the strongest old URLs first and redirecting only to real replacements is the clean version (r/content_marketing, 77). Redirecting expired domains purely for their links sits close to Google's expired domain abuse policy (March 2024). For a small business: buy a related domain only to run it as a real site or to absorb a real business (an acquisition), never to farm links.

## Never

- Buy links from "guest post" sellers or PBNs, accept "reciprocal" offers without checking (one exchange link was cloaked so only the recipient saw it, r/bigseo, 37), or hide keyword anchors in widget and infographic embed codes.
- Disavow routinely. Without a manual action there is no measured benefit: a review of case studies found no direction, and an operator who disavowed 1,200 domains during a suspected attack saw no recovery (r/TechSEO, 225 and 51). Many "toxic" links flagged by tools are cloaked pages that show links only to SEO bots (r/TechSEO, 2026). Disavow only with a manual action in GSC.
- Pay for "link building packages" without seeing the exact placements: one $500 package delivered a 200-word article on an irrelevant link farm and 3 nofollow forum links (r/SEO, 184).

## Tricks most owners miss

- Bing Webmaster Tools has a free backlink report that shows competitors' link sources (r/SEO, 135).
- New links often show in GSC Links before Ahrefs or Semrush (r/juststart, 70; r/TechSEO, 51).
- "Designed by" footer credits on client sites drive local agency rankings (r/SEO, 142); for agencies, a credit link on each client site (marked nofollow if paid) is still referral and brand reach.
- A launch press release is the easiest first link for a brand-new site (2 threads); a $200 PRWeb release was picked up on PR Newswire (r/juststart, 134). Expect brand reach, not rankings.
