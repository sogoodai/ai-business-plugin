# Fit, returns and the apparel product page

Returns are the hidden tax on apparel growth: 56% of apparel and footwear brands report return rates of 30% or more (Radial 2025), and 56% of apparel returns cite fit or size, 25% style or preference (Loop 2026). Fit work is the rare project that raises conversion and cuts returns at once. General PDP and checkout structure: `ecommerce-brand` references/shopify-dtc.md and `website-conversion`.

## Size guide: the minimum spec

A chart that says S, M, L is not fit guidance. Each product page needs (research report, 2026):

1. **Garment measurements, not only body measurements:** chest (pit to pit), waist, hip, length, inseam and rise for bottoms, sleeve, and stretch.
2. **A line saying which they are** (garment measured flat, or body).
3. **Model height, size worn and key measurements.**
4. **Intended silhouette:** oversized, boxy, cropped, relaxed, slim.
5. **What to do between sizes.**
6. **Inches and centimeters.** A premium brand selling to the US was told to add inch measurements to cut size hesitation and returns (r/ecommerce, 34-pt thread).

Wording that answers four questions at once (research report, 2026):

> "Alex is 5'11"/180 cm, chest 38"/97 cm, wearing M for the intended relaxed fit. Size down for a closer fit. Garment chest measurements are measured flat, pit-to-pit, then doubled."

Say it bluntly when a piece runs small: "The sizing is a bit small, so size up!!!" in a jacket launch post (r/streetwearstartup, 374-pt thread). Height-based answers in comments help too: for 6'4", "XL just due to arm length and a little shrinkage when washed" for a slightly baggy look, L for a normal fit (r/streetwearstartup, 953-pt thread). Put recurring answers on the page.

Shrinkage and tolerance belong on the page if they are real: state pre-shrunk or expected shrink, and fix grading at the factory with written tolerances ([production-and-sourcing.md](production-and-sourcing.md)).

## Customer measurement questions

Do not make shipping conditional on extra personal data. An Etsy seller kept asking a buyer for her weight after she sent measurements and explained her size choice; days later she asked for a refund and wanted to warn other shoppers (r/Etsy, 1,313-pt thread). Neutral wording from the same thread:

> "I noticed these are not quite what I usually see, I usually expect a range of x-x, just double checking that measurement was correct."

## Fit tools: only behind an experiment

- **Best case:** Fit Analytics on ARMEDANGELS, a 4-month randomized test on 105,000 shoppers: +29.9% conversion, -2.4% return rate, +47% AOV, +105.5% revenue per visitor after returns, vendor-projected 9x ROI (2024, vendor case).
- **Counter-evidence:** a 2025 peer-reviewed study of 496,365 items found size-finder users were 0.65% more likely to return, while heavier tool use went with 7.51% higher next-quarter CLV (order data 2015 to 2022, possibly outdated).
- **Rule:** randomize. Primary metrics: conversion, return rate by reason, bracketing rate (same item ordered in two sizes), net revenue after returns, tool cost. "Users convert better than non-users" is not causal: high-intent shoppers open the tool more.

## Photos and video

Shoppers need the silhouette before the mood. Keep, for every product (research report, 2026):
- One clean on-model front view and a back view (critical for back prints).
- Detail and texture close-up.
- A fit reference (model stats, or the piece on two bodies).
- Motion: video near the buy box.

Evidence:
- Replacing model shots with lifestyle images: -7.2% conversion and -9.0% add to cart on 8,808 visitors (LiftArchive 2026, not significant; a warning, not proof).
- Creator video on 3 core swimsuit PDPs: +4% conversion, +5% revenue, AOV held; the top videos showed the garment on relevant body shapes and sizes (Andie Swim, Hue vendor case).
- Employee videos on Foot Locker's top 12 PDPs: +6% conversion, +5% demand per visitor (Nov 2025, desktop 50/50, vendor case). The best video explained sizing, materials, product history and styling, and physically rotated the product. Use that as the brief for apparel PDP video.
- Floating video card on 135 apparel PDPs: 3.96% to 5.75% conversion on 50,476 visitors (Whatmore 2026, randomized, vendor). Plan on a few percent, not 45%.
- Almost every buyer viewed at least three product pictures and few read past the second paragraph (r/shopify, 274-pt thread): facts go in the images and the first lines.

Photo budget (operators disagree): "don't spend your whole budget on photoshoots" (r/ClothingStartups, 87-pt thread) vs "photos make or break your conversion rate" (r/ClothingStartups list post). Data rule: spend on clean on-model shots and video first, editorial last. Photos that look like an old phone drew criticism even on a viral post (r/streetwearstartup, 3,125-pt thread).

Mockups vs reality:
- POD sellers use close lifestyle mockups (Placeit, or Midjourney refined in Photoshop) in marketing and the provider's exact product image on the product page (r/printondemand, 79-pt and 84-pt threads).
- AI-generated product photos standing in for real ones drew credibility pushback on streetwear subs. Show at least one photo of the real garment.
- Before launch, check print size against the mockup on a real sample. Mockup tools misjudge placement and scale: Fourthwall misprinted 8 of 16 shirts by 2 to 3 inches (r/printondemand, 23-pt thread); a Bella Canvas 3001 printed much larger than the mockup (16-pt thread). Use the printer's template and inset guides.

## Copy for clothing pages

- **Explain each design choice and what it fixes** (seamless hems to stop chafing while running, r/shopify, 170-pt thread).
- **Make unfamiliar fibers concrete.** A yak-wool brand was told to explain why it beats the alternative and address "itchy"; for merino, under 17.5 microns is the commenter's luxury cue (r/ecommerce, 34-pt thread).
- **"100% cotton" is not a selling point on its own** ("People on Instagram don't really buy '100% cotton'", r/ClothingStartups, 27-pt thread). Pair it with weight (GSM), hand feel and what it does after washing.
- **Care instructions travel with the garment.** A former professional printer: wash DTG prints inside out at max 30°C, no fabric softener, no vinegar, do not iron the print. Gift recipients never see the listing, so put it on a printed card in the parcel (r/EtsySellers, 1,129-pt thread).
- **Wash-test proof** for printed goods: side-by-side photos after three washes (r/printondemand, 31-pt thread).
- **Legibility:** a slogan tee on black read as "ICE & FRIEND" because one ink vanished; only offer garment colors where the whole message reads (r/SCREENPRINTING, 1,389-pt thread).
- **Returns line next to Add to Cart** ("Free size exchanges within 30 days") and in the order confirmation (finsilabs harvest, MIT).

Sustainability wording rules are in [production-and-sourcing.md](production-and-sourcing.md); since 27 September 2026 they are legal limits in the EU, not style choices.

## Returns policy architecture

Merchants moved from blanket free refunds to exchange-first: 73.6% offer exchanges, 49.2% offer shop-now exchanges (51.7% of those add bonus credit, average $11.28), and 65.2% charge a fee on at least some outcomes, averaging $9.04 (Loop, 23.4M returns, Nov 2024 to Oct 2025). Shoppers still care: 82% call free returns a major purchase factor (NRF 2025). Design toward exchanges and credit, not punishment:

> "Free size exchanges within 30 days. Returns for refund incur a [actual fee] return-label deduction. Store-credit returns are free. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and returned with original tags."

Jurisdiction: EU and UK distance-selling withdrawal rights give shoppers a legal cancellation window, and rules on deducting return costs differ by country. Apply local consumer law instead of copying a US policy; ask the owner to confirm with their adviser before charging fees in the EU or UK.

## Track returns at the right grain

- **SKU x size x return reason x production batch,** not a storewide rate. If one M tee shows abnormal "too small" returns, the cause may be grading or shrinkage in that batch, not customer education (research report, 2026).
- **Bracketing rate:** orders containing the same style in two or more sizes. Rising bracketing means the size information is failing.
- **Display review-derived fit** ("runs small: [x]% of reviewers") once a product has enough fit answers.
- **Judge every page test on net revenue after returns.** A variant that lifts gross conversion while attracting more bracketing can lose money.
- **First-order care and fit email** after delivery for first-time buyers reduces returns; repeat buyers get the loyalty message instead (nexscope harvest, MIT).
