# Creators, Instagram, TikTok and TikTok Shop for apparel

General creator economics, rate tables, briefs and whitelisting terms are in the `ecommerce-brand` skill (references/creators-ugc.md and tiktok-shop.md). Posting cadence and platform craft are in `organic-social`. This file keeps what is specific to clothing.

## Measure reach and sales, not engagement rate

- **Try-ons look weak on engagement and win on reach.** Micro-creator TikTok try-ons got 4,716 median views vs 2,354 for all posts, at 6.3% engagement vs 9.8% for OOTD and fit checks (Kyra, UK and US fashion creators, Jul to Sep 2026). An agent that ranks content by engagement rate will cut the format that sells.
- **Ad-labelled posts earn less engagement and still sell.** Posts flagged as ads drew 0.69x the engagement of the same creators' organic posts on TikTok, 0.70x on Instagram, and TikTok Shop posts 0.54x (Kyra 2026). Sponsored fashion content trailed organic in every market Traackr measured (US 1.14% vs 1.65%, 2025).
- **Followers are a poor price basis.** TikTok engagement by followers falls from 1.86% for nano to 0.24% for mega creators, while engagement by views stays about 9.1% to 9.4% across tiers (Kyra 2026). Price creators on recent median views, audience fit and reusable footage.

Weekly creative report: spend or views, CTR, add to cart, purchase rate, return-adjusted contribution, and share of first-time customers, per asset.

## The apparel content matrix: one job per asset

| Asset | Job | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fit check, OOTD | Discovery | Highest TikTok engagement tag (9.8%, Kyra 2026) |
| Try-on, several sizes or bodies | Reach and sales, size confidence | Show the same piece on two body types and two sizes |
| Size comparison | Return prevention | Model height and size in the caption; "size down for a closer fit" |
| Fabric and construction close-up | Objection handling | GSM, stitching, print feel, stretch, wash result |
| Founder or factory behind the scenes | Differentiation | Sampling mistakes and fixes read as honest |
| Customer photos and UGC | Proof | Real bodies, real light |
| Three ways to wear | AOV, multi-item | Pairs a hero with the next item |
| Drop story and countdown | Urgency | Real quantities and dates only |
| Wash test or durability | Trust for printed goods | Side-by-side after several washes |

(Matrix from the research report, 2026, with operator additions.)

A hoodie brand's feed mix that a commenter reports beat "clean corporate" content: 40% lifestyle or UGC (night walks, coffee mornings, post-gym, late studio), 25% founder diary with faces and inventory mistakes, 15% factory and fabric BTS, 10% trends and Reels, 10% product detail (r/ClothingStartups, 27-pt thread). The same thread: "People on Instagram don't really buy '100% cotton.' They buy a feeling."

Video craft that keeps the garment visible (r/streetwearstartup, 944-pt thread, ad buyer's critique at 97 points):
- First 3 seconds both stop the scroll and filter for buyers.
- A text overlay says what is being sold and why.
- Export 1:1 and vertical.
- Several poses and a mirror shot to show fit; no constant camera movement or glitch effects. "Make sure your customer can see what they are buying."
- A moody lifestyle video is "an OK lifestyle video" and "a really shitty ad": use it for existing followers, not acquisition.

## Instagram mechanics that changed in 2025 to 2026

- **Original beats reposted.** 75% of US Instagram recommendations came from original posts after a 10-point rise in Q4 2025 (Meta, Jan 2026). Moodboard accounts built on reposted images and other people's photos lose recommendation reach. Every post the brand makes should be its own footage.
- **Trial Reels as the hook lab.** Trial Reels go to non-followers first, show metrics after about 24 hours and can auto-share to followers based on the first 72 hours (Meta). Test one variable at a time (first frame, spoken hook, product, offer, creator, edit) and promote the winning hooks into Meta and TikTok ads instead of asking a media buyer to invent creative.
- **Cadence:** a brand that posted 16 times in 10 months had its stall diagnosed as volume first; the advice was near-daily posts, several Stories a day and engaging with similar brands (r/ClothingStartups, 27-pt thread). Cadence depth: `organic-social`.
- **Give products distinctive names.** A Gen Z shopper searched the exact product name on TikTok and watched 10+ product videos before buying; generic names surface competitors (r/ecommerce, 598-pt thread). Publish the brand's ad UGC organically too, as a searchable library of views, fits and objections.

## Creator seeding for clothing

Selection checklist (research report, 2026, plus operator notes):
1. Content already looks like how the brand wants the piece shown.
2. Recent median views, not followers; comments that ask "where is this from".
3. Geography the brand ships to.
4. **Size compatibility.** Ask their size and fit preference before shipping; a wrong size kills the post. Offer two sizes if the fit is unusual.
5. Willingness to grant paid usage rights, priced separately.
6. Not promoting three competitors this month.

Rules:
- **No posting obligation on gifts.** Forced low-conviction posts look sponsored and lack enthusiasm (research report, 2026). Gift first; pay the responsive creators who decline gift-only (backpack brand, 7x average attributed return on product plus fees, r/Entrepreneur, 2,740-pt thread).
- **A cohort, not a celebrity.** Seed 20 to 50 tightly matched nano and micro creators instead of one macro bet (research report 90-day plan). 81% of fashion collaborations in Flowbox's data are repeat partnerships: rebook the ones who sold.
- **Match the look to the niche.** A gothic store gave product to influencers with a gothic look for a shoutout with a 10% to 20% code and made its sales through Instagram (r/shopify, 95-pt thread).
- **Track every creator:** product cost, shipping, fee, commission, usage fee; then content delivered, views, code or link revenue, assisted revenue, ad reuse and how long the footage keeps working.
- **Separate three rights in writing:** posting, organic reuse by the brand, paid usage (whitelisting, partnership ads, Spark codes). An operator reports whitelisted ads beat their usual creatives 9 times in 10 on thumbnail stops, CTR and watch time (r/shopify, 98-pt thread).
- **Recut, do not just boost.** About 20 source videos and photos became 50 to 100 ad variants by changing hooks, CTAs and angles (r/Entrepreneur, 2,740-pt thread).
- **Revenue-share platforms** (one 7-figure POD operator uses Trybe, where creators earn a cut of sales they drive) and inbound DMs from fans are cheap creative sources (r/printondemand, 35-pt thread).
- **Paid shoutout pages are mostly dead weight now;** if used, vet with Social Blade for bought followers and ignore unsolicited $20 offers (r/ecommerce, 281-pt thread, older account: $100 a shoutout on a 300k men's fashion page, $75 each for five).

## TikTok and TikTok Shop for apparel

- **One video can carry a year.** A 3-year clothing brand reports one TikTok at about 500k views drove $50k+ in sales, and says virality is now harder, so it invests in a vertical-content creative team (r/ClothingStartups, 87-pt thread).
- **TikTok's own hero-SKU checklist** (2025, platform guidance): 500+ units a month of inventory, 100+ samples a month, 15% commission, 15% product discount, 5 to 10 new videos per product per week. For apparel, multiply the inventory check by size: 500 units spread over six sizes and three colors is 28 per variant.
- **Plan stock with the affiliate program.** A creator spike on a size-split product sells out M and L first; set order caps per variant and keep back-in-stock capture on. Increase inventory only after creator output exists; hundreds of samples without a content system turn inventory into waste (research report, 2026).
- **Mass TikTok Shop apparel winners sit at $25 to $36.** An operator tracking 1.2M US listings reported an athletic tee at $28.77 gaining 311,823 units in a week (4.3 rating), a sweater at $35.37 (+244,491) and yoga sweatpants at $26.54 (+123,908) (r/ecommerce, 70-pt thread, self-reported data). Screen by 7-day unit growth, rating 4.0+, one product per shop; a $90 streetwear hoodie is not competing in that lane and should use TikTok for content and creators, with checkout on its own store if Shop economics fail.
- **Authorized affiliate videos can run as Shop Ads,** and seller reports split organic affiliate GMV from ad-attributed revenue (TikTok, Nov 2026). Commissions range 1% to 80% in Open Collaboration (TikTok Shop, Nov 2026).
- **POD and TikTok Shop SLAs collide.** A seller paying for Gelato Plus saw 100% late dispatch on 30 orders (r/printondemand, 22-pt thread). Another was told by support to pay Printify $4.50 to ship each tee to themselves and forward it with a TikTok label, adding 3 to 5 days, and started closing the shop (r/TikTokshop, 22-pt thread). Hold stock of TikTok Shop SKUs or skip Shop for POD lines.

## UGC and reviews from customers

- **Ask the fit question, not just stars.** "How did the fit compare with what you expected? Reply with your usual size, the size you bought, and whether it felt small / true / roomy" (research report, 2026). Show the resulting fit distribution on the product page.
- **Free product for a video, or a chance at a full refund for a qualifying video,** in the post-purchase email: one brand collected 300 videos in under 60 days (r/ecommerce, 104-pt thread; non-apparel brand, and the OP says not worth it under about $15k a month).
- **Record locally gifted pieces as Shopify orders** so the recipient gets the review request (r/shopify, 84-pt thread).
- **Group reviews by objection and body type** (fit, fabric, sizing for tall or curvy buyers) so shoppers find proof that matches them (r/ecommerce, 69-pt thread).
- **Never pay or discount for a positive review, ask only happy customers, or present a paid creator as an unpaid customer.** Platform and consumer rules: `ecommerce-brand` and `email-sms-loyalty`.
