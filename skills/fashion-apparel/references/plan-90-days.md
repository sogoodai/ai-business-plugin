# 90-day plans: a new label and a stalled one

Each phase ends with an exit check. Do not move to paid scaling until the checks before it pass. Daily loop: flag any ad or creator push where expected demand exceeds size-level stock cover. Weekly loop: rank creatives by spend, CTR, purchase rate, return-adjusted contribution and first-time-customer share. Monthly loop: landed cost, blended CAC, repeat, sell-through, returns, markdown exposure and cash in inventory (research report, 2026).

## Plan A: new label, from samples to the second drop

**Days 1 to 14: economics and setup**
- Pick 3 to 5 styles, 1 to 3 of them heroes. Not 20.
- Landed cost per style, price, return-adjusted contribution, break-even ROAS ([economics.md](economics.md)). If wholesale is wanted later, check landed at or under MSRP / 4 now.
- Set the audience's price ceiling and fit the garment to it.
- Order samples (or run the POD test for graphic tees). Use the eight factory questions and written tolerances ([scripts.md](scripts.md)).
- Tracking: Meta and TikTok pixels with server-side events, UTMs, creator codes, post-purchase "how did you hear about us", return reasons, inventory by size.
- Waitlist page that asks for size; email and SMS consent stored.
- Start building in public: sketches, fabric, sample fixes.
- Exit check: contribution per order after a planning return rate meets the owner's floor; sample ordered with a date.

**Days 15 to 30: validate before bulk**
- Post the physical sample on TikTok and Reels; use Instagram Trial Reels to test 3 to 5 hooks.
- DM about 20 people in the target community for honest feedback on design, name and price.
- PR boxes to friends and family who will post Stories; a strong post waiting on the profile.
- Seed 10 to 20 tightly matched nano and micro creators with samples, no posting obligation, size asked first.
- Story polls between colorways; record size choices from the waitlist.
- Decide run size from the list and its size split; 25 to 100 units per style, or a preorder window if cash is short.
- Exit check: list size and size curve known; one design clearly ahead on saves, votes or signups. If nothing pulls, change the design, not the ad budget.

**Days 31 to 45: production and the page**
- Confirm ship date in writing; set the drop date with buffer for freight and QC.
- Product pages: garment measurements, model stats and size worn, fit note, front and back photos, a try-on video, care card text, returns line next to Add to Cart.
- Flows live: welcome, cart, back-in-stock by size, post-purchase care and fit email. Depth: `email-sms-loyalty`.
- Tease arc: process, close-ups, then date and fit notes.
- Exit check: page complete on mobile; flows tested; checkout load-tested if the list is large.

**Days 46 to 60: the drop**
- Early access to the list (exact time and timezone), then public.
- Community posts (r/streetwearstartup or the niche's own forums), a local pop-up or event, the channel code.
- Sold-out sizes switch to back-in-stock capture; post-drop "missed it" flow.
- Fit review request with the usual-size question; collect creator and customer content with usage rights.
- Exit check: sell-through by size; site conversion against 1.76% to 2.77% (Shogun, Shopify 2026); return reasons by size; first creator who drove orders.

**Days 61 to 75: amplify what proved itself**
- Paid only if the gate passes ([paid-ads.md](paid-ads.md)): positive contribution after returns, fit info live, stock cover on the top sizes.
- Meta with 4 to 6 genuinely different concepts from the content that already worked; whitelisted creator ads; thank-you ad to 14-day buyers.
- Restock the hero sizes that sold out; plan drop 2 as a new colorway or graphic on the proven blank.
- Exit check: new-customer CAC below break-even; no stockout on hero sizes during spend.

**Days 76 to 90: review and second drop**
- Cohort review: CAC, 30/60/90-day repeat (compare 10.91% at 90 days, Adtribute 2026), return rate by SKU x size x reason, creator payback, sell-through, aged sizes.
- Second creator cohort chosen from the first cohort's data.
- If a SKU passes the 4x check, line sheet and 5 to 10 local boutiques ([scripts.md](scripts.md)).
- Exit check: one repeatable source of first-time customers; a dated drop calendar for the next two quarters.

## Plan B: stalled label with sales history

**Days 1 to 14: find where the money leaks**
- 12 months of orders by SKU, size, channel and month; refunds and exchanges by reason; landed cost refreshed (freight, duty, blank price changes).
- Contribution per channel after its own fees; contribution per SKU after returns.
- Aged inventory by size; days of cover on hero sizes.
- What the top 20% of SKUs have in common (fit, color, price band, graphic style) (r/ecommerce, 58-pt thread, 27-up comment).
- Repeat rate vs 19.1% for clothing at 365 days (By the Numbers 2025 to 2026); median days from first to second order.
- List health: suppress long-inactive subscribers after peak season (one operator uses no opens in 400 days, r/ecommerce, 71-pt thread); check consent records.
- Exit check: the three biggest leaks in money, not percentages (for example, returns on one fit, an unprofitable marketplace, dead stock in two colors).

**Days 15 to 30: stop the leaks**
- Reprice or drop SKUs and channels with negative contribution.
- Exchange-first returns policy; fit fixes on the top 5 SKUs by return cost; fix grading at the factory if one size returns abnormally.
- Clear aged sizes in a mystery bundle event, not a sitewide sale.
- Back-in-stock by size, post-purchase care, win-back at the brand's own repeat interval.
- Founder notes to 10 past customers written one by one; Google the customer list for people with audiences and ask them personally (operator method from a non-apparel brand, r/ecommerce, 116-pt thread).
- Exit check: return rate on the fixed SKUs trending down; cash freed from aged stock.

**Days 31 to 45: content reset**
- Original footage only; Trial Reels for hooks; the content matrix in [creators-and-content.md](creators-and-content.md).
- Rename products with distinctive, searchable names.
- New creator cohort picked on recent views, fit and size; usage rights priced.
- Exit check: at least two hooks beating the account's previous median views.

**Days 46 to 60: relaunch through a drop**
- A new colorway or graphic on the best-selling blank, plus a restock of core sizes.
- Waitlist with size question, exact time, early access, sold-out handling.
- Exit check: list growth, drop sell-through by size, conversion vs the last 90 days.

**Days 61 to 75: paid, diagnosed**
- Diagnose with the CPM, CTR, conversion table in [paid-ads.md](paid-ads.md) before cutting anything.
- Fix the catalog feed (size, color, gender, age group, item group); feed only in-stock sizes.
- Scale only what clears contribution with stock cover.

**Days 76 to 90: expand or stop**
- Test one new channel only if its contribution after fees clears the floor (boutiques, Depop for small-batch or vintage-adjacent lines, Etsy for niche or personalized, TikTok Shop for $25 to $36 try-on products).
- Exit check: contribution per month up; a drop calendar; a list of SKUs and channels the brand stopped.

## Questions to ask the owner at the start

1. What do you sell (cut and sew, blanks plus print, POD, resale, multi-brand boutique), at what prices, in which countries?
2. Landed cost per style, and who makes it (factory, print shop, POD provider, in-house)? Lead time for a reorder?
3. Last 12 months: revenue by channel, return and exchange rates, top sizes, aged stock.
4. List size by channel and how consent was collected.
5. Drops or always-on? How did the last drop sell through, by size?
6. Paid spend by platform and how results are measured.
7. Wholesale accounts or interest, and the terms used.
8. Offers you will and will not run; who approves prices, claims and messages.
9. Any sustainability, origin or "handmade" claims in use, and the evidence behind them.
