# Pricing, discounts, wholesale and marketplaces for apparel

General pricing psychology, price-increase notices and offer design: `offers-pricing`. Etsy, Amazon and TikTok Shop mechanics: `ecommerce-brand`. This file keeps the apparel rules.

## Setting the price

- **Price backward from the channel economics.** DTC apparel targets 55% to 65% gross margin; the industry ran at 56.88% in January 2026 (Shopify citing NYU Stern). A $60 tee at 60% margin can absorb only $24 of landed cost before fulfillment, fees, returns and marketing.
- **Operators' floor: 40% to 50% margin after ads, before tax** (r/ClothingStartups, 87-pt thread). Below that, scaling paid traffic rarely works.
- **If wholesale is in the plan, landed cost must be at or under a quarter of retail** ([economics.md](economics.md) section 5).
- **Set the audience's price ceiling before designing,** then fit fabric, trims and finishing inside it. A tech-pack designer's question: "Who's going to wear this, and will they actually pay for it?" Their middle-income kidswear line capped at about $20 and did well; a resortwear line sold up to $350 with local celebrity partners, special dyeing and in-house seamstresses (r/ClothingStartups, 30-pt thread).
- **Premium needs a visible reason.** $400 lambskin pants met "you can get designer pants for around 400 on sale / second hand"; commenters pointed to leather and slow-fashion communities and customizable pockets instead (r/ClothingStartups, 23-pt thread). A basic twill jacket got "why would I buy this from you when I could go get the Carhartt, Dickies, Stussy version" (62-pt thread).
- **Do not justify price with material cost.** A £75 silver ring held about £2.60 of metal; the advice was to frame it as design, time and equipment, or not justify it at all: "Never justify your price to anyone. Your price is your price." (r/streetwearstartup, 1,523-pt thread).
- **Check landed before pricing premium denim:** for selvedge at $50 a pair in materials, commenters split between $160 and "$200 minimum" if $50 was the delivered cost (r/ClothingStartups, 21-pt thread).
- **Low entry prices are a choice with a cost.** A streetwear drop listing "$15 Tees, $12 Hats, $10 beanies, $24 crewnecks! Limited supply!" sold out its tie-dyes, with the owner taking "a very small amount of profit" (r/streetwearstartup, 953-pt thread). Fine for a first community drop; it leaves nothing for ads or wholesale.
- **POD price floor:** $8 to $10 profit per sale after every fee; surviving Etsy POD shops priced $26 to $32 (r/printondemand, 170-pt thread). Details in `ecommerce-brand` pod-handmade.md.

## Discount cadence and markdowns

- **Leaders discount deeper, but only at events.** Top apparel brands averaged 10.2% discount depth vs 7.7% marketwide, peaking at 22.43% at Black Friday 2024 (market 12.55%) and back to 5.48% by September 2025 (Triple Whale 2025).
- **Black Friday expectations are about 40% off for clothing** (BCG France, 2025). That is an event benchmark. At 60% gross margin a 40% discount needs three times the units ([economics.md](economics.md) section 4).
- **Reserve the deepest storewide offer for one annual event.** A store running 25% off everything only at Black Friday hit 15.34% conversion vs its usual 3% to 5%, with many SKUs selling out (r/ecommerce, 196-pt thread).
- **Markdown by size and age, never the hero.** Full price while size availability is healthy and sell-through on plan; mild promotion for aged sizes; deeper markdown only when replenishment no longer makes sense (research report, 2026). Do not discount a sold-through hero because the rest of the store is slow.
- **Clear odd sizes in a recurring event** (mystery bundle) instead of sitewide codes (r/ClothingStartups, 23-pt thread).
- **Drop the always-on coupon.** A POD seller stopped renewing a daily 10% code and saw no big change in sales; a "10% off 3 items" offer stayed (r/printondemand, 84-pt thread).
- **Better than percent off:** spend thresholds ("spend X get Y" moved the needle for a 7-figure clothing brand's emails, r/ecommerce, 29-pt thread); post-purchase add-on at 15% to 20% off a matching item (red shorts after a red tee, r/dropship, 1,564-pt thread); a free tee or sticker as a 3-day gift with purchase after Black Friday instead of another discount (r/ecommerce, 71-pt thread).
- **Preorder price step:** a lower preorder price that rises when stock lands ($39.99 to $50, r/streetwearstartup, 1,297-pt thread) rewards early buyers without discounting stock.
- **Exclude winning product pages from discount popups** so the hero does not train buyers to wait (r/shopify, 179-pt thread).

## Wholesale to boutiques

Reorder mechanics, prepaid first orders, big-retail traps and MAP enforcement are in `ecommerce-brand` references/wholesale.md. Apparel specifics:

- **Retailers need about 50% margin, so MSRP is about 2x wholesale** (Faire 2026). Brand margin on wholesale must survive that ([economics.md](economics.md) section 5).
- **Faire fees:** outside North America 25% on marketplace-originated first orders (including a 10% surcharge), 15% after; in North America 15% plus a $10 new-customer fee on opening orders; boutiques the brand brings itself can be commission-free (Faire brand terms 2026). Pitch boutiques directly and send them the brand's own Faire referral link.
- **Four gates before saying yes** (research report, 2026): retailer margin, brand margin after rep commission or platform fee, MOQ and order economics, and replenishment lead time.
- **The line sheet carries:** style name and number, photo on body and flat, colorways, size run, wholesale price, MSRP, opening minimum (units or value), size-run prepacks if used, delivery window, reorder lead time, and sell-through evidence. Pitch template in [scripts.md](scripts.md).
- **Lead with sell-through, not story.** "Has sold through [real evidence] in [timeframe]" is the line buyers read (research report pitch).
- **Get paid upfront from new accounts.** A boutique chain asking for custom labels, net 60 and returns of unsold goods is a financing request; counter with a smaller prepaid order (r/smallbusiness, 925-pt thread, candle maker; same logic for apparel).
- **Consignment:** no robust public data on consignment sell-through. Treat it as a test for one or two stores, with written terms on split, payment date, damaged and unsold stock, and a pickup date.
- **Stockists abroad come later:** the Dublin brand reached five Japanese stockists and a Paris showroom after years of local scene-building (r/streetwearstartup, 2,082-pt thread).

## Marketplaces

| Channel | 2026 fees | Fits | Watch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Etsy | $0.20 listing, 6.5% on item plus shipping, processing, Offsite Ads 15% or 12% | Searchable niche designs, personalized apparel, POD with margin | Commodity tees cannot absorb fees plus ads |
| Amazon US | Referral 5% at $15 or less, 10% over $15 to $20, 17% over $20; $39.99 a month | Search demand for a defined niche; Merch on Demand for POD designs | A $35 tee pays $5.95 referral before fulfillment; GS1 barcodes |
| Depop | No selling fee in UK, US, AU; processing only; 12% on boosted sales of new UK and US listings from 23 Mar 2026 | Vintage, upcycled, small-batch streetwear | Organic vs boosted are different businesses; 10% elsewhere |
| Faire | 25% first order outside North America (15% plus $10 in North America), 15% reorders, 0% for retailers you bring | Boutique wholesale | Model the 25% first order |
| Zalando | Invite-only as of 29 Sep 2026; fees behind login | EU reach, 60M+ customers in 25 countries | Do not quote public "commission tables" |
| TikTok Shop | See `ecommerce-brand` tiktok-shop.md | $25 to $36 mass apparel, try-on friendly products | SLAs, size-split stock, affiliate commission |

Agent rules:
- **Contribution by sale source.** Boosted Depop, Etsy Offsite Ads and marketplace-originated Faire orders are not the same economics as organic sales on the same platform.
- **Use marketplaces to find demand, then own it.** Etsy when the brand has no audience, its own store once it has one (r/printondemand, 69-pt thread). Historic Merch by Amazon cases: aviation shirts made about $10k profit in a year from organic Amazon search at $3 to $6 per sale (r/Entrepreneur, 1,037-pt thread); 2016 royalties were about $8 on a $19.99 shirt (r/Entrepreneur, 1,800-pt thread; older, terms changed).
- **Resale signals demand:** niche-brand pieces "sell within seconds" on Fleek while Nike, Polo, Carhartt and older Adidas stay steady sellers (r/ClothingStartups, 18-pt thread). Check resale listings for the brand's own pieces; resale prices above retail mean the brand is underpriced or understocked.
