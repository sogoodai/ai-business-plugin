# Scripts for apparel brands

Exact wording for recurring conversations. Fill every [bracket] with real facts; never invent quantities, sell-through, certifications or quotes. Lines marked "verbatim" are quoted from the source; the rest are written for this skill from the research and operator patterns cited. The owner approves every message before it is sent.

## 1. Creator seeding

**Gift DM, no posting obligation** (adapted from the research report, 2026):

> Hey [name], your [specific video, e.g. "the thrift haul where you styled the oversized blazer"] is exactly how we'd want this piece shown. We make [one-sentence difference, e.g. "300gsm boxy tees cut in Portugal that don't shrink"]. I'd like to send you the [item] in your size, with no posting obligation. If you genuinely like it, we can also offer [X]% commission and pay separately for paid usage on any video you choose to make. Interested?

**Size question, before shipping:**

> Amazing, thank you. What size do you usually wear in tees, and do you like them fitted or loose? Ours runs [boxy/true/small]: most people [height] wear [size] for the intended fit. Happy to send two sizes if you're between.

**Creator who declined a gift-only offer:**

> Totally fair. Would a paid video work? We're thinking one [try-on / fit check / styling] video for TikTok, posted by [date], [fee] plus [X]% on sales through your code. If it performs we'd like to license it for ads; we'd agree that separately.

**After they post: thank them and ask for usage rights:**

> That video is so good, thank you. Could we run it as an ad from your handle (partnership ad on Instagram / Spark code on TikTok) for [30/60/90] days? We'd pay [fee], you approve the caption, and we'd only cut it for length, not change what you said. Can you also send the raw file?

**Affiliate or TikTok Shop invite:**

> We'd love to have you in our affiliate program: [X]% on every sale through your link, a free [item] for content, and early access to drops. On TikTok Shop we can set you a target commission of [X]% and send a sample this week. Want in?

**Rebook a creator who sold:**

> Your [month] video drove [N] orders, our best this season. We drop [collection] on [date]. Want first pick of the new pieces and the same terms, or a monthly retainer for [2] videos?

**Try-on brief for creators and staff videos** (built on the Foot Locker test, where the top video covered sizing, materials, history and styling and rotated the product):

> 1. First 3 seconds: you wearing it, the hook in on-screen text ("the tee that didn't shrink after 10 washes").
> 2. Say your height and the size you're wearing, and how it fits compared with your usual size.
> 3. Show front, back (the back print), side, and a close-up of fabric and print.
> 4. Move: sit, raise your arms, so people see drape and length.
> 5. One way you'd style it.
> 6. Keep the camera steady; no glitch effects. Under 45 seconds. Mention it was gifted or paid as the platform requires.

## 2. Drops

**Teaser caption (two to three weeks out):**

> Sampling round 3. The first one was too big in the print and too loose in the body, so we fixed both. Drop date soon; the list gets the link first. [link]

**Waitlist email** (research report wording, adapted):

> Subject: Early access opens [Thursday] at [10am ET]
>
> Your link goes live 30 minutes before public release. We made [real quantity, e.g. "120"] in this run because [real reason, e.g. "the fabric is a one-off deadstock roll"]. Sold-out sizes will move to the restock list, so if your size goes, tap the size and leave your email. [Fit note: "Boxy fit, take your usual size; size down for closer."]

**Drop SMS** (short; send only to opted-in numbers):

> [Brand]: [Drop name] is live for you now, 30 min before everyone. [link] Reply STOP to opt out

**Public release post** (exact time and timezone, like "T shirt releases 26th August 18:00 CEST", r/streetwearstartup):

> [Drop name] releases [date] [time + timezone]. [N] pieces, [sizes]. Sign up for the link: [link]. Model is [height], wearing [size].

**Last sizes:**

> Down to the last [S and XL] in [colorway]. M and L sold out; leave your email on the page and we'll tell you first if we restock.

**Sold out, with an honest restock line** (use "never coming back" only if true; "the jorts are never coming back, nor is this vest once they sell out" is verbatim from a brand that meant it):

> Sold out in [time]. Thank you. [Core piece]: restock in [colors] lands [month]; your size alert is saved. [Limited piece]: this one won't come back. Next drop: [date].

**Preorder page** (operator pattern: lower preorder price that steps up, stated ship date):

> Preorder: ships by [date]. Preorder price [$39.99] with free US shipping; it goes to [$50] when stock is live. You'll get an email at every production step, and if the date moves you can cancel for a full refund.

**Preorder delay, sent before anyone asks:**

> Hi [name], straight update on your [item]: the factory finished [stage] but [real reason]. New ship date: [date]. If that no longer works, reply "refund" and we'll refund in full today, no questions. Otherwise, thank you for waiting; we're adding [small real thing, e.g. a sticker pack] to your parcel.

**Back in stock, by size:**

> Your size is back: [item] in [size, color]. We restocked [N] and held the link for the alert list for [24 hours]. [link]

**Missed the drop:**

> You missed [drop], sorry. Closest thing in stock: [item] ([link]). Your size alert is saved, and the next drop is [date]. Want the link early again?

**Store closed between drops:**

> We're closed until [date]. Sign up for the drop link: [link]

**Reddit or community showcase opener** (verbatim lines that did well on r/streetwearstartup):

> Posting this here to get real feedback, not validation. If you think this is pointless or impractical, say it.

> I only made 40 of them! ... use code 'reddit' at checkout for 10% off your order!

## 3. Wholesale to boutiques

**First email to a buyer** (research report structure, adapted):

> Subject: [Brand] for [Store]: [category] that sold through in [timeframe]
>
> Hi [buyer name],
> We make [specific product, e.g. "heavyweight graphic tees and crewnecks"] for [specific customer]. Our top style retails at $[MSRP], wholesales at $[WSP], and sold [real evidence: "312 units in 6 weeks DTC" / "reordered twice at [stockist]"]. Opening order is [minimum units or value], with [size-run prepacks of S1 M2 L2 XL1 / open sizing]; reorders ship in [days].
> I think [2 to 3 named styles] fit your [named assortment, e.g. "the Carhartt and Dickies rail by the window"] and your [customer]. Can I send the line sheet, or drop off samples on [day]?
> [Name], [phone]
> P.S. If you prefer ordering on Faire, please use our link so the order comes through us: [link] [add Faire's current new-retailer offer only if it exists today]

**Follow-up after three days** (verbatim, from an operator who credits follow-ups with over half his press and retail opportunities; send Thursday or Friday, never Monday):

> Following up!

**Walk-in or DM to a local boutique:**

> Hi, I'm [name], I make [brand] here in [city]. I've got three pieces your customers would get: [styles]. Could I leave a sample and the line sheet for whoever does buying? Retail [$], wholesale [$], I can deliver myself.

**Opening order terms:**

> Thanks for the order. For first orders we take payment upfront (card or transfer) and ship within [days]. From the second order we can do [net 30]. Every order comes with a shelf card, product photos for your Instagram, and a one-line staff pitch.

**Reorder check-in, day 30:**

> How are the [styles] selling? If you're down to your last few in M and L, I can ship a top-up in [days] before the weekend.

**Consignment, only if the owner wants to test it:**

> Happy to try consignment for [8] weeks: [N] pieces, [60/40] split, paid on the [1st] of each month for what sold, unsold and damaged stock handled as [terms], pickup on [date].

## 4. Manufacturers and print shops

**First inquiry to a cut-and-sew factory** (factories answer clarity, not story):

> Subject: Sample + bulk inquiry: [garment], [fabric], [quantity] per style
>
> Hello,
> We are [brand], selling [channel] in [countries]. We'd like a quote for:
> - Garment: [boxy tee / zip hoodie / straight-leg denim], tech pack attached (measurements, tolerances, materials, trims, labels).
> - Fabric: [composition, GSM, knit or weave, color]; open to your in-stock equivalent for sampling.
> - Quantity: [N] per style, [N] colors, size run [XS to XXL], first order [N] total; planned reorders every [N weeks] if it sells.
> - Labels and packaging: [woven neck label, care label, polybag]; we can use your standard packaging if it lowers the MOQ.
> - Target ex-factory price: [$] at this quantity.
> Please confirm: sample cost and what it includes, MOQ per style, color and size, lead time after sample approval, and payment terms.
> Thank you, [name]

**Sample request: the eight questions to answer in writing:**

> Before we pay for the sample, please confirm:
> 1. What the sample price includes (fabric, trims, labels, shipping).
> 2. The real MOQ per style, per color and per size.
> 3. Who owns the pattern and tech pack.
> 4. What happens if the first sample is wrong, and the cost of a second.
> 5. Whether trims, labels, packaging and grading are included in the bulk price.
> 6. The timeline from sample approval to shipping.
> 7. How defects are handled and credited.
> 8. Expected shrinkage and stretch, and how you test it.

**Tolerance and QC confirmation, before the deposit:**

> Please confirm the tolerance on key measurements is plus or minus [1.5 cm] (chest, length, sleeve, waist, inseam), per the spec sheet. We'll approve lab dips, the print strike-off and trims before bulk, and we'll measure [5] pieces per size from bulk before the balance payment. If more than [x] are outside tolerance, we'll agree a fix before shipping.

**Quote request to a screen print shop** (written to avoid the "bulk order, it's gonna blow up" red flags shops describe):

> Hi, quote request for a confirmed order:
> - Blank: [brand + style number], [garment color]. Supplied by [you / us].
> - Quantity by size: S [n], M [n], L [n], XL [n], 2XL [n]. Total [n].
> - Front: [n] colors, [size, e.g. 4 in wide], [placement, e.g. left chest, 3 in below collar].
> - Back: [n] colors, [size, e.g. 12 in wide], [placement, e.g. 4 in below collar].
> - Ink: [plastisol / water-based / discharge], Pantone [codes].
> - Art: vector / 300 dpi at print size attached; [we need / we don't need] separations.
> - Need by: [date], [pickup / ship to address].
> Please quote setup and per-piece price, and the price at [2x quantity] so we can plan the reorder. We'd like to approve a first pull before the run.

**Defect claim to a supplier:**

> Order [number]: [n] of [n] pieces arrived [defect, e.g. "print placed 2 to 3 inches lower than the approved mockup"], photos and measurements attached against the approved proof. Please confirm by [date] whether you will reprint or credit [amount]. We have [n] customer orders waiting on this stock.

## 5. Customers

**Fit note under the size selector** (research report wording):

> Alex is 5'11"/180 cm, chest 38"/97 cm, wearing M for the intended relaxed fit. Size down for a closer fit. Garment chest measurements are measured flat, pit-to-pit, then doubled.

**Return policy** (research report wording; check local law before charging any fee in the EU or UK):

> Free size exchanges within 30 days. Returns for refund incur a [actual fee] return-label deduction. Store-credit returns are free. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and returned with original tags.

**Fit review request** (research report wording):

> How did the fit compare with what you expected? Reply with your usual size, the size you bought, and whether it felt small / true / roomy. We use this to improve the fit note for the next customer.

**Care card in every parcel** (from a former professional garment printer's advice):

> To keep the print sharp: wash inside out, 30°C max, no fabric softener, no vinegar, don't iron the print. Tumble low or hang dry.

**Double-checking an unusual measurement** (verbatim, r/Etsy):

> I noticed these are not quite what I usually see, I usually expect a range of x-x, just double checking that measurement was correct.

**Founder note to past customers, written one by one** (adapted from an operator who reports about 4x conversion vs automated emails; start with 10):

> Hi [first name], I'm the founder of [brand]. Just checking in: how's the [item] holding up after a few washes? We just dropped [new piece] and past customers get [10%] off this week. If anything about the fit or print let you down, tell me; I read every reply.

**Sustainability wording** (research report examples):

> Safe: "Body fabric contains 72% GRS-certified recycled polyester by weight."
> Risky: "Our sustainable hoodie is good for the planet."

## 6. Ad and caption hooks for apparel

- "I couldn't find a [heavyweight tee] that didn't [shrink/twist], so we made one."
- "5'6" and 6'2", both in M. Here's how it fits."
- "Three ways to wear the [cargo pant] this week."
- "Washed 20 times. Here's the print." (only with a real wash test)
- "Why this [hoodie] costs [$]: [GSM] cotton, [where] made, [construction detail]."
- "[N] made. [N] left in M." (only real counts)
- "We moved the print to the back. Here's why."
- "Made for [identity: night-shift nurses / drummers / climbers], not for everyone."
