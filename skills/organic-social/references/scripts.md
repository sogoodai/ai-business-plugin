# Scripts: hooks, captions, CTAs, DMs and requests

Fill the brackets with the business's real facts. Never invent a number, a result, a client or a price. Draft only; the owner approves and sends. "Verbatim" marks wording quoted from a source; the rest are templates built on the mechanisms in [formats-and-hooks.md](formats-and-hooks.md).

## Hooks

### By mechanism

Loss
- "Before you spend [$ amount] on [purchase], check these three things."
- "This [mistake] costs [customer type] about [$ / hours] a [month]."
- "Don't sign a [contract / quote] until you've asked these 3 questions."

Specific expertise
- "I [do the job] every week. These are the two [products / brands] I'd never buy."
- "[Number] years as a [role]. This is the [thing] I check first."
- Verbatim: "I spent 30 years watching people hold themselves back" (r/InstagramMarketing, 275 pts)

Counterfactual
- "If I had to get my first ten [customers] again, I'd do this first."
- "If I were buying a [product] tomorrow, here's exactly what I'd do."

Diagnostic
- "If your [thing] looks like this, don't replace it yet."
- "Three signs your [thing] needs [service], and one sign it doesn't."

Comparison
- "[$50] [product] vs [$500] [product]: the difference that actually matters."
- "We tested [option A] and [option B] on [real job]. Here's what happened."

Situation, constraint, fear (verbatim pattern, r/InstagramMarketing 113 pts)
- "If you're a single mum in your 20s or 30s, who only has 45 minutes to work out, and you're nervous about going to the gym, here's what you need to know."
- Template: "If you're a [who] with [constraint] and you're worried about [fear], here's what you need to know."

Specific action, timeframe, unexpected result (verbatim, r/InstagramMarketing 69 pts)
- "I used a foam roller daily for 60 days and my mobility got worse."
- Template: "We [did X] for [time] and [surprising result]."

Tested on customers (verbatim, r/Coaching 37 pts: 8 DMs, 2 clients)
- "I tested 5 recovery methods on 30+ clients over 40. Here's what actually reduced soreness and improved performance."
- Close: "The real magic is combining these, not doing one perfectly."

Story and curiosity (verbatim, r/InstagramMarketing 179 pts)
- "Can I tell you a secret? But don't tell my wife."
- "Watch what happens when [X] does [Y]." (r/InstagramMarketing, 182 pts thread)

First reveal (verbatim, r/InstagramMarketing 68 pts)
- "I just received the samples for the first collection for my small business."

Paid for free (r/socialmedia, 24 pts)
- "Get this [paid thing] for free."

## Captions

**Search-first caption**
```
[Exact phrase the buyer searches, as the first line]
[Two to four sentences that answer it, with one real number or detail]
[One CTA: comment KEYWORD / save this / send it to someone who ...]
[0 to 3 specific hashtags or none]
```
Example: "Kitchen remodel cost in Leeds (2026). Our last 12 kitchens ranged from £14k to £31k. Cabinets were 35 to 40% of every budget. Comment PRICE and I'll send the full breakdown."

**Soft-opinion ending** (instead of a question, r/socialmedia 123 pts): "This feels underrated." "This doesn't scale past 10 staff." "Most people skip step 2. That's the one that matters."

**Carousel last slide** (verbatim pattern, r/InstagramMarketing 97 pts)
- "Save this for your next interview."
- "Send this to someone job hunting right now."
- Template: "Save this for your next [moment]." "Send this to the [person] who [situation]."

**Avoid:** "Exciting news", "We're thrilled to announce", "Agree?", "Let that sink in", one sentence per line broetry, emoji bullets (coreyhaines31 social skill, MIT). Never "Message me for my program" inside teaching content.

## Comment-to-DM flow

**On-screen and spoken CTA**
- "Comment PRICE and I'll send you the exact cost breakdown."
- "Comment GUIDE and I'll DM you the checklist."
- Verbatim: "comment banana and I'll send you my guide" (r/socialmedia, 276 pts)

**Public auto-replies (rotate 3 to 5 variants)**
- "Sent, check your DMs."
- "Just messaged you."
- "In your inbox now, [first name]."

**First DM**
```
Here it is: [link or file]
Quick one so I send the right extra info: are you looking at this for [option A], [option B] or just curious?
[button A] [button B] [button C]
```

**After the answer**
```
[A]: Got it. Most [A] projects land between [range]. The biggest factor is [factor].
Want me to send our booking link, or would you rather send a photo and I'll give you a rough number?
```

**Email capture step (when the asset lives off-platform)**
```
I'll email you the full version so you have it later. What's the best email?
```

## DM scripts

**Price question**
```
Hi [name], thanks for asking. [Service] usually runs [range] here.
What moves it up or down is [1 to 2 factors].
If you tell me [the one detail you need], I can give you a closer number today.
```

**Welcome a new follower** (short, no pitch; r/InstagramMarketing 97 pts)
```
Thanks for the follow, [name]. Saw you found us through the [topic] post. Anything you're working on with [topic]?
```

**Hot lead handoff** (to a human within minutes)
```
Thanks [name]. I can do [day] at [time] or [day] at [time] for a quick call. Which works?
```
On a yes: "Does Thursday at 10am work?" beats a calendar link (r/LinkedInTips, 57 pts).

**Separate opt-in** (r/content_marketing, 38 pts)
- After value: "I have this [free thing] if you want it. Let me know and I'll send it to you."
- After they used it: "I have this [product]. If you want details on it, let me know and I'll send them over."

**Not now**
```
Totally fine. When would be a better time to check back? I'll message you then.
```
Then message on that date; in between, send one useful post or result every few weeks instead of "any update?" (r/LinkedInTips, 57 pts).

**After-hours auto-reply**
```
Thanks for the message. We're [closed / on a job] right now and will reply by [time] tomorrow. If it's urgent, call [number].
```

## LinkedIn

**First lines** (first-person event; no question opener)
- "We lost a client last month because of [specific thing]. Here's what we changed."
- "I reviewed [number] [documents / quotes / audits] this quarter. The same mistake showed up in [X] of them."
- "Here's what happened when I tried [specific experiment]." (replaces "Here's how to ...", r/LinkedInTips 22 pts)

**Resource post** (r/content_marketing, 65 pts pattern)
```
[Problem that costs the audience money, in one line]
We built [the resource] for our own team. It [does what, with a number].
[3 bullets of what is inside]
Comment "[KEYWORD]" and I'll send it over.
```

**Comment on a decision-maker's post** (verbatim template, r/LinkedInTips 42 pts)
```
This is exactly what we kept hitting too. The thing that finally moved the needle for us was [specific thing]. What have you tried so far?
```

**Connection note** (only when personal; verbatim pattern r/LinkedInTips 22 pts)
- "Hi Liam, your interview with Debbie Millman was wonderful. This line gave me chills: [quote]. Incredible."
- "Hey Nicole, congrats on 40k! You are absolutely crushing."

## Partnerships and requests

**Collab pitch to a complementary business**
```
Hi [name], I'm [owner] from [business]. Our customers keep asking about [their category], and we send people your way.
Idea: a joint post on [specific topic], filmed at [your place / ours], posted as an Instagram Collab so it shows on both profiles.
We bring [what you contribute]. Takes about 30 minutes. Open to it next [week / month]?
```

**UGC permission request**
```
Hi [name], we love this [photo / video] of your [product / result]. Can we share it on our Instagram and website, with credit to @[handle]?
We'd only use it in organic posts [and in ads, if you're happy with that: just say yes to both].
```

**Customer story request** (adapted from the jewelry brand in the creator digest)
```
Would you film a 30-second video telling us who you are and why you chose [product / service]? Phone video is perfect.
As a thank-you we'll [specific small reward].
```

**Creator brief** (verbatim core, r/Entrepreneur 2,740 pts)
```
"Just make stuff your audience will watch & enjoy."
No discount codes or scarcity lines. Tell them honestly what you like and one thing you'd change.
Must include: [product shown in use], [disclosure #ad or paid partnership label], [tracked link or code].
```

**Employee brief**
```
This week's raw material: [fact / chart / photo / customer situation].
Angles you could take: [1], [2], [3]. Write it in your own words or skip it. No client names, no prices.
Mention that you work at [company] if you're talking about our services.
```

**Founder weekly prompt**
- "What did we learn this week that would save one of our customers time, money or risk?"
- Then: observation, mistake, consequence, procedure, one question.

## Giveaway

**Caption** (structure from r/content_marketing, 96 pts)
```
GIVEAWAY: win [our product, with value]
1. Follow @[brand]
2. Comment [simple specific answer, e.g. your ring size]
Bonus entry: share to your Story and tag us
Winner announced live on [day] at [time and time zone]. [Country] only. Not sponsored or endorsed by Instagram.
```

**Naming contest** (verbatim, r/ecommerce 178 pts)
- "Help us name the next product and if we use your suggestion we'll send you one for free!"
