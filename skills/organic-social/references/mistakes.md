# Expensive mistakes

Two tables: what the data and platform policies show, and what practitioners report losing.

## From the data and platform policy

| Mistake | What it costs | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Reposting others' clips with cosmetic edits (speed, borders, captions, stitched reactions) | Facebook deprioritizes it and removes recommendation and monetization eligibility (Meta, Mar 2026); original Reels views roughly doubled H2 2025 vs H2 2024 for those who filmed their own | Film your own work, product and people |
| Long unrelated captions and hashtag blocks on Facebook | Distribution limited to followers (Meta, Apr 2025) | Short relevant caption, 0 to 2 tags |
| Hashtag stuffing anywhere | Instagram caps at 5 (Dec 2025), TikTok at 5 (Aug 2025, reported); 3+ hashtags about -7% on LinkedIn (operators, 130K posts) | Keywords in the first line, on screen and spoken |
| "Reels only" on Instagram | Carousels outperformed Reels on engagement (0.55% vs 0.52%) and average views at every follower tier (Socialinsider 2026). Spending more production time for worse results | Carousels for teaching and proof, Reels for discovery and demos |
| Optimizing for likes | Sends and watch time are the discovery signals (Mosseri, Jan 2025) | Track sends and saves per reach |
| Forcing every TikTok under 30 seconds | 120 to 180 s videos had 11,136 median views vs 1,000 for 15 to 30 s (Socialinsider 2026). Explanatory reach left untested | Keep a long-video test cell |
| Posting more because views dropped | TikTok brand views -23% YoY while posting rose about 40% (Socialinsider 2026); Sprout: volume fell, engagement rose almost 20% (2025) | Marginal-frequency test; fewer, better posts |
| Company LinkedIn page as the only publisher | 659 vs 1,006 median impressions per post for personal profiles (AuthoredUp 2025 to 2026) | Founder plus 2 employees publish; page as archive |
| Comparing benchmarks from different vendors | Socialinsider Instagram 0.45% vs Hootsuite 3 to 5%: a healthy account looks broken or vice versa | One benchmark family, tracked consistently |
| Leaving social DMs for days | Fitness: Instagram inquiries answered in 37 h on average, 57% never answered (Keepme 2026) | Response-time owner, minutes in business hours, after-hours auto-reply |
| Engagement pods and auto-commenters on LinkedIn | LinkedIn says it suppresses pods and automated comments (2026); a pod member's audience narrowed to the pod (operators) | Real colleagues, real comments, no rotations |
| Fake reviews, testimonials or bought followers | Banned by the FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule (in force 21 Oct 2024); civil penalties per violation | Real customers with written permission |

## From practitioners

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Scaling content on views with no conversion path | 1M views a day for 40 days, no revenue (r/Instagram, 661 pts thread); 134,000 YouTube subscribers, 260 emails (r/youtubers, 695 pts) | Comment-to-DM or lead magnet on every high performer |
| Buying engagement or followers | Months of work earned about $8 while bought engagement cost more (r/socialmedia, 276 pts); an 80K account deleted over half its followers to recover reach (r/InstagramMarketing, 99 pts) | Never buy; remove bots if inherited |
| Follow/unfollow and pods | 13 to 200 followers in a month with Reels still under 200 views (r/InstagramMarketing, 87 pts); bot followers and a damaged engagement rate (80 pts) | Substantive comments on target accounts |
| Copying viral production styles without fit | A month of 3 posts a day copying Explore aesthetics, then a month replicating findings from 10,000 accounts: still under 200 views (r/InstagramMarketing, 87 pts) | Adapt formats that fit the business and can be shot in under an hour |
| Hours of editing per post | Reels taking about 5 hours peaked at 1,000 views (r/InstagramMarketing, 167 pts); 3 hours per Canva carousel, 400 impressions, abandoned after 3 (r/socialmedia, 40 pts) | Fixed templates; phone footage; 25 to 30 minutes per carousel |
| Outsourced posts that do not sound like the founder | $500 a month for 12 LinkedIn posts, acquaintances spotted it, zero leads (r/socialmedia, 40 pts) | Interview the founder, draft from their words, they edit |
| Paying creators on engagement stats alone | 2 of 5 LinkedIn creators produced zero visits: $500 of $1,250 wasted (r/LinkedInTips, 52 pts) | Tracked page per creator; ask for median views and audience location; pay after posting |
| Gifting product to "influencers" with promo-only feeds | Products promoted to other promoters; next to no useful exposure (r/socialmedia, 56 pts) | Screen comments for real customers before gifting |
| Big generic giveaway prize | iPad giveaway: 8,900 entries, 12% follower retention vs 85% for an own-product giveaway (r/content_marketing, 96 pts) | Prize = your product; 1 required step |
| Too many giveaway steps | 84 comments vs 7,200+ for a one-step entry (same thread) | 1 required plus 1 or 2 optional |
| Long publishing gap | Comeback Reel after 17 days: 185 views vs 400 to 1,900 for every other Reel (r/socialmedia, 24 pts) | Two-week backlog before launch |
| Teaching everything in 30 seconds | Musician account stuck for 4 months; one debate per Reel brought 1.1M+ views in a week (r/InstagramMarketing, 139 pts) | One idea per post |
| Off-topic virality | Followers who never engage with later posts; a horror author's views tanked outside the niche (r/InstagramMarketing, 130 pts) | Stay adjacent to what you sell |
| Recruiting friends and family as followers | Uninterested followers who watch 10 seconds and dilute signals (r/youtubers, 392 pts, mechanism unproven) | Let buyers follow voluntarily |
| Unsolicited link drops in communities | Bans and downvotes (r/youtubers, 101 and 175 pts) | Full-value native posts; follow each group's promo rules |
| Extension-based LinkedIn schedulers or scrapers | Client accounts banned (r/LinkedInTips, 83 pts, vendor claim); founder accounts banned in an outreach campaign (r/LinkedInTips, 28 pts) | API-connected tools; human-paced activity |
| Theme-page and repost tactics on a brand account | A 2M-follower repost page lost its view bonus after a copyright strike and was sold (r/InstagramMarketing, 1,000 pts) | Credited reposts with written permission only |
| Unpaid exclusivity in a creator or brand deal | 15 months of category lockout for $300 (r/InstagramMarketing, 69 pts) | Price exclusivity separately (see `creator`) |
| Scoping holiday coverage loosely (agencies) | Client assumed all-weekend Black Friday monitoring (r/socialmedia, 38 pts) | Pre-schedule plus two fixed reply windows; live coverage as a paid add-on |
