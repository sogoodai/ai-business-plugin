# Turning views into customers

Views are free; customers are not. A creator with 100,000+ followers reached 1M views a day for 40 days and made no money (r/Instagram, 661 pts thread). A 134,000-subscriber YouTube channel had 260 emails (r/youtubers, 695 pts). An account at 5.4M monthly views stayed fully booked while its follower count stalled for 3 months (r/InstagramMarketing, 60 pts). Build the conversion layer before chasing reach. Exact wording for every step is in [scripts.md](scripts.md).

## The design rule

**Promise, deliver, qualify, offer.** Never "comment keyword, then pitch". The content earns the comment, the DM delivers what was promised first, one question qualifies, and only then the next step (booking, quote, product link, email signup). (Research report synthesis, 2026.)

## 1. The profile is a landing page

Most buyers check the profile before they DM, call or click. Fix this before posting more.

- **Bio line 1: who it is for and the outcome.** "3D visuals and motion design for DJs, YouTubers and brands" beats a list of services (r/InstagramMarketing, 67 pts). Add the location for local businesses.
- **Bio line 2: the series promise** if you run one ("New kitchen teardown every Tuesday").
- **Bio CTA: one action with a keyword.** "DM 'VISUALS' for rates." "Book a free quote below."
- **Pinned posts answer buyer questions, not show your most viral post:** why us, how it works, who it is for, what it costs or a real result (r/content_marketing, 67 pts). Three pins on Instagram and TikTok, Featured on LinkedIn.
- **Highlights (Instagram):** Prices or "Start here", Results or Reviews, Process, FAQ, Book.
- **One link, or a short link page with at most 3 to 5 options** in the order buyers need them (book, shop the featured item, free guide). Threads allows up to 5 profile links (Meta, Mar 2025).
- **LinkedIn:** headline names the specialty, not "Owner at" (a pressure-washing owner changed his to "fleet washing specialist" and a prospect asked for a quote in week one, r/LinkedInTips, 23 pts); custom button to "Book a call" when the website is not targeted; website on the banner (r/LinkedInTips, 21 pts); banner shows one problem solved plus a few customer logos.
- **Facebook page and Pinterest name:** include the main search keyword ("Fontana | Indoor water fountain ideas").

## 2. Comment-to-DM automation

**Why.** It turns the content itself into the offer, moves intent into a private channel and avoids the "go hunt for the link in bio" step. Operators report 2 to 3x the conversion of link-in-bio (r/InstagramMarketing, 223 pts, unmeasured definition). A city travel page sends a free guide to keyword commenters and reports 100 to 120 leads a day per page and about $4,000 a month from tours and rentals (r/InstagramMarketing, 261 pts). A creator selling a $20 guide used "comment banana and I'll send you my guide" on every Reel and sold 300+ in month one (r/socialmedia, 276 pts).

**Vendor case studies (selected winners, not forecasts).** ManyChat: Amy Porterfield 3,243 leads, 85% of commenters registered for a webinar; Jenna Kutcher 3,187 emails and 1,164 purchases (reported 37% comment-to-purchase); Micha Menezes 767 organic activations in a week. A ManyChat/HypeAuditor comparison of 3,000 creators with automation vs 3,000 without found 46 vs 18 comments per post among micro creators, which is correlation, not a 156% lift (ManyChat, 2025 to 2026).

**Platform rules to respect (Meta developer docs, checked Nov 2026; recheck before launch)**

- A business can send **one private reply per comment, within 7 days** of the comment. Live comments only during the broadcast.
- Further messages require the person to reply; then the **standard 24-hour window** applies from their last message.
- The Human Agent tag extends manual replies to 7 days for unresolved inquiries; it needs its own permission and is not for automated promotion.
- Rate limit: 750 private replies per hour per professional account.
- Chats started from click-to-Instagram or Messenger ads can get a longer window ("up to 7 days" since late Sept 2026, reported). That is a `paid-ads` lever.
- Tools: ManyChat, LinkDM, Meta's built-in automations. Use an official Meta partner; never a tool that needs the account password.

**Set-up checklist**

1. One keyword per offer, short and unambiguous (GUIDE, PRICE, MENU, QUOTE). Accept common misspellings.
2. Say the keyword in the video, put it on screen and in the first caption line. Pin a comment with the keyword.
3. Public auto-reply varies its wording (several variants) so the comment section does not look like a bot farm.
4. First DM delivers the promised thing or asks one click to confirm (the click opens the 24-hour window).
5. One qualifying question with buttons (budget band, timeline, need A/B/C).
6. Route: hot answers go to a human within minutes during business hours; others get the email capture and nurture.
7. Tag every contact with post ID and keyword so revenue maps back to the post.
8. Test the flow from a personal account before going live.

**When not to use it.** Low-ticket impulse products where a shoppable tag or link sticker is one tap away; regulated advice; anything where the "free thing" does not exist yet.

## 3. Lead magnets that fit social

Principles (adapted from coreyhaines31/marketingskills lead-magnets, MIT): one specific problem, matched to the buyer's stage, consumable in under 10 minutes, with a natural path to the paid offer.

| Business | Lead magnet that works from a post | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| Trades, home services | "Real cost breakdown for a [job] in [city]" | Photo quote via DM |
| Clinic, salon, studio | "Which [treatment] is right for you" 5-question quiz | Book consult |
| Restaurant, cafe | Secret menu, tasting night list, birthday club | Reservation |
| Ecommerce | Size or fit guide, gift guide, "which one is for you" quiz | Product link with code |
| B2B services, SaaS | Calculator, template, the exact checklist from the post | Call or trial |
| Coach, course | Free first lesson, workbook, 3-email mini course | Application or call |
| Real estate | Neighborhood price sheet, first-time buyer cost list | Consultation |
| Local retail | New-arrivals list, VIP early access | Visit or reserve |

**Tricks most owners miss**

- **Resources go viral on LinkedIn when they solve a costly problem.** An SEO writing guideline, an ROI calculator and a list of 60 Facebook groups for finding writers: one post drove 1,300 visits, 75 trials, 7 customers and $1,650 MRR in 3 days (r/content_marketing, 65 pts). A Google Sheet analyzing 100 posts of a 300K-follower creator got 19K impressions, 250+ comments and 6 qualified leads in 24 hours (r/LinkedInTips, 15 pts).
- **Personal resource pages per source.** One SaaS gave each sponsored LinkedIn creator their own Notion resource page with a trial link and a "book a demo" button; creators replied to every comment with the link. $1,250 across 5 creators produced 114 signups, 39 trials, 19 paying customers at $99 a month and 8 demos (r/LinkedInTips, 52 pts). Translated pages for a French creator's audience beat the generic page.
- **Qualify comment lists.** Only 10 to 20% of LinkedIn lead-magnet commenters are qualified (r/LinkedInTips, 83 pts, tool vendor). Score them before adding to the pipeline.
- **Separate opt-ins:** offer the free thing, deliver it, wait until it is used, then offer the product ("I have this {product}. If you want details on it let me know") (r/content_marketing, 38 pts, reported 10x close rate, no baseline).
- **Prepare the destination before the post can go viral.** A founder story went viral with only a tip link; the newsletter launched a day late and still got 100 signups (r/content_marketing, 117 pts).

## 4. DMs: speed and scripts

- **Reply in minutes.** Purchase-intent DMs from curiosity content go cold fast (r/InstagramMarketing, 101 pts). Measured in fitness: membership inquiries took 37 hours to answer on Instagram and 17.5 on Facebook, vs 4 hours by email; 57% got no reply at all (Keepme 2026, 45 brands, 1.6M conversations, vendor). Assign an owner and a response-time target for social inboxes during business hours, with an auto-reply after hours that promises a time.
- **Welcome new followers without pitching.** One short message referencing the post they probably came from; one operator saw old posts regain distribution after weeks of this plus more commenting (r/InstagramMarketing, 97 pts; combined changes).
- **Never answer "price?" with only a price.** Give the range, what changes it, one question, and the next step (template in scripts.md).
- **Propose a time, not a calendar link,** once someone says yes: "does Thursday at 10am work?" (r/LinkedInTips, 57 pts).
- **Remove "message me for my program" from teaching content;** let the profile and pinned posts sell (r/InstagramMarketing, 113 pts: a coach grown past 800,000 followers).

## 5. Link in bio, Stories and shopping

- **Stories carry conversion,** feed and Reels carry discovery (r/InstagramMarketing, 196 pts: 1 Reel a day for discovery, 3 to 5 Story frames for retention, 3 to 4 carousels a week for saves). Use the link sticker on 1 Story a day at most when promoting a lead magnet (r/InstagramMarketing, 92 pts).
- **Promote giveaways in Stories and Reels** as well as the feed post; operators report 2 to 3x the reach of the feed post alone (r/content_marketing, 96 pts).
- **Broadcast channels** work as mini newsletters for existing fans (r/socialmedia, 92 pts; unquantified).
- **Shoppable tags and TikTok Shop** shorten the path for impulse products; depth in `ecommerce-brand`.
- **Facebook link posts:** post an image with the link in the first comment; operators report link posts reaching almost nobody (creator digest, r/youtubers 69 pts thread).

## 6. Borrowed audiences: collabs, partners, giveaways

- **Instagram Collab posts with 5K to 10K-follower niche accounts:** operators claim 3 to 5x reach (r/InstagramMarketing, 223 pts) and 200 to 500 targeted followers from one swap with 1K to 10K accounts (r/InstagramMarketing, 69 pts). One complementary-brand collab a week was part of the DTC turnaround above.
- **Local partners:** a food truck visits other local restaurants on its closed days, posts with the owners, and reposts when they visit back (r/foodtrucks, 203 pts). Personal trainers tag the gym in Stories so it reposts them to members (r/personaltraining, 76 pts).
- **Pitch partners with data:** audience overlap, 2 to 3 concrete ideas, your numbers, and "what would we create if we had to launch something in 2 weeks?" (coreyhaines31 co-marketing, MIT).
- **Giveaways, measured by one operator over 100+ giveaways (r/content_marketing, 96 pts):**
  - Entry steps: 1 required plus 1 or 2 optional. "Follow us + comment your ring size = entry" got 7,200+ comments; a 10-follow, 5-tag, share, like, answer, visit-site giveaway got 84.
  - Prize = your own product: a skincare line giveaway had 2,100 entries and 85% follower retention after 30 days; an iPad giveaway had 8,900 entries and 12% retention.
  - Numbered entry list and an exact announcement time ("Winner announced Friday 3pm EST").
  - One week is the default; engagement falls sharply after week two on 30+ day contests.
  - Draw live: 30 to 40% of winner announcements draw "rigged" comments; one outdoor brand's TikTok Live draw had 12,000 viewers and gained 3,400 followers.
  - Cap at 8 to 10 giveaways a year; a $165K first-year brand also used "Help us name the next product and if we use your suggestion we'll send you one for free!" (r/ecommerce, 178 pts).
  - Check local contest law and platform promotion rules; no "share to enter" on Facebook personal timelines.
- **Never buy shout-outs or followers to fake proof;** see [mistakes.md](mistakes.md).

## 7. LinkedIn engagement into pipeline (organic side)

Cold outreach mechanics belong to `b2b-outbound`. What organic content adds:

- **Prospect from your own engagers weekly.** Scrape and qualify likers and commenters; operators claim 50 to 70% reply rates (r/LinkedInTips, 83 pts, tool vendor).
- **Warm before you DM.** 2 to 3 substantive comments on a prospect's posts over 5 to 7 days, then a pitch-free DM referencing one post: about 60% replies reported (r/LinkedInTips, 23 pts). Connect after someone engages with you three times, share a relevant post, do not pitch (r/LinkedInTips, 40 pts).
- **Competitor-content engagers convert better than scraped lists:** acceptance and reply 70%/47% vs 30%/15% for SaaS (r/LinkedInTips, 21 pts, 450+ campaigns, vendor). Commenters on competitors' lead-magnet posts converted 4x better than random industry contacts (r/LinkedInTips, 21 pts).
- **Daily 20 minutes of commenting on decision-makers' posts:** "This is exactly what we kept hitting too. The thing that finally moved the needle for us was [specific thing]. What have you tried so far?" Inbound conversations began within weeks with no outbound DMs (r/LinkedInTips, 42 pts).
