# 90-day plan

For a new or stalled business account. Order matters: instrument, then find the hook and format that earn views, then attach conversion, then put people on camera, then allocate by return. Each phase ends with a one-screen owner report: what changed, the scorecard ([measurement.md](measurement.md)), and the decisions needed.

At day 90 the agent should know five things with evidence: which customer problem earns discovery, which opening earns the view, which format holds attention, which CTA creates intent, and which theme produces revenue (research report, 2026).

## Days 1 to 15: instrument and prepare

**Week 1**

- Audit the last 30 posts per platform: views, non-follower share, saves, sends, profile visits, DMs. Mark the median, not the best.
- Choose at most two primary platforms where the buyers already consume expertise or browse products ([SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) platform table). Everything else gets cheap repurposing (auto crosspost Instagram to Facebook; Pinterest for planning categories).
- Fix the profile as a landing page: bio (who, outcome, place), one CTA keyword, three pinned posts that answer buyer questions, highlights, one link ([conversion.md](conversion.md)).
- Define one conversion event and wire attribution: UTMs with post IDs, CRM source fields, "how did you hear" question with a "which post" field, codes per platform for products.
- Verify Instagram, TikTok and YouTube in Google Search Console platform properties where eligible.
- Set the response-time owner for DMs and comments; write the after-hours auto-reply.

**Week 2**

- Build the question library: 30 questions from sales calls, quote requests, reviews, support, DMs, competitor comments, `site:reddit.com` searches and TikTok Search Insights. Tag each discovery, evaluation or conversion.
- Write 30 content hypotheses (topic plus hook mechanism plus format plus CTA). Do not publish 30 pieces.
- Build the swipe file: 5 established and 5 fast-growing small accounts in the niche; log formats and openings.
- Batch a 2-week backlog: 6 to 8 videos in one session, 3 carousels from one template.
- Set up the content database with topic, hook, format and offer as separate columns.

**Exit check:** profile fixed, tracking live, backlog ready. Owner decisions: platforms, offers allowed in CTAs, who answers DMs, which staff will appear on camera.

## Days 16 to 30: format and hook experiments

- **Instagram:** the same idea as a Reel and as a carousel; risky hooks as Trial Reels (3 opening variants of the best idea, first 3 lines changed only). Read trials after about 24 hours; promote winners.
- **TikTok:** each idea in a 15 to 30 second version and a 60 to 180 second explanation; spoken search phrase in the first sentence.
- **Shorts:** 11 to 20 and 30 to 45 second versions; judge on viewed vs swiped away, then APV.
- **LinkedIn:** founder posts 3 a week, first-person event openers, image or PDF on at least one.
- Retention editing checklist on every video: hook under 2 seconds, payoff by 10, visual change every 3 seconds, no pauses over about 1.2 seconds, no end cue, captions burned in.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour with real sentences; 15 minutes a day of substantive comments on niche and customer accounts.

**Exit check:** for each platform, the hook mechanism and format whose median beats the account's prior median on non-follower reach and sends or saves. Kill the bottom third of hypotheses; log them as failed.

## Days 31 to 45: the conversion layer

- Take the 3 topics with the most qualified attention (profile visits, relevant comments). Attach one native CTA to each: a keyword for a lead magnet, a price guide or a booking link.
- Launch comment-to-DM: one keyword per offer, rotated public replies, deliver first, one qualifying question, hot leads to a human within minutes. Respect the 7-day private-reply and 24-hour messaging windows.
- Track comment to DM to email or lead to qualified to sale per post.
- Stories carry the conversion: link sticker once a day at most, proof and openings.
- Do not forecast vendor case-study rates (85% registration, 37% purchase). Use your own numbers.

**Exit check:** leads per 1,000 views and DM-to-qualified-lead rate by post. Owner decision: which lead magnet becomes permanent, and whether the offer needs work (`offers-pricing`).

## Days 46 to 60: people, not just handles

- B2B: founder plus at least 2 employees publish on LinkedIn; the page keeps case studies, PDFs and hiring. Give employees raw material and angles, not copy.
- B2C: put the owner or a specialist on camera for the format that won; start a recurring series and write it into the bio.
- Start a customer content loop: ask happy customers for a 30-second phone video with a small thank-you; written permission for reuse.
- Run one Collab post a week with a complementary business or a niche account with 5,000 to 10,000 followers.
- Founder weekly prompt: "What did we learn this week that would save one of our customers time, money or risk?"

**Exit check:** reach and DMs from people-led posts vs brand-only posts, same topics.

## Days 61 to 75: scale winners, not formats

- A winner beats the account median on both a distribution metric (non-follower reach, sends or saves) and a commercial metric (DMs, leads, sales). A Reel with 2x views and no profile visits is entertainment, not growth; a carousel with ordinary reach and 10 qualified DMs is an asset.
- For each winner: a new hook on the same topic, a carousel and a Reel version, a follow-up answering the top comment, and the same format on the next topic.
- Seed the second platform with the winners from the first; package natively (title, on-screen text, caption).
- Run the marginal-frequency test: add one post a week on the primary platform for 3 to 4 weeks; keep it only if per-hour results hold.

## Days 76 to 90: allocate by return

- Rank every pillar and topic by gross profit (or qualified pipeline) per production hour. Cut the bottom one; double the top one.
- Evergreen winners go to search surfaces: Pinterest pins with one query each, YouTube titles with the exact phrase, captions refreshed from Search Console queries.
- Hand the best organic winners to `paid-ads` as proven creative (with usage rights for any customer or creator content).
- Write the next 90 days: platforms, cadence floor, pillars, the CTA set, the people on camera, the weekly scorecard.

**Owner report at day 90:** the five answers above with evidence, revenue attributed (UTM plus self-reported), hours spent, what stops, what scales, the decisions needed.

## First 30 days for a brand-new account

- Browse the niche 3 to 5 days if you want (optional; agencies report no warm-up needed).
- Post the 2-week backlog at the sustainable floor; never go silent more than 7 days.
- No follow/unfollow, no pods, no bought followers, no giveaways yet.
- Every post carries one of: a reason to send, a reason to save, or a reason to DM.
- Personally message the first 50 people who engage, with no pitch.
