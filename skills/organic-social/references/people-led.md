# People-led content: founder, employees, customers and creators

People outperform logos on distribution, trust and conversion. The evidence, with its limits:

| Finding | Number | Source and caveat |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn personal vs company page, median impressions per post | 1,006 vs 659 | AuthoredUp, 704,259 profile and 189,909 page posts, Jul 2025 to Jun 2026 (via JAC Growth Marketing). Different posts and accounts, not paired |
| Same comparison, median engagement rate | 2.4% personal vs 2.6% page | Same. Pages are not dead; they win on rate, lose on reach |
| Small accounts, comments per post | 1.66 personal vs 0.57 page (+194%); impressions 655.54 vs 699.76 | Metricool Social Media Study 2026 |
| Largest account band, impressions | 15,932.84 personal vs 6,452.97 page | Metricool 2026 |
| Employee profiles vs company page, impressions per 1,000 followers | 70,651, 9,269 and 12,769 vs 980 | Refine Labs, Q3 2025, 4 accounts, one outlier, agency selling the idea |
| Share of LinkedIn engagement from employee advocacy | 53% average, 84% in structured programs | Sociuu x Brandwatch 2025, vendor |
| Employee vs company attribution of identical message (randomized, n=242) | Authenticity 3.23 vs 2.70 (d=1.26), trust 3.35 vs 2.79 (d=1.13), intent to apply 3.06 vs 2.85 (d=0.29) | MDPI 2026, recruitment context only |
| On-camera presenter vs faceless, sponsor conversion | 3 to 4x | Agency with $1M+ (2022) and $4M (2023) in deals, r/youtubers 190 and 99 pts |

## Who posts what

| Voice | Owns | Typical posts |
|---|---|---|
| Founder or owner | Point of view, decisions, customer patterns, lessons | "What we changed after losing a client", "the job I turned down and why", price explanations |
| Employees and specialists | Demonstrations, behind the work, specialist knowledge | Install-day clips, "what I wish customers knew", a day in the job |
| Company page or brand account | Institutional archive, product, proof, hiring, announcements | Case studies, launches, reviews, customer stories, PDFs |
| Customers | Proof in their own words | Reviews on video, before/after, unboxing, "why I chose them" |
| Creators | Reach into audiences you do not have | Honest reviews, try-ons, demos in real use |

## Founder-led content

**Rules with evidence**

- **Start with the person, keep the page.** B2B teams that moved effort from a company page with tens of thousands of followers to the CEO's profile got dozens of likes and thousands of views within weeks (r/content_marketing, 218 pts; r/socialmedia, 147 pts).
- **Write from what happened.** LinkedIn openings with "I", "my" or "we" and an event got +20% within-person engagement; question openers -15% (r/LinkedInTips, 152 pts). On text-first feeds, a first-person position or admission scored 40 vs 20 for leading with a named company and 10 for leading with a number (3,640 Substack Notes, r/Substack 74 pts; best proxy for Threads and LinkedIn).
- **Weekly prompt:** "What did we learn this week that would save one of our customers time, money or risk?" Turn it into: the observation, the mistake, the consequence, the procedure, one question (research report, 2026). Never invent a heroic story around a generic lesson.
- **Origin stories with a twist outperform polished brand stories.** A chef's husband opened with "Can I tell you a secret? But don't tell my wife", explained he started the account to prove her wrong ("Did I do it out of spite? Yes. Is this petty? Absolutely.") and closed with a follow race: "She currently has 1,300 followers. I'm at 10... So hit follow and help me prove a point. Or don't, and let her win." 6,000 followers in 48 hours on the 6th post (r/InstagramMarketing, 179 pts).
- **Founder story plus one usable tip plus visual proof.** A skincare brand pairing each founder beat with one technique viewers could use without buying went from 2.1% to 6.3% engagement in 7 weeks and tripled saves (creator digest, r/InstagramMarketing, 225 pts).
- **Film the reveal of the first samples.** A founder filmed himself in his car unboxing the first collection's samples ("I just received the samples for the first collection for my small business"): 2M views and 27,000 followers; the repeat got 82,000 views and 900 followers (r/InstagramMarketing, 68 pts). Repeat formats decay; keep the angle fresh.
- **Translate what already works.** A CEO's strongest native-language posts adapted into English for LinkedIn beat writing new opinions on his behalf (r/content_marketing, 218 pts).
- **When the founder is too busy,** write for other senior managers and have the founder engage early; managers' posts got almost the same engagement as the CEO's (same thread, historical feed behavior).
- **Ghostwriting must sound like the founder.** Interview 20 minutes a week, record, transcribe, draft from their words; never send a draft they have not edited.

**Camera-shy owners.** Read the script once, then paraphrase to camera; batch 8 to 10 videos in 90 minutes; subtitles, cut pauses, change something every 3 seconds, on-screen text hook for 2 seconds (r/realtors, 174 pts). Alternatives: hands-and-voice demos, screen recordings, text carousels written in first person, or the specialist employee as the face.

## Employee content and advocacy

- **Give raw material, not copy-paste posts.** Facts, charts, photos, customer situations and suggested angles; identical advocacy text reads as spam and the LLM ranker treats it as duplicate content (research report, 2026; LinkedIn, Mar 2026).
- **Series employees can own:** "A day in the life", "What I wish more people knew about our job", "Behind the work" micro lessons, "One thing our team is learning right now". Share the publishing calendar so colleagues engage when posts go live (r/LinkedInTips, 27 pts).
- **Run it as a program:** one person oversees 3 to 5 profiles (including the CEO's), some write their own, the manager tracks combined audience and reach (r/LinkedInTips, 27 pts).
- **Early engagement from real colleagues is fine; pods are not.** Operators claim 3 to 5 overlapping-audience colleagues engaging in the first 10 minutes adds 30 to 50% reach (r/LinkedInTips, 83 pts, unverified). LinkedIn says it suppresses pods and automated comments (2026). Real colleagues commenting with substance is safe; a rotating WhatsApp group is not.
- **Make it voluntary and protected.** Written social policy: what employees may share (no client data, no pricing promises, no confidential work), disclosure of employment when promoting the company, and the right to stop.

## Customer content (UGC)

- **Ask for it at the moment of delight,** with a small thank-you. A jewelry brand offered a small discount for a Reel on who the customer is and why they commissioned their piece; these became its best content (creator digest, r/InstagramMarketing 134 pts).
- **Free product for an honest video review,** accepting unpolished entries: 300 videos in under 60 days for an ecommerce brand (r/ecommerce, 104 pts; the operator says this matters little under $15K a month in sales).
- **Branded submission hashtag** that followers learn to use, reposted with credit, became one 450K account's main content source (r/socialmedia, 96 pts).
- **Physical prompts:** an in-store sign offering a monthly prize for photos tagging the account and location (r/socialmedia, 60 pts); a photo wall with the town name and local history that "every family took a picture" at (r/restaurantowners, 2,608 pts); branded packaging that cost 50 cents and drew more tags than the product (r/shopify, 300 pts).
- **Customer interviews as a content engine.** Shoot customer interviews every other week: about 90 minutes of video and 100+ images per shoot, cut into a 20-minute piece plus 40-second objection clips (r/FacebookAds, 58 pts). Run 40+ transcripts through an AI notebook to build a quote table (quote, person, company, use case) (r/copywriting, 346 pts).
- **Permission in writing, every time.** Get a written yes in the DM or a release before reposting, state where it will be used (organic only, or ads too), and credit the person. Paid usage in ads is a separate permission.
- **Never fabricate.** No invented testimonials or AI "customers"; the FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule (in force since 21 Oct 2024) bans fake reviews and testimonials and buying followers or views (see `creator` for disclosure depth).

## Working with creators (business side)

Pricing a creator's deal is the `creator` skill's job; for product seeding and creator programs see `ecommerce-brand` and `fashion-apparel`. What a business running organic social needs:

- **Brief the outcome, not the script.** "Just make stuff your audience will watch and enjoy", no discounts or scarcity, honest pros plus one real con; a backpack brand's program averaged 7x return and fixed the con the creators named (r/Entrepreneur, 2,740 pts).
- **Pick on real traffic, not engagement stats.** Of 5 paid LinkedIn creators, 3 brought customers and 2 brought zero website visits for $500 of a $1,250 budget despite good engagement numbers (r/LinkedInTips, 52 pts). Ask for median views of the last 10 posts and audience location; screen comment sections for other promo accounts (r/socialmedia, 56 pts).
- **Recruit creators who already promoted competitors;** negotiate, quotes dropped 2 to 3x; pay after the post is live (r/LinkedInTips, 52 pts).
- **Give each creator a tracked destination** (own page, code or UTM) and track visits, leads and sales per creator.
- **Repurpose with rights.** If creator videos will run as ads or Spark/Partnership ads, agree usage in writing and price it (see `creator`).
- **Disclosure:** every paid or gifted post needs a clear disclosure in the content itself (#ad, paid partnership label).

## LinkedIn: personal profile vs company page

**Decision:**

- Solo or founder-led service business: the founder's profile is the main channel; the company page exists for search, credibility and job posts. Post on the page 1 to 2 times a week with reshared proof.
- Team of 5 to 20 selling B2B: founder plus 2 subject-matter employees publish; the page carries case studies, PDFs, product news and hiring; the page reshares employee posts with a line of context.
- Brand with a product consumers follow (B2C): page-first is fine on Instagram and TikTok; LinkedIn page mainly for hiring and B2B partners.

**Page tricks:** pages win on engagement rate (2.6% vs 2.4% median), so post the institutional assets there (data, case studies, launches); invite engagers to follow the page; comment as the page on relevant posts (creator digest, r/Substack 84 pts); PDF documents from existing decks.

**Profile tricks:** connect 10 to 20 relevant group members or target buyers a day until LinkedIn starts suggesting the right people, then stop manual sourcing (r/content_marketing, 218 pts); 15 minutes of comments before and after posting (r/LinkedInTips, 42 pts); 5 substantive comments a day on 10,000 to 50,000-follower niche accounts can bring more profile visits than a post (r/LinkedInTips, 83 pts thread).
