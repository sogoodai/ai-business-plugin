# Review request and reply templates

Copy, fill the brackets, keep it short. Every request asks for an honest review, never stars, keywords or staff names, and goes to every customer the same way. Rules and timing: [reviews.md](reviews.md).

## Requests

**SMS, right after the job (default, under 160 characters)**
> Thanks for choosing [Business] today, [Name]. If you'd like to share your experience, here's our Google review link: [link]. We read every review.

**SMS with the job named in your own words (primes detail without scripting it)**
> Hi [Name], thanks for having us [fix the roof leak / do your balayage / cater the party] today. If you have a minute, an honest Google review helps neighbors know what to expect: [link]

**One reminder, about 3 days later, then stop**
> Hi [Name], just a nudge in case it got buried. If you'd like to leave a quick review of [Business], here's the link: [link]. No worries either way.

**In person, at the relief moment (field trades, salons)**
> Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It genuinely helps people find us. I'll text you the link right now so you don't have to hunt for it.

Then send it while you are still there. Adapted from r/GoogleMyBusiness (2025, 2026).

**Email (higher-ticket jobs, one per client)**
> Subject: Thank you from [Business]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for trusting us with [project]. It was a pleasure working with you.
>
> If you'd like to share your experience, you can leave a Google review here: [link]. Positive or critical, we read every one and it helps us improve.
>
> [Owner first name]
> [Business], [phone]

**Restaurant receipt, table card or bag QR**
> How was your visit? Scan to leave an honest Google review. No purchase or reward is tied to your rating.

**Restaurant message after payment or next morning**
> Thanks for visiting [Restaurant]. If you'd like to share your honest experience, you can leave a Google review here: [link]. Positive or critical, we read every one.

**Salon, spa, clinic: next-day text from the booking system**
> Hi [Name], thanks for coming in to see [Stylist] yesterday. If you'd like to share how it went, here's our Google review link: [link]

Naming who they saw as context is fine. Asking them to name that person in the review is not.

**After you resolved a complaint**
> Thanks for giving us the chance to make this right. If you'd like to update or add to your review, you're welcome to, but there's absolutely no obligation.

**Reactivating a dead profile: last 20 customers, sent one by one, plain text**
> Hi [Name], it's [Owner] from [Business]. We did [job] for you in [month]. We're a small team and reviews are how most new customers find us. If you have a minute, an honest review would mean a lot: [link]. Thanks either way.

**Unsolicited thank-you from a customer**
> That means a lot, thank you! If you'd be willing to say that publicly, here's our Google review link: [link]. Either way, thanks for telling us.

**Never send any of these** (each breaks Google policy, the FTC rule or both):
- "Leave us a 5-star review"
- "If you loved it, leave a review; if not, tell us privately"
- "Please mention [keyword / city / staff name]"
- "Show us your review for 10% off" or "a free [item] for every review"
- "We rely on 5 star reviews, if we did a great job please..." (a real template shared on r/localseo, 2026: it implies only happy customers should post)

## Replies

Length: positive 2 to 3 sentences, mixed 3 to 4, negative 2 to 4. Never open two replies in a batch the same way. Never open with "Thank you for your feedback". Sign with a real first name. Mention the job, never private details.

### Positive

> Thanks, [Name]. Glad the [water heater install in Scottsdale] went smoothly and you have hot water again. Give us a call when it's time for the annual flush.

> Thanks [Name]. [Tech] said the crawlspace was a tight one, glad it's running right. Give us a shout when it's time for the fall check.
(adapted from anthropics knowledge-work-plugins, Apache-2.0)

> [Name], thank you! [Staff] will be thrilled, we'll share this at the team meeting. Save room for the tiramisu next time.
(restaurant, staff named by the reviewer)

> Thanks for trusting us with your [AC repair in North Austin], glad we could get your system running before the heat wave.
(r/localseo, 2026)

### Mixed (answer both halves or it reads as ducking)

> Thanks, [Name]. Glad you liked [the cut / the pasta], and you're right that the wait at check-in was too long. We've added a second person at the desk on Saturdays. Hope to see you again.

### Negative, real customer

Pattern: name the specific problem, say what changed only if something did, give a real offline route with a name, stop. No policy lectures, no "but".

> Nine days for a blower motor is too long, and you shouldn't have had to chase us for updates. We've changed how we order parts so this doesn't repeat. Call me directly at [phone] and I'll make the wait right. [Name]
(adapted from anthropics knowledge-work-plugins, Apache-2.0)

> Thanks for flagging this, [Name]. A [45-minute wait / cold entree / missed allergy note] is not the experience we aim to deliver. I've shared this with the team and we're correcting [specific process]. If you're willing, please contact me at [email] so I can understand what happened.
(restaurant pattern, deep research 2026)

> I'm sorry for any miscommunication, as I felt we nailed it at your appointment. I'd love a chance to improve the areas that aren't working for you. Please call me at [phone] to schedule.
(stylist reply, r/hairstylist, 2024)

A stylist who texted privately first, got no answer, then posted a kind public reply saw the client delete the review (r/hairstylist, 2025). Order: private message first, public reply second.

### Negative about extra work or scope (write for the next customer)

> We were glad to deliver the [website project] in [month]. After launch we made about [12] hours of additional changes at no charge. Going forward, further updates are billed at our standard rate, which we explained before starting. We're always happy to help with future updates.
(pattern endorsed by 280 upvotes, r/smallbusiness, 2026; use only facts you can prove)

### Booking dispute (date taken by another client)

> Thank you for meeting with us, we enjoyed the consultation. Dates are reserved once the contract is signed and the retainer is paid. We didn't receive either, and another couple completed both first. We're sorry it didn't work out and wish you a wonderful wedding.
(r/WeddingPhotography, 2026, top comment 178 upvotes)

### Not a customer, mistaken identity

> We don't have a record of a [service call / appointment] under this name or address, so this may be a mix-up with another business. If we did work for you, please call me at [phone] so we can sort it out. [Name]

Do not write "you were never a customer" for walk-in or cash businesses. Readers find it less credible and feel tracked (r/smallbusiness, 2026, replies with 122 upvotes).

### Fake or coordinated attack (after reporting)

> We take feedback seriously, but this review does not reflect an actual customer experience with our business. We've reported it to Google for investigation.

> Sorry to hear this. If you were a customer, please contact us at [phone] so we can make it right.
(use when you are not sure it is fake)

### Extortion

> This review is part of an extortion attempt: we received a demand for payment to remove it. We have reported it to Google and do not pay for reviews to be removed.

### Rejected job applicant or ex-employee

> Hi, we're sorry we weren't able to offer you the position. We wish you the best in your search.
(calm version, r/smallbusiness, 2026. A sharper "this review is from an applicant we did not hire" got the review deleted immediately for one owner, 877 upvotes, but it can read as petty.) Then report it under conflict of interest.

### Healthcare, dental, therapy, legal (never confirm the relationship)

> Thank you for taking the time to share this. We take every experience seriously. Please contact our office at [phone] and ask for [name] so we can discuss your concerns directly.

### Platform tone

- **Google:** warm, 2 to 4 sentences.
- **Yelp:** more formal, offer email rather than phone, never ask the reviewer to update.
- **Tripadvisor:** hospitality voice, "we hope to welcome you back".
- **DoorDash and Uber Eats:** about the order only, refund or credit through the app.

Never promise free food, refunds or compensation in a public reply, and never offer anything in exchange for changing or deleting a review.
