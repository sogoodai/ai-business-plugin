# By type of business

What changes from the core playbook. Sources in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Home and field services (plumbers, HVAC, cleaners, pressure washing, landscapers, movers, junk removal)

- **Usually a service-area business:** hide the home address, up to 20 named areas within about 2 hours, wider grids (11x11 or 13x13), expect weaker and more scattered maps than storefronts.
- **GBP is the main free channel.** Owners attribute 90 to 95% of new customers to it once reviews build (r/sweatystartup, 2022; r/pressurewashing, 2025). 150 door hangers gave zero calls for one washer while Google gave 90% of work (r/pressurewashing, 2025). A knife sharpener started on Nextdoor (90% of early business) then moved to GBP as the majority source by asking every customer for a review (r/sweatystartup, 2021).
- **Categories:** the narrow one for the money service ("Air conditioning repair service", "Water heater installation service"), then every true secondary. Services list with 10 to 15 entries per category is common.
- **Hours:** emergency trades win night searches when they really answer.
- **Proof photos:** vehicle wraps, uniforms, equipment, before and after. They double as suspension defense.
- **Reviews:** ask at the relief moment; QR on the badge or clipboard; text before leaving. Typical target 5 to 10 a month (practitioner, uncited).
- **Local Services Ads (US and some countries):** need GBP in good standing; calls began for one new cleaner after 5+ reviews (r/sweatystartup, 2026). Missed in-hours calls held over 20 seconds can now be billed, so answer fast (Google, 2025 to 2026). Badge is now "Google Verified" (since Oct 2025). Details belong to the paid-ads skill.
- **Content:** cost questions per city ("cost to replace a furnace in [city]"), problem posts tied to local housing and climate, seasonal posts 4 to 6 weeks before the season.
- **Spam-heavy categories:** locksmith, garage door, water damage, movers. Run the spam SOP early.

## Salons, barbers, spas, lash, nails, massage, med spa

- **Storefront or suite:** address shown with signage. Booth renters inside another salon: check whether a separate listing is allowed (distinct business, own signage and phone); otherwise rely on the salon's listing plus a personal site and booking marketplace.
- **Categories:** "Nail salon", "Eyelash salon", "Barber shop", "Day spa", "Massage therapist", "Medical spa": specific beats "Beauty salon".
- **Booking button:** Reserve with Google through a supported booking tool, or a location-specific booking link. Each location's button goes to its own booking page.
- **Prices, services and real work photos on the listing.** Travelers and new residents decide from the listing alone (r/Nails, 2026, 5,411-upvote thread).
- **Reviews:** next-day automated texts from the booking system built 88, 58 and 39 reviews for three solo practitioners (r/localseo, 2026). Turn off any "private feedback for unhappy clients" feature in Boulevard, Phorest and similar (deep research, 2026). Never trade a discount for a review.
- **Clinical words win:** a massage therapist contrasted "Stress Relief Massage" (Craigslist) with "Clinical Massage Therapy" (Google); others add "orthopedic" (r/MassageTherapists, 2024). Use the service names people search: "post-op lymphatic massage", "prenatal massage", "wet set lashes".
- **Med spa and clinical:** never confirm a patient in replies; no before and after claims without consent and typical-result disclosure; brand-name treatments (Botox, Invisalign, CoolSculpting) are high-intent keywords and brand "find a provider" locators are citations.
- **Marketplaces matter for AI and Apple:** Fresha, Treatwell, Booksy, Vagaro, StyleSeat listings get cited and send bookings.

## Restaurants, cafes, bakeries, bars, food trucks

- **GBP is a commerce surface:** exact category, structured menu (section, dish, description, price), dine-in, takeout, delivery attributes, reservation and order links, preferred order provider set, special hours for holidays.
- **Preferred menu source:** when Google finds several menus, choose yours to stop stale third-party prices (Google Help, 2026). Review any menu Google auto-extracts from a photo or PDF before it goes live.
- **Direct ordering:** set your own site as the preferred order link and test it. A customer reports choosing the direct link when it works and going back to UberEats when it breaks (r/smallbusiness, 2026).
- **Proximity is extreme** for "coffee near me": 5x5 or 7x7 grids at 1 to 3 km.
- **Review count is not everything:** in one 2026 study, restaurants in positions 4 to 20 had a higher median review count than the top 3 for "restaurant" (Suff Digital). Category, distance, open-now and rating decide.
- **Reviews:** QR on receipts and bags, message after payment or next morning. Same request after good and bad visits. Half of restaurants answer none of their last 20 reviews (Suff Digital, 2026): easy edge.
- **Platforms:** Google first, then Tripadvisor (asking is allowed, Review Express exists; no incentives or gating), Yelp (never ask), OpenTable and Resy. ChatGPT cites Yelp, Tripadvisor and OpenTable heavily (BrightLocal, 2026).
- **Write the menu facts as text** on the site: dietary options, private room size, late-night kitchen, kids menu, parking. Google's "Ask Maps" (Oct 2026) answers natural-language dining questions from photos, reviews and menus.
- **Monthly realistic-location check** of 5 to 10 discovery intents ("birthday dinner [city]", "private dining [city]", "bakery near me", "catering [neighborhood]") to see what Google understands about you (deep research, 2026).
- **New opening:** claim the listing and build the landing page and list weeks before opening.
- **UK:** AI Mode can book tables through partners including TheFork, SevenRooms, ResDiary and OpenTable (Google, Apr 2026). Check which integrations exist in your country.

## Shops and retail

- **Products on GBP** with price and photo; local inventory through Merchant Center if you sell online too (keeps AI and Google answers current).
- **Hours and holiday hours** drive "open now" footfall. Opening earlier than nearby rivals is a legitimate edge.
- **Photos:** storefront from the street, interior, best-selling products.
- **Reviews:** QR at the counter and on receipts; ask for a photo of the purchase.
- **Sell online too? List products in Bing Merchant Center.** One Shopify seller saw its first ChatGPT-referred sales (tracked by UTM source) after listing products there and tuning for Bing (r/shopify, 2025, one report). ChatGPT search leans on the Bing index.
- **Look back at your best sales day of the last 90 days** and find what caused it (event, festival, post) before buying any marketing (r/smallbusiness, 2026).

## Tutors, coaches, schools, studios, classes

- **Often home-based:** hide the address unless students come to a real, signed space; list the towns served; online-only tutoring is not eligible for a GBP.
- **Categories:** "Tutoring service", "Music school", "Driving school", "Yoga studio", "Preschool": specific.
- **Reviews come from parents:** ask after a milestone (exam result, recital, first lesson), not at random. Lower velocity is normal; aim for no 3-week gaps. A music school puts one quiet review line with the direct link in every monthly parent email and reached 20 reviews, with Google prospects arriving "partly convinced" (r/MusicTeachers, 2026).
- **Outcomes, not adjectives, in the GBP description:** replace "We offer high quality music instruction" with "Our students have performed at X, competed in Y, gone on to study music at Z". About 20 minutes of work (r/MusicTeachers, 2026). Same for tutors: schools students got into, exam score ranges, years teaching.
- **AI prompts parents use are long:** "SAT tutor in [town] for a junior who struggles with math, evenings". Put subjects, levels, exam boards, formats (in person, online), prices and availability as text on the site.
- **Avoid the preschool astroturf playbook** (self-ranked "best of" articles and sockpuppet answers, r/localseo, 2025). Get onto real local parent lists and school or association directories instead.
- **Google ended school reviews on 30 April 2025** (claude-seo, 2026): check whether your category still shows reviews before building a review plan on it.

## Health, legal, finance (regulated)

- Never confirm a patient or client in a reply. Healthcare precedent: a $30,000 HIPAA fine (2023) over review replies (localseoskills).
- Practitioner listings are allowed for independent practitioners, each with its own phone or extension; they can outrank the practice.
- Bar rules govern testimonials and words like "specialist" and "expert".
- Lower review velocity is normal (legal 3 to 5 a month, practitioner estimate).
- Vertical directories (Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Avvo, Justia) feed AI answers.
