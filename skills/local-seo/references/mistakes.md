# Expensive local SEO mistakes

What owners and agencies report it cost, and what to do instead. Sources in [sources.md](sources.md).

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Buying Google reviews | 20 bought in 2022: 12 vanished in 3 weeks, 8 a month later, rankings fell below the start (r/smallbusiness, 2025) | Ask every real customer the same way |
| Free product or discount for reviews | 50+ reviews in 3 weeks all deleted, profile suspended, 30-day review block; new reviews still hidden after reinstatement (r/GoogleMyBusiness, 2026) | No incentive of any kind on Google |
| "5-star review = $30 coupon" brochure | Plan shared publicly by a repair business (r/smallbusiness, 2026); breaks Google policy and the FTC rule twice (incentive and sentiment condition) | Neutral link card, no offer |
| 5% off for a review (salon) | Reported as part of a working setup (r/localseo, 2025) but a live policy violation | Remove the discount; keep the ask |
| Routing best-product buyers to Trustpilot to "dilute" bad reviews | Used across several companies (r/ecommerce, 2025); selective solicitation that Trustpilot and Google ban | Ask every buyer on the same platform |
| Review tool that sends only 4 to 5 stars to Google | Gating banned by Google and Tripadvisor; vendors still ship it (Boulevard, Phorest, ClientMax features) | Turn off satisfaction routing |
| Staff quotas, per-review bonuses, "mention me by name" | A client with 1,500+ reviews, 90 to 95% naming staff, underperformed in grids (r/localseo, 2026; cause disputed). Google bans requested content and quotas | Reward service quality, not review counts |
| Underquoting early jobs to "buy" reviews | Losses on early cleaning jobs (r/sweatystartup, 2025) | Charge full price; ask everyone |
| Paying an extortionist | Hit again a week later (r/GoogleMyBusiness, 2025) | Merchant extortion form, evidence, no contact |
| Sending details to unsolicited "review removal" DMs | A new fake review arrived right after declining (r/GoogleMyBusiness, 2026) | Ignore; report |
| Claiming "you were never a customer" as a walk-in business | Readers trust the reply less (r/smallbusiness, 2026) | "We can't find a record, please call me" |
| Keyword-stuffed GBP name with no real rebrand | #1 suspension cause (localseoskills, MIT) | Real-world name; full DBA rebrand if the owner chooses |
| Virtual office to verify | Suspended after third video; LSA at $150,000 a month put at risk (r/GoogleMyBusiness, 2024) | Staffed, signed, real location or a hidden-address SAB |
| Generic city-center street as a "service area" address | Five-year top-3 profile suspended (r/GoogleMyBusiness, 2026) | Hide the address; list service areas |
| Home address shown on a service-area profile | Two suspensions in three months (r/GoogleMyBusiness, 2025) | Hide it |
| Removing the address and setting the whole US as service area | All rankings gone overnight (r/localseo, 2026) | Realistic areas within about 2 hours' drive |
| Creating a new profile after a suspension | Loses reviews, looks like evasion | Appeal with evidence |
| Repeated new Community posts for one case | Posts locked as duplicates (r/GoogleMyBusiness, 2025) | One thread, keep replying |
| Giving a consultant Owner access | Anyone added can change the listing (r/GoogleMyBusiness, 2025) | Manager role only |
| Listing 24/7 without coverage | 1-star reviews from people who found it closed, seen twice (r/localseo, 2026) | Only hours you answer |
| Changing regular hours for a one-day closure | Inconsistency with site, Yelp, Facebook; one owner suspended right after an hours edit (r/GoogleMyBusiness, 2026) | Special hours |
| 40 thin city pages | HVAC firm lost 80% of rankings and 63% of traffic in the March 2024 core update (via claude-seo) | Fewer, real pages that pass the swap test |
| Rebrand announced early, citations half-updated | Weeks of identity cleanup (r/localseo, 2026) | Switch site, GBP and citations together |
| "Coming soon" page left up for a year | Slow indexing after launch (r/localseo, 2026) | Launch a basic real site early |
| Agency reporting fake calls | A few thousand dollars a month for 6 months, 2 real calls (r/localseo, 2026) | Call recordings and CRM outcomes |
| Paid "superboosts" / CTR services | Rural gains, urban drops, 4 high-paying clients lost (r/localseo, 2026) | Spend on reviews, pages, categories |
| Yelp ads without written terms | $130 charged before the promised free period; no refund (r/smallbusiness, 2020) | Start date, credit and cancellation in writing |
| Leaving Yelp ad settings to Yelp | Keywords re-added, radius changed, best category removed, $300 credit plus $165 burned in 16 days (r/smallbusiness, 2026) | Recheck targeting weekly |
| Blocking all AI crawlers with one plugin | Also blocks OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot, removing you from AI answers (r/smallbusiness, 2026) | Block training bots only, if at all |
| Posting daily for rankings | No measured rank effect (Sterling Sky, older tests) | One useful post a week; time on reviews and pages |
| Geotagging photos | No effect; Google strips EXIF (WebFX via claude-seo) | Real photos for customers |
| Pointing an action link to a generic or other-branch page | Google requires location-specific action links (r/localseo, 2025) | Each location's own booking page |
| Hiding the menu in a photo or PDF only | Google may misread prices and tags; AI cannot quote it reliably (deep research, 2026) | Text menu on site and structured GBP menu |
| Broken preferred order link | Customers fall back to UberEats and its commission (r/smallbusiness, 2026) | Test the direct order link monthly |
| Reporting one rank screenshot as a win | Night-time checks inflate results when rivals show closed (r/GoogleMyBusiness, 2026) | Geogrid at a fixed time |
