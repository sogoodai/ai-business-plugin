# Scripts

Adapt names, places and numbers; keep it short and plain. Labels: **(verbatim)** = quoted from a practitioner thread, **(research template)** = from the 2026 research pass, **(house script)** = written for this skill from practitioner patterns. Never send automated texts without consent, never call registered numbers to solicit a listing, never imply a person wrote an automated message, and never state a fact about the market you cannot source.

## New inquiry (portal, website, Meta)

- **Auto-text, under a minute (verbatim, Realtor.com's old default):** "Hey this is Bryan! When is a good time to talk!"
- **Auto-text naming the request (house script):** "Hi [Name], it's [Agent] with [Brokerage]. Got your question about [address]. I can answer it now by phone or text. Which is easier?"
- **Meta form intro (verbatim opening):** "Hey [Lead's Name], it's [Your Name] here! Excited you're considering working with us..." then one question about their timeline.
- **First call opener (house script):** "Hi [Name], it's [Agent], you asked about [address] a few minutes ago. I'm looking at it now. Before I tell you what I know about it: are you already working with an agent, or are you looking on your own for now?"
- **Lead form qualifying order (practitioner pattern, r/realtors 1025):** beds and baths, area, when they plan to buy, then "Which email should I send your custom list to?", then phone.
- **Day 10 check (house script):** "Should I keep sending homes like these, or has your plan changed? Either answer is fine."
- **Long nurture touch (house script):** "[Name], a [3-bed] on [street] in the area you saved just sold for [price] after [days] days. Want me to keep an eye out for ones like it, or is the timing different now?"

## Sphere and past clients

- **Referral ask as a group text (research template):** "Quick favor: if someone in your circle starts talking about moving, you don't need to sell them on me. Just make a group text: 'You two should talk, [Agent] helped me with my move and will give you a straight answer.' I'll take it from there."
- **Review request (research template):** "Would you be willing to mention one concrete thing that was useful: pricing, negotiation, communication, or handling a problem? Specific reviews help future clients understand what working with us is actually like." Add: "If it's natural, mention the neighborhood." Same request to every client; no incentives.
- **Home anniversary (house script):** "Happy one year in the house, [Name]! Two homes on your street sold this year, at [price] and [price]. If you ever want to know what yours would do, I'm one text away. How's the [project they mentioned] going?"
- **Pop-by intro in a farm (practitioner pattern, r/realtors 1200):** "Hi, I'm [Agent], the person who keeps sending you those mailers. I wanted to put a face to them. We've got an open house at [address] Saturday; if you know anyone who wants to live near you, bring them."
- **Event-triggered touch (house script):** "[Name], the house two doors down from you just went under contract in [days] days. Buyers are asking about your street. No agenda, just thought you'd want to know."

## Expired listings (mail, door, or scrubbed numbers only)

The seller is getting 30 to 50 calls a day (r/realtors 230, 2026). Lead with a diagnosis, not a pitch.

- **Written diagnosis letter (house script):** "Your home at [address] was listed [days] days. Here is what I see from the outside: the list price sat [$X] above the [3] closest sales, it was filtered out of the [$500,000] search bracket, there was no floor plan, and the photos showed [issue]. Three similar homes sold in that time at [prices]. If you plan to sell again, I'd walk you through what I would change, in 20 minutes, with no obligation to list. If you've decided to rent it out instead, I can help with that too. [Phone]"
- **Call, number scrubbed or EBR (house script):** "Hi [Name], it's [Agent] with [Brokerage]. I know your phone has been busy since the listing came off, so I'll be quick. I looked at why it didn't sell and wrote it down on one page. Do you still plan to sell, or have plans changed?" If plans changed: "Understood. Mind if I mail you the page anyway? It's useful whenever you decide." If yes: "What do you think stopped it from selling?" Then listen, then: "Would [day] or [day] work to go through it at the kitchen table?"
- **When they say "I'll relist with my agent" (house script):** "That makes sense. Ask them three things: what changes on price, what changes in the photos and floor plan, and what happens if there are fewer than two showings a week. If those answers are good, stay with them."

## FSBO (DNC check first; a buyer's agent with a real buyer may call about that buyer)

- **Buyer's agent with a buyer (house script):** "Hi, I'm [Agent]. I have a preapproved buyer looking for a [3-bed] in [area] and your home fits. Would you consider a showing this week? My buyer would cover my fee in their offer, or we can talk about it."
- **Value offer, mail or door (house script):** "Most owners selling themselves in [area] already have a buyer in mind. If you do, the riskiest part is the contract and the money. I can review the contract and check the buyer's proof of funds and lender before you hand over keys, for a flat fee, without listing. If you'd rather sell to the open market later, I'll show you what three similar homes sold for." (State law and broker rules govern limited-service offers.)
- **Weekly follow-up (house script):** "Hi [Name], [Agent] again. How many showings did you have this week? Any offers? Two homes like yours sold in [area] this week at [prices], if that's useful."

## Open house

- **Sign-in question (research template):** "Are you mainly comparing this home, this neighborhood, or trying to work out what your own place would sell for?"
- **Digital sign-in consent line (house script):** "[ ] Text me new listings like this one (optional; reply STOP anytime)." Unticked by default.
- **Same-day follow-up, consent on file (house script):** "Thanks for coming by [address] today, [Name]. You mentioned [the kitchen / the commute to X]. Here are two others with that: [links]. Want to see them this week? If so, we'd start with a 20-minute plan so you know exactly how I work."
- **Seller-curious visitor (house script):** "You said you're wondering about your own place on [street]. I can send you what the last three homes like yours sold for and why. Would that be useful?"
- **Next-day call (house script):** "Hi [Name], it's [Agent] from yesterday's open house on [street]. Did anything you saw change what you're looking for?"

## Buyer consultation

- **Booking line (research template):** "Before we tour, we'll spend 20 minutes on three things: your search strategy, how offers are actually winning in this market, and exactly how my compensation works. You'll know what you are agreeing to before we start viewing homes."
- **Compensation explanation (house script):** "My fee is [2.5% / $X]. Sellers often pay part or all of it; when they don't, we can ask for it in the offer, or it becomes part of your cost. You'll know which before we write any offer. The agreement lasts [90 days] and covers [area or property]; here's how you end it if I'm not doing my job."
- **Preapproval before showings (practitioner pattern, r/realtors 351):** "I'd love to show you this. Good homes are going under contract before buyers finish getting approved, and if you fall in love with one $100,000 over budget, it makes the ones you can afford feel worse. Let's get you a quick preapproval first; it takes [a day]."
- **Negotiation trust (verbatim):** "Leave it to me, you hired me to negotiate for you so please trust me on this."

## Listing presentation and close

- **Opening frame (research template):** "Our launch plan is built around the things buyers actually use to decide whether a home earns a viewing: pricing position, excellent photography, an accurate floor plan, strong portal presentation and immediate distribution. Video is additional reach, not a substitute for those basics."
- **Pricing evidence (house script):** "Here are the 3 closest sales, the 4 homes you'd compete with today, and the 2 that went pending. Buyers search in brackets; at [$499,000] you show up in every 'up to $500k' search. At [$510,000] you lose them."
- **Overpricing story (practitioner pattern, r/realtors 1019, 2026):** "A seller near here turned down a strong first-weekend offer to hold out for $50,000 more. Sixty days and several cuts later, they sold for $47,000 less than that first offer. The first two weeks are when the most buyers see a home."
- **Price review agreement (verbatim, r/realtors 426):** "If it doesn't sell within 2 weeks, we will adjust the price." "Don't be surprised if the offers we receive ask for this." "Any offer within 5% of asking price is a good offer. Doesn't mean we immediately accept it, but it's at least a great starting point to negotiate something we can agree on."
- **Close with the launch calendar (house script):** "To hit the [Thursday] launch, photos and the floor plan need to happen [Tuesday]. Do you want the morning or afternoon slot? I'll bring the agreement so we can lock it in today."
- **Cancellation terms up front (practitioner pattern, r/realtors 271):** "If you cancel before closing, there's a flat [$1,000] for photos and marketing already spent. If you relist with me within a year, it comes back to you at closing."
- **Price reduction conversation (house script):** "We've had [n] showings in [14] days; the plan we agreed said fewer than [2] a week means we adjust. A [$10,000] cut changes a buyer's payment by about [$63] a month, which nobody notices. To reach a new group of buyers we'd go to [$X], about [$200] a month less, and into the [$475,000] search bracket."
- **Resisting an over-asking offer (practitioner pattern, r/realtors 426):** "What number were you hoping for?" Then net sheets side by side.

## Property management owner pitch

- **Opening (research template):** "We'll show you what the property should rent for, the maintenance issues most likely to become expensive, and exactly how owner communication works before you sign anything."
- **Switcher call (house script):** "Most owners who call us aren't unhappy with the fee, they're unhappy with not knowing. Here's what you'd get: a monthly update on a fixed date with rent collected, open work orders, maintenance spend against plan, lease risks and the one decision we need from you; actual vendor invoices with photos, no markup; and nothing over [$250] spent without your approval except emergencies, which we tell you about the same day. What's the one thing your current manager doesn't do that you wish they did?"
- **Monthly owner update (research template):** "This month: rent collected, outstanding actions, maintenance spend versus plan, lease and renewal risks, and the one decision we need from you."
- **Value-event review ask (house script):** "Glad we got the [AC fixed the same day / unit leased in 9 days]. If you have a minute, a review that mentions it would help other owners in [city] find us: [link]."
- **Declining a bad-fit owner (house script):** "At [$X] above what similar homes rent for, and with no showings while you're living there, we'd struggle to lease it on your timeline. I'd rather tell you now. If you can price at [$Y] and allow showings with 24 hours' notice, we'd be glad to take it on."
- **Ad (research template):** "Rental owner in [market]? Get a one-page rent, vacancy and maintenance-risk review, plus the three issues most likely to create an expensive call this year."

## Loan officer to agent

- **First ask (research template):** "I'm not asking you to hand me your database. Give me one buyer who needs a second opinion. I'll make the financing options easy to understand, keep you updated on the file, and never tie anything I do for you to a referral."
- **Service promise (house script):** "Here's what you get on every buyer: a full preapproval [same business day], a call from me to the listing agent on every offer, and a text to you at appraisal, approval and clear to close. If a file is hard, I'll tell you on day one, not day twenty."
- **Assumable angle (house script):** "On your next showings, ask the listing agent if the seller has an FHA or VA loan. I'll tell you within an hour if the buyer can assume it and what the gap financing looks like."
- **Refinance honesty (practitioner pattern, r/Mortgages 148):** "You'll bring [$15,000] to closing, but [$12,000] of that is prepaids, about [$8,000] comes back from your old escrow in 30 days, and you won't make a [$3,500] payment next month. Net, about [$3,500] out of pocket. That's a gap in timing, not a skipped payment."
- **Asset-rich borrower (verbatim, r/RealEstate 2283):** "Sir, I am sorry, you do not qualify for a $700k home loan, even if you are putting 50% down. However, could I interest you in a substantially larger apartment building loan that also carries a lower interest rate to boot?"

## Ads (all under the housing or financial special ad category)

- **Seller lead (research template):** "Thinking of selling in [area] in the next year? Get the three numbers an online estimate won't show you: likely buyer search bracket, competing active inventory, and the price-reduction risk zone for your property."
- **Buyer list (practitioner pattern, r/realtors 1025):** "Hot list: homes under $[X] in [area], updated weekly." Qualifying questions before the list.
- **Relocation (practitioner pattern, r/marketing 604):** "10 reasons not to move to [city], and how locals deal with each one."
