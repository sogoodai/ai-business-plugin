# Expensive mistakes

Two tables: what the data shows (measured, or arithmetic on measured numbers) and what practitioners report losing.

## From the data

| Mistake | What it costs | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Letting website inquiries sit unowned | 68% of brokerage website inquiries got no reply in 24 h; 18% reached an agent (Pied Piper 2026); 54% of buyers hire the first agent they talk to (Zillow 2026) | Auto-acknowledge, 5-minute human reply, backup owner, daily orphan report |
| Comparing Meta and Search on CPL | $13.74 Meta vs $157.59 Search CPL are different intents and funnel stages (WordStream, LocaliQ 2026) | Compare cost per two-way contact, per appointment, per signed client |
| Buying Zillow without modeling close rate | $223 or $139 per connection; at Zillow's 10% target, $2,230 or $1,390 per closing before splits (Zillow 2026) | allowable cost per connection = net per closing x your connection-to-close rate |
| Spending on cinematic video before floor plans and photos | Video is the top listing feature for 4% of buyers; floor plan 33%, photos 26% (Zillow 2025) | Floor plan, photos, 3D first; video for reach |
| Forecasting FSBO and expired conversions from vendor stats | FSBO is 5% of sales, 62% knew the buyer (NAR 2026); REDX relist rates describe the population | Score prospects; measure your own contact-to-appointment rate |
| Paying for llms.txt or "GEO hacks" | Google says they are not needed (2026) | Original local evidence, clean pages, reviews |
| Building SEO on FAQ rich results | Google stopped showing them on 7 May 2026 | Use the effort on unique local pages |
| Templated city pages at scale | scaled content abuse risk (Google) | Fewer pages with real local data |
| Paying a partner for referrals, or disguising it as marketing | RESPA Section 8 violation | FMV for real services, pro rata co-marketing, compliance sign-off |
| Calling DNC-registered expireds and FSBOs | up to $53,088 per violation (FTC 2025); $500 to $1,500 per call in private suits | Scrub within 31 days, mail and door instead |
| "#1 agent" claims from board counts (UK) | ASA rulings; boards and portal stock are inadequate substantiation | Define metric, area and period; keep the evidence |
| EU prospecting from bought lists | EUR 900,000 fine plus EUR 10,000 a day (Solocal, CNIL 2025) | Consent records per contact |
| Letting a PM owner relationship drift | 23% rate PM value excellent; 57% cite poor communication as the switching reason (Buildium 2026) | Fixed-date owner update and churn alerts |
| Optimizing a lender's marketing to applications | $785 pre-tax profit per loan (MBA 2025 data) leaves no room for low pull-through | Optimize to funded loans by source |

## From practitioners

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Rejecting a strong first offer to chase $50k more | sold 60 days later for $47,000 less than that offer (r/realtors 1019, 2026) | Show the seller this case at listing; agree the price review rule up front |
| Pricing $15k over the agent's advice | 6 months, sold $22,000 under the original recommendation (same thread) | Competing listings and pendings in the presentation |
| Listing at $420,069 | drops out of every $420,000 search for $69 (r/RealEstate 3022, 2023) | Price at or under search brackets |
| Wrong MLS for weeks | showings only started once fixed; lost the spring market (r/RealEstate 915, 2025) | Day-one listing audit |
| Not checking the showing system | 12 missed showing requests, then a $13,000 cut (r/RealEstate 799, 2025) | Check the log weekly, confirm sellers get notices |
| Promising a bidding war | buyers came back lower; agent lost two neighborhood listings when the story spread (r/realtors 1019, 2026) | Present odds, not promises |
| Rejecting a $40k inspection concession | sold months later for $30k less than the concession net (r/RealEstate 2953, 2025) | Compare the concession with the cost of losing the buyer |
| Taking an overpriced listing with marketing banned | fired after a month at $70k over market (r/realtors 400, 2025) | Walk away or get price and marketing terms in writing |
| Absorbing endless prep for a seller who will not maintain the home | 6.5 hours cleaning, 38 showings, no sale, quit (r/realtors 92, 2026) | Two frank talks, a task plan, then exit |
| Serving a buyer who will not commit | a year, 250 showings, $1,500 in gas, no purchase (r/realtors 318, 2023) | Agreement before tours; exit after repeated firing |
| Handing off a client without a written split | lease commission lost entirely (r/realtors 546, 2025) | Written split before any handoff |
| Accepting team Zillow leads without the math | 40% fee + 50% split + $250 fee; agent left the team (r/realtors 288, 2024) | Compute take-home per source first |
| Opcity-style referral contracts at 35% | heavy app reporting, low-quality "screened" leads, hard exit (r/realtors 1200, 2021) | Read screening and exit terms; compare net per closing |
| Funnel leads (instant valuation, exclusive homes) | $4,000 for one $2,000 commission; no Google closings in 2 years (r/realtors 1200, 2021) | Qualify in the form; judge after a 6-month nurture |
| Months on branding before prospecting | six months, zero income (r/realtors 184, 2022) | Prospect first; brand later |
| Cold-calling the expired avalanche | 30 to 50 calls a day to one seller; the industry looks bad (r/realtors 230, 2026) | Written diagnosis by mail or door |
| Doorstep collection of a photo fee | seller says it would have killed repeat business (r/realtors 271, 2025) | Disclose up front, request by email |
| PM with zero showings for months | owner lost about $10,000 and fired the manager (r/realestateinvesting 1101, 2023) | Weekly leasing report, price and staging action at 14 days |
| Accepting "reluctant landlords" at above-market rent | "terrible business every time" (r/PropertyManagement 59, 2026) | Screen owners before signing |
| Waiting for quotes on a July AC failure | risks a long-term tenant to save $50 (r/PropertyManagement 150, 2025) | Pre-approved emergency limits |
| Advertising a rare loan exception | sets expectations other borrowers cannot meet (r/RealEstate 1259, 2024) | Advertise repeatable products only |
| Missing a mortgage contingency deadline | nearly cost a client $10,000 (r/realtors 437, 2025) | Deadline calendar with alerts at 5 and 2 days |
| AI-written cold emails | underperformed personal emails (r/CommercialRealEstate 135, 2025) | AI for research and drafts, human voice for the send |
