# 90-day plans

Order matters for every sub-model: instrument the funnel, fix response handling, reactivate the owned database, then add one high-intent source, then test lower-intent sources, then cut and scale. The reason: response handling is the industry's biggest measured leak (68% of website inquiries unanswered in 24 hours, Pied Piper 2026), and referral, past-client and community sources are the largest share of first contacts (Zillow 2026). Targets below are internal operating targets, not industry benchmarks; store them as such.

Every phase ends with a short owner report: what changed, scorecard numbers, decisions needed.

## Shared days 1 to 15: instrument

- One source taxonomy (source, sub-source) applied to every form, number, portal and referral.
- Lifecycle stages with required loss reasons; dedupe contacts; tag consent per channel and DNC status.
- Tracked numbers and form audit: submit every form and call every number yourself; time the reply.
- Baseline by source: inquiries, two-way contacts, appointments, agreements or signed clients, closings or funded loans, revenue.
- Do not buy more leads until these fields fill reliably.

## Buyer and listing agent (solo)

**Days 16 to 30: answer and reactivate**
- Auto-acknowledge and 5-minute callback with a backup (another agent, ISA or answering service).
- Past client and sphere list cleaned; every closed client asked for a review (neighborhood named); group-text referral ask to the 20 best relationships.
- Count owners in the database; expected moves = owners / 12. Write down your capture target.

**Days 31 to 45: the two products**
- Buyer consultation built (agreement, compensation explanation, closed-sale reality check, lender intros).
- Listing presentation rebuilt around pricing evidence, search brackets, launch assets in buyer priority order, and a written price review rule.
- One-page expired diagnosis template.

**Days 46 to 60: one high-intent source**
- Pick by owner preference and market: Google seller and buyer search plus GBP (hand to `local-seo` and `paid-ads`), or a portal with answering proven, or a farm (300 to 1,000 doors, 10 pieces planned over 12 months), or open houses (two back-to-back each weekend day).
- Offline conversion imports for appointments and agreements.

**Days 61 to 75: test Meta as a separate funnel**
- Housing special ad category; seller "three numbers" offer or price-capped buyer list with qualifying questions; immediate text plus call; 6-month nurture bucket.
- Compare cost per two-way contact and cost per appointment with Search, not CPL.

**Days 76 to 90: compound**
- Video system (8 to 10 videos batched, client questions) or a local group plus one event.
- Scorecard: conversations per day (target 5+), appointments, agreements, listings taken, pipeline value, review velocity.
- Exit check: response median under 5 minutes in staffed hours; every lead has a next action; 2+ appointments a week.

## Team lead

**Days 16 to 30: routing and SLAs**
- Live transfer to whoever is free; round robin with 5-minute accept and backup; portal standards tracked weekly (Redfin 5 minutes, 55% acceptance, 80% list compliance).
- Per-agent conversion: two-way contact, appointment, agreement per 100 leads. Route more to the best converters; cap the rest.
- Daily orphan-lead report to the lead.

**Days 31 to 45: economics by source**
- Team contribution per closing and agent take-home per source, with every fee and split in the right order. Flag any source where agents net less than a sphere deal's share.
- Renegotiate or drop the worst source; check ZIP pricing yourself, not with the rep.

**Days 46 to 60: leverage**
- ISA or VA for first calls, dispositions, listing alerts and CRM hygiene; TC for contract to close; showing agent for low-intent buyers.
- Written split policy for every handoff.

**Days 61 to 75: team database**
- Each agent's past clients into the team CRM with event triggers; team review and referral campaign.
- One team-level listing source (farm, expired diagnosis program, or seller search).

**Days 76 to 90: scale or cut**
- Scale only sources with positive contribution after agent take-home, met SLA and no open compliance issue.
- Recruiting offer written from what you actually provide (leads, TC, CRM, splits, caps).
- Exit check: contribution per closing by source known; agent churn reason known; SLA met 4 weeks running.

## Property manager

**Days 16 to 30: churn first**
- Owner list with doors, fee, tenure, last complaint, last contact. Call every owner with an open issue.
- Fixed-date monthly owner update live; approval threshold and emergency rules written and sent to owners.
- Churn split: sold vs switched vs self-manage, last 12 months.

**Days 31 to 45: the switching offer**
- One-page fee sheet with every fee; vendor proof policy (photos, receipts, notes); owner-facing service page answering rent, vacancy, maintenance, screening, reporting, arrears, after-hours.
- Value-event review asks to owners whose problems you solved this quarter.

**Days 46 to 60: Google**
- GBP and owner reviews (hand to `local-seo`); search campaigns for owner intent ("property management [city]", "switch property manager"); compare against LocaliQ's $76.71 PM CPL as a sanity check.

**Days 61 to 75: referral sources**
- Five listing agents with stale listings or investor buyers; investor meetups with real numbers; accidental-landlord mail to expired and withdrawn listings.
- Owner screening checklist in use.

**Days 76 to 90: measure**
- Cost per signed door by source, payback months, churn rate, leasing days on market vs last year, owner update open and reply rate.
- Exit check: zero owners without a monthly update; no vacancy over 30 days without a written action plan.

## Loan officer

**Days 16 to 30: partner audit**
- List every agent who sent a file in 24 months: files, funded, pull-through, last contact. Ask each closed-deal agent for a 10-minute debrief.
- Written service promise (preapproval turnaround, listing-agent call on every offer, milestone texts).

**Days 31 to 45: database**
- Past clients segmented by rate, loan type, equity, PMI, ARM reset; refinance and HELOC or second-mortgage triggers with honest dollar math.
- Consent check before any automated rate-alert text.

**Days 46 to 60: become one of four**
- Target 20 agents: those on the other side of your deals, then high-volume buyer agents in your price bands. Ask for one second-opinion file each.
- Agent education: assumable loans, condo warrantability, buydown vs price-cut math.

**Days 61 to 75: RESPA-clean co-marketing**
- Any shared ad or event split pro rata by actual use, each party paying its own share; compliance sign-off; nothing tied to referrals.
- Open house support where you pay only your own costs.

**Days 76 to 90: score and focus**
- Partner score (files, funded, pull-through, speed, satisfaction); double time on the top five; drop the bottom third of coffee meetings.
- Exit check: 3+ agents sending files monthly; funded loans by source known; every ad passes Reg Z trigger-term review.

## After day 90: the decision policy

- Scale a channel if contribution after acquisition cost is positive, the sample is big enough for the owner's risk tolerance, the response SLA is met, and no compliance issue is open.
- Fix the funnel before adding spend if lead volume is healthy but two-way contact or appointment rates fall.
- Fix the offer or traffic if contact handling is healthy but qualified rate is weak.
- Fix the sales process if appointments are healthy but agreements are weak.
- Fix service and retention if acquisition is healthy but referrals, reviews, owner retention or pull-through fall.
