# Video creators: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram (and LinkedIn)

Playbook for a creator whose audience lives on video platforms. General social posting systems, scheduling and business-account content are in sibling `organic-social`; this file is the creator-business view: what grows a channel, what pays, and what the numbers mean.

Distribution is content-level now, not follower-level. YouTube says each video is judged on fresh performance data and a weak upload does not penalize the channel by itself; Instagram tests eligible original posts on small non-follower groups first; TikTok's Creator Rewards formula weights originality, play duration, search value and engagement (platform docs, 2024 to 2026). A small account with the right video can reach millions. A big account with the wrong video reaches its own followers and stops.

## YouTube

### What the system rewards (YouTube Help, 2026)

- Ranking seeks long-term viewer satisfaction: whether a viewer picks the video when offered, how long and how much they watch, likes, dislikes, "not interested", post-watch surveys.
- Shorts: chose-to-view versus swiped, average view duration, percent viewed, likes, surveys. No minimum upload cadence. Shorts do not hurt long-form recommendations just by existing.
- Native A/B test: up to three titles, thumbnails or combinations, runs up to two weeks, picks the winner by **watch-time share**, not CTR. Creators had run 40M+ tests by Oct 2026. Since Made on YouTube 2026, you can also test up to three different cuts of the video itself (openings and structure).
- CTR falls as a video reaches people outside the core audience; total views can still rise. Do not panic on a CTR drop when impressions are climbing.

### Packaging (the biggest lever for small channels)

- **CTR targets.** In one creator's 120-video dataset, CTR explained about 60% of view variance; videos at 8%+ CTR almost always beat videos at 4% (r/NewTubers, 171 pts). Below 4%: rework the thumbnail and title before anything else. Correlation only, and CTR norms vary by traffic source.
- **Package before you produce.** Pick the title and thumbnail concept before the video idea (three threads agree, r/NewTubers 490, 470, 359 pts). If you cannot write a title you would click, do not film it.
- **Test an anti-style variant.** One creator always tests two styles they like plus one opposite; viewers kept picking the one expected to lose (r/NewTubers, 360 pts). Make variants differ in the viewer promise, not punctuation (YouTube's own guidance).
- **Thumbnail text.** Top thumbnails of channels at 300k to 3M subscribers averaged 3.05 words, white text most common, almost never more than two text colors, one focal point, at most two subjects (r/NewTubers, 762 pts). Thumbnail opens the question, title explains it; long explanatory thumbnail text performed worst (346-thumbnail designer, r/NewTubers, 824 pts).
- **Browse vs search packaging.** Entertainment: add a new emotion ("I Quit My Job" with "WHY?" on the thumbnail). Tutorials: state the fix ("Fix Doothingy Fast") (r/PartneredYoutube, 301 pts). Problem-plus-solution titles got 40% of views from search vs under 10% for clever titles (r/NewTubers, 171 pts).
- **Concrete numbers.** 58% of MrBeast titles contain a specific number, 33% a dollar amount (r/PartneredYoutube, 191 pts). "The most impossible mod ever created" with thumbnail text "it takes 32000 hours" did 100,000 views in 4 days on a 30-subscriber channel (r/NewTubers, 173 pts).
- **Revive a dead video.** Change only the weaker of title or thumbnail, wait a couple of days, then the other (r/youtubers, 587 pts). Test evergreen videos first: YouTube itself calls older videos lower-risk test candidates. A new video on an old topic revived a 3-year-old video from 25 to 2,000 to 3,000 daily views in 4 days (r/youtubers, 145 pts).
- **Swipe file.** Screenshot 20 videos you actually clicked while browsing and compare colors, text, faces, question vs statement titles, mobile readability (r/NewTubers, 1,368 pts).

### Concepts that package themselves

- **The pancake test.** Replace "playing a game" with "baking a pancake": an ordinary pancake needs a hook (biggest, blindfolded, 1,000 in 24 hours). "If the event would happen to a regular player playing normally, it is not interesting enough." From a review of 116 small gaming channels (r/NewTubers, 992 pts). "Dark Souls Playthrough Part 1" becomes "I Beat Dark Souls with Only a Shield."
- **Cheap opinion beats expensive research.** A 5-hour "what MMO should you play this year" got "loads of views"; a week-long cost analysis got almost none. A one-day opinion video got 16k views in a month against 2k in a year for a week-long research video (r/NewTubers, 1,660 and 308 pts). Opinion gives current fans a reason to disagree and prospects a reason to watch.
- **Follow the breakout.** First 30 videos peaked at 100 views; one adjacent topic hit 250,000 in days; 200+ videos since at 20,000 to 200,000 each (r/NewTubers, 1,131 pts). Test adjacent topics, then niche around the winner.
- **Format x empty niche, or language x proven topic.** Video essays in a game nobody covered got 1,000 subscribers in 4 to 5 videos (r/NewTubers, 441 pts). A mod covered in English but not Spanish for 7 years: 100,000 views in 4 days (r/NewTubers, 173 pts).
- **Outlier mining.** Find channels under 50,000 subscribers pulling 30,000+ daily views and adapt the topic, not the video; one creator monetized in 40 days this way and a 90-upvote commenter repeated it (r/PartneredYoutube, 390 pts).
- **Expensive assets are series.** A $20k bus video got 70 views as one upload; car channels split a $100,000 purchase into 8 to 10 episodes (r/NewTubers, 900 pts).

### Hooks and retention

- **Kill the greeting.** "Hey guys, welcome to the channel, my name is..." is the most flagged opening by an editor reviewing 100+ channels (r/NewTubers, 811 pts). Deliver the promise in 5 to 10 seconds; cold open first.
- **30-second retention predicts reach.** Videos keeping over 70% at 30 seconds averaged 3x the impressions of videos under 50% (r/NewTubers, 171 pts).
- **Goal, motivation, stakes.** "I built the safest base in Palworld (goal) to protect myself from an invasion (motivation), and if my defenses fail all my pals will get stolen (stakes)." Creator went from about 100 to 10,000 subscribers in 6 months (r/NewTubers, 992 pts).
- **"But" and "therefore", never "and then".** Changing only the connectors in one weak script improved retention throughout (r/NewTubers, 253 pts).
- **Re-hook every 2 to 3 minutes** in 10+ minute videos and keep three open questions running, one always minutes from payoff (r/NewTubers, 1,848 and 992 pts).
- **Delete the end cue.** "So that was it guys" was the exact drop-off point in one channel's retention (r/youtubers, 102 pts).
- **One end screen, one pitch.** Five to ten seconds pitching one specific video, "it's on the screen right now", a single end-screen element: 10 to 15% and 8 to 12% end-screen CTR in two separate reports (r/PartneredYoutube, 541 and 342 pts).
- **Ideas over polish.** Four threads agree: blown exposure and bad audio with a strong idea beat cinematic videos (r/NewTubers 490, 441, 290, 395 pts). Exception: a laptop webcam lost viewers within 20 seconds; a tracking webcam and two cheap lights improved retention (r/PartneredYoutube, 317 pts).
- **Leave mid-roll pauses.** Tightly cut videos got zero automatic ad slots and about 99% of manual placements flagged as disruptive (r/PartneredYoutube, 140 pts).

### Length and cadence

- **Length pays.** At matched views: 11 minutes, 71,000 views, $708; 23 minutes, 72,000 views, $877; a 30-second Short, 79,000 views, $10 (r/PartneredYoutube, 152 pts). Two-hour travel videos earned $43.88 RPM against a $14.86 channel average (r/PartneredYoutube, 185 pts).
- **TV viewers change the format.** Channels with 40 to 90% TV watch time do better with 20 to 30 minute documentaries and long sessions; a 90%-TV loop channel had a $36.39 CPM (r/PartneredYoutube, 185 and 118 pts). Check Studio's device report before choosing length.
- **Cadence evidence conflicts.** Weekly beat fortnightly by 22% views per video in one dataset (r/NewTubers, 171 pts); evergreen channels grew during month-long breaks; a weekly creator out-earned a daily one (r/NewTubers, 495, 408, 383 pts). Rule: the most frequent cadence that keeps packaging quality. Daily is a burnout trap.
- **Budget:** routine videos 5 to 10 hours, 1 to 2 a week, about 100 a year; 20 to 40 hour productions a few times a year (r/NewTubers, 514 pts).

### Shorts

- **Shorts pay cents.** Shorts RPM is $0.02 to $1.48 by niche, 3 to 14% of long-form RPM; most channels need 11,000 to 34,000 Shorts views to equal 1,000 long-form views (AIR Media-Tech, 274 channels, Sep 2026). Operators report 1.6M Shorts views paid $0.38 and 100M views paid $1,100 (r/NewTubers 382 pts; r/PartneredYoutube 798 pts).
- **Shorts subscribers watch less long-form:** 60% lower long-form viewing rate than other subscribers in one channel (r/NewTubers, 171 pts).
- **Use Shorts for reach and links.** Link the related long-form video from each Short; treat Shorts as discovery and judge them on profile visits and long-form sessions, not RPM.
- **Auto dubbing check.** Three channels saw views collapse with odd foreign traffic after auto dubbing; disabling it and deleting dub tracks restored views in two (r/PartneredYoutube, 156, 179, 336 pts). Causation unproven. If a drop coincides with new-language traffic and short retention, test turning it off.

### Getting to YPP

- **Thresholds (YouTube Help, 2026):** 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months, or 1,000 subscribers plus 10M Shorts views in 90 days. Shorts-feed views do not count toward watch hours. Inactive channels (no upload or post for 6 months) can lose monetization.
- **Stop sending videos to friends and family.** Three creators saw growth after stopping (one went from 210 to 1,000 subscribers in three weeks); the mechanism is unproven, but personal-network views add only 30 to 40 off-target views (r/NewTubers, 645 pts). Niche communities where the video answers a question did help.
- **Prepare inventory:** 50 to 100 ideas and 5 finished scripts before launch (r/NewTubers, 645 and 607 pts).
- **Kill or pivot rule:** concept review after 10 videos or 3 to 6 months; change niche or format by 6 to 12 months without traction (r/NewTubers, 482 and 216 pts). Cautionary: 1,000+ videos over 10 years at about 200 views.

### YouTube money beyond AdSense

- Long-form RPM median $2.30 (AIR, 300 channels, May 2025 to May 2026); Education & Science median $10.22; Kids $0.33. Full table: [benchmarks.md](benchmarks.md). Geography dominates: a 125M-view mostly-India Short earned about CA$1,000 (r/PartneredYoutube, 152 pts).
- AdSense is often the small line: $120,000 offer revenue against $1,841 AdSense on 5,900 subscribers; $100k+ community sales against $9,020 ads; $20k+ affiliate against under $1,000 AdSense (r/PartneredYoutube 185 and 219 pts; r/youtubers 74 pts).
- Memberships at small scale: about 250 members at $6 a month on 4,500 subscribers (r/NewTubers, 379 pts).
- Livestream replays with manual mid-rolls: $20 to $40 RPM in segmented 3 to 4 hour streams (r/PartneredYoutube, 528 pts). AIR found a stream view out-earns a video view in 8 of 11 niches; channels streaming 8+ times a month earn a median 50% of ad revenue from live (AIR, 404 channels, Jul 2026).
- Before going full time: 3 to 4 income sources each at least equal to AdSense (600k-subscriber creator, r/PartneredYoutube, 297 pts).

## TikTok

- **Search is the brief.** Creator Search Insights shows searched topics and "content gap" topics with demand and few videos. Build videos as answers: query, spoken answer, on-screen words, caption with the same terms (TikTok Newsroom). Start from "which query does this satisfy", not hashtags.
- **Creator Rewards** pays only for original videos over 1 minute, weighting originality, play duration, search value and engagement (TikTok Newsroom, 2024). Eligibility at launch: 18+, 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in the last 30 days. If the creator is eligible, overlap high-search topics with 60s+ formats.
- **Engagement benchmark:** median 7.7% engagement by views, 13.0% at P75 (Kyra, 21,084 UK and US creators, Q3 2026). Do not compare with follower-based rates.
- **Judge a series, not a post:** median qualified views over several posts and downstream conversion. One 47,000-view video gave 15 clicks and 0 sales; an 800-view video sold (r/Affiliatemarketing, 62 pts).
- **Paid creators on TikTok buy views, not ad revenue.** TikTok Creator Rewards payouts vary widely by niche and country; operators report from $3.45 per 1.5M views to $7,000 to $8,000 months (r/PartneredYoutube, 388 pts). Never plan income on it.
- **Watermarks.** Remove the TikTok watermark before cross-posting: Instagram downranks visible watermarks (Meta) and operators report "night and day" (r/InstagramMarketing, 87 pts).
- **Promote:** about $550 over two weeks brought 179K views and 7,372 net followers in one test (r/InstagramMarketing, 374 pts). Only boost posts with organic traction; boosting posts with none was wasted in music tests ([music.md](music.md)).

## Instagram

- **Originality is distribution.** 75% of US recommendations were original posts in Q4 2025 (Meta, Jan 2026). Aggregators and identical reposts lose recommendation eligibility.
- **Trial Reels as the test lane.** Trial Reels show to non-followers first. In Meta's analysis of 400,000+ creators, 40% posted more Reels after adopting them and 80% of those saw higher non-follower reach a month later (observational, Jun 2025). Operators: retest any Reel that passed 1M as a Trial with a small change (mirror, text, filter, font) about 4 days apart; one 1M Reel got 250K, 400K+ and 350K+ on later trials, 99% non-followers (r/InstagramMarketing, 98 pts). Counter-signal: one agency saw non-follower share jump after archiving all Trial Reels (r/InstagramMarketing, 81 pts). Test both if non-follower reach collapses.
- **The second-5-to-8 cliff.** Four threads place the main drop at seconds 5 to 8. Put a re-hook (prop, name-drop, music shift, zoom, question) 1 to 2 seconds before your measured drop (r/InstagramMarketing, 565, 164, 105, 66 pts). One Reel at 97% that fell to 15% by second 7 flopped; one holding 75% at 7 seconds and 60% at 10 hit 7M (r/InstagramMarketing, 164 pts).
- **Shares predict the tail.** High-share Reels kept gaining for 1 to 2 weeks (r/InstagramMarketing, 128 pts). Pre-filter: no specific reason to save or share, no film.
- **Judge at 24 to 48 hours.** Four threads debunk the first-hour rule: Reels took off after 6 to 10 hours, 3 days, or 10 days via reposts. Never delete flops.
- **Hashtags are minor.** Current consensus is 3 to 6 narrow tags or none, with keywords in the first caption line and on-screen text ("Instagram analytics for small creators") (6+ threads, r/InstagramMarketing).
- **Carousels for save-heavy niches.** Career carousels 4 a week for 6 months: 18K to 25K reach, 450 to 800 saves each, 40K followers (r/InstagramMarketing, 99 pts). Instagram can resurface slide 2 to someone who skipped slide 1, so every slide must stand alone.
- **Swipe file: 5 big plus 5 small.** Five niche accounts over 50K and five under 10K that grew recently; sort their posts by views; keep references where likes are at least 5% of views and comments at least 1% (r/InstagramMarketing, 329, 167, 193 pts). Copy structure, never words: copying a viral caption verbatim lowered numbers (r/InstagramMarketing, 128 pts).
- **Launch new niche accounts isolated** from friends and unrelated audiences: 10K in one week, 20K in two (r/InstagramMarketing, 465 pts).
- **Profile conversion:** profile visits should be 2 to 4% of reach; below 2% means the content lacks a reason to see more (r/InstagramMarketing, 225 pts).
- **Engagement benchmark:** median 4.5% likes plus comments over plays, 7.5% at P75 (Kyra, Q3 2026, UK and US creators).
- **Follow/unfollow:** works only as a slow first-1K tactic targeting followers of niche accounts, with daily caps; three threads report bots and dead engagement. Skip it for brand-deal creators: sponsors check follower quality.

## LinkedIn (for B2B and expert creators)

- LinkedIn's March 2026 ranking update uses LLM-based content understanding and suppresses engagement bait, recycled content, automated comments and pods (LinkedIn, 12 Mar 2026). Write for a job context: role, industry, workflow, tool, customer type.
- Benchmark: 5.8% median engagement by impressions, 11.0% at P75 (Vista Social, 7,238 profiles, Aug to Oct 2026, not creator-only).
- Operators report LinkedIn as the largest channel for professional newsletters: 6 posts a week on something specific you did or saw grew 42,000 followers and 12,000 free subscribers (r/Substack, 109 pts).

## Diagnostics an agent runs every week

```
packaging problem   = impressions fine, CTR below channel median        -> new title/thumbnail test
promise problem     = CTR fine, 30-second retention under 50%           -> fix opening or package overpromise
pacing problem      = good 30 s, steep drop mid-video                   -> cut side plots, re-hook every 2 to 3 min
audience problem    = views up, returning viewers and subscribers flat  -> topic drifted from the core viewer
money problem       = views fine, RPM below niche median                -> check geography, length, mid-rolls, Shorts share
non-follower share  = reach from non-followers / total reach            -> discovery health on Instagram and TikTok
```

Pull the last 20 to 50 uploads and use medians by format, never averages: one viral post distorts the mean (research report 90-day method). Log every upload's first 48-hour views against the channel's typical range, because YouTube's own "typical" range drifts down as videos underperform (r/youtubers, 83 pts).
