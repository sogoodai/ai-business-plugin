# Brand deals: pricing, rights, negotiation, media kit

How a creator prices, sells and protects sponsorships on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, newsletters and podcasts. Verbatim emails and replies: [scripts.md](scripts.md). Disclosure rules: [disclosure-and-policy.md](disclosure-and-policy.md). Brand-side influencer buying is not in scope.

## Market reality

- **Brands buy views, not followers.** The median sponsored post collected first-month views equal to 25.8% of the creator's followers (TikTok 26.1%, Instagram 28.2%, YouTube 7.6%) (Kolect, campaigns Aug 2025 to Oct 2026, vendor). A follower-based rate card overprices most creators and underprices a few.
- **Asks run high, deals close lower.** Average ask vs actual brand spend: YouTube $675 vs $418, Instagram $364 vs $212, TikTok $350 vs $200 (Collabstr, 15,000+ collaborations, 2025; marketplace skews small).
- **Income is concentrated.** Creator brand-deal income: median $3,000, mean $11,400; the top 10% take 62% (CreatorIQ, 2025 data). Never promise an owner "typical" sponsorship income.
- **Budgets tightened in 2023 and stayed selective.** One talent agency saw YouTube integration CPMs fall from $30 to $40 (2021) to $20 to $25 (2023), first-time deals capped near $10,000, and brands preferring several creators at $2,000 to $5,000 each (r/PartneredYoutube, 213 and 178 pts; one agency, dated).
- **Usage is the money.** 77% of brands repurpose creator content in paid ads; 67% fold usage rights into the initial contract (Aspire, about 900 marketers and creators, 2026, vendor). Pricing usage separately is the single biggest lever most creators skip.

## Step 1: forecast views

```
forecast views (organic) = median views of the last 10 to 12 comparable posts or videos, first 30 days
                           (exclude the top and bottom outlier; never use followers)
YouTube lifetime         = 30-day views / 0.62   (38% of YouTube views arrive after day 30, 27% after day 90; Agentio via Kolect, 2026)
newsletter               = active subscribers x median unique open rate, plus median clicks per sponsor placement
podcast                  = downloads per episode at 30 days (IAB-style), plus show-notes clicks
```

## Step 2: base fee from CPM

```
base fee = forecast views / 1,000 x target CPM   (floor: the creator's minimum fee)
```

Realized creator CPMs (fee per 1,000 first-month views; median and middle half; Kolect, Oct 2026, vendor sample in consumer tech, home and lifestyle, mostly US and Europe):

| Platform | Median | P25 to P75 |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | $6.73 | $2.04 to $27.15 |
| Instagram | $17.96 | $4.51 to $59.61 |
| YouTube (30-day views, overstates lifetime CPM) | $74.24 | $27.08 to $184.75 |
| All platforms | $15.46 | $4.41 to $62.28 |

Typical mid-range CPMs from 400+ disclosed deals (RevenueLab, May 2026, directional): YouTube integration (60 to 90 s) $20 to $50; YouTube dedicated video $50 to $120; Shorts $8 to $25; TikTok in-feed $15 to $40; Reels $10 to $30; newsletter primary sponsor $30 to $80. Podcast host reads $18 to $26 (Libsyn, Oct 2026).

Price per 1,000 typical views by creator size (Kolect median, all platforms): nano under 10K $78.76; micro 10K to 100K $30.32; mid 100K to 500K $15.68; macro 500K to 1M $16.31; mega 1M+ $10.75. Small creators earn more per view, which is why a 40K account at 10% engagement can charge $3,500 per Reel while a 2M theme page gets $500 to $600 per post (r/InstagramMarketing, 174 and 1,002 pts).

By niche (Kolect, all platforms, per 1,000 typical views): shopping and deals $54.57, auto $47.90, tech $26.41, beauty $20.47, lifestyle $17.91, art $13.25, entertainment $12.04. By creator country (median fee per collaboration; per 1,000 views): Canada $575, $32.69; US $500, $27.45; UK $600, $26.72; Germany $700, $25.20; Australia $419, $24.39; France $560, $21.54.

Pick the target CPM: start at the platform median; move toward P75 for B2B, finance, software, high-income audiences, US/UK/CA/AU share above 60%, or proven conversions; toward P25 for entertainment, kids, low tier-1 share.

Operator rate points to sanity-check (self-reported): finance YouTube at about 30,000 subscribers, $500 to $1,000 per 60-second read (r/youtubers, 110 pts); guitar lessons at 35,000, offers of $400 to $1,500 (r/PartneredYoutube, 185 pts); 90,000 subscribers with 50k to 100k views per video, $800 to $1,000 per sponsor (r/PartneredYoutube, 177 pts); 1M+ view creators, a brand group paying $18,000+ (r/PartneredYoutube, 178 pts).

## Step 3: add line items (never bundle)

| Item | Benchmark | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Integration depth | flat read 1x; demoed product 2 to 3x; multi-video series with CTA 4 to 6x | RevenueLab, 2026 |
| Organic repost by brand (30 to 90 days) | 0 to 15% of base | Gigapay compilation, Oct 2026 |
| Paid usage (brand runs the asset from its own account) | 15 to 35% of base per 30 days (talent managers: 20 to 30% per month) | Aspire 2026; Net Influencer 2026 |
| Paid social 90 days / 12 months | 30 to 45% / 70 to 100% of base | UGC Bloom via Gigapay, Aug 2026 |
| Whitelisting, Spark Ads, Partnership Ads (ads run from the creator's handle) | 25 to 50% of base per 30 days | InfluencerFee via Gigapay, 2026 |
| Whitelisting premium by platform | Instagram +50 to 100%, TikTok +30 to 60%, YouTube +40 to 80% | InfluencerFee via Gigapay, 2026 |
| Whitelisting access only, 30 days (no new content) | nano $75 to $150; micro $150 to $400; mid $400 to $1,200; macro $1,200 to $3,500 | CreatoHunt, 90+ negotiations H1 2026, via Gigapay |
| Perpetual buyout, all channels | 150 to 300% of base | UGC Bloom via Gigapay |
| Category exclusivity | 15 to 30% of deal for 30 days; 25 to 50% for 90 days (or 1.5 to 2x for a 90-day category exclusive) | InfluencerFee via Gigapay; RevenueLab |
| Cross-platform posting (Meta + TikTok) | 1.4 to 1.7x single platform | Gigapay compilation |
| Extension negotiated mid-flight | 60 to 75% of the original rights rate | CreatoHunt via Gigapay |
| Repeat series of 4 | 2.5 to 3x a one-off, rarely 4x | RevenueLab, 2026 |
| Raw footage, extra revision rounds, AI training or synthetic likeness | quote separately or exclude | research report, 2026 |

Gigapay is a payments vendor compiling partly undated sources; treat its bands as negotiation ranges, not market medians. Aspire is the stronger survey. When sources disagree, quote the Aspire range and use the others as ceilings.

Worked example: a YouTube creator with a 40,000 median 30-day view count, tech audience, 70% US/UK.
```
lifetime views ~ 40,000 / 0.62 = 64,500
base (integration) = 64.5 x $35 CPM = $2,258  -> quote $2,250
paid usage 30 days at 25% = $563; 90 days at 40% = $900
whitelisting 30 days at 35% = $788
category exclusivity 30 days at 20% of deal = $450
```
The organic fee is the floor of the conversation. Twelve months of paid usage at 25% per 30 days is $6,750 on a $2,250 asset, three times the fee (same arithmetic as the research report's $2,000 example).

## Step 4: negotiate

Rules from agencies and operators:
1. **Ask for their budget, campaign goal and paid-media plan first.** "If my face carries a six-figure media buy, a flat $500 usage fee is not the same as a 30-day test" (research report). Ask: expected paid spend, duration, which accounts run the ad, countries.
2. **Cut scope, not price.** Against a low budget: 45 seconds instead of 60 to 90, integration later than the first third, remove usage rights, exclusivity and view guarantees (agency, r/PartneredYoutube, 213 pts).
3. **Trade fee for performance.** When an $800 fee gets pushed down, ask for 5 to 8% commission on code sales instead (r/PartneredYoutube, 248 pts). Or flat fee plus affiliate (one hybrid made $43k, r/Affiliatemarketing, 42 pts).
4. **Offer a cheaper inventory tier** for small budgets: Shorts, Stories, Community posts, newsletter mentions (agency, r/PartneredYoutube, 213 pts).
5. **Quarterly, not monthly.** Budget-cut brands moved to one campaign per quarter; pitch that cadence to keep them (r/youtubers, 101 pts).
6. **Protect ranking assets.** A tech creator refused a sponsor link above the fold in a video ranked #1 on Google and YouTube and countered with cash, licenses, a new standalone video and a pinned comment; the sponsor accepted (r/youtubers, 67 pts).
7. **Price exclusivity as its own line and cap the lock.** A creator with 52K TikTok and 36K Instagram followers signed 12 videos for $300 ($25 each) plus 12 unpaid months of exclusivity, a 15-month competitor lockout (r/InstagramMarketing, 67 pts). Compute per-deliverable rate and paid months of exclusivity before signing.
8. **Start with a 30-day paid license** and quote renewals in advance. Never perpetual by default; talent managers call perpetuity a non-starter (Net Influencer, 2026).
9. **Price first sponsors low, then raise as inventory fills** (r/NewTubers, 1,890 pts). Limited slots are the honest scarcity.
10. **Edit sponsor reads with hard cuts and natural pauses** so YouTube's editor can remove the segment cleanly if the brand breaks the deal (r/PartneredYoutube, 150 pts).

## Step 5: contract checklist

Every deal memo names: deliverables (format, length, count, platforms), draft and approval dates, revision rounds (default 1 or 2), go-live window, disclosure wording (non-negotiable), fee and payment terms, deposit for first-time brands, late-payment clause, rights clause, exclusivity, kill fee, and who owns raw footage.

Rights clause fields (research report, 2026): asset; advertiser and legal entity; permitted accounts; platforms; countries; organic vs paid; creator-handle advertising (whitelisting) yes or no; start and end date; edit rights; raw footage; AI training and synthetic voice or likeness (excluded unless licensed); competitor exclusivity (category, duration); renewal price.

Hard nos: perpetual paid usage inside the base fee; AI training rights; exclusivity without a fee; brand-dictated wording that removes the disclosure; claims the creator cannot verify; payment only "on results" without a floor.

## Finding sponsors

- **Thresholds that brands use:** about 5,000 average views for an exact-fit sponsor (a 3D-printing channel to a printer maker); about 50,000 minimum for broad direct-to-consumer sponsors, 100,000 ideal (agency, r/youtubers, 188 pts). Below that, sell to niche brands, sell affiliate, or bundle with other creators.
- **Pitch brands that sponsored your niche in the last 30 days**, with one product, the video idea, the fit, and the budget needed. Unusual concepts got picked "at much higher rates" (agency, r/youtubers, 188 pts).
- **Dream 100:** scan the disclosed sponsors of the 10 largest similar creators for 10 to 20 paying brands, add their competitors, find the contact (r/PartneredYoutube, 150 pts).
- **High-frequency buyers:** in 350k videos, NordVPN sponsored 543 creators across 3,800 videos (about 7 each), BetterHelp 537 creators (r/PartneredYoutube, 170 pts; the poster sells the tool). A 28k-subscriber channel landed NordVPN the next day.
- **Pitch launches:** "I saw you're launching product X..." (1.8M-subscriber creator, r/PartneredYoutube, 798 pts).
- **Referral pod:** 3 to 4 niche creators swap sponsor intros and pricing and pitch as a package; marketers often need 10 to 15 creators per campaign (r/PartneredYoutube, 150 pts).
- **Be findable:** About page and bio with explicit topic and audience keywords, because agencies search with keyword tools; a public media kit and contact form; tell the audience you take sponsors, since some viewers own businesses (r/PartneredYoutube, 213 pts; r/youtubers, 89 pts).
- **Affiliate results are the pitch.** Show brands your clicks and conversions (r/NewTubers, 1,890 pts).
- **Pre-audience deals exist:** sending unboxing footage to the shop where the product was bought got a $600 accessory reimbursed for footage rights at 0 subscribers (r/NewTubers, 479 pts). That is UGC, priced on production, not reach.
- **Off-niche sponsors fail:** a wallet ad on an astronomy channel sold zero (r/NewTubers, 421 pts). Kid audiences are blacklisted by most brands (agency, r/PartneredYoutube, 178 pts).
- **On camera and native converts best:** personality-led reads converted 3 to 4x faceless, and skits sometimes renewed despite missing goals (agency, r/PartneredYoutube, 178 pts).

## Agencies and managers

Non-exclusive, 15 to 20% commission at most (agency, r/PartneredYoutube, 213 pts). Stricter: 20% only on deals the agency sources, 0% on self-sourced deals and AdSense (r/PartneredYoutube, 150 pts). Never sign a manager who takes a cut of platform revenue or of deals that existed before them.

## Media kit template (one page)

Brands now divide price by results. Give them what they compute (newsletter-sponsor vetting questions adapted from coreyhaines31/marketingskills, MIT).

```
[Name / channel], [one-line positioning: who it is for and what they get]
Audience: [primary viewer in one sentence: role, situation, life stage]
Reach (last 90 days, medians, not averages):
  - YouTube long-form: [median 30-day views], [returning viewers %], [avg view duration]
  - Shorts / Reels / TikTok: [median views], [non-follower share %]
  - Newsletter: [active subs], [median unique opens], [median clicks per sponsor placement]
  - Podcast: [downloads per episode at 30 days]
Geography: [top 3 countries with %]   Age and gender: [top bands]
Audience proof: [survey result: % who are X role / bought Y in last year]
Past partners and results: [brand, format, result: clicks, code uses, renewals]
Formats and starting rates: [integration from $X], [dedicated from $Y], [Shorts from $Z], [newsletter slot from $W]
Usage, whitelisting and exclusivity: quoted separately, 30-day windows
Contact: [email], [booking link]
Updated: [date]
```

Rules: medians, never the best video; the update date on top; show sponsor renewals because they are the best evidence a list converts; never show a metric you cannot screenshot.

## UGC (content without posting)

UGC is production for the brand's own channels; price it on production plus usage, not on the creator's reach. Operators report UGC priced separately from posting at $500 to $12,000, about $700 average (offers-pricing sibling). Always quote UGC with usage months attached.

## Tricks most creators miss

- Separate "brand may run my video from its account" (paid usage) from "brand may run ads from my handle" (whitelisting). The second carries the creator's identity and costs more.
- Quote CPM and the resulting fee; brands negotiate CPM, and a CPM anchor survives budget pushback better than a flat number.
- Newsletter and podcast sponsors re-book when results are visible: one tracked link and code per placement, and send a results note after each run.
- Every deal is also a case study for the next pitch: ask for permission to cite results before signing.
