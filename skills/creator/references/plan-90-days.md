# 90-day plans by sub-model

Goal for a new or stalled creator business: one repeatable discovery engine, one owned-audience capture, one monetization path with known unit economics (research report, 2026). Not maximum output. Every plan starts with the same two weeks, then splits by sub-model. Each phase ends with an exit check; do not move on until it passes or the owner decides otherwise.

## Days 1 to 14: every creator

1. **Pull data.** Last 20 to 50 posts, videos, issues or episodes. Compute medians by format, never averages. Platform-specific inputs in [video-creators.md](video-creators.md), [newsletter.md](newsletter.md), [podcast.md](podcast.md), [music.md](music.md), [affiliate.md](affiliate.md).
2. **KPI tree.** Discovery (impressions, views) → qualified consumption (retention, completion) → action (profile visit, click, follow) → owned audience (email, membership) → revenue. Mark where the biggest drop is.
3. **Pick one primary money model** using the intent table in [monetization-math.md](monetization-math.md). Write the unit economics with the owner's numbers.
4. **Capture.** One email capture with a context-matched lead magnet ("Get the spreadsheet", "Get the next teardown"), linked from every bio and description. A generic "join my newsletter" is not enough (research report).
5. **Compliance pass.** Disclosure wording, YPP-safe formats, music rights ([disclosure-and-policy.md](disclosure-and-policy.md)).

Exit check: baseline medians written down; one primary model chosen; capture live.

## Video creator (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram)

| Days | Actions | Exit check |
|---|---|---|
| 15 to 30 | YouTube: three genuinely different title/thumbnail promises on 3 evergreen videos with native A/B testing. Instagram: Trial Reels for the riskiest topics. TikTok: one cluster of 5 videos from Creator Search Insights content-gap queries. Rewrite openings: no greeting, promise in 5 to 10 seconds. | At least one test with a clear winner; 30-second retention measured per video |
| 31 to 45 | Outlier mining: 10 videos from small channels beating their size in the niche; adapt 3 topics. Swipe file of 20 clicked thumbnails. One end-screen pitch per video. Link long-form from every Short. | Median CTR or non-follower share above the day-14 baseline |
| 46 to 60 | First monetization: affiliate links on every review or tutorial; media kit with medians; pitch 20 brands that sponsored the niche in the last 30 days (script 1). If YPP-eligible, enable mid-rolls with natural pauses. | 1 paid deal or affiliate EPC known |
| 61 to 75 | Scale the winning format into a series; kill formats below the channel median after 5 attempts. Price deals from views x CPM plus rights ([brand-deals.md](brand-deals.md)). | Kill/scale rule written and applied |
| 76 to 90 | Membership or product test for the most engaged segment; quarterly sponsor package; check RPM by geography and length. | Second income line measurable |

## Newsletter

| Days | Actions | Exit check |
|---|---|---|
| 15 to 30 | Turn on platform recommendations; recommend 3 to 5 adjacent letters. Signup page: one benefit line, no scroll. Welcome flow: deliver asset, best piece, one question, first offer. Substack: 30-minute daily engagement quota, 2 to 6 Notes a day following the statement-first formula. | Source tracking on every signup |
| 31 to 45 | 5 swap partners chosen by growth rate (script 11). Referral program with a near-free first milestone. One test of Meta lead forms at $10 to $20 a day with an audience callout, or a SparkLoop buy, capped. | Cost per subscriber and 2-week open rate by source |
| 46 to 60 | Paid tier only if there is a separate paid job (data, templates, archive, community). Fixed paid day, mid-story paywall, annual default. Launch with a dated founding price. Or sell 2 to 3 direct sponsor slots if over a few thousand engaged readers. | Paid conversion vs 0.62% median; first sponsor or decision not to sell |
| 61 to 75 | Price-increase announcement with grandfathering; conversion emails that sell what is coming. Media kit with median clicks per placement. | Paid conversion rate by source |
| 76 to 90 | Cancel flow and dunning on; list hygiene (sunset non-openers); second revenue line (product, affiliate, sponsor). | Churn and recovery tracked monthly |

## Podcast

| Days | Actions | Exit check |
|---|---|---|
| 15 to 30 | Retitle the last 20 episodes as specific outcomes under 55 characters; keywords in the show name; 15 to 20 second opening. Independent host with own RSS. | Baseline downloads per episode at 7 and 30 days |
| 31 to 45 | Pitch 20 shows for guest spots (script 18), expect 10% replies. 2 trailer or feed swaps with same-niche shows. Clips of 60 to 90 seconds judged on show follows. | 3+ guest spots booked |

| 61 to 75 | Sell 2 direct host reads to niche sponsors at CPM with a floor fee, or a sponsor package for a season. Spotify Partner Program if eligible. | First sponsor or a priced package ready |
| 76 to 90 | Email list via an episode companion; services or product offer for the listener profile. | Revenue per episode tracked |

## Musician

| Days | Actions | Exit check |
|---|---|---|
| 15 to 30 | Own "This Is"-style playlist with all your songs, newest first. Spotify for Artists audience data. Pitch the next single 8 to 10 days early. 2 to 3 TikTok/Reels accounts split by mood, 3 to 5 posts a day each, song as the subject. | Saves per 1,000 views and streams per listener baseline |
| 31 to 45 | Meta conversion campaign: pixel-tracked landing page → own playlist, $5 to $15 a day, judged on cost per new Spotify listener and save rate. Feature.fm or Hypeddit action page that collects email. | Cost per real listener known; email list started |
| 46 to 60 | Release the next single with front-loaded spend; read days 6 to 12 for algorithmic share. Recommendation-list and slideshow formats. DM 20 creators in matching hashtags with a snippet. | Algorithmic share rising; repeat listening above 2.0 |
| 61 to 75 | Shows: 5 to 6 cities on a circuit, QR postcards, talk to every attendee. Merch tied to fans' interests. Ads toward tickets and merch. | Ticket and merch revenue per show |
| 76 to 90 | College radio campaign or sync outreach (20 agencies, supervisors via IMDb and AdWeek). Crowdfunding only after real traction. | Second income line beyond streams |

## Faceless or AI-assisted channel

| Days | Actions | Exit check |
|---|---|---|
| 15 to 30 | QC gate on every planned video ([faceless-ai.md](faceless-ai.md)). Separate any experimental channel from the main business accounts. AI disclosure decisions documented. | No template-identical uploads |
| 31 to 60 | Find 10 outliers in the niche; produce original-thesis versions; test voice and visual styles. Long-form first for money, Shorts for reach. | Median retention at or above niche peers |

## Affiliate site

| Days | Actions | Exit check |
|---|---|---|

| 31 to 45 | Protect money pages: comparison tables, "Best for [situation]" roles, "A vs B" pages, one free tool or comparison sheet. Email capture on every money page. | Email capture rate per page |

## Weekly rhythm after day 90

- Monday: last week's medians vs baseline, one kill/scale decision per active test.
- Tuesday to Thursday: production at the cadence that keeps packaging quality.
- Friday: pipeline (sponsors, swaps, guests), invoices, link audit, disclosure check on everything published.
- Monthly: RPM and EPC by source, paid conversion by source, media kit update, withdraw platform balances.

## Owner questions before day 15

1. What income do you need by when, and how many hours a week can you give?
2. Will you show your face and voice? Which topics are off-limits?
3. Which brands would you never promote? Any existing exclusivity or management contract?
4. Which countries is your audience in, and which do you want?
5. Do you already sell anything (product, service, membership)?
6. Who can approve deals, prices and ad spend?
