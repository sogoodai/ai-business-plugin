# Scripts

Exact wording for recurring creator conversations. Fill the brackets with real numbers only. Never invent a metric, a result, a past partner or personal use of a product. Items marked "verbatim" are quoted from the source; the rest are templates built from the sources in [sources.md](sources.md). Draft only: the owner sends.

## Brand deals

### 1. Sponsor pitch (cold, to a brand that sponsored the niche recently)

Subject: [Product] x [Channel]: [one-line concept]

> Hi [first name],
>
> I saw [Brand] sponsored [similar creator] last month and that you're launching [product or feature]. I run [channel], where [specific audience, e.g. "home bakers who sell at markets"] watch [format].
>
> Median [30-day views / opens / downloads] over my last 10 [videos / issues / episodes]: [number]. [X]% of my audience is in [US/UK/...].
>
> Idea: [one sentence concept that shows the product doing a job for this audience].
>
> I price the organic integration separately from paid usage, so you can test the creative before licensing it longer. Want the one-page concept and forecast?
>
> [Name]

Built from the research report's outreach script and an agency's pitch rules (one product, the idea, the fit, the budget; r/youtubers, 188 pts). Launch angle, verbatim from a 1.8M-subscriber creator: "I saw you're launching product X. I have a format that went viral doing Y. Here's how it would help you with a succesful launch." (r/PartneredYoutube, 798 pts)

### 2. Rate reply (inbound "what are your rates?")

> Thanks for reaching out. For [platform], my fee for an organic [integration / dedicated video / Reel] is $[X], based on a median of [N] views in the first 30 days and the production involved. That covers publication on my account.
>
> Paid usage, whitelisting or Partnership Ads, exclusivity, raw footage and extra platforms are licensed separately. For paid media I usually start with a 30-day license so you can test the creative, at $[Y], with renewals at $[Z] per 30 days.
>
> What's the campaign goal, timing, and do you plan to run the content as ads? That helps me suggest the right package.

Adapted from the research report's commercial response (2026).

### 3. Negotiation counter (budget below the fee)

> I can't include [perpetual / 12 months of] paid usage in the base content fee. Two options that fit $[their budget]:
>
> 1. A shorter integration: [45] seconds, placed after the first third of the video, organic only, no exclusivity.
> 2. The full integration at $[fee], with 30 days of paid social for $[Y]. If the ad meets your performance threshold, renewals are $[Z] per 30 days.
>
> AI training, synthetic voice or likeness, and category exclusivity are excluded unless licensed separately.

Built from the research report counter and an agency's scope-cut rules (r/PartneredYoutube, 213 pts).

### 4. Fee-for-commission trade

> I understand the budget is fixed at $[X]. I can do it at that fee if we add [6]% commission on sales through my code [CODE] for [90] days, tracked in your affiliate dashboard.

Operator source: ask for 5 to 8% on code sales when a fee gets negotiated down (r/PartneredYoutube, 248 pts).

### 5. Usage-rights clarification (contract asks for perpetual or "all media")

> Before I sign, can we change clause [N]? I license paid usage in 30-day windows: [platforms], [countries], from [start] to [end], run from [Brand]'s accounts. Running ads from my handle (whitelisting) is a separate line at $[W] per 30 days. Perpetual rights, AI training and use of my voice or likeness are not included.

### 6. Protecting a ranking video

> That video ranks #1 for [query] on YouTube and Google, so I keep the top of its description clean. Instead I can offer [$fee], a new standalone video on [Product], and a pinned comment with your link on the existing video.

Pattern from a creator whose counter was accepted (r/youtubers, 67 pts).

### 7. Follow-up (no reply, day 5 to 7)

> Hi [first name], one more idea in case timing is better now: [new angle tied to a date: season, launch, event]. Happy to send the forecast. If this isn't a fit this quarter, who should I talk to about [next quarter]?

### 8. Results recap after a campaign (sets up renewal)

> Quick recap for [campaign]: [views at 30 days], [clicks], [code uses or signups], [best comment or question from viewers]. The top-performing moment was [timestamp / slide]. If you want to run it as an ad, the 30-day license is $[Y]. I have [date] open for a follow-up integration.

### 9. Late payment

> Hi [name], invoice [#] for $[X] was due on [date] under Net [30]. Could you confirm the payment date? Per our agreement, [late fee terms] apply after [N] days. Paid-usage rights start once the invoice is settled.

### 10. Declining a bad offer

> Thanks for thinking of me. At [$X] for [deliverables] plus [N] months of exclusivity, I'd be working below my rate, so I'll pass for now. If the scope changes, I'm happy to look again.

## Newsletters

### 11. Newsletter swap request

> Hi [name], I write [Newsletter], [N] [active] readers who are [audience]. Our readers overlap: [one concrete reason]. Would you be up for a recommendation swap? I'd feature [their newsletter] in my [date] issue with a short note on why my readers should read you, and you do the same when it suits. If your list is bigger than mine, I'm happy to run [3] mentions for your [1]. I'll share the signups I send either way.

Rules behind it: pick partners by growth rate, balance by subscribers delivered, 3 for 1 to a list 3x larger (r/Newsletters, 55 pts; r/Substack, 84 pts).

### 12. Newsletter sponsor cold call (verbatim)

> "Hi, my name is X. I run an email newsletter called X we have an engaged community of X people who open our emails every single day/week. We're looking for sponsors that would be a good fit for our audience, and I thought you would be a good fit. Is that something you might be interested in?"

(r/Newsletters, 15 pts; cold calling converted best in that operator's tests.)

### 13. Price increase announcement (free and paid lists)

> On [date], the price of [Newsletter] goes from $[old] to $[new] a [year]. If you're already a paid subscriber, nothing changes: you keep $[old] for as long as you stay. If you've been meaning to upgrade, you can still lock in $[old] until [date, time zone]: [link].

Operator result: grandfathering plus announcing to free readers converted more than any sequence, with zero churn (r/Substack, 109 pts).

### 14. Free-to-paid email (forward-looking)

> Next [weekday], paid readers get [specific upcoming piece: the data, the template, the teardown]. Here's what's in it: [3 bullets]. [One line on why it matters this month]. Join for $[X] a [month/year]: [link].

Rule: sell "what you're about to miss", never "here's what we covered" (r/Substack, 109 pts).

### 15. Referral CTA

> Know one person who'd use this? Send them your link: [link]. 1 referral unlocks [digital asset]; 3 unlocks [premium archive or tool]; 10 unlocks [high-value access]. Your progress: [X/Y].

Research report template; first milestone must cost almost nothing.

### 16. Signup page copy

Benefit line: "We interview NBA athletes to show you their mental preparation before games" (verbatim example, replacing "Your go-to place for NBA interviews"). Button: "Yes, I hate instant coffee." Promise: "Sign up and we will immediately send you 10 resources every marketer must read." (r/Newsletters, 18 pts)

### 17. Substack Note formula

> "I think [position], and here is the thing that happened to me that made me think it." (verbatim structure, r/Substack, 205 pts). Statement, first person, 73 to 100 words, no link.

## Podcasts

### 18. Guest pitch (you as the guest)

> Hi [host], I listened to your episode with [guest] on [topic], especially [specific moment]. I [credential in one line] and could bring [one specific story or data point] on [topic their audience asks about]. Three angles: [1], [2], [3]. I'll share the episode with my [N] [newsletter readers / followers]. Here's a 2-minute clip of me on another show: [link].

### 19. Trailer or feed swap

> Hi [host], we both talk to [audience]. Want to swap trailers in our next episodes, or run a full episode in each other's feed with a short personal intro ("MY SHOW PRESENTS...")? I'd pick [their episode] for my listeners.

Feed-swap label verbatim (r/podcasting, 74 pts).

### 20. Host-read sponsor structure

> This episode is sponsored by [Product]. If you're [specific listener identity] and [specific recurring pain], this is worth your attention. [Product] lets you [measurable outcome] without [common objection]. [True experience: "I've used it for X months for Y" or "I went through their setup this week"]. Go to [URL] and use [CODE] for [offer].

Research report structure plus mandatory disclosure line.

## Music

### 21. Playlist curator pitch (verbatim lines)

> "This would blend nicely between track 4 and 5 - where it gets more moody and introspective."
> "Reminds people of that Orion Sun track you featured - different pocket but similar mood."
> "Also have an instrumental cut if your list leans more ambient."

Structure: what it sounds like, why it fits their list, one link, under three sentences (r/musicmarketing, 93 pts).

### 22. Sync supervisor first email

> Subject: [Show] season [N]: [genre] songs in the vein of [song they used]
> "Here's a playlist of a few songs of mine that are along the lines of what you used in season 1" [link]. I own 100% of master and publishing, one-stop cleared.

Verbatim first line from a music supervisor's advice; link in the first email, follow up after 3 to 7 days with something new (r/musicmarketing, 162 pts).

### 23. Release-day fan DM

> "yo thanks for your answer earlier, here's the new track on spotify, would love to hear your thoughts!"

Sent to everyone who answered a Story poll about where they listen (r/musicmarketing, 198 pts; dash in the original replaced with a comma).

### 24. Booking email to a venue

> Subject: [Date], [City]: [Artist] ([genre], for fans of [X])
> One line on draw: "I can email 30 subscribers when tickets go live" (verbatim, r/musicmarketing, 229 pts), the crowd clip link, phone number.

## Affiliate

### 25. Commission raise request (after 90 days of steady sales)

> Hi [manager], over the last 90 days I've sent [N] customers to [Brand] ([revenue] in sales, [conversion]% from my clicks). I'd like to grow that with [planned content: comparison page, video, email feature]. Could we move my rate from [X]% to [Y]% and set up an exclusive reader code [CODE] for [Z]% off?

Operator results: raises to 30% lifetime after consistent referrals; a custom 10% code listed by coupon sites (r/Affiliatemarketing, 38 and 36 pts).

### 26. Affiliate disclosure (FTC wording)

> "I get commissions for purchases made through links in this post." (FTC Endorsement Guides Q&A, verbatim)

## Video in-content lines

- End screen (verbatim): "check it out, it's on the screen right now!" with one element and a 5 to 10 second pitch for one video (r/PartneredYoutube, 541 pts).
- Next-video bridge (verbatim): "Now that you know how to fix pacing, here's how to write better YouTube titles" (r/NewTubers, 409 pts).
- Membership (verbatim): "Become a member today and get instant access to all my future content. Link below" (r/PartneredYoutube, 170 pts).
- Opening to delete (verbatim anti-pattern): "Hey guys, welcome to the channel, my name is ___, don't forget to like and subscribe, today we'll talk about ___" (r/NewTubers, 811 pts).
- Instagram resource CTA instead of a sales pitch: "comment X to get Y" (r/InstagramMarketing, 223 pts). Avoid "Message me for my new program" in educational content (r/InstagramMarketing, 113 pts).
- Avatar hook (verbatim): "If you're a single mum in your 20s or 30s, who only has 45 minutes to work out, and you're nervous about going to the gym, here's what you need to know" (r/InstagramMarketing, 113 pts).

## Disclosure lines

- Video: say "This video is sponsored by [Brand]" before the segment and show "Ad" or "Sponsored" on screen; also tick the platform's paid-promotion setting.
- Gifted: "[Brand] sent me this for free."
- Instagram or TikTok caption start: "Ad: ..." (UK: "Ad" or "#ad" upfront).
- Livestream: repeat the sponsor line every time the product is mentioned and at regular intervals.
- Newsletter: "Sponsored by [Brand]" above the block.
- France: "Publicité" or "Collaboration commerciale" for the whole content.
