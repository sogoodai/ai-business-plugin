# Podcasts: audio and video shows

Playbook for an independent podcaster or a creator adding a show. The commercial truth: most shows are small, and the money comes from selling a specific listener to a specific sponsor, or from what the show leads to (clients, products, a newsletter), not from network ad scale.

Operators report the same: 7,000 social followers still meant about 80 listeners per episode after 40 episodes; 5,000 total downloads took 18 months (r/podcasting, 42 and 123 pts). Social followers are not listeners. Set the owner's expectations with these numbers in the first session.

## Monetization by size

```
host-read revenue per episode = downloads in 30 days / 1,000 x CPM x ad slots
niche direct deal value       = listeners x share in sponsor's ICP x sponsor's value per qualified prospect
Spotify Partner Program       = 50% of recognized revenue on Spotify-sold ads + Premium video engagement payouts
```

- **Under 1,000 downloads per episode:** do not wait for networks. Sell category access directly: a 500-download show heard by 300 CFOs can be worth more to a B2B software company than 10,000 general downloads (research report inference; Libsyn notes targeted business inventory commands higher CPMs). Also: services and consulting leads, a paid "episode companion", affiliate links in show notes, the show's newsletter.
- **1,000 to 20,000:** direct host reads priced on CPM with a floor fee, dynamic insertion on the back catalog, Spotify Partner Program if eligible (US, Canada, UK, Australia and about 50 other markets in 2026).
- **20,000+:** network or ad-sales partner (Libsyn marketplace, Megaphone, an independent sales house). Operators report a sales partner handling brand deals earned more than self-pitching (r/podcasting, 104 pts).

Podcast media kit specifics: listener job titles and industries (from a listener survey), geography, completion or consumption hours where available, newsletter overlap, past sponsor results (codes redeemed, renewals). Download count alone sells cheap. Template: [brand-deals.md](brand-deals.md).

## Growth: what works, what does not

**Works (operators report)**
- **Guest on other shows.** One host found guesting brought more subscribers than three months of Instagram combined (r/podcasting, 83 pts). Expect at most a 10% reply rate on pitches (r/podcasting, 123 pts).
- **Trailer swaps and feed swaps with same-niche shows** "worked better than social" (r/podcasting, 113 pts). Feed swap label: "MY SHOW PRESENTS..." with a personal intro (r/podcasting, 74 pts). Choose swap partners by audience fit, not size.
- **Specific-outcome titles.** "How we grew organic traffic 300% in 6 months" style titles averaged 2.3x the downloads of "marketing insights with [name]"; a title rewrite raised average downloads 25% (80-episode analysis, r/podcasting, 83 pts). Titles under 55 characters with a long-tail phrase; guest name only if people search it.
- **Keywords in the show name.** "80s, 90s, 00s Pop Culture Podcast: Be Kind Let's Rewind" "helped us a ton organically" (r/podcasting, 41 pts). Search your own show in Apple and Pocket Casts to check findability.
- **Overcast category ads** are the only consistently cheap paid channel reported: $0.63 per subscriber (true crime), $1.43 (business), about $2 platform-wide, rising to $2.62 on repeat runs, so pause months between runs (r/podcasting, 249, 138, 58 pts). Comedy in leisure: $5 per subscriber (r/podcasting, 112 pts).
- **Release day matters for workweek audiences:** Tuesday and Wednesday about 30% better than Friday and weekend in one B2B show (r/podcasting, 83 pts).
- **Clips:** 60 to 90 second clips on LinkedIn got 3x the engagement of 3 to 5 minute clips (r/podcasting, 83 pts). Self-contained 60-second clips reach 5,000 to 10,000 for shows under 500 listeners, but clip viewers are their own audience (r/podcasting, 74 pts); a comedy show got almost no subscriptions from clips (r/podcasting, 112 pts). Judge clips on follows of the show, not views.
- **Episode email list:** a gated "Episode Companion" (summary, steps, resources) and new-episode emails twice a week built a 5,000-person list (r/podcasting, 138 pts).

**Does not work (operators report)**
- **Counting on guests to share.** Across 40+ guests only about 3 drove visible traffic (30 to 60 extra first-week downloads); guest follower count had zero correlation with downloads across 80 episodes; the top 3 episodes had guests under 5,000 followers (r/podcasting, 82 and 83 pts). Exception: on YouTube video shows, a big guest in the thumbnail can move a 4,000-view show to 25,000.
- **Spotify audio ads:** $315 for 5 new listeners, about $63 each (r/podcasting, 112 pts). **Podcast Addict banner:** $100 per subscriber. **Meta:** $12.50 per download (r/podcasting, 83 pts).
- **Inbound PR guest pitches** produced guests that needed heavy editing; invite directly (r/podcasting, 82 pts).

## Video podcasts and YouTube

- YouTube is a podcast platform now; a video show is searchable and earns AdSense. Operators report video episodes over 90 minutes average far more listeners than under 20 minutes (MillionPodcasts report, 34,000 shows, 2026, correlation). RSS-ingested audio-only on YouTube grows "very slowly" (r/podcasting, 104 pts).
- Use separate titles for YouTube (packaging rules in [video-creators.md](video-creators.md)) and for audio apps (search phrase).
- A paid big-name guest ($3,000) took one channel from about 350 to 2,000 to 3,000 subscribers and into YPP, earning about $4,000 after (r/podcasting, 38 pts). Treat as a launch tactic, not a habit.

## Length and format

Evidence conflicts: 15 to 25 minutes solo, 25 to 35 interviews (r/podcasting, 185 pts); B2B interviews completing best at 35 to 50 minutes with a sharp drop around minute 45 past 60 (r/podcasting, 83 pts); video over 90 minutes doing best. Rule: start with the shortest length that delivers the episode promise, then read completion curves per format. Open with a 15 to 20 second who-and-what intro; one show's content started at minute 6:30 and had not grown in 4 years (r/podcasting, 178 and 41 pts).

## Sponsor read

Structure (research report): "If you're [specific listener identity] and [specific recurring pain], this is worth your attention. [Product] lets you [measurable outcome] without [common objection]. I used it for [true context]. Go to [URL or code] and [specific action]."

Rules: disclose before the read ("This episode is sponsored by..."); never fabricate personal use; use a unique code and URL per show so the sponsor sees results; leave clean edit points so a dynamic or baked read can be swapped.

## Risk

- **Do not host your RSS on a platform that can delete it.** Spotify for Creators bans music tracks and DJ mixes "regardless of licensing"; one episode with 10 self-made AI tracks got a 6-year, 168-episode show and its RSS deleted (r/podcasting, 428 pts). Use an independent host and keep the feed URL under your control.
- Posting 3 to 4 episodes a week caused burnout with no interaction gains; one host cut to 2 to 3 a month (r/podcasting, 383 pts).
- Verify any ad app's claimed cost per subscriber in your host's analytics.

## Tricks most podcasters miss

- A real new-listener signal is plays rising across the previous 15 episodes, not just the latest (r/podcasting, 106 pts).
- Publish late by even an hour and analytics can suffer for weeks; schedule releases (r/podcasting, 181 pts).
- Book guests through referrals at release: "who in your network would fit the show?" after a gift and a good experience. Pitch big names right after publishing a recognizable peer (r/podcasting, 138 pts).
- Put the newsletter CTA in the show's own currency: "if you want to be a guest like [Speaker], subscribe to our newsletter where we'll tell you how" (r/podcasting, 113 pts).
