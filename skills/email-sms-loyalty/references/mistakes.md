# Mistakes: what they cost, what to do instead

## From the data

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Buying traffic while welcome, cart and post-visit flows are missing | Flows are 5.3% of sends and about 41% of email revenue (Klaviyo 2026); automated $3.41 vs $0.155 per email (Omnisend 2025) | Build the four triggers before raising ad spend or newsletter frequency |
| Weekly blasts as the restaurant CRM | Blasts brought back 0.27% of recipients vs 7.2% for welcome (Bloom 2025 to 2026) | Welcome, second-visit, lapse and milestone triggers first |
| Treating SMS as cheap email | Campaign SMS $0.55 EPM at p75 vs $10.95 for cart and $13.34 for back-in-stock (Postscript 2026); opt-outs burn a channel you can't rebuild | Texts for intent and time-sensitive inventory; broadcast SMS 2 to 4 a month max |
| Discounting before diagnosing | Back-in-stock converts 6.72% with no discount (Omnisend 2025); win-back at 10% off showed 26% rebooking with no control (Mangomint 2026) | Test reminder, timing and proof against the discount; discount only the residual group |
| Optimizing popups on raw signups | Discount popups move full-price buyers onto codes; about 25% of signups are repeat claimers (operator) | Judge on incremental contribution per 1,000 visitors; dedupe; test value exchange |
| Quoting open-rate folklore | Apple MPP inflates opens; WhatsApp measured 79.6%, not 98% (Chatarmin 2026) | Rank on clicks, replies, bookings and revenue |
| Letting stale contacts in | 0.3% complaints is Gmail's and Yahoo's ceiling: 30 complaints per 10,000 | Suppress on clicks and purchases, sunset, re-permission old lists in small batches |
| Using WhatsApp utility templates for promotions | Meta reclassifies or pauses; Brazil marketing $0.0625 vs utility $0.0068 tempts it (Oct 2026 rates) | Utility only for real transactions; earn attention with them, then a separate marketing template |
| Importing phones and emails without consent provenance | CASL and the Spam Act put the proof burden on the sender; TCPA $500 to $1,500 per text | Store source, timestamp, wording and channel; fail closed |
| Hard-coding "9 pm" for US texts | Several states end at 8 pm (Twilio toolkit 2025) | Recipient local time, strictest rule; default 9 am to 8 pm |
| Counting attributed loyalty revenue as incremental | Members return near 30% vs 7% but chose to join (Toast 2026) | 10 to 15% holdout (Paytronix) |
| Loyalty first reward too far away | More than half of diners quit programs because rewards were too hard to earn (Toast 2026) | First reward inside one or two normal visit cycles |
| Comparing RPR across vendors or AOV bands | $2.39 p25 vs $16.40 p75 cart RPR is just AOV ($83 vs $291 bands, Klaviyo 2026) | Use the row for your revenue band and AOV |

## From operators

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Annual memberships without dunning | About 23% of a gym's annual members stopped paying on failed cards within months (1,353-pt thread) | Card-expiry warnings, smart retries, day 0/3/7/10 emails, no-login update link |
| Flat-dollar cart coupon left running | A $5-off code built for $30+ products was used on a $6 item two years later (operators report) | Percent codes with minimums; audit codes when the catalog changes |
| Reusable thank-you code | Coupon extensions spread it to first-time buyers (47-pt comment) | Unique single-use codes |
| Marketplace cart coupons on autopilot | Etsy sent them within an hour, to shoppers still browsing (21-pt and 12-pt comments) | Only if margin absorbs it on every item |
| Guilt-trip reactivation | "You made Duolingo sad" lost a long-time user (32-pt comment) | Acknowledge the gap, ask what happened, no pitch |
| Broadcasting one client's problem | A graphic hygiene newsletter drew "I'd stop coming" replies (14-pt and 10-pt comments) | Handle the individual privately |
| Punch-card fine print | A $25 to $30, four-times-a-week regular never returned after "extra protein doesn't count" at first redemption (6-pt comment) | Redeem the usual order |
| Unanswered complaint email | A years-long delivery regular sent a photo of mold, got no reply and never ordered again (13-pt comment) | Reply within a day even when no refund is asked |
| Discount justified by future referrals | Referred clients expected the same 25% off and were the same kind of client (300-pt comment) | Referral rewards on paid conversion, never pre-paid with a discount |
| Not telling regulars about a move | Clients tracked the therapist down upset (5-pt comment) | Notify before the move, through every channel you're allowed to use |
| App-based refer-a-friend with a generic 15% | "Little response" for a subscription box (154-pt thread) | Personal founder ask at a milestone, gift to a named friend |
| Slow fulfillment killing repeat | China shipping times killed repeat business for a dropshipper (257-pt thread) | Fix delivery before building retention flows; no email fixes a bad second experience |
| Email signup at the bottom of the page | Seen by 10% of visitors (132-pt thread) | Place it where 50%+ scroll; signups tripled |
| Template text left in the signup form | "[yoursitrname]" in the newsletter box made the store look like a scam (3-pt comment) | Preview every form and footer before paid traffic |
| Lead sources faster than the team can answer | Extra spend lowers booked-job economics (research 2026) | Alert when unanswered inquiries pile up; fix response time first |
