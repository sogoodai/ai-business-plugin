# By niche: what changes

The mechanics in this skill are shared. What changes is the clock (how often people come back), the trigger that matters most and the channel people answer. Segment skills hold the deeper angles; this file keeps the messaging consistent with them.

## Beauty and wellness (salons, barbers, lash, nails, massage, med spa)

- **Clock:** service cadence. Blended inter-visit gap about 63 days (Mangomint 2026) hides a 3-week barber, a 2 to 3-week lash fill and a 6 to 8-week color. Lapse = expected next visit + grace, or 2x the client's own gap.
- **Top triggers:** reminders (transactional), post-visit review plus rebook, due-soon with two named slots, gap fill to established clients, membership renewal and usage.
- **Numbers:** Phorest users 44% open, 1.7% click, 4.2% click-to-book (2025 data). Vagaro: 60%+ rebooking healthy, under 40% warning.
- **Channel:** SMS and booking-app messages for anything this week; email for news and education.
- **Never:** treat the booking phone number as marketing consent; discount the whole list for two empty slots. Depth on deposits, rebooking and memberships: `beauty-business`.

## Restaurants, cafes, bakeries, food trucks

- **Clock:** coffee and bakery in days, QSR one to two weeks, casual dining weeks, fine dining months, catering buyers on their meeting cycle. Measure from POS.
- **Top triggers:** signup welcome with a first-return reward, second-visit nudge, lapse by rhythm, visit-four milestone, birthday (email 14 days before, SMS 7 days before, email on the day; ai-restaurant-claude, MIT).
- **Numbers:** Bloom 2025 to 2026 return rates: blast 0.27%, welcome 7.2%, win-back 6.5%, milestone 19.8%, post-visit 29.9%. Toast: loyalty near 30% return vs 7%.
- **Caps:** one promotional email a week, 2 SMS a month (ai-restaurant-claude, MIT), consistent with the `restaurant` skill.
- **Capture:** online ordering, reservations, Wi-Fi, receipt and table-tent QR with a job. Catering buyer is a separate persona with a "next meeting" reminder; 43% of organizations ran recurring meal programs in 2025 (ezCater).
- Depth on reward types and recognizing regulars: `restaurant`.

## Home and local services (cleaning, HVAC, plumbing, lawn, pest, detailing, repair)

- **Clock:** seasonal and maintenance intervals (HVAC twice a year, gutters seasonal, cleaning biweekly). Many customers buy once; the play is a maintenance plan or route density, not a newsletter.
- **Top triggers:** missed-call text, quote follow-up (same day, 24 h, day 3, day 7, day 14, day 30), post-job review request (Jobber defaults: reminders day 3 and 5, suppress 6 months), seasonal service-due reminder, maintenance-plan renewal.
- **Numbers:** no sourced email benchmark. 20% of pros reply within an hour while more than half of customers expect it (Jobber 2026). Referrals and repeat work each a top source for 59%.
- **Tricks:** sticker on the equipment with the next service date; photo of wear with a replacement timeline; seasonal customers as the list for the next season's add-on (50 to 60 gutter customers became the pressure-washing list, 200-pt thread); a commercial cleaner replies to every client email within the hour and visits the same day for critical issues (428-pt thread); another lost 2 of 101 clients in a year by inspecting buildings and staying in contact (1,990-pt thread).
- **Referral fees:** default to client benefits or reciprocal introductions in licensed trades.

## Education and coaching (tutors, music and language teachers, coaches, course creators)

- **Clock:** packages, terms and exam seasons. Planned completion (exam passed) is not churn.
- **Top triggers:** lead magnet or 5-day mini-course ending in a discovery call; trial or assessment follow-up with a summary and worksheet the same day; progress report every 3 to 5 sessions (baseline, current evidence, next target, parent action, next measurement date); next package offered before the current one ends; graduation into a testimonial and referral ask.
- **Numbers:** Mailchimp education 35.64% open, 3.02% click, 0.18% unsub. Only 6% of music-school cancellations cite cost (Opus1 2026).
- **Channel:** standard text is the only channel some coaching clients answer (3-pt comment); WhatsApp and WeChat groups for tutors abroad.
- **Parents are the buyer:** a phone number from an intake form is not marketing consent. Depth: `education-coaching`.

## Small ecommerce (Shopify, Etsy, Amazon, TikTok Shop, DTC)

- **Clock:** the product's reorder interval. Consumables 30 to 60 days; apparel 60 to 90; home goods 90 to 180 (finsilabs, MIT). 365-day repeat 16.3% median for stores under $1M (By the Numbers 2026).
- **Top triggers:** welcome, checkout and cart, browse, post-purchase, back-in-stock, replenishment, win-back, sunset. Back-in-stock is the most under-used (0.6% of brands, Omnisend 2025).
- **Numbers:** see [benchmarks.md](benchmarks.md); always the row for your revenue band and AOV.
- **Marketplaces:** buyer contact is restricted; use inserts and an owned site. Etsy's own abandoned-cart coupons go out within an hour.
- **Wholesale:** reorder prompts before the ~45-day cycle; push accounts to a fifth order (Faire 2026).
- Depth on store CRO, BFCM offers and marketplaces: `ecommerce-brand`.

## Choosing the main channel by country

| Market | Default marketing channel | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| US, Canada | Email + SMS | 10DLC, TCPA quiet hours; WhatsApp marketing templates limited to US numbers |
| UK | Email + SMS | PECR soft opt-in for own similar products |
| Germany, Netherlands | Email (double opt-in) | WhatsApp marketing is the most expensive in Europe ($0.1365 in Germany) |
| Spain, Italy, France | Email + WhatsApp | France SMS filtered outside 08:00 to 21:30 Mon to Sat |
| Brazil, Mexico, rest of LatAm | WhatsApp | Booking and sales happen in chat |
| India, Indonesia | WhatsApp | Cheapest marketing rates; template quality is the constraint |
| Australia | Email + SMS | Inferred consent from one purchase generally not enough (ACMA 2026) |
