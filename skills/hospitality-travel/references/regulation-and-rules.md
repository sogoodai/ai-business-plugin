# Regulation and platform rules

Not legal advice. These are the checks an agent runs and the questions it routes to the owner or a local adviser. Verify the current rule for each address before acting. Sources in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Short-term rental rules are a channel risk

Platforms enforce registration themselves. A missing or expired number can block inventory even when demand is strong. Keep each listing's registration number, expiry date, night caps and principal-residence status in the same system as channel logins, and recheck on a schedule.

| Jurisdiction | Rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| European Union | Regulation (EU) 2024/1028 harmonizes STR registration procedures and platform data sharing where member states run registration schemes | Applies from 20 May 2026 |
| England | National short-term let registration scheme expected to start March 2027; Furnished Holiday Let tax regime abolished April 2025 | Not in force as of November 2026 (GOV.UK, updated 2 October 2026) |
| British Columbia | STRs limited in many communities to a principal residence plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit; municipalities can be stricter | In force |
| New South Wales | Statewide STR register: A$65 new registration, A$25 annual renewal; non-hosted STRs capped at 180 days a year in Greater Sydney and some named areas; bookings of 21+ consecutive days excluded from the cap | In force |
| US cities | Local rules vary (permits, caps, primary residence); a host cancelled a year-old booking two weeks out because "the city has asked me to cancel my bookings" (r/vrbo 779) | Check per address |

Agent checklist per address: is STR allowed for this property type, is a permit or registration number required on listings, night caps and how long stays count, occupancy limits, taxes collected by platforms vs by host, renewal date.

## Review rules

- No incentives or discounts for reviews, no gating, no asking for specific content or star counts. US FTC rule on fake reviews since 21 October 2024; UK enforcement with fines since 6 April 2025; Google's own rule is stricter (see `local-seo`).
- Airbnb: reviews tied to extortion or threats can be removed; keep the evidence in Airbnb messages.
- Tripadvisor allows neutral asks; bans employee incentives.

## Platform conduct rules that bite

- **Off-platform:** do not take payment or move a live OTA reservation off-platform. Use only consented, post-stay contact for direct marketing.
- **Entering the unit:** on Airbnb a host needed each guest's written acceptance for mid-stay cleaning entry, even when disclosed (r/airbnb_hosts 4,143). Get written permission per visit.
- **Parties:** the platform toggle alone was ruled insufficient; write it in house rules (r/airbnb_hosts 1,755).
- **Cancellations:** host cancellations hit Superhost, Vrbo Premier Host and minimum standards, plus fees ($119 Vrbo invoice, r/vrbo 316).
- **Amenities and accuracy:** platforms refund guests for missing or broken advertised amenities; Vrbo refunded about two-thirds of a stay where the pool, laundry and hot water failed (r/vrbo 241).
- **Booking.com EEA:** contractual parity removed July 2024; external prices not used for default ranking or Preferred and Genius eligibility in the EEA; from September 2026 not for Booking Sponsored Benefit eligibility.

## Marketing consent

- An OTA booking does not grant marketing consent. Capture consent yourself at check-in, on Wi-Fi or by QR, with a record of source and date.
- SMS and WhatsApp need separate consent and quiet hours. Depth: `email-sms-loyalty`.

## Pricing honesty

- Show mandatory fees in the total. Several jurisdictions regulate drip pricing; the safe default is a total that does not change between search and checkout for the searched guest count.
- Honor booked rates. Asking a guest to pay more after booking, or adding new "host fees" after payment, led to platform intervention and guests leaving for hotels (r/vrbo 261 and 412).

## Tours and advisors

- Tours: platform quality limits (GetYourGuide at or under 1.0% cancellations, 0.2% no-shows) act like rules. Check local guiding licenses and permits for protected sites.
- Advisors: seller-of-travel registration exists in some US states; update terms and conditions with a travel lawyer (practitioners point new agents to a known travel-industry lawyer's webinars, r/travelagents 57). National licensure is debated (r/travelagents 55); check the current state.
