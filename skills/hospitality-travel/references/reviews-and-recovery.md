# Reviews, badges and service recovery

Reviews decide badges, badges decide visibility and click-through, and one bad stay handled well often ends as 4 or 5 stars. Google review law and replies in general: `local-seo`. Sources in [sources.md](sources.md).

## The thresholds to manage toward

| Program | Gate | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| Airbnb Guest Favorite | 5+ reviews in 4 years, 1+ in 2 years; daily evaluation on ratings, category scores, cancellations, quality incidents, review-removal rate, communication | Listing-level badge in search; top 1%, 5%, 10% labels |
| Airbnb Superhost | 4.8+, under 1% cancellations, 90%+ response in 24 h, 10 stays or 3 totaling 100 nights; quarterly | Account badge and filter |
| Vrbo Premier Host | 4.6+, 99% acceptance, 0% host cancellations, 5+ Vrbo reviews, 5 bookings or 60 nights | Automatic search-position increase |
| Vrbo minimum standards | Over 2.0 rating, over 50% acceptance, under 10% cancellations | Staying listed |
| Tripadvisor Popularity | Quality, recency, enough quantity | Local rank; recent reviews weigh more |
| GetYourGuide | First 3 reviews (3x booking likelihood), recent performance | Ranking and conversion |

Agent checks each month: rating by category (cleanliness, accuracy, check-in, communication, location, value), reviews in the last 90 days, cancellations in the window, response rate. Name the one category that is lowest and fix that first.

Note the fragility: one Top 1% listing lost the label after two 4-star reviews (r/airbnb_hosts 680); another lost it over Accuracy and Value 4s from guests who could not find cookware stored under the stove, despite free early check-in and late checkout (r/airbnb_hosts 441, 2026). Freebies do not protect ratings; accurate expectations do.

## Asking for reviews

- Same neutral ask to every guest, on checkout day, after any known issue is resolved. Airbnb recommends a scheduled checkout message; both sides have 14 days.
- Wording (research pass 2026):

> Thanks again for staying with us. Airbnb has opened the review window today. If the stay matched what you expected, we'd really appreciate an honest review, especially anything future guests should know about the cleanliness, check-in, accuracy or location. If anything fell short, please message us directly as well so we can fix it.

- Keep the automated message free of praise for the guest ("you were wonderful guests"), so it remains sendable after a bad stay (r/airbnb_hosts 1,690).
- Do not ask for 5 stars, and never imply "anything under 5 is a failure"; a 60-upvote comment suggested exactly that to a returning guest; it pressures and breaks Google's and the FTC's spirit. Ask for honest feedback.
- Do not count on gratitude: a host refunded a $700+ strict-policy booking for guests denied entry at the border and got no review after three requests (r/airbnb_hosts 442).
- Tours: hand-off moment at the end of the experience plus the platform's own request. Use GetYourGuide Creator Reviews for a new product (labeled, typically within 48 hours).
- Hotels and B&Bs: the neutral Google request after checkout; Tripadvisor allows neutral asks; never use review kiosks or staff quotas (`local-seo`).

## Never trade money for reviews

- A guest asked for a refund of 16 unused nights in exchange for not writing a bad review. The host screenshotted it and called Airbnb next morning; support said the review would be removed if posted (r/airbnb_hosts 435).
- One host paid $100 extra on top of a $150 AC refund because it was cheaper than fighting a threatened review, and got 5 stars (r/airbnb_hosts 723). The agent must not recommend this: it is a paid review in effect and invites repeats. Recommend: screenshot, report, offer only the remedy the issue deserves.
- Refunding to prevent a bad review fails: full refund over disclosed cats, still 2 stars (later removed) (r/airbnb_hosts 905).

## Replying to reviews

- **Short, factual, for future guests.** Suggested reply to a hostile review (52 upvotes): "We go to a great deal of effort to make sure our guests have a great experience and build incredible memories. Regrettably, it seems we could not please [name]. We ask potential guests to look at our long series of happy, 5-star reviews and make up your own mind."
- **Amenity complaints:** state what is provided and the price positioning (suggested wording, 7 upvotes; 29 and 28-upvote comments agreed public clarification helps): "thank you for the comments. We do provide quality Starbucks coffee just not in the Keurig format to reduce plastic waste. Since we are a high value property focused on affordable rates, we have chosen not to offer other food or gift basket type items. Some of our competitors who charge more do offer these items. Had you contacted me with your concerns during your stay, I may have been able to address them and improve your satisfaction."
- **Unfair 3-star on a homestay:** a host politely listed disclosed exclusions and the free extras given (early check-in, parking, grocery run, a home doctor), and the guests asked Airbnb to remove their own review (r/airbnb_hosts 829). Firm facts work; sarcasm does not.
- **A 5-star with one alarming line** buried mid-paragraph: replying may spotlight it; a 31-upvote comment advised no reply, a 56-upvote one a brief thank-you. Default: brief thank-you that does not repeat the alarming phrase.

## Getting reviews removed (Airbnb)

- Frame around facts and policy, not feelings. A guest who brought 10 people against an 8 limit threatened a "trash ass review"; the first request ("I feel it is retaliatory") failed, the second with screenshots of the threat, the in-app enforcement messages and the policy succeeded (r/airbnb_hosts 1,128; 70 and 72-upvote comments).
- Call instead of chat, and escalate: one host needed the third support level (5 upvotes); another removal followed a call (20 upvotes).
- A host reports a review was removed because it mentioned the price paid (r/airbnb_hosts 2,829, host's account, not verified policy). Worth asking about; the star ratings stayed.
- Disputes: ask for photos of alleged problems in Airbnb messages and offer to walk through them (11 upvotes). Document a refund already given and the guest's completed stay when Airbnb adds a second payout to the guest; one host got most of it reversed (r/airbnb_hosts 594).

## Service recovery that saves the stay

Patterns that worked or at least kept the booking:

| Problem | Recovery | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Coffee machine died after check-in | Took orders, delivered Dunkin' coffee ($15), replacement same day | Host credits this style for many 5-star reviews (r/airbnb_hosts 415) |
| Cleaners skipped the unit, 2 am | Immediate reply, offered cleaning fee plus 15%, hotel, spare sheets; next morning full refund, breakfast offer, pastries and a handwritten note | 4 stars overall, 2 for cleaning, glowing text with no negatives (r/airbnb_hosts 410) |
| Odor at check-in | Bought the group dinner ($100) while treating the unit; ozone treatment about $250 | Booking saved (r/AirBnB, 71 upvotes) |
| Hair in the drain | Refunded the cleaning fee | Guest happy and apologized (r/AirBnB 237) |
| Turnover emergency, missed 3 pm | Explained, offered cancel or stay with partial refund | Guests stayed (r/airbnb_hosts 782) |
| Appliance swap mid-stay | Offer drinks at a nearby bar while it happens | Not tested (guest escalated first) |
| Hotel guest downgraded on a sold-out night | Contact before they complain, move next day, comp dinner, breakfast or valet | Practice of a former front-desk manager (52 upvotes) |

Rules: respond within the hour, fix first, offer a proportionate credit in writing inside the platform, and follow up personally. Do not let an unapproved early arrival push out the cleaner: a guest who did that later rated hygiene 3 stars (r/airbnb_hosts 418).

## Refunds and cancellations without losing the review or the money

- **Refund only what rebooks,** after the replacement stay completes, at equal value. Scripts in [scripts.md](scripts.md). Guests often keep the booking once offered this (r/airbnb_hosts 1,151; r/bedandbreakfast 467).
- **Never cancel on the guest's behalf.** Ask them to cancel themselves ("Regretfully, I am unable to cancel your booking. For questions, please refer to the booking conditions and terms of service.", 14 upvotes); a self-described Airbnb team lead warned that telling a guest to cancel can be logged as host-caused (23 upvotes, disputed).
- **Early departure over a holiday:** keep the policy, refund the night if it rebooks, else offer 50% off one future night (r/airbnb_hosts 1,753).
- **Weather:** offer the cleaning fee plus any rebooked nights, or shift dates to better forecast days (r/airbnb_hosts 1,151; the guest kept the booking).
- **Gift certificates for resold nights** (B&B): "We will give you a gift certificate for days that we resell that is good for a year." OTA guests cancel through the OTA (r/bedandbreakfast 467).
- **Card-testing and refund scams:** callers booking large blocks for 10+ days, pushing to pay, then cancelling to refund another card. Say before charging that refunds go only to the original card and a 3% fee is retained; the caller hung up (r/bedandbreakfast 39, about $6,000 attempted).

Tricks most owners miss: the lowest category score, not the overall rating, is what moves Guest Favorite; communication happens to be a scored input, so slow replies cost stars that cleanliness cannot buy back; and a written threat in Airbnb messages is the single best tool for removing a retaliatory review, so keep all contact on-platform.
