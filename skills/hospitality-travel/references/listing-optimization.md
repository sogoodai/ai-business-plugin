# Listing optimization: photos, copy, disclosures, amenities, checkout

Goal: more clicks from search, more bookings per view, and fewer "not as described" refunds and reviews. Sources in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Audit order

1. Read the listing as a guest on a phone, from search to checkout, for the most common stay. Write down the total at each step.
2. Compare the cover photo and total price with the top 10 comparable listings for the same dates.
3. Check every claim against reality: walking times on Google Maps, amenities working today, bed counts, photos matching current rooms.
4. List what a guest could be surprised by after booking. Each item goes into the description, the house rules and the post-booking message.
5. Check auto-generated platform text (highlights, badges, translated text).

## Photos

Evidence: Airbnb's photography program cohort earned 21% more and booked 19% more over 365 days (14,700+ listings, 2024 to 2025, observational). Top photo-quality quartile listings earned 31% more than the bottom quartile and had a median 35 photos vs 25 (Surge, 3,103 listings, 2026). Cover-photo shot scale changes click intention (peer-reviewed, 2025).

- **Cover:** the single strongest differentiator readable at thumbnail size (view, hot tub, design, space). Test cover changes one at a time and judge on click-through for 2 to 4 weeks.
- **First five:** hero space, best bedroom, strongest amenity, exterior or location context, living and social space. These must sell the stay without text.
- **Every room guests use,** including bathrooms. Outdated photos (a bathroom with two windows that no longer exist) led to a full refund plus a free night offered by Airbnb (r/AirBnB 181).
- **Exterior and views:** privacy-conscious guests match rooflines on satellite view and Street View, and skip listings they cannot identify (r/airbnb_hosts, 23-upvote comment). A listing with all blinds drawn lost a booking to the house down the street (78 upvotes).
- **Honest constraints:** stairs, small bathrooms, odd sleeping layouts, parking. These photos prevent "reality check" guest photos, which are associated with a 10.4% performance decline (2025 study).
- **No attraction galleries.** "19 photos, mostly monkeys, waterfalls... I don't even know if the property had a toilet" (r/AirBnB 675). One or two location shots, clearly captioned with distance.
- **Captions and amenity fields** carry the differentiators; Google Vacation Rentals reorders images by query, so tag amenity photos clearly.
- **New amenity relaunch:** after adding a hot tub, a BC host added photos and the keyword to the title the same day, then raised summer rates; February to April went nearly full (r/AirBnB 226, 2022). Before buying, check whether nearby listings with that amenity fill their off-season calendars.

## Copy

Write for decision uncertainty, not poetry. Opening template (research pass 2026, recommended procedure):

> Walk to [specific demand generator] in [X minutes]. Sleeps [N] in [actual bed configuration], with [2 to 3 highest-value differentiators]. Best for [segment]. Note before booking: [material constraint].

Lead with friction removers when you have them. One host's first lines (r/AirBnB):

> A tidy private apartment in a multi unit building. Super low cleaning fee ($25), and no list of chores to check out!

Rules:

- **Walking times verified on Google Maps.** An "8-minute walk to the beach" that was 22 to 24 minutes cost a 3-star review on a $2,500 four-night stay; the host then rewrote it as an 8-minute drive (r/AirBnB 222).
- **Seclusion claims must survive a weekday.** Shared driveways, a gardener with a leaf blower six days a week, the advertised pool belonging to the main house: guests left and were refunded (r/airbnb_hosts 725 thread, 37 upvotes).
- **Host proximity in plain words, twice:** "Host lives in house 30 meters away. You will be left completely alone except during routine pool maintenance, etc." (23 upvotes). Repeat it in check-in instructions because guests skim. Stop yard work and projects while guests are there (16 upvotes).
- **Neighborhood, neutrally.** A Quebec listing said roughly: "all guests should know that we are proud to be in an economically and socially diverse area. To that end, you may see folks around the neighborhood who are here using local social services, as well as others visiting local coffee shops and businesses" (215-upvote comment). For a rural place: dogs barking, farm noise. A guest left 4 nights into 14 over a neighbor's dog; the host refunded 10 nights against a 50% policy (r/airbnb_hosts 475).
- **Sleeping spaces, not just "sleeps N."** "Sleeps 5" with two queens and a $15-per-night extra plus guest-supplied bed led to a dispute (r/vrbo 238).
- **Pets in the home:** say where they can and cannot go and enforce it; an allergic guest booked because the cat rules were clear (51 upvotes).
- **Offsite amenities:** "community pool, 0.4 miles" not "pool."
- **Required confirmation for hard limits:** one host opens with "please read the entire listing before booking," then asks confirmation questions about known issues before accepting.

## Disclose before booking, or do not charge it

Each of these produced a refund, a bad review or a guest who switched to hotels:

| Item | What happened |
|---|---|
| Checkout chores | $600-a-night house revealed laundry, bin hauling and hot-tub chemicals a week before arrival (r/airbnb_hosts 1,779) |
| Hot tub surcharge | $50 a night plus 48-hour notice, buried in house rules; guest moved and left 1 star (r/AirBnB 74) |
| AC extra | Discovered after arrival on a $3,000 week (r/AirBnB 64) |
| ID upload on a third-party site | Announced 24 hours before check-in (r/AirBnB 64) |
| Cot fee | $25 charged for an unfolded, unused cot; 2-star review (r/AirBnB 103) |
| Sofa-bed fee forgotten, bed locked | Airbnb refunded the rest of a 7-night stay plus 30% of nights used and a $90 credit (r/airbnb_hosts 650) |
| Contract after the refund deadline | Notarized signatures, 8 am checkout, pool capped at 78°F; guest cancelled and booked elsewhere (r/vrbo 443) |
| Broken hot tub not mentioned | Broken since April, guest learned before a July birthday stay (r/AirBnB 375); a hotel guest chose the hotel for its hot tub and posted negative reviews (r/hotels 169) |
| Construction during stay | "Big back yard" in the title, yard and driveway lost mid-month; 30% refund, $1,500 (r/AirBnB 103) |

Rule for the agent: when an amenity breaks, message every booked guest within 24 hours, update the listing, and offer a choice (cancel with full refund, or stay with a stated credit).

## Fees and total price

- Airbnb ranks total price against local comparables (2026). A cheap nightly rate with a big cleaning fee is uncompetitive in search, not just at checkout.
- A Google result at $77 a night that reached $329 at checkout ($193 cleaning) sent the guest to a Holiday Inn for good (r/vrbo 3,071; a 152-upvote comment said the same).
- Per-guest fees must show in the quote for that guest count from search, not appear at checkout.
- Fold token fees into the rate ($4 cleaning on a $45 room, 43 upvotes).

## Checkout instructions

Strong agreement across threads: short checkout lists win reviews when a cleaning fee is charged.

- "1. go outside, take a deep breath and look at the beauty of nature. 2. remember this moment. 3. head out. We got this. You paid a cleaning fee." Review: "Best. Checkout. Instructions. Ever!!!" (r/airbnb_hosts 3,808; a guest with a $1,000 to $2,000 nightly budget wanted a filter for hosts like this, 251 upvotes).
- "Please lock the front door on your way out." and "Have a safe trip home!" (r/vrbo, 148 upvotes).
- "nothing other than return our key to the key safe, grab your stuff, and please stay again." (134 upvotes)
- "turn off lights, lock the door, key back in lockbox and have a safe trip home" (54 upvotes)
- Optional asks that help cleaners: start the dishwasher if full, send a note when you leave.
- Keep 2 to 3 full linen sets per bed so turnovers never depend on guests starting laundry (r/airbnb_hosts 266).
- Noon checkout with free later departure when the calendar allows, and arrival instructions sent at noon the day before that offer noon check-in if no one checks out (r/airbnb_hosts 3,808 thread).
- Remove threats of fees for crumbs or leftover food (123 upvotes: "Hotels from now on").

## Amenities and comfort that show up in reviews

- **Beds decide stars.** A guest at nearly $600 a night refused 5 stars over a sagging mattress and creaking box spring (r/vrbo 1,211). Replace before asking for 5 stars; quiet mattress protectors, not crunchy plastic; 2 pillows per guest in different firmness (15 upvotes); a host credits Novella mattresses from Wayfair plus laundered linens for repeated bedding compliments (43 upvotes).
- **Supplies scale with stay length.** 2 dishwasher pods and 3 toilet rolls for 14 nights; the guest had to shop at about half of 15 long stays (r/AirBnB 96).
- **Cook in your own kitchen.** Cook 1 to 2 meals to find what is missing; if it is a hotplate and microwave, say so (r/AirBnB 207).
- **One key per guest** for groups and monthly stays, or disclose the limit.
- **Drop what nobody uses.** A Keurig unused by 40 to 50 guests was donated and not missed (6 upvotes). Inverse caution: an advertised amenity that fails becomes the review.
- **Unadvertised perks become expectations.** Fresh bread and jams mentioned in reviews led to complaints when they stopped; removing them entirely had no visible booking effect (r/airbnb_hosts 717).
- **Upgrades can backfire.** A Top 1% queen listing replaced the bed with a king and soundproofed; two 4-star reviews followed and summer was the worst ever (r/airbnb_hosts 680, causes mixed). Change one thing at a time on a winning listing.
- **Locally made details get noticed:** local welcome gifts, commissioned curtains from nearby weavers (r/airbnb_hosts 893 thread).
- **Smoke-alarm and lock batteries every 6 months** on a calendar reminder; a chirping alarm cost 27 nights, $7,000 (r/airbnb_hosts 1,458).
- **Film cabinets at turnover;** an $800 HexClad set was swapped for cheap pans across six bookings and could not be traced (r/airbnb_hosts 625).

## Post-booking message (catches skimmers)

Send right after booking, repeating the limits most likely to surprise. Verbatim example from an Amsterdam homeshare (r/airbnb_hosts 631; it did not stop one couple who read nothing, but it documents disclosure):

> Thank you for booking with us. We are looking forward to hosting you!
> Important information to note:
> • This is a homeshare, you will have your own private room but the bathroom is shared with the hosts.
> • There is no kitchen or microwave you can use, there's a mini fridge in the room and coffee/tea making facilities.
> • The location isn't in the center of Amsterdam. It takes about 30 minutes to get to the center by public transport.
> • If any of the above comes as a surprise please re-read the listing. You have 48 hours to cancel with a full refund after booking.
> If you have any questions please let us know, we're happy to help.

The 48-hour window was this host's own policy, not a platform rule. Use the property's actual cancellation terms.

Tricks most owners miss: the photo you choose for the cover is a ranking input through click-through; walking times are the most checkable lie in a listing; and every disclosure you move from house rules into the first screen reduces both refunds and 4-star "accuracy" scores.
