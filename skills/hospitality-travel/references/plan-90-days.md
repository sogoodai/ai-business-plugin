# 90-day plan

For a new or stalled property, tour or advisor business. Each phase ends with an exit check. Adapt the order to the biggest money gap found in the first session. Sources in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Days 1 to 30: repair economics and the listing

Week 1: measure

- Pull 365 days by channel: views where available, conversion, bookings, cancellations, ADR, occupancy, RevPAR, booking window, length of stay, review score and count, net revenue after fees.
- Build a P&L per channel (Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, direct); fee structures differ.
- Filter GA4 for bots before reading any website numbers.
- Registration and permit check for every address.

Week 2: money leaks

- Reprice Airbnb for the 15.5% host-only fee and compare the guest total with 10 to 20 comparables on five date patterns.
- Audit settings on every channel: discounts on, custom minimums, hidden or inactive listings, calendar sync, seasonal rates loaded 12 months out, event dates.
- Compute fee load; decide cleaning fee structure.

Week 3: the listing

- New cover photo if weaker than top local competitors; book a professional shoot if the set is amateur; at least every room, exterior and the hero amenity; 20+ images if going to Google Vacation Rentals.
- Rewrite the opening with the template; move every surprise into the first screen and house rules; verify walking times.
- Shorten checkout to three lines.

Week 4: pricing engine and messages

- Configure dynamic pricing with base, floor, weekday and weekend, far-out minimums, gap minimums, event rules, last-minute floor. Audit 10 dates after setup.
- Load the message set from [scripts.md](scripts.md): post-booking limits, arrival, mid-stay permission, checkout, review request.

Tours: load 12 months of inventory, check cancellation and no-show rates, rewrite the product page, decide on the launch offer.
Advisors: compute net economics by host, choose one niche, publish one fee policy.

Exit check: net payout per channel known; no unwanted discounts or rogue minimums; listing disclosures complete; review request live; pricing tool configured with a control comparison.

## Days 31 to 60: build direct demand

- Hotels and B&Bs: Hotel Center free booking links through the booking engine; price accuracy check.
- Rentals: direct-booking site from the PMS; Google Vacation Rentals through a connectivity partner.
- Consent capture at check-in (card, Wi-Fi, guidebook QR) with source and date stored.
- Four flows: checkout and review, post-stay rebooking, abandoned booking, cancellation recovery (`email-sms-loyalty`).
- Direct perk on the home page and booking engine.
- First 3 to 5 local partners with redemption codes; mid-term listing or relocation contacts if the calendar has long gaps.
- Advisors: one high-intent niche asset; questionnaire and fee in every first reply.
- Tours: Creator Reviews or launch offer; hotel and rental partnerships.

Exit check: direct booking path works end to end on mobile; consent capture live; first flow sends; partners have codes; at least one new review per 3 stays in the last 30 days.

## Days 61 to 90: controlled experiments

Run one experiment per funnel stage, each against a comparison period or comparable listing:

- Search click-through: cover photo or title only.
- Listing conversion: opening copy, cancellation terms or fee structure.
- Pricing: one set of minimum-stay or lead-time rules.
- Email: behavior segment vs broad send.
- Tours: Accelerate or extra commission on a subset of products or dates, measured on net contribution.
- Advisors: deposit vs no deposit on inquiries from one source.

Exit check at day 90: keep only changes that improved net revenue, RevPAR, conversion, review velocity or direct share in revenue without raising cancellations, complaints or workload unacceptably. Report the result in money.

## Owner questions to ask along the way

- Which dates or guests do you refuse, and why?
- What would you do with a free Tuesday in February?
- Which past guests would you love back?
- Which local businesses do your guests ask about most?
- What is the lowest nightly price you would accept after cleaning?
