# Scripts

Exact wording. "Verbatim" means copied from the source (capitalization and punctuation lightly normalized, long dashes replaced); "template" means adapted. Fill brackets, keep the tone, send inside the platform's messaging for OTA guests. The owner approves any new offer before it is sent. Sources in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Before and at booking

**Post-booking limits message** (verbatim, r/airbnb_hosts 631; replace with your property's limits and real cancellation terms)

> Thank you for booking with us. We are looking forward to hosting you!
> Important information to note:
> • This is a homeshare, you will have your own private room but the bathroom is shared with the hosts.
> • There is no kitchen or microwave you can use, there's a mini fridge in the room and coffee/tea making facilities.
> • The location isn't in the center of Amsterdam. It takes about 30 minutes to get to the center by public transport.
> • If any of the above comes as a suprise please re-read the listing. You have 48 hours to cancel with a full refund after booking.
> If you have any questions please let us know, we're happy to help.

**Host proximity disclosure** (verbatim listing line, 23 upvotes)

> Host lives in house 30 meters away. You will be left completely alone except during routine pool maintenance, etc.

**Neighborhood disclosure** (approximate wording recalled by a guest, 215 upvotes)

> All guests should know that we are proud to be in an economically and socially diverse area. To that end, you may see folks around the neighborhood who are here using local social services, as well as others visiting local coffee shops and businesses.

**Listing opener** (template, research pass 2026)

> Walk to [demand generator] in [X minutes]. Sleeps [N] in [beds]. [2 to 3 differentiators]. Best for [segment]. Note before booking: [constraint].

**Fee-light opener** (verbatim, r/AirBnB)

> A tidy private apartment in a multi unit building. Super low cleaning fee ($25), and no list of chores to check out!

**Silent long booking over a peak event** (template, from r/airbnb_hosts 2,554 thread)

> Hi [name], we're looking forward to your stay [dates]. To get the house ready, could you confirm how many guests, how many cars (we have [N] parking spots), and roughly what brings you to town? Thanks!

## Arrival, mid-stay, checkout

**Arrival instructions** (template from operator practice, r/airbnb_hosts 3,808 thread): send at noon the day before; if no one checks out that day, add:

> Good news: the place is free earlier, so you're welcome to arrive from noon instead of 3 pm.

**Mid-stay entry permission** (template; disclosure alone was not enough, r/airbnb_hosts 4,143)

> Hi [name], our housekeeper is scheduled to refresh towels and linens tomorrow at [time]. Is that OK with you? Please reply yes, or tell me a better time. If we don't hear back, we won't come in.

**Mid-stay cleaning offer for 7+ nights** (template, guest report, 13 upvotes)

> You're halfway through your stay. Would you like a free mid-stay clean and fresh linens? Pick a time that suits you.

**Checkout lines** (verbatim, pick one)

> Please lock the front door on your way out. Have a safe trip home!

> Leave the house clean and tidy. Lock the doors. Additional cleaning is always appreciated but not required.

## Reviews

**Review request on checkout day** (verbatim, research pass 2026)

> Thanks again for staying with us. Airbnb has opened the review window today. If the stay matched what you expected, we'd really appreciate an honest review, especially anything future guests should know about the cleanliness, check-in, accuracy or location. If anything fell short, please message us directly as well so we can fix it.

**Reply to a hostile review** (verbatim suggestion, 52 upvotes)

> We go to a great deal of effort to make sure our guests have a great experience and build incredible memories. Regrettably, it seems we could not please [name]. We ask potential guests to look at our long series of happy, 5-star reviews and make up your own mind.

**Reply to an amenity complaint** (verbatim suggestion, 7 upvotes)

> Thank you for the comments. We do provide quality Starbucks coffee just not in the Keurig format to reduce plastic waste. Since we are a high value property focused on affordable rates, we have chosen not to offer other food or gift basket type items. Some of our competitors who charge more do offer these items. Had you contacted me with your concerns during your stay, I may have been able to address them and improve your satisfaction.

**Removal request to Airbnb** (template; factual framing worked on the second try, r/airbnb_hosts 1,128)

> On [date] the guest wrote: "[exact threat]" (screenshot attached) after we enforced the [rule] stated in our house rules (screenshot of in-app messages attached). Airbnb's review policy states [quote policy]. The review posted on [date] repeats the threatened content. We request removal under that policy.

## Cancellations, refunds and departures

**Guest asks you to cancel** (verbatim, 14 upvotes)

> Regretfully, I am unable to cancel your booking. For questions, please refer to the booking conditions and terms of service.

**Refund only for resold nights** (verbatim, r/airbnb_hosts 467)

> If you initiate the cancellation on your end right away, it will allow me to reopen my calendar. I will then commit to refunding you for any of those nights that I am able to successfully re-book with a new guest.

**Early departure over a holiday** (verbatim, r/airbnb_hosts 1,753)

> However, given the circumstances if you cancel and it books then we will give you a full refund for that night (minus any taxes and fees). If it does not book, we would like to offer you 50% off of one night on a future stay.

**B&B non-refundable cancellation** (verbatim, r/bedandbreakfast 467)

> Request via their system. We will give you a gift certificate for days that we resell that is good for a year.

**Weather cancellation two hours before arrival** (template from r/airbnb_hosts 1,151)

> I'm sorry about the forecast. Here are three options: (1) cancel, and I'll refund the cleaning fee plus any nights I manage to rebook; (2) shift your stay to [Sunday and Monday], when the forecast is better; (3) move to any open dates on the calendar.

**Scam check before taking a big phone booking** (template from r/bedandbreakfast 39)

> Before I take payment: our processor can only refund to the original card, and we retain a 3% service fee on any cancellation.

**Turnover emergency, late check-in** (template from r/airbnb_hosts 782)

> I'm so sorry: [what happened]. The unit will be ready at [time]. You can cancel now for a full refund, or stay and I'll refund [amount] for the delay. Your choice, and I'll make it right either way.

## Service recovery

**Broken amenity on arrival** (template)

> I'm sorry the [item] isn't working. A replacement arrives [today at time]. In the meantime [immediate fix, for example: I'll bring coffee over, what can I get you?].

**Major lapse (cleaning missed)** (template from r/airbnb_hosts 410)

> This is on us, and I'm sorry. Right now I can [send a cleaner at a time you choose / pay for a hotel tonight]. I've refunded [cleaning fee plus X%]. Fresh sheets are in [place]. I'll check in with you in the morning.

## Travel advisors

**First reply to a new inquiry** (template from r/travelagents 105 thread)

> Thanks for reaching out! Here's what I do: [services]. [One line connecting to their trip]. To start, please fill out this short questionnaire [link]. My planning fee is $[X], credited to your booking if you book with me.

**Planning fee** (verbatim, research pass 2026)

> My planning fee is $[X]. It covers destination and supplier research, itinerary design, booking management and pre-departure support. Supplier commissions, where paid, are separate and do not reduce your trip price.

**Can't match a cheaper offer** (template from 36-upvote practice)

> If you don't need my help on this one, take that rate. Just know that if anything goes sideways with that booking, I won't be able to step in.

**Price drop after booking** (verbatim, 24 upvotes)

> Hey, great news! The price has dropped significantly, and we have 2 ways to take advantage. I could get you each a bit of money back, or I could get you all upgraded to way better rooms/perks!

**Commission explanation to friends** (template from 9-upvote practice)

> The cruise line pays my commission, so using me doesn't add to your price.
