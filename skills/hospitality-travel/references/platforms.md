# Platforms: ranking, thresholds, fees and setting traps

Pull the current help page before acting; rules below were checked in the November 2026 research pass. Sources in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Diagnose by funnel stage first

| Symptom | Check first |
|---|---|
| Low impressions | Availability (blocked dates, rogue minimum stays), total price vs comps, restrictive minimum stays, missing amenities, cancellations, response rate |
| Impressions but few listing views | Cover photo, displayed total price, title, badge |
| Views but few bookings | Full photo set, fees, cancellation policy, reviews, bed layout, amenities, copy, booking friction (request vs Instant Book) |
| Bookings but sliding rank | Cancellations, complaints, category scores, review velocity, response time |

Airbnb's own documentation supports this split (2026). Change one thing per stage and watch 2 to 4 weeks.

## Airbnb

- **Ranking:** quality (photos, video, reviews, communication, amenities, service issues, cancellations), popularity (saves, messages, bookings), price (total price relative to comparable local listings), location, availability, minimum-stay flexibility, Instant Book and response behavior (Airbnb, 2026).
- **Fee:** 15.5% host-only for most home hosts in 2026; generally 16% in Brazil and Mexico. Reprice ([economics.md](economics.md)).
- **Superhost (account level):** 10 reservations or 3 totaling 100+ nights, 90%+ response within 24 hours, under 1% cancellations, 4.8+ overall, assessed quarterly on the last 365 days.
- **Guest Favorite (listing level, visible in search):** 5+ reviews in 4 years, 1+ in the last 2 years, evaluated daily from ratings, category scores, cancellations, quality incidents, review-removal rate and communication. Labels for top 1%, 5%, 10% and bottom 10% of eligible homes.
- **Keep communication on-platform.** Airbnb says host and guest messages feed quality and Guest Favorite evaluation. Off-platform chats also leave you without evidence in disputes.
- **Professional photography program:** +21% earnings and +19% bookings for 14,700+ listings (2024 to 2025 cohort, observational).
- **Experiences:** 20% host fee; surfaced in search, trip planning, emails and in-trip suggestions.

Setting traps operators hit:

- **Custom trip lengths from a pricing tool.** A PriceLabs sync set alternating 90 and 135-night minimums on every date for two years: impressions and clicks rose, bookings went to zero (r/airbnb_hosts 1,416, 2025). After connecting any tool, open the calendar and check minimums on 10 random dates.
- **Smart Pricing plus stacked discounts.** A host accepted a gap booking "barely worth hosting" after Smart Pricing cut gap nights and the guest added a 10% non-refundable discount (r/airbnb_hosts 1,005).
- **Auto-added highlights.** Airbnb added "Extra spacious" to a small studio from two old reviews and refused to remove it (r/airbnb_hosts 739). Read your listing as a guest monthly; counter false impressions in the first lines of the description.
- **The party checkbox is not enough.** A booking for 10 hosted a 60-person wedding; Airbnb said the "No parties" toggle alone was insufficient and suspended the listing for 30 days (r/airbnb_hosts 1,755). Write the rule in house rules too.
- **"Host Success" calls.** A caller claiming to be from Airbnb said minimum stays hurt rank; following it led to a one-night local party (r/airbnb_hosts 1,690). Verify callers inside the app.
- **Mid-stay entry.** Disclosed weekly cleaning was not enough; Airbnb required each guest's written acceptance after a cleaner entered (r/airbnb_hosts 4,143).

## Vrbo

- **Ranking:** reviews, bookings, response behavior, acceptance, cancellations, Instant Booking, availability. With all else equal, the Instant Book property ranks higher, and Instant Book inventory can show across Expedia Group sites (Vrbo, 2026).
- **Market definition:** partly the search term travelers use, not the municipality (a Kissimmee home can compete in "Orlando"). Edit the comp set in the dashboard to remove non-comparables before trusting its pricing metrics.
- **Premier Host (listing level, from 1 January 2026):** 99%+ acceptance, 0% host cancellations, 4.6+ rating, 5+ Vrbo reviews, 5+ bookings or 60 booked nights. Automatic search-position increase, badge and filter.
- **Minimum standards (from 15 September 2026):** rolling 12 months; established listings below over 50% acceptance, under 10% host cancellation or a 2.0+ rating can be permanently removed. Declines now cost twice: the booking and the metric.
- **Fees:** 12% on rent plus host-added fees in the standard pay-per-booking model; up to 15% on some professional plans in Europe, Australia and New Zealand (per research pass).

Setting traps:

- **Hidden or incomplete listings take bookings.** Five stays booked on an incomplete hidden listing, three clashing with Airbnb (r/airbnb_hosts 2,554); an inactive listing sold New Year's Eve for AUD 150. Delete unused listings through support; screenshot status first; escalate fee disputes to a supervisor (a $600+ fee was removed, 383 upvotes).
- **Auto-enabled discounts.** Vrbo turned on booking discounts and emailed instructions; a host tried to recover $31.40 from a guest the day before arrival (r/vrbo 643, 2025). Check promotion settings after every Vrbo announcement.
- **Reopened calendars at old rates.** Vrbo reopened dates before seasonal rates were loaded: a June oceanfront request at the $99 winter rate.
- **Cancellation penalties are real.** $119 invoiced for a cancellation 20 minutes after acceptance, with suspension threatened (r/vrbo 316). When a guest asks to cancel, they cancel from their own account, even if support says otherwise (a host ended at a negative $130 payout, r/vrbo 217).
- **Calendar sync fails.** Of 3 manually blocked days, 1 stayed blocked (r/vrbo 1,171 thread). Check cross-channel availability weekly, and the next 30 days daily in peak.

## Booking.com

- **Ranking is a booking-probability model:** features, price, review scores, content and photo quality, advance-booking behavior, length of stay, conversion and click-through. The consumer page simplifies it to click-through, gross bookings, net bookings after cancellations (2026).
- **Paid visibility:** Genius, deals, Preferred, Preferred Plus and Visibility Booster can influence default ranking; Preferred and Booster cost extra commission. Buying reach does not fix conversion.
- **Commission is contract-specific.** Pull the Extranet agreement.
- **EEA parity is gone:** contractual parity removed July 2024 under the DMA; the Commission says Booking.com does not use external prices for default ranking or Preferred and Genius eligibility in the EEA, and from September 2026 not for Booking Sponsored Benefit eligibility either. Delete "must always match the lowest web price" from EEA playbooks.
- **Net bookings trick:** refundable discounts that drive gross volume and cancellations can be worse for rank than fewer firm bookings. Track conversion and cancellation together.

## Expedia, hotels.com and other OTAs (hotel side)

- Guests on OTA reservations are the OTA's customer for changes and refunds: "we sold them a room, and they sold you a room, we cannot change their reservation" (r/hotels, 35 upvotes). Say it kindly at check-in when it comes up.
- Map rooms carefully. Run-of-house mappings treat bed counts as requests; accessible rooms got booked by guests who did not need them, leaving none for those who did (r/hotels 162 thread). Book connecting rooms through reservations (108 and 40 upvotes).
- Keep every channel's claims identical. An adults-only hotel admitted families while its site, Booking.com header and Instagram still said 21+; guests sought chargebacks (r/hotels 1,338).

## Google

- **Hotel free booking links:** no click fee; ranked on user utility, value, landing-page experience and historical price accuracy. Buying Hotel Ads does not lift the free link (Google 2026).
- **Vacation Rentals:** free referral and booking links, no Google referral or booking fee, through an approved connectivity partner or eligible direct integration; accurate prices and availability required; under 5 images ineligible, 8 recommended, 20+ strongly recommended, landscape 1920x1080. Google reorders images by query (pool shots for pool searches), so upload strong amenity shots.
- **AI Mode:** by 27 August 2026 AI Mode could compare hotels and let some US English users book through integrated partners (Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, major chains). A small property gets that surface only through accurate feeds and partners; an attractive site alone does not.
- **Price accuracy matters both ways.** Guests reported hotel sites charging $30 to $100 more than the direct rate Google displayed (r/hotels, 12 and 147 upvotes). Click through your own Google listing monthly.
- **Things to do:** feed-based placements with photos, prices and booking links in Search and Maps; from Q3 2026 Google began migrating Things to do campaigns toward Search campaigns for Travel.

## Tripadvisor

- **Popularity Ranking:** review quality, recency and enough quantity, with more weight on recent reviews. Past a confidence level, more reviews do not mechanically raise rank.
- **"Featured" sort for tours** is commercial: revenue for Tripadvisor, browsing preferences, click frequency and review volume and quality. Do not merge Tripadvisor rank and Viator booking rank mentally.
