## Platform rules and fees

- Etsy fees (listing $0.20, transaction 6.5%, payment processing, Offsite Ads 12 to 15%): https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy and https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy
- Gumroad fees (10% + $0.50 direct, 30% Discover): https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees
- Lemon Squeezy fees (5% + $0.50, 30% marketplace): https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees
- Payhip pricing: https://payhip.com/pricing
- Fixed-fee share under $9 (14 to 29%): computed in the `digital-products` skill from the fee pages above.
- Notion Marketplace (8% + $0.40, biweekly payouts, $20 minimum): https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace. Some older third-party guides say 10%; Notion's help page wins.
- Fiverr seller fee 20%: Fiverr Help Center. Upwork freelancer fee 0 to 15%: Upwork Help Center and https://www.upwork.com/resources/upwork-vs-fiverr
