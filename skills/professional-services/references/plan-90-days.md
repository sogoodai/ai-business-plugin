# 90-day plan

For a new or stalled firm of one to about 20 people. Order matters: measure and fix intake and pricing first, then referrals and pages, then paid. Every phase ends with a short owner report: what changed, scorecard numbers, decisions needed. Licensed approvers sign every amber asset ([compliance.md](compliance.md)).

Adjust by sub-model: tax firms entering January compress acquisition and focus on capacity; PI and other high-case-value consumer law can test paid media earlier once intake is proven; advisors spend more of the 90 days on ICP, referral and COI systems (research report 2026).

## Shared spine

### Days 1 to 10: economics and ICP

- Import 12 months of clients, leads, invoices and time. Normalize source to person or partner level. Target: 90%+ of recent clients with a usable source.
- Compute signed CAC by channel, revenue and margin by service and client, realization, first response time, capacity headroom ([economics.md](economics.md)).
- Score 3 to 5 candidate niches ([niching.md](niching.md)). Draft ICP and "not a fit" rules.
- Check ownership of website, domain, GBP, ad accounts and call recordings.
- Owner decides: primary niche, minimums to test, compliance approver.

### Days 11 to 20: intake and Google profile

- Instant acknowledgment, human-response SLA, backup coverage, missed-call text-back, booking links with routing questions, lead states in the CRM.
- Mystery-shop the firm by form, phone and email; record times.
- GBP audit (categories, services, practitioner listings, hours, tracked links); neutral review request to every client at a defined milestone (`local-seo` for mechanics).
- Exit check: median human response under 5 minutes in staffed hours; 100% of leads with a state.

### Days 21 to 30: pricing and offer

- Minimums by service; deposits, retainers and payment gates; 3 packages with scope boundaries and change-order rules; engagement letter updates.
- Price-increase cohort by realization; notices drafted ([scripts.md](scripts.md)).
- Exit check: every package has a scope limit and a price review date; lowest-margin clients repriced or scheduled to exit.

### Days 21 to 45: referral and COI engine

- Top 30 clients and top 20 COIs listed with what each needs; individualized asks; two COI meetings a week.
- Former-client and partner touch program (cards, annual mailing, quarterly calls) within the profession's gift rules.
- Exit check: every new client attributed to a person or partner; reciprocal referral log.

### Days 31 to 45: high-intent pages and one authority asset

- Service pages that answer fit, problem, process, proof, price model, next step; bios with licenses; pricing page with ranges and minimums.
- One asset competitors cannot reproduce by prompting a model: a niche benchmark, deadline calculator, jurisdiction checklist or anonymized case series.
- Reviewer, jurisdiction and last-reviewed date on every page.

### Days 46 to 60: one paid channel and expansion

- One channel, offline conversion import to signed or funded clients, CAC cap signed by the owner. Lawyers: LSA and Search reported separately. B2B: LinkedIn only against a named ICP.
- `next_best_service` populated from triggers; 20 to 30 expansion conversations scheduled ([retention-and-expansion.md](retention-and-expansion.md)).

### Days 61 to 75: measure and prune

- Search Console standard and AI reports, GBP calls, branded search, converting pages. Remove or update thin pages.
- Compare sources by signed CAC, revenue, margin, response time, sales cycle and retention. Pause sources with cheap leads and poor signed economics.

### Days 76 to 90: scale and lock governance

- Increase budget in measured steps on the winner; second authority asset; more COIs.
- Lock approved templates, prohibited claims, jurisdiction rules, testimonial rules, archive process.
- Exit check: one repeatable paid or content channel plus a referral engine with known signed-client economics.

## Tracks by sub-model

### Bookkeeping / CAS

- Week 1: list current clients by hours vs fee; flag under-realized ones for the 90-day repricing review.
- Weeks 2 to 3: books-access quote process, diagnostic video template, cleanup pricing (12 months of fees + 10 to 25%, prepaid hours).
- Weeks 3 to 8: 5 CPA or EA partners met; Indeed and Upwork prospect routine (2 hours a week); one niche page.
- Weeks 8 to 12: annual prepaid option; client handbook; first niche case study.
- Scorecard: MRR, hours per client, effective hourly, new clients by partner.

### Tax preparation

- If before January: minimums and new-client premium published; fall engagement letters and ACH; increases set in December; virtual receptionist or answering service booked; portal-only intake.
- If in season: no broad acquisition; extension and late-arrival retainers; payment before e-file; note which clients to reprice or drop.
- After April 15: referral asks to satisfied clients, COI lunches, advisory and planning offers to the right 20%.
- Scorecard: revenue per return, realization, write-offs, returns per preparer, complaints after increase.

### CPA with advisory

- Days 1 to 30: tiered packages; trigger list; IRC 7216 consent language before any non-tax cross-sell.
- Days 31 to 60: 20 trigger conversations; one niche CE talk or association article.
- Days 61 to 90: price advisory as retainers; measure expansion revenue separately.

### Law, consumer

- Days 1 to 20: intake (5-minute SLA, after-hours service, conflicts-safe acknowledgment, budget qualification for criminal and family).
- Days 21 to 45: GBP and practitioner listings; free directory profiles (state bar, Avvo, Justia); bar referral service; former-client mailing within state rules.
- Days 46 to 75: LSA and Search, separate P&Ls, weekly search-term audit, results on landing pages with state disclaimers.
- Days 76 to 90: scale only the channel with the lower signed CAC; capacity check before raising intake.
- Scorecard: answer rate, time to human, consult-to-signed by practice area, signed CAC by channel, fee per matter.

### Law, business

- Days 1 to 30: referral-source list ranked by origination; daily call routine; ICP by industry.
- Days 31 to 60: one niche authority asset; LinkedIn posts by the lawyer; client feedback interviews.
- Days 61 to 90: two speaking slots or association articles; cross-practice introductions.

### Insurance, lead-driven lines

- Days 1 to 14: scripts, routing, notes template, manual follow-up schedule before buying volume (r/InsuranceAgent 65); consent and scrub process.
- Days 15 to 45: two or three vendors in tiny batches; weekly scorecard; 7-call and 14-email cadence; daily 10-minute call review.
- Days 46 to 90: scale the vendor with real contactability and refunds; post-sale service; referral ask at policy delivery.
- Scorecard: reached rate, conversations over 2 minutes, policies per presentation, CPA, chargebacks, net commission.

### Insurance, commercial

- Days 1 to 30: niche and carrier access; 20 COIs (realtors, lenders, CPAs, title).
- Days 31 to 60: Friday office visits; one networking meeting a day; payment-before-binding rule.
- Days 61 to 90: owners' personal lines cross-sell; renewal calendar.

### Financial advisor

- Days 1 to 15: written ICP, value proposition and marketing plan; Calendly routing with asset minimums; CCO-approved templates.
- Days 16 to 45: list clients' CPAs and ask for warm intros; advisor directory listings; one-page plan meeting format.
- Days 46 to 75: one acquisition test: a paid-entry class (budget about $12,000 to $15,000 per class), a workplace planning pilot, or an ungated Meta video funnel at $50+ a day.
- Days 76 to 90: weekly or biweekly YouTube or case-study content if the advisor will commit for 6+ months; household consolidation offers to existing clients.
- Scorecard: appointments, show rate, plan meetings, close rate, net new assets or fees, CAC vs Kitces medians.

## Owner questions at day 90

1. Which channel has the lowest signed CAC with acceptable client quality?
2. Which clients should be repriced or released before next season?
3. Is capacity the binding constraint? If so, raise minimums before buying more leads.
4. Which compliance approvals slowed launches, and can templates be pre-approved?
