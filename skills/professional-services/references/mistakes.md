# Expensive mistakes

Two tables: what the data shows, and what practitioners report losing.

## From the data

| Mistake | What it costs | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Buying legal leads while inquiries go unanswered | 26% of law firms never personally replied within 7 days; an unworked lead is 100% wasted spend (Hennessey 2025) | Intake SLA and backup coverage before any budget increase |
| Optimizing law ads to CPL | Search $92 CPL vs LSA $192 looked better, but signed-case CAC was $3,812 vs $2,354 (Pareto Legal 2026) | Report cost per signed matter by channel and practice area |
| Treating adviser social media as direct response | $48,836 CAC vs $3,198 for referrals and $3,020 for SEO (Kitces 2026) | Social supports trust; acquisition budget goes to referrals, COIs, listings, SEO |
| Client appreciation events and seminars without attribution | $44,191 and $15,447 CAC vs $16,925 all-tactic average (Kitces 2026) | Paid-entry classes with booked lab sessions; revenue per event tracked |
| Not raising accounting fees from fear of churn | 28% hesitate; two-thirds of raisers lost nobody or kept profit stable (Ignition 2025) | Annual increase on a fixed date, allocated by realization |
| Paying for or gating reviews | Google review restrictions; FTC rule penalties since 21 Oct 2024; for lawyers a 7.2(b) problem | Same neutral request to everyone |
| Publishing hundreds of generic AI articles | Informational clicks fell to 8% with AI summaries vs 15% without (Pew 2025); Google rejects special GEO tricks (May 2026) | Original data, checklists with jurisdiction and date, cost pages, case studies |
| Lead generators in regulated advice without supervision | ASIC reviewed 15 licensees using lead generators (Report 845, Nov 2026) | Licensee approval for every funnel; scripts archived |
| Repeating "5-minute response = 21x conversions" | A claim based on old contact-rate studies (Caseflow 2026); undermines credibility with owners | Use the measured competitive bar: under 5 minutes is the fastest 25% |
| Copying New York ad templates written before June 2026 | Out-of-date labels and blackout rules (NYSBA, Sept 2026) | Check the state rule version date on every legal template |

## From practitioners

| Mistake | What it cost | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Inflated retainer to scare off a bad prospect | $30,000 asked instead of $5,000; several paid; every one had to be fired within a year (r/Lawyertalk 2,537) | Decline directly |
| Flat misdemeanor fee without a trial clause | $2,500 fee, about $10,000 of work (r/LawFirm 182) | Price trial separately or phase fees |
| Google reps adding campaigns quarterly | $17,000/month family law account bidding on "employment lawyer" and courthouse queries; most spend wasted (r/LawFirm 91) | Weekly search-term audit; reject campaigns not tied to signed-case data |
| Paying for SEO promises | $10,000 for a month of "top three" SEO, zero clients (r/LawFirm 110) | Pay for pages and links you can see; judge on signed clients |
| Unchecked freelancer ad build | $125 for keyword-stuffed copy (r/LawFirm 106) | Review keywords and ads before paying |
| Answering service sending calls to voicemail | $240/month for missed calls (r/LawFirm 169) | Test with mystery calls monthly |
| Starting legal work before retainer is paid | Suing prospects for $2,000 LLC formation fees (r/LawFirm 110) | Signed engagement and payment first |
| Anchoring a PI client to a net number | Client refused to sign over $4,000 on a $650,000 settlement (r/LawFirm 173) | Give ranges, never a net figure before liens are final |
| Referring a case through a middleman lawyer | Referrer got $5,000 instead of a third on a $100,000 settlement, then stopped sending cases (r/LawFirm 182) | Refer direct; honor referral splits where allowed |
| Underpricing clients for hoped-for referrals | No referrals; a difficult client at capacity while turning away work (r/taxpros 128) | Full price; referral reward only where rules allow |
| Heavy discounts to build the book | Client count that "should generate twice the revenue"; a $300 return worth $800 (r/taxpros 157) | Minimums from day one |
| 550 returns for $120,000 | Volume trap after 17 years (r/taxpros 75) | Reset fees, shed bottom clients |
| Quoting from last year's return | $925 bill with heavy unpaid advisory, fight over $50 (r/taxpros 87) | Review, engagement letter, deposit, work, payment, signature, filing |
| Expediting before confirming payment | Rushed a $300 return; client could not pay until Friday anyway (r/taxpros 266) | Payment timing confirmed before moving anyone up the queue |
| Fee concession to a complainer | $255 cut to $190, client still left (r/taxpros 262) | Hold the fee; offer timing flexibility |
| Telling clients about a planned firm sale early | Two clients left for unlicensed preparers; sale price hurt (r/taxpros 203) | Announce with the buyer, with a transition plan |
| Unlimited support without exclusions | Clients expected AR and AP work (r/taxpros 119) | List exclusions in the letter |
| Auto-creating CRM accounts for every booking | $15 per extra prospect account a year in TaxDome (r/taxpros 67) | Create accounts after engagement |
| Bookkeeping retainer quoted from the call | $1,700/month estimate at 1.5 h/week became 5 h/week (r/Bookkeeping 36) | Confirm reconciliation frequency, close deadlines and response expectations; 90-day review |
| Cleanup discounted to win monthly work | $600 for 6+ accounts; client refused the $350/month follow-on and left no review (r/Bookkeeping 89) | Price cleanup on its own; monthly is a separate decision |
| $100/month first client | Locked into an uneconomic fee out of embarrassment (r/Bookkeeping 87) | Quote from real hours after 3 months; annual review clause |
| Accepting a 3-day VAT cleanup with no capacity | Lost a major client (r/Bookkeeping 94) | Check documents, reviewer and weekend ownership before saying yes |
| A year relying on marketplaces and Facebook | Lost a year before in-person networking (r/Bookkeeping 147) | Meet COIs and owners in person early |
| Binding a $90,000 commercial policy before the down payment | Up to $22,500 minimum earned premium exposure (r/InsuranceAgent 41) | Collect and process payment before binding |
| One offshore live transfer | $25,000 settlement demand (r/InsuranceAgent 160) | Consent records, litigator scrubs, vendor indemnity |
| "Exclusive" leads resold to 10 agents | Thousands a month for 1 to 3 real conversations per 8-hour day (r/InsuranceAgent 58) | Opt-in date and resale count in writing; small tests |
| 30+ life policies, more lead spend than commission | $13,000 leads for about $10,000 deposited (r/InsuranceAgent 72) | Net-of-chargeback math before scaling |
| Three weeks dialing "free" leads already rejected | Wasted time and angry prospects (r/InsuranceAgent 61) | Check prior contact history first |
| $5,000 annuity lead package | Leads undelivered six weeks later (r/InsuranceAgent 40) | Written delivery dates and cancellation terms |
| Texting unsolicited life quotes to the book | Clients stopped answering service calls (r/InsuranceAgent 52) | Trigger-based, consented cross-sell only |
| Adviser selling health insurance for early revenue | Planning prospects slipped (r/CFP 190) | Protect prospecting time for the core offer |
| Hourly exception for a $16M prospect | Disputes, $50,000 in retainers, plan never implemented (r/CFP 191) | Refer prospects who want a different model |
| Lead-magnet email gate before a video | About 2 appointments from the nurture; removing the gate halved cost per appointment (r/CFP 158) | Let prospects watch, then book |
| Dinner seminars on autopilot | Several thousand dollars of pasta, one small prospect (r/CFP 50) | Paid classes, tight targeting, booked lab sessions |
| Accepting every client regardless of distance | Travel burden once established (r/CFP 52) | Distance and size rules from the start |
| Ballpark fee quoted too early to a $29M prospect | Quote reportedly above competitors (r/CFP 43) | Discovery first, then fee with the plan |
| Bringing a senior advisor to look credible | Lost the deal (r/CFP 190) | Run your own meetings |
| Agency owns the website and domain | Lock-in when leaving (r/localseo 29) | Contract for ownership of site, domain, GBP, ad accounts, call recordings |
