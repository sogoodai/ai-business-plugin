# Seasonality and capacity

Treat peak season as a capacity-allocation problem, not a marketing quarter. Lead generation that outruns delivery raises response time and error risk, and errors lose clients faster than delays (r/taxpros 145).

## US tax calendar for a tax or accounting firm

| Months | Marketing job | Operations job |
|---|---|---|
| May to June | Recovery; client survey; referral asks to happy clients; price review notes | Post-mortem: realization per client, who was late, who to drop |
| July to September | COI meetings (bookkeepers and CPAs meet outside season, r/Bookkeeping 260; advisors buy CPAs lunch after season, r/CFP 392); content and pages; small September bump in bookkeeping inquiries before the October extension deadline (r/Bookkeeping 46) | Process changes in summer (r/taxpros 81); software decisions |
| October to December | Fee changes announced; fall engagement letters for next season with ACH in January (r/taxpros 114); sell planning, projections and CAS; prune low-margin clients; set the year's average increase in December (r/taxpros 111) | Organizers and onboarding automation; capacity plan by preparer |
| January | Accept new clients only up to the capacity plan; virtual receptionist or answering service live before the rush (r/taxpros 387) | Portal-only intake; payment gates set |
| February to April 15 | No broad acquisition unless the workflow shows spare capacity; high-fit, high-margin only; late arrivals pay retainers ($100 extension retainer for prior non-finishers, $500 for last-two-weeks arrivals, r/taxpros 114) | Sundays off and a closed April 16 (r/taxpros 145) |
| April to October 15 | Extensions; amendment work quoted before clients file against advice and scheduled after season ($150 per amendment, r/taxpros 114) | Extension clients billed full fee upfront when new |

Data: the 2026 IRS filing season opened 26 January with a 15 April deadline and about 164 million individual returns expected; professionals had e-filed 73.46 million returns by 25 April 2025 (IRS). February arrivals switching preparers are a screening red flag for some firms (r/taxpros 130), and a source of higher-paying extension clients for others (r/taxpros 104). Decide by capacity.

Peak-season time is worth more: one preparer valued peak hours at $200 vs $80 off-season and priced amendments to match (r/taxpros 114).

## UK and Australia

- **UK Self Assessment, tax year 6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026:** paper return 31 October 2026, online filing and payment 31 January 2027, second payment on account 31 July where applicable (HMRC). Peak is December and January; sell in spring and summer. Do not copy a US calendar.
- **Australia:** individuals self-lodging generally face 31 October; registered tax agents run separate lodgment programs and clients must be on the agent's list by the cut-off to get extended dates. The source guidance predates 2024; verify the current ATO lodgment program before automating reminders.

## Other professions

- **Insurance:** renewal-driven. Forecast renewal workload and quote volume by month; suppress prospecting into service teams when renewal SLAs slip. Medicare: Annual Enrollment Period 15 October to 7 December drives the year; ACA open enrollment from 1 November. Aged internet leads re-shop around day 30 to 35, plausibly at renewal (r/InsuranceAgent 38).
- **Advisors:** client-specific triggers (tax year-end, benefits enrollment, RMDs, year-end planning) beat a generic content calendar. Market drops are retention events: call first (r/CFP 63, 97). Paid Meta ads can be paused in the last week of each month to stay within budget without hurting appointment flow (r/CFP 71).
- **Bookkeeping:** spring and September inquiry spikes, slow summers (r/Bookkeeping 46). Use summer for COI building and cleanup projects.
- **Law:** less seasonal; divorce inquiries are widely reported to rise after holidays (practitioner folklore, no current dataset found). PI depends on case flow and staffing more than calendar.

## Capacity rules

- Write the capacity plan before the season: available hours by person, committed hours, target new clients.
- **Throttle triggers:** median first response over target; next consult slot over 7 days away; work-in-progress past due; staff overtime above the agreed limit.
- **Throttle order:** pause the most expensive paid source, then raise new-client minimums, then waitlist, then refer out.
- PI firms deliberately cut intake from about 50 to 30 cases a month to fix staffing, workflow and cash flow (r/LawFirm 121, 109).
- A solo tax and bookkeeping practitioner caps business subscriptions at about 50 clients, grows only from referrals, and went from $287,000 (2021) to an estimated $610,000 (2024) with no employees (r/taxpros 99).
- A medical-practice CPA stopped taking new clients and works a maximum of 45 hours a week in season, about 32 otherwise (r/taxpros 119).
- Insurance live calls: route only when the agent is free, with instant pause and caps (r/InsuranceAgent 65).
- Advisors: standardize service for smaller households, batch reviews, transfer small clients to a partner or junior when full (r/CFP 55, 163).
- Use overflow networks: traffic-ticket lawyers pay $100 to $125 per appearance to local coverage attorneys (r/LawFirm 135); PI solos refer out-of-range cases for a referral fee where allowed (r/LawFirm 113, 61).
