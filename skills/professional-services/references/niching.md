# Niching by industry or client type

## What the evidence says

- **Accounting:** CAS practices with over half of revenue in defined industries reported 38% higher median CAS revenue and 51% higher net revenue per client; firms with a formal CAS business plan earned nearly $10,000 more per client (CPA.com/AICPA, Dec 2024, 2023 data, self-selected, association only).
- **Buyer side:** 353 US business decision-makers said they would pay 25% more for a specialized accountant; businesses over $1M revenue were about twice as likely to hire one; 98% of those who left a specialist chose another specialist (TaxDome 2025, vendor survey).
- **Advisers:** firms with a written ideal-client persona, value proposition and marketing plan gained 67% more new clients and 68% more new-client assets (Schwab 2025, 1,288 RIAs). Partly a management-quality effect.
- **Insurance:** a Canadian commercial agent at $20,000 a month credits three things: a niche, access to overseas markets, limited competition (r/InsuranceAgent 29). Broad personal lines niche less well because carriers set price.
- **Law:** extreme niches tied to a prior career brought first clients before the website existed (r/LawFirm 198); public-employer litigation experience brought statewide plaintiff-lawyer referrals (r/LawFirm 89).

## Score a candidate niche (1 to 5 each)

1. Annual client value (fees, AUM, premium, case value).
2. Workflow repeatability (same chart of accounts, same forms, same documents, same policy set).
3. Density of COIs and associations that already gather the niche.
4. Proof the firm already has (clients, cases, prior career, data).
5. Addressable demand you can reach (local search volume, association membership, LinkedIn titles, licensing lists).

"Small businesses" scores 1 on almost everything. "Veterinary groups with 2 to 10 locations" changes keywords, landing pages, packages, partners, benchmarks and prospect lists. Present the top two to the owner; the licensed owner chooses.

## How to test without betting the firm

- New advisors: try 3 to 5 niches, narrow when overwhelmed (r/CFP 142). Established firms: one primary, one secondary.
- Build one niche landing page, one niche case study, one niche partner list and one niche package before spending on ads.
- Make the niche visible in delivery, not just the homepage: onboarding checklist, chart of accounts, benchmark report, document templates, policy review list, partner roster (research report 2026).
- Measure niche vs non-niche: revenue per client, hours per client, referral rate, close rate. Keep the niche if two of four are better after two quarters.
- Do not drop profitable generalist clients on day one; raise their prices and let the niche replace them.

## Traps

- A niche without access: an Aflac agent in a 40,000-person town with 3 districts and 10 agents found prospect lists nearly empty after checks against existing accounts (r/InsuranceAgent 81).
- A niche without money: nurses at a Catholic hospital had small 403(b)s (r/CFP 50).
- Niching by marketing only. Prospects test expertise in the first call; a niche page with generic intake questions loses.
- Licensing: an advisor or CPA claiming "specialist" status, or a lawyer claiming certification, triggers rules. Use "focuses on" or "practice limited to" ([compliance.md](compliance.md)).
