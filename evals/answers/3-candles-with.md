## Why Meta ads lose money right now

- **Most you can pay for a customer:** about $11, because that's your profit on one order.
- **What sellers actually pay:** the median ecommerce cost to win a customer on Meta is **$38.99**, at a return of **1.88x** the ad spend (Triple Whale, 2025–26). Cheaper products sometimes do better, but even at $20 a customer you lose about $9 on each first order.
- **Repeat buyers don't close the gap:** small Shopify stores see a median **16.3%** of customers buy again within a year. That lifts a customer's value to roughly $13, still far below the cost of getting them.
- **Testing costs too much:** a meaningful test runs several hundred dollars a month or more. That's close to your whole monthly profit, and it's money you probably won't get back.

## Why moving to Shopify doesn't pay yet

- **Fee savings:** Shopify Payments takes 2.9% + $0.30, about $1.17 on a $30 order, versus about $3.30 in Etsy fees. That saves roughly $2.10 an order, or **$64 a month** across 30 orders.
- **Costs:** about $39 a month for the Basic plan (less if paid yearly; check current pricing), plus a domain and apps. That leaves about **$20 a month** at best, and only if all 30 orders followed you.
- **The real problem is traffic:** home goods stores convert about **1.29%** of visitors (Dynamic Yield via Shopify, 2026). Getting 30 orders would take about **2,300 visitors a month**. Etsy sends you those today; a new site wouldn't. Moving would likely cut your sales, not save fees.

## What to do instead

1. **Make each order bigger.** Offer a 2-pack around $44 and a 3-pack or gift set around $60. Extra candles in the same box add mostly materials, not more shipping or fees. Profit per order could reach $20–30, the range where ads start to make sense.
2. **Raise your single-candle price in about 10% steps** and watch whether your conversion rate holds. Handmade soy candles usually sell for more than $24.
3. **Build your own audience within Etsy's rules.** Use Instagram, TikTok or Pinterest videos of your pours, and local markets where you can collect emails. Check Etsy's current policy before putting your site in packages.
4. **Look at wholesale through Faire or local gift shops.** Candles reorder well: on Faire, 70% of retailers reorder within 90 days.

## When each move makes sense

- **Shopify:** when you can send it traffic yourself, roughly 1,000+ email subscribers or an active social following. Run it alongside Etsy and keep both.
- **Meta ads:** when profit per order is at least ~$25 (an order value around $45–50) and you've seen how many customers come back. Spend no more per new customer than that profit.
