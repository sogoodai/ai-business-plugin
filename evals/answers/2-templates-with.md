## Your idea: a quarterly tax and income tracker for US freelancers

Make a Google Sheets template (one that also works in Excel) for freelancers, people who get 1099 forms instead of a paycheck. They log income and expenses, and the sheet estimates what they owe each quarter, so the April tax bill doesn't surprise them.

**Why this one:**
- **The buyer is easy to name.** "A freelancer who got hit with a surprise tax bill." Specific products sell much better than general ones. Sellers report generic planners and budget trackers barely selling, while narrow trackers for one situation sell from the first month.
- **People search for it on a deadline.** Quarterly payments are due Jan 15, Apr 15, Jun 15 and Sep 15. Today is Nov 24, so if you launch by about Dec 20 you catch New Year budgeting, the January deadline and tax season.
- **It can sell again every year.** Tax brackets change, so you can release a "2027 edition."
- **It's easy to build in 5 hours a week.** It's formulas, not design work, and you can build it yourself without AI.

**The downside:** demand drops in summer, and tax numbers have to be right. Label it "an estimate, not tax advice." Cover federal income tax plus self-employment tax, and have the buyer type in their own state's rate rather than building state rules yourself.

## Where to sell it

- **Etsy first.** You have no audience, and Etsy has buyers already searching.
- **Pinterest for free traffic.** Personal finance does well there. Post 3–5 pins a week, each aimed at one search phrase, such as "freelancer quarterly tax tracker" or "1099 expense spreadsheet."
- **Later:** once you have an email list, add Gumroad or Payhip and send your own audience there, where the fees are lower.

## Pricing (net is what you keep after Etsy's US fees)

| Product | Price | You keep (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Mileage and expense log (cheap item that gets people into your shop) | $9 | $7.70 |
| Tax and income tracker (the main product) | $15 | $13.12 |
| Freelancer bundle: tracker, expense log and invoice/client tracker | $29 | $25.80 |

Spend most of your effort pushing the bundle. Below about $10, Etsy's fixed fees take a big share of each sale. If an order comes through Etsy's Offsite Ads, Etsy takes another 15% (so you'd keep about $21.45 on the $29 bundle). Price so you're still fine when that happens.

## What $500 a month takes

- **Orders:** about 20 bundles, or 38 trackers, or a realistic mix of about **28 orders a month (roughly 1 a day)**.
- **Traffic:** sellers report earning about $0.45–$0.63 per visitor when a product works. That means about **800–1,100 visitors a month** to your listings.
- **Products:** start with 3–5 listings, then build more around whatever sells. Good next versions are the tracker tailored to one type of worker, like rideshare drivers, photographers or Etsy sellers.
- **Time to get there:** usually 4–8 months, and some shops never do. Earnings in this space go mostly to a small number of sellers, so treat $500 as a target, not a promise.

## Your $200

- **About $15:** buy the best-selling competing tracker on Etsy and study it.
- **About $5:** Etsy fees ($0.20 per listing, plus any one-time setup fee).
- **About $80–100:** pay a tax preparer (a CPA or enrolled agent) on Upwork to check your formulas. The math being right is your biggest risk, and "checked by a CPA" is also a selling point.
- **Keep the rest.** Only spend it on Etsy Ads (about $1 a day) after a listing is already selling without ads. New shops usually lose money on ads.

## The first 4 weeks at 5 hours a week

1. **This week:** search Etsy for 10 phrases. Note how many reviews the top listings have, and look for searches where the results are weak. Choose the main version and one or two profession-specific versions.
2. **Weeks 2–3:** build the tracker. Include a "How to use" tab, sample data, locked formulas and a "make a copy" link. Make the listing images: a dashboard filled with numbers, a GIF of numbers changing as you type, and "DIGITAL DOWNLOAD" in both the image and the first line.
3. **Week 4:** list 3 products and the bundle. Add a delivery PDF with a 3-step setup guide so buyers don't have to message you. Start posting pins.
4. **After that, use these rules:** if a listing gets fewer than 1 click per 100 views, change the main image and title. If it gets clicks but no sales after a few hundred visits, change the preview or the price, not the spreadsheet.

If you're outside the US, the same plan works with your country's version, such as a UK Self Assessment tracker.
