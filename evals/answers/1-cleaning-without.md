# 60-day plan: 10 new recurring clients for under $1,000

**Timing:** today is Nov 24, so the 60 days run to about Jan 23. That window includes the pre-holiday cleaning rush, a slow week around Christmas, and New Year's, when a lot of people decide to get organized. The plan is built around those three periods.

**How many leads you need:** suppose roughly half of people who get a quote book a first clean, and about 60% of first cleans turn into recurring service. Then 10 recurring clients takes about **35 real leads** and 17 first cleans. Your best channels are already referrals and Google, so most of the effort and money goes there.

**Check capacity first:** 10 biweekly clients adds about 5 cleans a week, or roughly 15–20 hours for two people. Before you start, decide which days and neighborhoods have open slots. Booking new clients near your current ones keeps drive time down.

---

## Week 1 (Nov 24–30): Setup. Thanksgiving week, so keep it light.
- **Set your offer.** Example: "$40 off your first deep clean when you book biweekly. No contract. If anything's missed, we come back free within 24 hours." Make the recurring discount clear, for example biweekly at 15% less than one-time.
- **Fix up your Google Business Profile.** This is free and the most important item this week.
  - Add 15–20 recent photos: before/after shots, the two of you, your supplies.
  - List each service separately: recurring, deep clean, move-out.
  - Make sure the service area lists your exact neighborhoods.
  - Reply to all 23 reviews, especially any under 5 stars. Read those low reviews closely; they show what makes people hesitate.
- **Apply for Google Local Services Ads** (the "Google Guaranteed" listings shown above the map). The background check and verification can take 1–3 weeks, so start now.
- **Make three lists:** current clients, past clients who stopped, and people who got a quote but never booked.
- **Set up simple tracking.** A spreadsheet with columns for name, source, date, quoted, first clean, and recurring (yes/no) is enough.

## Week 2 (Dec 1–7): Referrals and reviews. Expect your most clients from this week.
- **Start a referral program.** The referrer gets $50 off a cleaning after the new client's second visit, and the new client gets $40 off their first. You only pay when it works.
- **Text every current client personally.** Don't send a mass email. For example:
  > "Hi Sarah! We're opening up a few spots for new biweekly clients before the holidays. If you know anyone who'd love a clean house, send them our way. You'll get $50 off your next cleaning and they'll get $40 off their first. Thank you for being one of our favorites!"
- **Contact past clients:** "We have openings in December and would love to have you back. Holiday deep clean is $X." Former clients are the easiest people to win back.
- **Ask for reviews.** Text happy clients your direct review link. Try to get from 23 to 35+ reviews in 60 days. More recent reviews help your Google ranking as much as the star average.
- **Post on Nextdoor.** Set up a free business page and ask your happy clients to recommend you there. One recommendation from a neighbor is worth more than an ad.

## Week 3 (Dec 8–14): Door hangers and ads go live
- **Door hangers:** print 750–1,000 for about $150 and place them only on streets near your current clients. Message: *"We clean homes on your street every other Tuesday. Holiday deep clean special, book by Dec 20."* Do 100–150 a day while walking between jobs or in the evening.
- **Turn on Local Services Ads** once approved, with a budget of about $100/week. You pay per lead, not per click. In Austin, expect around $25–50 per lead.
- **Reply to every lead within 5 minutes.** A quick reply matters more than anything else here, because whoever responds first usually gets the job. Quote by text with a price range and offer two specific time slots.

## Week 4 (Dec 15–21): Turn holiday cleans into recurring clients
- **Ask every one-time holiday client before you leave:** "Want us to keep it like this? We have a biweekly slot on Thursdays." Offer to lock in the discounted rate if they book by New Year's.
- **Follow up on every quote** that hasn't booked, once by text.
- **Join 2–3 Austin neighborhood or parent Facebook groups** and answer the "anyone have a cleaner recommendation?" posts. Better yet, ask a client to tag you.

## Week 5 (Dec 22–28): Slow week
- **Promote gift cards** to current clients: "Give someone a clean house." Recipients often become recurring clients.
- **Cut ad spend** to about $50 this week.
- **Prepare the January campaign:** a new door hanger design, a Nextdoor post, and an email.

## Week 6 (Dec 29–Jan 4): New Year campaign
- **Message:** "Start 2027 with a clean house every other week. First clean $40 off, no contract."
- **Send it to everyone:** past clients, quotes that didn't book, the Nextdoor page, and Facebook groups.
- **Return ad spend** to about $100/week. Searches for recurring cleaning go up in January.

## Weeks 7–8 (Jan 5–18): Do more of what's working
- **Check the spreadsheet** to see which source produced recurring clients, not just leads.
- **Move the remaining budget** to the top channel. That usually means a second round of door hangers near new clients, or more ad spend.
- **Send a second referral reminder,** especially to clients who just paid their January invoice.

## Week 9 (Jan 19–23): Review results
- Count new recurring clients by source.
- Whatever worked becomes your ongoing system: the referral program and review requests continue permanently.

**Skip Thumbtack and Angi.** Their leads tend to want the lowest price for a one-time clean, which is the opposite of what you're looking for.

**The two habits with the biggest payoff:** reply to every lead within minutes, and ask every happy client for a referral and a review. If you do those two things consistently, 10 clients is very achievable.
