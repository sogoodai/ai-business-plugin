# Rental property tracker spreadsheets for small landlords

## The idea

Sell a **Google Sheets and Excel template for landlords who own 1–10 units**. It tracks rent collected, expenses by tax category, late payments, a tenant directory and maintenance logs, plus a year-end summary they can give their accountant.

**Why this one and not budget planners or habit trackers:**
- **The buyers have a real problem.** A landlord tracking rent and receipts in a shoebox will pay $20 to fix it without much thought. Someone buying a cute daily planner won't.
- **It's less crowded.** Budget and planner printables are flooded with thousands of near-identical and AI-made listings. Landlord templates have competition, but it's thinner and the quality is often poor.
- **The timing is good.** Landlords look for help at tax time, from January to April. If you launch in the next 4–6 weeks you're ready before the busiest season.
- **One product grows into a range.** You can add a multi-property version, a short-term rental (Airbnb) version, a tenant screening checklist and a lease-renewal tracker. More listings means more chances to be found in search.

## Where to sell

**Start with Etsy.** It has buyers searching for "rental property spreadsheet" right now, and as a new seller with no audience you need that traffic. Fees are roughly $0.20 per listing, a 6.5% transaction fee and about 3% + $0.25 for payment processing. Plan on losing **10–12% per sale** to fees.

**Add Payhip or Gumroad later, around month 3.** Use it for a link you control, which you can share on Reddit (r/realestateinvesting, r/landlord), in BiggerPockets forums, or in a free "landlord tax checklist" you give away.

## What $500/month takes

- **Revenue:** $500 net ÷ about 88% left after fees ≈ **$570 gross per month**.
- **Sales:** at an average of about $21 per sale, that's **about 27 sales a month, or roughly 1 a day**.
- **Traffic:** Etsy digital listings typically convert about 1–3% of views. You'd need **about 1,000–2,500 listing views a month**.
- **Listings:** that much traffic usually takes **15–25 listings** (the same product in different variations and for different keywords), several of them with reviews.
- **Time:** **6–9 months** is realistic. Expect a few sales in month 1, $100–200 in months 3–4 (helped by tax season), and $500 around months 6–9 if you keep adding listings. Many shops never reach this point, and the usual reason is that they stop at 3 listings.

## Your 5 hours a week

- **Weeks 1–2: research and build the main template.**
  - Read the 1–3 star reviews on competing listings. Those complaints are your feature list.
  - Make the template genuinely easy to use: a dropdown for each tax category, a dashboard that fills itself in, and clear instructions.
- **Week 3: create 7–10 listing images and a 15-second screen-recording video.** Video noticeably improves conversion. Your first image should show the dashboard, not a laptop mockup.
- **Week 4: launch 3–5 listings.** Put keywords in titles and tags, for example "landlord spreadsheet," "rental income tracker," "property management template google sheets," and "Schedule E" (the US tax form landlords use for rental income).
- **Ongoing: 1 new listing or variant a week**, plus about an hour checking which search terms bring views and adjusting titles to match.
